B16001B Driver frontal airbag not connected

DTC description

B16001B Driver frontal airbag not connected

Fault symptoms

The SRS partially fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Clock spring fault

  3. Driver airbag fault

  4. Airbag controller fault.

Fault setting conditions

Driver frontal airbag not connected

Fault triggering conditions

The airbag controller receives the driver frontal airbag disconnection signal, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the airbag DTC
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the airbag interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the airbag interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check for the same DTC.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of clock spring.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector (airbag side) from the clock spring.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector (airbag side) of clock spring is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of clock spring.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G13 from the clock spring.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of clock spring is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of airbag controller.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG10 from the airbag controller.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of airbag controller is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the driver airbag circuit is short-circuited to GND.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 1 of clock spring and GND.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 2 of clock spring and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    Wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 1 of clock spring GND Always Above 10 kΩ
    Wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 2 of clock spring
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check whether the driver airbag circuits are short-circuited to each other.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector terminal 1 of clock spring and the wiring harness connector (driver airbag side) terminal 2 of airbag.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    Wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 1 of clock spring Wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 2 of clock spring Always Above 10 kΩ
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check whether the internal circuit of clock spring is open-circuited.
  1. Separate the short-circuiting bar of the wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminals 1 and 2 of clock spring with a thin plastic sheet.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 1 of clock spring and the wiring harness connector G13-1 of clock spring.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 2 of clock spring and the wiring harness connector G13-2 of clock spring.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    Wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 1 of clock spring Wiring harness connector G13-1 of clock spring Always Below 1 Ω
    Wiring harness connector (airbag side) terminal 2 of clock spring Wiring harness connector G13-2 of clock spring
  4. Check whether the wiring harness and connector are normal.
    5. Replace the clock spring.
8
Check whether the circuit from the airbag controller to the clock spring is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector KG10-44 of airbag controller and the wiring harness connector G13-1 of clock spring.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector KG10-28 of airbag controller and the wiring harness connector G13-2 of clock spring.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    KG10-44 G13-1 Always Below 1 Ω
    KG10-28 G13-2
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
9
Replace the driver airbag and check the DTC.
  1. Replace the driver airbag and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Read the DTC of SRS.
  4. Clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC of SRS.
  7. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    8. The system is normal. Replace the airbag controller.
