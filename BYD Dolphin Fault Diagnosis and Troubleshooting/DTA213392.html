Conventional fault diagnosis

Visual inspection

  1. Confirm the customer's problems.

  2. Visually inspect for obvious signs of mechanical or electrical damage and obvious signs of collision deformation.

  3. Check the system circuit that is easy to see or can be seen.

  4. If the problem observed or raised is obvious and the cause has been found, the cause must be corrected before proceeding to the next step.

  5. If the visual inspection is passed, identify the fault and refer to the Fault Symptoms Table.

 Failure of the compressor to work

Abnormal sound when the A/C is turned on

  1. fault of compressor.

  2. The blower.

  3. The cooling fan.

  1. If an abnormal sound is heard when the A/C is turned on, please check whether the compressor bearing is damaged or whether the compressor is worn internally.

  2. If an abnormal sound is heard when the A/C is turned off and the blower is turned on, check the blower for foreign matter or internal wear.

  3. If an abnormal sound occurs when the cooling fan is started, check for any interference between the cooling fan and other components or the abnormal wear of the fan.
