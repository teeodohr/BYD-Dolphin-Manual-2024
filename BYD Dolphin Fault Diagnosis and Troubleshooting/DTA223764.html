Diagnostic instructions

The window regulation system features window regulation, automatic window regulation, power window delay and anti-pinch functions. It consists of a power window switch and a power window motor.

For any fault of the window regulation system, it is necessary to understand and get familiar with the working principle of the memory system before diagnosis. If the cause of the fault cannot be found through visual inspection, please talk patiently with customers who complain about the system. Customers are the best source of information for such faults, especially for intermittent faults. The fault symptoms and occurrence can be found out through conversation with customers. This is also helpful to determine whether the situation described by the customer is normal. In addition, please check the relevant service bulletin information.

It is more difficult to diagnose intermittent faults than continuous faults. Most intermittent faults are caused by poor electrical connections or faulty circuits. Therefore, an inspection of the suspected faulty circuit can help avoid replacing a functioning part. In addition to checking the relevant circuits, it is also important to check the system mechanically.

General-purpose equipment

  • Socket wrench set

  • Screwdriver

  • Pry plate for interior trimming

  • Hexalobular socket bit

  • Torque wrench

  • VDS

  • Multimeter

Reminder

  • When measuring the voltage with a multimeter, select the range that is closest to the voltage.

  • When measuring the resistance with a multimeter, select the range that is closest to the resistance.

Precautions

Warning

  • Do not use sharp tools to remove interior trimming.

  • To avoid damage, do not remove the interior trimming by force

  • Do not pull the wiring harness when disconnecting the connector, so as to prevent damage to the wiring harness.

Caution

  • When you need to enter the vehicle to work, be sure to cover the seats and other parts to avoid contaminating them with oil.

  • After installation, the fasteners must be checked according to the specified torque.

  • The interior trimming should be removed with a pry plate for interior trimming. If the pry plate is unavailable, a screwdriver or an equivalent tool can be used instead, but it should be wrapped.
