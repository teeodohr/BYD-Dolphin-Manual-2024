P1EC200 Too high voltage at low-voltage side during voltage step-down

DTC description

P1EC200 Too high voltage at low-voltage side during voltage step-down
Fault symptoms Low-voltage power supply system fault.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. 12 V battery fault.

  2. DC internal fault.
Fault setting conditions Low-voltage side voltage exceeds the specified threshold.
Fault triggering conditions After the vehicle is powered on, when the system detects that the low-voltage side voltage is greater than the specified threshold, a DTC will be generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of DC-DC assembly.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check for the same DTC.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the voltage of DC output wiring harness of integrated front drive controller.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the DC output positive pole connector.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  4. Measure the voltage between DC output positive pole and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    DC output positive pole GND Always 11~14 V
  5. Check whether the result is normal.
    6. Replace the integrated front drive controller.
3
Check the voltage of 12 V battery.
  1. Measure whether the voltage of 12 V battery is normal.
    2. Replace the 12 V battery.
