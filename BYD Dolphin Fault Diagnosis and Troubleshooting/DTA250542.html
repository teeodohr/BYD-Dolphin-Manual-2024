P157B00 Overcurrent on AC side

DTC description

P157B00 Overcurrent on AC side
Fault symptoms Unable to charge or discharge.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Overcurrent on AC side

  2. Fault of on-board power supply assembly.
Fault setting conditions The current on AC side is greater than the specified threshold.
Fault triggering conditions When the vehicle is in AC charging state or in discharging state, the system detects that the current on AC side is greater than the specified threshold, and generates a DTC.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check whether the interior socket can discharge normally.
  1. Connect the discharging equipment externally.
  2. Check whether the discharging is normal.
    3. Go to Step 5.Go to Step 5.
2
Check the DTC of on-board power supply assembly.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  5. Connect an external AC charging equipment for charging test.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  7. Check for the same DTC.
    8. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
3
Check the AC charging port.
  1. Check whether there is any foreign matter inside the charging port.
  2. Check the AC charging port terminal for water ingress, deformation, erosion, etc.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Clean foreign objects or replace the AC charging port.
4
Check the external AC charging equipment.
  1. Replace the external AC charging equipment to the vehicle for charging test.
  2. Check whether the vehicle can be charging normally.
    3. Replace the external AC charging equipment. Replace the on-board power supply assembly.
5
Check the AC charging port.
  1. Check whether there is any foreign matter inside the charging port.
  2. Check the AC charging port terminal for water ingress, deformation, erosion, etc.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Clean foreign objects or replace the AC charging port.
6
Check for short circuit on AC side.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports L and N.
  3. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports L and PE.
  4. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports N and PE.
    AC charging port Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    L N Always Above 10 KΩ
    L PE
    N
  5. Check whether the result is normal.
    6. Replace the interior socket assembly.
7
Check the wiring harness and connector of on-board power supply assembly.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the on-board power assembly.
  2. Check the wiring harness and connector for deformation, erosion, water ingress, etc.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
8
Check the on-board power assembly for internal short circuit.
  1. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports L and N.
  2. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports L and PE.
  3. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports N and PE.
    AC charging port Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    L N Always Above 10 KΩ
    L PE
    N
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace the on-board power supply assembly.
9
Check the wiring harness and connector of interior socket assembly.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector from the interior socket assembly.
  2. Check the wiring harness and connector for deformation, erosion, water ingress, etc.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
10
Check the interior socket assembly for short circuit.
  1. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports L and N.
  2. Measure the resistance between the AC charging ports L and PE.

    5. Interior socket assembly. Replace the AC charging port.
