Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the left domain control unit passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 8.Go to Step 8.
2
Check the fuse of positive fuse box.
  1. Check whether the fuse F4/03(200A) of positive fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connectors KG64(A)_4, BG64(B) and KG64(J) from the left domain control unit.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the power supply of left domain control unit.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector KG64(A)_4-1 of left domain connector and GND.

    Connector

    		 Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    KG64(A)_4-1

    		 GND Always 11~14 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Go to Step 6.Go to Step 6.
5
Check whether the left domain control unit power supply is open-circuited.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KB64_C from the instrument fuse box.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector KG64(A)_4-1 of left domain control unit and the wiring harness connector KB64_C-1 of instrument fuse box.

    Connector

    		 Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)

    KG64(A)_4-1

    KB64_C-1

    		 Always Below 1 Ω
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the positive fuse box.
6
Check the GND of left domain control unit.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BG64(B)-2/12 of left domain control unit and GND.

    Connector

    		 Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)

    BG64(B)-2

    		 GND Always Below 1 Ω

    BG64(B)-12
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check the CAN circuit of left domain control unit.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector KG64(J)-44/45 of left domain control unit and GND.

    Connector

    		 Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    KG64(J)-44

    		 GND Always

    1.5~2.5 V

    KG64(J)-45

    		 2.5~3.5 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Go to "Gateway system" diagnosis. Replace the left domain control unit.
8
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Read the DTC of left domain control unit with the VDS.
  2. Check whether there is any DTC in the result.
    3. Perform diagnosis according to the DTC.
9
Check the DTC of EPB controller.
  1. Read the DTC of EPB controller with the VDS.
  2. Check whether there is any DTC in the result.
    3. Go to "EPB system" diagnosis.
10
Check the DTCs of other modules.
  1. Is any fault in communication with BCM read from other modules?
    2. Replace the left domain control unit. Replace the EPB.
