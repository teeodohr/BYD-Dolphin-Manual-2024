B22A800 Exterior detection antenna circuit fault (rear)

DTC description

B22A800 Exterior detection antenna circuit fault (rear)

Fault symptoms

Partial fault of smart access and start system.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fault of wiring harness or wiring harness connector.

  2. Fault of trunk lid detection antenna.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.

Fault setting conditions

The right trunk lid detection antenna is open-circuited.

Fault triggering conditions

The left domain control unit receives the fault signal of the trunk lid detection antenna, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of the trunk lid detection antenna
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K26 from the trunk lid detection antenna.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the trunk lid detection antenna is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG64(J) from the left domain control unit.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the wiring harness of the trunk lid detection antenna is short-circuited to the power supply

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K26-1/2 of the trunk lid detection antenna and GND.

6
Replace the trunk lid detection antenna and check the DTC

  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. The system is normal. Replace the left domain control unit.
