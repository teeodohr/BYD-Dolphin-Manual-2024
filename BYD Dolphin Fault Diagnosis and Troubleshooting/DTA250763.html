Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of rear domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the rear domain control unit interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the rear domain control unit interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the fuse of instrument fuse box.
  1. Check whether the instrument fuse box fuses F2/13(30A) and F2/12(30A) are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of EPB.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K31 from the EPB.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of EPB is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the constant power supply of EPB.

  1. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K31-14/19 of EPB and GND.

5

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G82 from the instrument fuse box.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of instrument fuse box is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
