P1EC300 Too low voltage at low-voltage side during voltage step-down

DTC description

P1EC300 Too low voltage at low-voltage side during voltage step-down
Fault symptoms Low-voltage power supply system fault.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. 12 V battery fault.

  2. DC low voltage output circuit fault.

  3. DC internal fault.
Fault setting conditions Low-voltage side voltage is less than the specified threshold.
Fault triggering conditions After the vehicle is powered on, when the system detects that the low-voltage side voltage is less than the specified threshold, a DTC will be generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of DC-DC assembly.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the DC-DC assembly interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  7. Check for the same DTC.
    8. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the state of 12 V battery.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Test the status of 12 V battery.
  3. Check whether the 12 V battery passes the test.
    4. Replace the 12 V battery.
3
Check the voltage of DC output wiring harness of integrated controller.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G01F of integrated controller.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Measure the voltage between DC output of integrated controller and GND.

    Integrated controller

    		 Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    DC output of integrated controller

    		 GND Always 11~14 V
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace the integrated controller. Start the vehicle by connecting external power, make DC-DC work, charge the 12 V battery and replace it if necessary.
