B2A4B12 Circulation control motor short-circuited to power supply

DTC description

B2A4B12 Circulation control motor short-circuited to power supply
Fault symptoms All A/C system functions fail.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Harness or connector is faulty.

  2. Circulation control motor is faulty.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions If the circulation control motor is short-circuited to other power supplies, this DTC will be generated in continuous memory and as needed.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of circulation control motor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G22 (C) from circulation control motor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of circulation control motor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the circulation control motor.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector pins of circulation control motor.
