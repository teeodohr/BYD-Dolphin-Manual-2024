B167011 Right side collision sensor short-circuited to GND

DTC description

B167011 Right side collision sensor short-circuited to GND

Fault symptoms

The SRS partially fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Right B-pillar side collision sensor fault.

  3. Airbag controller fault.

Fault setting conditions

Right B-pillar side collision sensor short-circuited to GND.

Fault triggering conditions

The airbag controller receives the signal that the right B-pillar side collision sensor is short-circuited to GND, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the airbag DTC
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the airbag interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for several seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the airbag interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check for the same DTC.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of right side collision sensor
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K05(B) of right side collision sensor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of right side collision sensor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of airbag controller
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG10 from the airbag controller.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness and connector of airbag controller are normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the circuit of right side collision sensor is short-circuited to GND

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K05(B)-1 of right side collision sensor and GND.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K05(B)-2 of right side collision sensor and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    K05(B)-1 GND Always Above 10 kΩ
    K05(B)-2
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Replace the right side collision sensor and check the DTC
  1. Replace the right side collision sensor and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Read the DTC of SRS.
  4. Clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC of SRS.
  7. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    8. The system is normal. Replace the airbag controller.
