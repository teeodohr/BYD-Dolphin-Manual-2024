B2A4B92 Failure of circulation control motor to rotate into position

DTC description

B2A4B92 Failure of circulation control motor to rotate into position
Fault symptoms The interior and exterior circulation function of the A/C system fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Circulation control motor is faulty.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions This DTC is generated when the left domain control unit detects that the target signal voltage set by the circulation control motor is inconsistent with the actual feedback voltage.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the mechanical structure of circulation damper.
  1. Remove the circulation damper motor.
  2. Check whether air control linkages and doors can move freely.
    3. Repair or replace them as necessary.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of circulation damper motor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G22 (C) from circulation damper motor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of circulation damper motor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the feedback power supply of circulation damper motor.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector G22 (C)-4 of circulation damper motor and the wiring harness connector G22 (C)-6 of circulation damper motor.
