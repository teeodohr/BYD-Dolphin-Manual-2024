Conventional fault diagnosis

Visual inspection

  1. Confirm the customer's problems.

  2. Visually inspect for obvious signs of mechanical damage and collision deformation.

  3. If the problem observed or raised is obvious and the cause has been found, the cause must be corrected before proceeding to the next step.

  4. If the visual inspection is passed, identify the fault and refer to the Fault Symptoms Table.

Conventional Fault Symptoms Table

Symptoms Possible causes Recommended maintenance measures
Abnormal sound of transmission shaft

  1. Insufficient lubrication of transmission shaft universal joint

  2. Contact between transmission shaft and other components

  1. Check the transmission shaft for dust cover damage, lubricating oil leakage and other phenomena. If abnormal, repair the faulty part.

  2. Check whether the transmission shaft is entangled by other sundries and in contact with other components. If abnormal, remove the sundries or repair the faulty part.

Vibration during high-speed driving

  1. Wheels out of balance

  2. Excessive radial runout of front wheel

  3. Incorrect installation of transmission shaft

  1. Balance the wheels.

  2. Replace or repair the wheel.

  3. Reinstall or replace the transmission shaft.
