U027E04 BIC4 cascade communication fault

DTC description

U027E04 BIC4 cascade communication fault

Fault symptoms

The instrument displays "Powertrain fault", and charging/discharging is prohibited.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. High-voltage battery pack fault

  3. Integrated front drive controller fault

Fault setting conditions

No message of BIC is received continuously.

Fault triggering conditions

No message of BIC is received continuously, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the battery execution and sampling unit interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for several seconds.
  6. Check for the same DTC.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of high-voltage battery pack.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK51 from the high-voltage battery pack.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of high-voltage battery pack is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check whether there is voltage input in the power circuit of the high-voltage battery pack chip.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK51-4 of high-voltage battery pack and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    BK51-4 GND Always 11~14 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the high-voltage battery pack.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of integrated front drive controller.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK45(A) from the integrated front drive controller.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the integrated front drive controller is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the power circuit of the high-voltage battery pack chip is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BK45(A)-1 of integrated front drive controller, the wiring harness connector BK51-4 of high-voltage battery pack and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    BK45(A)-1 BK51-4 Always Below 1 Ω
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the repair kit of drive MCU assembly.
