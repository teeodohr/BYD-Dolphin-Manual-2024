U111786 Private CAN message signal invalid

DTC description

U111786 Private CAN message signal invalid
Fault symptoms The adaptive cruise control system fails.
Possible causes of the fault Private CAN message signal invalid
Fault setting conditions Private CAN message signal invalid
Fault triggering conditions

  1. The controller voltage ranges from 9 V to 16 V.

  2. The vehicle is powered on and initialized for 3 s.

  3. The private CAN is not busoff.

  4. The factory mode is disabled.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of front mmWave radar.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the front mmWave radar interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the front mmWave radar interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the fuse of multi-purpose camera.
  1. Check whether the fuse F1/39 (5 A) of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of multi-purpose camera.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector P13 from the multi-purpose camera.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of multi-purpose camera for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the power supply of multi-purpose camera.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector P13-1 of multi-purpose camera and GND.

    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness connector.
5
Check whether the GND circuit of multi-purpose camera is open-circuited.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector P13-5 of multi-purpose camera and GND.

6
Connect the wiring harness and connector of front mmWave radar.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector DB60(C) from the front mmWave radar.
  2. Check the wiring harness connector of front mmWave radar for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check whether the private CAN circuit is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector P13-10 of multi-purpose camera and the wiring harness connector DB60(C)-5 of front mmWave radar.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector P13-11 of multi-purpose camera and the wiring harness connector DB60(C)-6 of front mmWave radar.

    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
8
Check whether the private CAN circuit is short-circuited to GND.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector P13-10 of multi-purpose camera and body GND.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector P13-11 of multi-purpose camera and body GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
9
Check whether the private CAN circuit is open-circuited to power supply.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector P13-10 of multi-purpose camera and body GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector P13-11 of multi-purpose camera and body GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    P13-10 GND Always Not present
    P13-11
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
10
Check the multi-purpose camera
  1. Replace the multi-purpose camera with a normal one and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the multi-purpose camera. Replace the front mmWave radar.
