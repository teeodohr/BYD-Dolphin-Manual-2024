Insufficient refrigeration of A/C

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the temperature at the A/C air outlet.
  1. Adjust the air volume to a low wind speed.
  2. Turn on the recirculation mode of the A/C.
  3. Measure the temperature at the A/C air outlet with a thermometer.
    Position Condition Temperature value
    Air outlet Always 5~8 ℃
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. The system is normal.
2
Check the condenser.
  1. Check the condenser for excessive fouling.
    2. Clean or replace the condenser.
3
Check the refrigerant pressure of the A/C system.
  1. Connect the refrigerant recovery and filling machine, turn the Start/Stop switch to OK position, and turn on the A/C.
  2. Check the refrigerant pressure of the A/C system.
    Pressure gauge Condition Pressure value
    System pressure Turn off the A/C refrigeration mode 0.73~0.83 MPa
    High pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 1.47~1.67 MPa
    Low pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 0.15~0.25 MPa
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Check the distribution of A/C mode dampers.
4
Check the A/C piping for leakage.
  1. Vacuumize the A/C piping with a refrigerant recovery and filling machine (for about 30 min).
  2. Close the manual valves of high and low-pressure gauges on the refrigerant recovery and filling machine.
  3. Wait for 20 minutes and observe the reading of the pressure gauge. If the reading increases by more than 2 kPa, there is a leakage in the A/C system.
  4. Check the A/C system for leakage.
    5. Use electronic leak detection or fluorescent dye leak detection methods to troubleshoot and replace the relevant tubing.Fill refrigerant, and replace the electric compressor if the refrigeration fails to meet the standard.
