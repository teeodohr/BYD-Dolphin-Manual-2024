B134711 A/C pressure sensor short-circuited to power supply

DTC description

B134711 A/C pressure sensor short-circuited to power supply
Fault symptoms The A/C system partially fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. A/C pressure sensor is faulty.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions If the A/C pressure sensor circuit is short-circuited to other power supplies, this DTC will be generated in continuous memory and as needed.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the pressure feedback signal of A/C pressure sensor.
  1. Connect the wiring harness connector B13 of A/C pressure sensor.
  2. Measure the voltage between the pressure feedback signal B13-3 of A/C pressure sensor and GND from the rear end.

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the left domain control unit.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of A/C pressure sensor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B13 from A/C pressure sensor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of A/C pressure sensor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG64(G) from the left domain control unit.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of domain control unit is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the power supply circuit of the A/C pressure sensor is short-circuited to power supply.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between wiring harness connector B13-1 of A/C pressure sensor and GND.
