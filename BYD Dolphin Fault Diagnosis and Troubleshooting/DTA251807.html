Precautions for checking control modules and electrical parts

Caution

  • Turn off all electrical appliances before disconnecting the negative terminal of the 12 V battery.

  • Before performing electrical operations, power off the vehicle, disconnect the negative terminal of the 12 V battery and wait for 2 minutes to let the backup power supply of the airbag module run out.

  • Disconnecting the negative terminal of the 12 V battery may delete the memories of the clock, audio system, and DTC. Therefore, it is necessary to confirm these memories before disconnecting the negative terminal.

  • Install genuine parts of the OEM only.

  • Never reversely connect the terminals of the 12 V battery.

  1. Check the input and output of relevant parts and their functions before replacing the control module.

    1. When disconnecting parts:

      • Do not apply too much force when disconnecting a connector.

      • If the connector is installed with fastening bolts, loosen the bolts and then disconnect the connector.

    2. When connecting parts:

      • Before installing a connector, make sure that its terminal is not bent or damaged before proper connection.

      • When installing the connector with fastening bolts, tighten the mounting bolts to the specified torques.

    3. Do not drop or hit the control module, in order to avoid damage.

    4. Pay attention to avoid condensation in the control module due to rapid temperature change, and avoid water droplets or raindrops. For any water on the control module, dry the control module completely before installing it on the vehicle.

    5. Be careful not to drop any oil/fluid on the control module connector.

    6. Avoid cleaning the control module with volatile oil/fluid.

    7. When using a multimeter, be careful not to allow its test probes to touch each other, thereby causing a short circuit. Avoid damaging the 12 V battery due to a short circuit of the power transistor inside the control module.

    8. Use the specified test adapter when checking input and output signals of the control module.

  2. Check the fuse.

    1. Check and confirm that the fuse wire is not disconnected.

    2. If the fuse wire is disconnected, check whether the circuit is short-circuited.

    3. When replacing the fuse, be sure to use a fuse with the same amperage rating.
