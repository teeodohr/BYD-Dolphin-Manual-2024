U01EA83 Private communication check error

DTC description

U01EA83 Private communication check error
Fault symptoms The intelligent driving assistance system fails.
Possible causes of the fault Private communication check error
Fault setting conditions 3 consecutive failures of any monitoring check
Fault triggering conditions

  1. The controller voltage ranges from 9 V to 16 V.

  2. The diagnosis start condition is 3 s after bus initialization.

  3. 3 s after power-on

  4. The private CAN is not busoff.

  5. The factory mode is disabled.

  6. 3 s after the DTC is enabled by the service

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of rear left corner radar.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the BSD interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the BSD interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the rear right corner radar fuse.
  1. Check whether the fuse F2/10(10A) of instrument fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of rear right corner radar.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KR07 from the rear right corner radar.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of rear right corner radar for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the power supply of rear right corner radar.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector KR07-1 of rear right corner radar and GND.
