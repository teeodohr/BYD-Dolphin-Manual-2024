Detection and repair of fuses

  1. Check the blown fuse line

    1. Remove the blown fuse, measure the resistance between the load end of the blown fuse and GND, and disconnect all circuit switches connected to the fuse. In this case, if the measured resistance is about 0 Ω, it indicates a short circuit somewhere between the switch and load. If the measured resistance is displayed as "OL", it indicates no short circuit, but the instantaneous overvoltage of the load may also cause the fuse to blowout.

      Reminder

      Main causes for short circuit include the following:

      • The outer surface of the wiring harness is damaged due to wear or overheating.

      • Water enters the connector.

      • There is a man-made fault.

    2. When replacing a fuse, make sure that the current rating of the new fuse is correct and never use a fuse above or below the current rating.
