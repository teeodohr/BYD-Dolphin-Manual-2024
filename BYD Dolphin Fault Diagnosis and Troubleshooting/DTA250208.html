U012787 Loss of communication between instrument and TPMS

DTC description

U012787 Loss of communication between instrument and TPMS

Fault symptoms

The tire pressure monitoring fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fuse fault.

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  3. Tire pressure monitoring controller fault

Fault setting conditions

Any monitoring message is lost 10 times consecutively.

Fault triggering conditions

  1. The controller voltage ranges from 9 V to 16 V.

  2. The vehicle is powered on and initialized for 3 s.

  3. The common CAN is not busoff.

  4. No power-off notification from BCM is received.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the tire pressure monitoring controller passes network detection.
    5. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the fuse of tire pressure monitoring controller.
  1. Check whether the fuse F2/11(7.5A) of instrument fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of tire pressure monitoring controller.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K33 from the tire pressure monitoring controller.
  3. Check whether the power terminal and connector of tire pressure monitoring controller are normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the constant power of tire pressure monitoring controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K33-1 of tire pressure monitoring controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    K33-1

    		 GND Always 11-14V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check the GND circuit of tire pressure monitoring controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K33-2 of tire pressure monitoring controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)

    K33-2

    		 GND Always Below 1 Ω
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check the CAN circuit of tire pressure monitoring controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K33-3 of tire pressure monitoring controller and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K33-4 of tire pressure monitoring controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    K33-3

    		 GND Always 1.5~2.5 V

    K33-4

    		 GND Always 2.5~3.5 V
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Go to "Gateway system" diagnosis. Replace the tire pressure monitoring controller.
