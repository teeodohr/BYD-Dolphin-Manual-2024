B1B5712 Front left corner sensor signal circuit short-circuited to power supply or not grounded

DTC description

B1B5712 Front left corner sensor signal circuit short-circuited to power supply or not grounded
Fault symptoms The parking assist system partially fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fault of wiring harness or wiring harness connector.

  2. Front left radar sensor fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions -
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the left domain control unit again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the front left radar sensor.
  1. Replace the front left radar sensor with a normal one and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the front left radar sensor.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of front left radar sensor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector DB11(A) from the front left radar sensor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of front left radar sensor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the signal circuit of front left radar sensor is short-circuited to power supply.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector DB11(A)-2 of front left radar sensor and GND.

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BG64(E) from the left domain control unit.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check whether the GND circuit of front left radar sensor is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the left domain control unit BG64(E)-10 and the wiring harness connector DB11(A)-1 of front left radar sensor.

    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the left domain control unit.
