B224913 Rear right window motor open-circuited

DTC description

B224913 Rear right window motor open-circuited

Fault symptoms

Failure of the rear right window to work

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Rear right window regulator motor fault

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault

Fault setting conditions

No current is detected when the window motor is driven.

Fault triggering conditions

  1. The controller voltage ranges from 9 V to 16 V.

  2. The window motor is driven.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  7. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    8. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the fuse of rear right window regulator motor
  1. Check whether the fuse F2/16(20A) of instrument fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse after checking that there is no short-circuit.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of rear right window regulator switch
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector W01_2 from the rear right window regulator switch.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of rear right window regulator switch for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the faulty wiring harness.
4
Check the constant power circuit of rear right window regulator

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector W01_2-4 of rear right window regulator switch and GND.

5

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G82 from the rear right window regulator motor.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of rear right window regulator motor for water ingress, corrosion, or damage.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the faulty wiring harness.
6

Check the rear right window regulator motor
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Replace the rear right window regulator motor with a normal one and restore the vehicle.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace the rear right window regulator motor. Replace the left domain control unit.
