How to deal with intermittent faults

Reminder

To determine the status of an intermittent fault, first ask the customer about driving conditions, weather conditions, fault occurrence frequency, and fault symptoms of the vehicle, and then try to simulate the fault. Determine whether the circuit under detection is damaged or normal; identify and eliminate the fault. Refer to the Conventional Fault Symptoms Table for each system to narrow down the scope of possible faults.

  1. Vibration method

    1. Gently vibrate the possibly faulty sensor with your fingers, and check whether the fault recurs.

    2. Gently shake the connector in the horizontal and vertical directions to see whether the fault recurs.

    3. Gently shake the wiring harness and connector in the horizontal and vertical directions to see whether the fault recurs.

  2. Heating method

    1. Heat the possibly faulty parts with an electric hair dryer or a similar article to see whether the fault recurs.

      Caution

      Do not damage the component structure when heating.

  3. Water spraying method

    1. Spray water on the vehicle to see whether the fault recurs.

      Caution

      • Do not spray water directly on the front compartment and high-voltage and low-voltage components.

      • Be careful when conducting a water spraying test on the vehicle, because water leakage of the vehicle may cause damage to some electrical components.

  4. Others

    1. Connect all loads to see whether the fault recurs.
