B2CCF16 Voltage too low

B2CCF16 Voltage too low
Fault symptoms Front millimetre-wave radar system fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. 12 V battery fault

  2. Blown fuse.

  3. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  4. On-board power supply system fault

  5. Front mmWave radar fault
Fault setting conditions Power supply voltage lower than 9 V for 1000 ms
Fault triggering conditions The vehicle is powered on and initialized for 3 s.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of front mmWave radar.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the front mmWave radar interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the front mmWave radar interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the on-board power supply system.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OK position.
  2. Check whether the MIL of the instrument low voltage power supply system illuminates.
    3. Go to "DC-DC assembly" diagnosis.
3
Test the state of the 12 V battery.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Test the state of the 12 V battery.
  3. Check whether the 12 V battery passes the test?
    4. Replace the 12 V battery.
4
Check the fuse of front mmWave radar.
  1. Check whether the fuse F1/39 (5 A) of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
5
Connect the wiring harness and connector of front mmWave radar.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector DB60(C) from the front mmWave radar.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of front mmWave radar is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check whether the GND of front mmWave radar is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector DB60(C)-4 of front mmWave radar and GND.
