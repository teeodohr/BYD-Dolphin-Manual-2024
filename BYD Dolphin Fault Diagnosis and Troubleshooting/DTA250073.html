P1BB800 Collision signal fault of front drive motor control unit

DTC description

P1BB800 Collision signal fault of front drive motor control unit

Fault symptoms

The instrument displays "EV function limited", and discharging is prohibited.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Airbag controller fault.

  3. Front drive motor control unit fault

Fault setting conditions

Collision signal fault received

Fault triggering conditions

When the vehicle is powered on, a collision signal fault is received, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the airbag passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 7.Go to Step 7.
2
Check the fuse of airbag controller
  1. Check whether the fuse F1/38(10A) of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of airbag controller
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG10 from the airbag controller.
  3. Check whether the connector of airbag controller is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the IG1 power supply of airbag controller

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector KG10-21 of airbag controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    KG10-21 GND Always 11~14 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Go to Step 6.Go to Step 6.
5
Check whether the power supply of airbag controller module is open-circuited

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1C from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector KG10-21 of airbag controller and the wiring harness connector B1C-27 of front compartment fuse box.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    KG10-21 B1C-27 Always Below 1 Ω
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the front compartment fuse box.
6
Check the GND circuit of airbag controller

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector KG10-20 of airbag controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    KG10-20 GND Always Below 1 Ω
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the airbag controller.
7

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector KG10-18 of airbag controller and GND.

  1. Read the airbag DTC with the VDS.
