Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the left domain control unit again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of the interior rear detection antenna
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K25 from the interior rear detection antenna.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the interior rear detection antenna is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG64(J) from the left domain control unit.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the interior rear detection antenna is open-circuited

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K25-1 of the interior rear detection antenna and the wiring harness connector KG64(J)-9 of the left domain control unit.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K25-2 of the interior rear detection antenna and the wiring harness connector KG64(J)-8 of the left domain control unit.
    Connector Condition Resistance

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the wiring harness of the interior rear detection antenna is short-circuited to the power supply

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K25-1/2 of the interior rear detection antenna and GND.

7
Replace the interior rear detection antenna and check the DTC.
  1. Replace the interior rear detection antenna and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. The system is normal. Replace the left domain control unit.
