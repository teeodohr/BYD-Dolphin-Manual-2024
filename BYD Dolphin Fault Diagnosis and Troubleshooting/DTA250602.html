Diagnostic instructions

Before diagnosing the fault of the vanity mirror light, understand and get familiar with its working principle, and then start diagnosis. In this way, it is helpful to determine the correct fault diagnosis steps in case of a fault, and more importantly, it is also helpful to determine whether the situation described by the customer is normal. Any fault diagnosis of the vanity mirror light should start with the inspection of the vanity mirror light. The maintenance personnel should be guided to take the next logical step for fault diagnosis.

General-purpose equipment

  • Multimeter

Reminder

  • When measuring the voltage with a multimeter, select the range that is closest to the voltage.

  • When measuring the resistance with a multimeter, select the range that is closest to the resistance.
