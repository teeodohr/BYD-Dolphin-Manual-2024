U014087 Fault in communication with BCM

DTC description

U014087 Fault in communication with BCM

Fault symptoms

-

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fuse fault.

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.

  4. Power domain control unit fault.

Fault setting conditions

Abnormal communication with BCM

Fault triggering conditions

When the vehicle is powered on, the power domain control unit does not receive the BCM message, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network of left domain control unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the left domain control unit passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 10.Go to Step 10.
2
Check the IG3 power supply fuse of left domain control unit.
  2. Check whether the fuse F1/12(7.5A) of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    3. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the constant power fuse of left domain control unit.
  2. Check whether the fuse F4/03(200A) of positive fuse box is normal.
    3. Replace the fuse.
4
Check the wiring harness and connector of left domain control unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the power terminals KG64(A)_4, BG64(B) and KG64(J) from the left domain control unit.
  3. Check whether the power terminal and connector of left domain control unit are normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check the constant power of left domain control unit.

  1. Measure the voltage between the power terminal KG64(A)_4-1 of left domain control unit and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    		 GND Always 11~14 V
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check the IG3 power supply of left domain control unit.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BG64(B)_32 of left domain control unit and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    BG64(B)_32

    		 GND Always 11~14 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Go to Step 8.Go to Step 8.
7
Check whether the power supply of left domain control unit module is open-circuited.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1D from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector B1D-9 of front compartment fuse box and the wiring harness connector BG64(B)-32 of left domain control unit.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BG64(B)-2 of left domain control unit and GND.

9
Check the CAN circuit of left domain control unit.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector KG64(J)-55 of left domain control unit and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector KG64(J)-56 of left domain control unit and GND.

    5. Go to "Gateway system" diagnosis. Replace the left domain control unit.
10
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit
  1. Read the DTC of left domain control unit with the VDS.
  2. Check for other DTCs.
    3. Go to "Left domain control unit" diagnosis.
11
Check the DTC of power domain control unit
  1. Read the DTC of power domain control unit with the VDS.
  2. Check for any DTC.
    3. Go to "Power domain control unit" diagnosis.
12
Check the DTCs of other modules
  1. Read the DTCs of other modules with the VDS.
  2. Check whether the DTC of fault in communication with BCM is read from other modules.
    3. Replace the left domain control unit. Replace the high-voltage battery pack.
