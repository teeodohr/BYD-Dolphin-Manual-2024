B2A2F09 A/C piping in high-pressure or low-pressure status

DTC description

B2A2F09 A/C piping in high-pressure or low-pressure status
Fault symptoms The compressor stops working, and some functions of the air conditioning system cannot be used.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. System refrigerant pressure is too high or too low.

  2. The cooling fan fails to work.
Fault setting conditions The left domain module receives an incorrect pressure signal.
Fault triggering conditions Power up the vehicle and turn on the refrigeration mode.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the cooling fan.
  1. Put the Start/Stop switch to OK position and turn on the A/C.
  2. Check whether the cooling fan works normally.
    3. NoDiagnose "radiator fan does not work".
2
Check the refrigerant pressure of A/C system.
  1. Connect the refrigerant recycling and filling machine, put the Start/Stop switch to OK position and turn on the A/C.
  2. Check the refrigerant pressure of A/C system.
    Pressure gauge Condition Pressure value
    System pressure Turn off the A/C refrigeration mode 0.73~0.83 MPa
    High pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 1.47~1.67 MPa
    Low pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 0.15~0.25 MPa
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Check the A/C for leakage and adjust the refrigerant pressure of the A/C system. Replace the A/C pressure sensor.
