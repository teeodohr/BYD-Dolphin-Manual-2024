B119700 Rear right turn signal fault

DTC description

B119700 Rear right turn signal fault

Fault symptoms

The rear right turn signal does not illuminate.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault

  2. Rear right turn signal fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the multi-function switch passes network detection.
    5. Determine a communication failure of the multi-function switch.
2
Actively control the rear right turn signal with the VDS.
  1. Actively control the rear right turn signal to illuminate.
  2. Can the rear right turn signal illuminate?
    3. Replace the multi-function switch.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of right taillight cluster.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K18 from the right taillight cluster.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of right taillight cluster is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the GND circuit of right taillight cluster is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K18-1 of right taillight cluster and GND.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG64(G) from the left domain control unit.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
