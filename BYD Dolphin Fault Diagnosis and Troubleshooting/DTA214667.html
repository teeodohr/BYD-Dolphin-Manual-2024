B24D800 Undervoltage of handle input power supply

DTC description

B24D800 Undervoltage of handle input power supply
Fault symptoms The multi-function switch fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. 12 V battery fault.

  2. Blown fuse.

  3. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  4. Charging system fault.

  5. Multi-function switch.
Fault setting conditions The supply voltage is lower than 9 V for ≥ 2 s.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of multi-function switch.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. The fault is triggered by the disconnected negative terminal of 12 V battery or low voltage.
2
Check the charging system.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OK position.
  2. Check whether the MIL of the instrument charging system illuminates.
    3. Go to "DC-DC assembly" diagnosis.
3
Test the status of 12 V battery.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Test the status of 12 V battery.
  3. Does the 12 V battery pass the test?
    4. Replace the 12 V battery.
4
Check the fuse of multi-function switch.
  1. Check whether the fuse F2/11(7.5A) of instrument fuse box and the fuse F1/37(7.5A) of front compartment fuse box are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
5
Check the wiring harness connector of multi-function switch.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G02 from the multi-function switch.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of multi-function switch is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check the constant power supply and IG1 power supply of multi-function switch.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
