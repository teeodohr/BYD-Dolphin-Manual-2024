B1CA271 Rear left door lock motor stalling

DTC description

B1CA271 Rear left door lock motor stalling

Fault symptoms

The rear left door lock fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault

  2. Rear left door lock fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault

Fault setting conditions

 Detected control circuit current ≥ 5 A when the rear left door lock works

Fault triggering conditions

 -

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the left domain control unit again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of rear left door lock motor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to "OFF" position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector V04 from the rear left door lock motor.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of rear left door lock motor for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG64(F) from the left domain control unit.
  2. Check the wiring harness connector of left domain control unit for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the rear left door lock motor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Replace the rear left door lock motor with a new one and restore the vehicle.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace the rear left door lock motor. Replace the left domain control unit.
