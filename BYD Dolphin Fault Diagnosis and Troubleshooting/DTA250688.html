B2A5811 Driver's face level vent temperature sensor short-circuited to GND

DTC description

B2A5811 Driver's face level vent temperature sensor short-circuited to GND
Fault symptoms The A/C system partially fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Driver's face level vent temperature sensor fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions Check that the sensor output voltage is lower than 0.1 V.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of driver's face level vent temperature sensor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector GaG75 of driver's face level vent temperature sensor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of driver's face level vent temperature sensor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the driver's face level vent temperature sensor.
  1. Measure the resistance between wiring harness connector pins of driver's face level vent temperature sensor.
    Connector Condition Lower limit (kΩ) Upper limit (kΩ)
    (+) (-)
    1 2 -20°C 14.82 16.38

  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Replace the driver's face level vent temperature sensor.
4
Check whether the signal circuit of driver's face level vent temperature sensor is short-circuited to GND.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector GaG75-2 of driver's face level vent temperature sensor and GND.
