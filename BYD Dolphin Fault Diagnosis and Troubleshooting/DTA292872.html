U10C286 Loss of communication with VCU

DTC description

U10C286 Loss of communication with VCU

Fault symptoms

The BSD system partially fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fuse fault.

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  3. Integrated controller fault.

  4. Rear left corner radar fault

Fault setting conditions

Any monitoring message is lost 10 times consecutively.

Fault triggering conditions

  1. The controller voltage ranges from 9 V to 16 V.

  2. The vehicle is powered on and initialized for 3 s.

  3. The common CAN is not busoff.

  4. The factory mode is disabled.

  5. No power-off notification from BCM is received.

  6. 3 s after the DTC is detected by the service

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the integrated controller passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 7.Go to Step 7.
2
Check the fuse of integrated controller.
  1. Check whether the fuses F1/9(15A) and F1/33(15A) of front compartment fuse box are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of integrated controller.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connectors BK45 (A) and BK45 (B) from the integrated controller.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of integrated controller for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the power supply of integrated controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-20 of integrated controller and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-21 of integrated controller and GND.
  4. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-26 of integrated controller and GND.
  5. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-27 of integrated controller and GND.

7
Check the DTC of integrated controller.
  1. Enter the integrated controller interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  4. Enter the integrated controller interface again and read the DTC.
  5. Check for any DTC.
    6. Go to "IPB system" diagnosis.
8
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit
  1. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit again and read the DTC.
  5. Check for any DTC.
    6. Go to "Left domain control unit" diagnosis.
9
Check the DTC of rear left corner radar.
  1. Enter the BSD interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  4. Enter the BSD interface again and read the DTC.
  5. Check for any DTC.
    6. Go to "BSD system" diagnosis.
10
Check the DTCs of other modules.
