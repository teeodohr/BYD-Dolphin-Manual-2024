B134211 Three-way coolant valve control motor short-circuited to power supply

DTC description

B134211 Three-way coolant valve control motor short-circuited to power supply
Fault symptoms  
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Three-way coolant valve control motor is faulty.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions If the three-way coolant valve control motor is short-circuited to other power supplies, this DTC will be generated in continuous memory and on demand.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of three-way coolant valve control motor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B56 from three-way coolant valve control motor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of three-way coolant valve control motor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the three-way coolant valve control motor.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector pins of three-way coolant valve control motor.
