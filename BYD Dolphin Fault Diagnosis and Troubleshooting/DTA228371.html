B116212 Coolant temperature sensor short-circuit

DTC description

B116212 Coolant temperature sensor short-circuit

Fault symptoms

-

Possible causes of the fault

Coolant temperature sensor fault

Fault setting conditions

Coolant temperature sensor short-circuited

Fault triggering conditions

When the vehicle is powered on, the coolant temperature sensor is detected to be short-circuited, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of power domain control unit
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the interface of power domain control unit and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the power domain control unit interface again, and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of plate heat exchanger coolant temperature sensor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B57 from the plate heat exchanger coolant temperature sensor.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of plate heat exchanger coolant temperature sensor for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of integrated front drive controller.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK45(B) from the integrated front drive controller.
  2. Check the wiring harness connector of integrated front drive controller for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the signal circuits of coolant temperature sensor are short-circuited to each other.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors B57-1 and B57-3 of plate heat exchanger coolant temperature sensor.

7
Check the plate heat exchanger coolant temperature sensor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Replace the plate heat exchanger coolant temperature sensor with a normal one and restore the vehicle.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace the plate heat exchanger coolant temperature sensor. Replace the integrated front drive controller.
