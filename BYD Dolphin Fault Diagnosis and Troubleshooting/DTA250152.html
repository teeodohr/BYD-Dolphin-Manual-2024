Conventional fault diagnosis

Visual inspection

  1. Confirm the customer's problems.

  2. Visually inspect for obvious signs of mechanical damage and collision deformation.

  3. If the problem observed or raised is obvious and the cause has been found, the cause must be corrected before proceeding to the next step.

  4. If the visual inspection is passed, identify the fault and refer to the Fault Symptoms Table.

Conventional Fault Symptoms Table

Symptoms Possible causes Recommended maintenance measures
Excessive wear of tire rim

  1. Insufficient tire pressure

  2. Incorrect wheel alignment

  1. Adjust the tire pressure.

  2. Perform four-wheel alignment.
Vehicle vibration caused by wheels or tires

  1. Tire pressure too high

  2. Wheels or tires out of balance

  3. Abnormal tire wear

  4. Water accumulated in the tire tread

  5. Wheel deformation

  6. Incorrect tire bead installation

  7. Excessive radial runout of wheel or tire

  8. Loose wheel nuts or enlarged wheel bolt holes

  9. Foreign matters between wheel mounting surface and hub

  10. Brake disc out of balance

  11. Loose or damaged wheel bearing

  1. Adjust the tire pressure.

  2. Balance the tires and wheels.

  3. Identify the cause of abnormal wear and replace the tire if necessary.

  4. Remove the water accumulated in the tire tread.

  5. Replace the wheel.

  6. Rotate the wheel on the vehicle and check the joint between the wheel and tire. If a certain part swings during rotation, it may be caused by improper assembly of the wheel and tire. Remove the tire and clean the joint between the wheel and tire.

  7. Use a dial indicator to measure the radial runout of wheels or tires. If the radial runout is not within the specified range, it is necessary to further check the radial runout of wheel bolts. If the bolt radial runout is out of range, replace the wheel bolts.

  8. Tighten or replace damaged components.

  9. Clean the mounting surface.

  10. Check the brake disc for mud, debris or other serious dirt, and check whether it is out of balance due to excessive wear and damage.

  11. Repair or replace damaged components.
