B224771 Front right window regulator switch stuck

DTC description

B224771 Front right window regulator switch stuck

Fault symptoms

The front right window partially fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Front right window regulator switch fault

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault

Fault setting conditions

Effective resistance continuously acquired for more than 20 S

Fault triggering conditions

  1. The controller voltage ranges from 9 V to 16 V.

  2. Enable signal

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  7. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    8. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the front right window regulator switch for mechanical fault
  1. Check the front right window regulator switch for jamming, failure to return, water corrosion, or damage.
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the front right window regulator switch set.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of front right window regulator switch
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector U05 from the front right window regulator switch.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of front right window regulator switch for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the faulty wiring harness or connector.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector TG64(I) from the left domain control unit.
  2. Check the wiring harness connector of left domain control unit for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the faulty wiring harness or connector.
5
Check whether the signal wiring harness of front right window regulator switch is open-circuited

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector U05-2 of front right window regulator switch and the wiring harness connector TG64(I)-2 of left domain control unit.

8
Check the front right window regulator switch
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Replace the front right window regulator switch set with a normal one and restore the vehicle.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace the front right window regulator switch set. Replace the left domain control unit.
