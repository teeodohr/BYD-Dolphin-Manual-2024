P157400 Power supply equipment fault

DTC description

P157400 Power supply equipment fault
Fault symptoms AC charging fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fault of external AC charging device.

  2. Internal fault of on-board charger.
Fault setting conditions No AC voltage input is detected within the specified time after closing switch S2.
Fault triggering conditions During the AC charging process, no AC voltage input is detected within the specified time after closing switch S2.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the external AC charging device.
  1. Replace the external AC charging equipment to the vehicle for charging test.
  2. Check whether the vehicle can be charging normally.
    3. Replace the external AC charging equipment. Replace the on-board power supply assembly.
