U01A186 Vehicle speed message invalid RC and CS

DTC description

U01A186 Vehicle speed message invalid (RC and CS)

Fault symptoms

Functions of the EPS partially fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. IPB controller.

  3. EPS fault

  4. The CAN communication wiring harness fault.

Fault setting conditions

 After the Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position for 2000 ms, 8 V≤voltage≤18 V, and 1000 ms after bus is off and restored.

Fault triggering conditions

 Checksum error or scroll counter error last for 60 ms.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the electro-hydraulic brake module passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 8.Go to Step 8.
2
Check the wiring harness and connector of IPB controller
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B03_1 from the IPB controller.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of IPB controller is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the power supply of IPB controller

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B03_1-36 of IPB controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    B03_1-36 GND Always 11~14 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Go to Step 6.Go to Step 6.
4
Check the fuse of IPB controller
  1. Check whether the fuse F1/42 (5 A) of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
5
Check whether the constant power supply of IPB controller is open-circuited

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1C from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector B03_1-36 of IPB controller and the wiring harness connector B1C-10 of front compartment fuse box.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    B03_1-36 B1C-10 Always Below 1 Ω
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the front compartment fuse box.
6
Check the GND circuit of IPB controller

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector B03_1-1 of IPB controller and GND.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector B03_1-30 of IPB controller and GND.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    B03_1-1 GND Always Below 1 Ω
    B03_1-30
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check the CAN circuit of IPB controller

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B03_1-19 of IPB controller and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B03_1-5 of IPB controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    B03_1-19 GND Always 1.5~2.5 V
    B03_1-5 2.5~3.5 V
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Go to "Gateway system" diagnosis. Replace the IPB controller.
8
Check the DTC of electro-hydraulic brake module
  1. Read the DTC of electro-hydraulic brake module with the VDS.
  2. Check whether there is any DTC in the result.
    3. Go to "Electro-hydraulic brake module" diagnosis.
9
Check the DTC of power control system.
  1. Read the DTC of power control system with the VDS.
  2. Check whether there are other DTCs in the result.
    3. Go to "EPS system" diagnosis.
10
Check the DTCs of other modules
  1. Is vehicle speed message loss read from other modules?
    2. Replace the IPB controller. Replace the EPS control.
