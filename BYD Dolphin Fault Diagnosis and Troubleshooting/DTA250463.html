P157016 Low voltage at AC side

DTC description

P157016 Low voltage at AC side
Fault symptoms The instrument reports "AC charging fails. Please check the on-board charging system."
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Leakage of HV components.

  2. Low grid voltage.

  3. External AC charging equipment fault.

  4. Charging port assembly fault.

  5. Internal fault of on-board power supply assembly.
Fault setting conditions The AC side voltage is less than the specified threshold.
Fault triggering conditions When the vehicle is in AC charging state, if the system detects that the AC side voltage is less than the specified threshold value, a DTC will be generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of on-board power supply assembly.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the on-board power supply assembly, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  6. Connect the external AC charging equipment to the vehicle for charging test.
  7. Check for the same DTC.
    8. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Enter the battery execution and sampling unit interface with the VDS.
  3. Check for power supply leakage related DTCs.
    4. Go to "Battery execution and sampling system" diagnosis.
3
Check the AC charging port.
  1. Check whether there is any foreign matter inside the charging port.
  2. Check the AC charging port terminal for aging, deformation, ablation or fracture.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Clean foreign matters or replace the charging port assembly.
4
Check the AC voltage of grid.
  1. Measure the AC voltage of grid with a multimeter.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    L N Always 220V±10%
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair the grid.
5
Check the external AC charging device.
  1. Replace the external AC charging device to the vehicle for charging test.
  2. Check whether the vehicle can be charging normally.
    3. Replace the external AC charging device.
6
Check the wiring harness and connector of on-board power supply assembly.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the AC connector from on-board power supply assembly.
  3. Check the wiring harness or connector for poor contact, fracture or erosion.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check whether the circuit from AC charging port to on-board power supply assembly is open-circuited.
  1. Measure the resistance between terminal L of AC charging port and positive terminal of on-board power supply assembly AC connector.
  2. Measure the resistance between terminal L of AC charging port and positive terminal of on-board power supply assembly AC connector.
  3. Measure the resistance between PE terminal of AC charging port and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    AC charging port terminal L Positive terminal of on-board power supply assembly AC connector Always Below 1 Ω
    AC charging port terminal N Negative terminal of on-board power supply assembly AC connector
    AC charging port terminal PE GND
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace charging port assembly. Replace the on-board power supply assembly.
