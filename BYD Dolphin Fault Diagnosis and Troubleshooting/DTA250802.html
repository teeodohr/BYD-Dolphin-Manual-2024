C053B00 Power supply circuit of ABS valve open-circuited OBD

DTC description

C053B00 Power circuit of ABS valve open-circuited - OBD
Fault symptoms The IPB system fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Blown fuse.

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  3. IPB controller fault
Fault setting conditions -.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of electro-hydraulic brake.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the electro-hydraulic brake interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the electro-hydraulic brake interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the fuse of IPB controller.
  1. Check whether the fuses F1/42(5A), F1/44(60A) and F1/30(60A) of front compartment fuse box are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of IPB controller.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B03_1 from the IPB controller.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of IPB controller is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of front compartment fuse box.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1C from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the IG1 power supply of IPB controller is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector B1C-10/4/1 of front compartment fuse box and the wiring harness connector B03_1-36/14/46 of IPB controller.

6
Check the IBC system assembly.
  1. Replace the IBC system assembly with a new one and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the IBC system assembly. Replace the front compartment fuse box.
