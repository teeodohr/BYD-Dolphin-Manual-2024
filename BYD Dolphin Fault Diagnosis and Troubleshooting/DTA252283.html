B1BE801 Fault of front wiper HI mode switch hardwire detection signal

DTC description

B1BE801 Fault of front wiper HI mode switch hardwire detection signal

Fault symptoms

When the wiper switch is turned to HI mode, the wiper does not work.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Multi-function switch fault.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.

Fault setting conditions

 No hardwire signal of front wiper HI mode collected for 5 consecutive seconds when the received CAN signal of multi-function switch is front wiper HI mode

Fault triggering conditions

 The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the wiring harness and connector of multi-function switch.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G02 from the multi-function switch.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of multi-function switch is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
2
Check whether the wiring harness of front wiper HI mode of multi-function switch is short-circuited to GND.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector G02-8 of multi-function switch and GND.

3
Check whether the wiring harness of front wiper HI mode of multi-function switch is short-circuited to power supply.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector G02-8 of multi-function switch and GND.

    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the wiring harness and connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G64(L) from the left domain control unit.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of left domain control unit is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the signal circuit of front wiper HI mode is open-circuited.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector G64(L)-3 of left domain control unit and the wiring harness connector G02-8 of multi-function switch.

    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check the left domain control unit.
  1. Replace the left domain control unit with a new one and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the left domain control unit. Replace the multi-function switch.
