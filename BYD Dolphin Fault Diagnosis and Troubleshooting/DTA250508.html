P1BB200 Generally high temperature alarm of front drive motor

DTC description

P1BB200 Generally high temperature alarm of front drive motor

Fault symptoms

The instrument reports that the drive function is limited.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Cooling system fault

  2. Front motor control unit fault

  3. Front drive motor assembly fault.

Fault setting conditions

The front drive motor temperature exceeds the specified threshold.

Fault triggering conditions

When the vehicle is powered on, if the front drive motor temperature exceeds the specified threshold, a DTC will be generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of drive cooling system (integrated in power domain control unit).
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the power domain control unit interface.
  5. Check for temperature related DTCs.
    6. Go to "Power domain control unit" diagnosis.
2
Check the DTC of front drive motor control unit
  1. Read the DTC of front drive motor control unit with the VDS.
  2. Clear the DTC.
  3. Drive the vehicle to an open space and keep it running normally for about 10 min.
  4. Read the DTC of front drive motor control unit with the VDS.
  5. Check for the same DTC.
    6. The system is normal.
3
Check the temperature sensor of motor winding
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Remove the end cover of front drive motor control unit
  3. Disconnect the temperature and resolver connectors.
  4. Measure the resistance between temperature and resolver connector terminals 2 and 7.
    Connector Temperature (°C) Standard resistance (kΩ)
    (+) (-)
    2 7 30 2280
    -20 1190
    -10 646.9
    0 364.9
    10 212.5
    20 127.7
    30 78.88
    40 50.04
  5. Check whether the result is normal.
    6. Replace the drive motor assembly.
4
Replace the front drive motor control unit and check the DTC.
  1. Replace the front drive motor control unit.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Drive the vehicle to an open space and keep it running normally for about 10 min.
  5. Read the DTC of front drive motor control unit again with a VDS.
  6. Check for the same DTC.
    7. The system is normal. Replace the drive motor assembly.
