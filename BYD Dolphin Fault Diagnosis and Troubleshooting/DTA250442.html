B2A0801 Evaporator outlet refrigerant pressure sensor short-circuited to power supply

DTC description

B2A0801 Evaporator outlet refrigerant pressure sensor short-circuited to power supply
Fault symptoms The A/C system partially fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Evaporator outlet refrigerant pressure sensor is faulty.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions If the circuit of evaporator outlet refrigerant pressure sensor is short-circuited to other power supplies, this DTC will be generated in continuous memory and as required.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the pressure feedback signal of evaporator temperature and pressure sensor.
  1. Connect the wiring harness connector B55 (A) of evaporator outlet pressure & temperature sensor.
  2. Measure the voltage between the pressure feedback signal B55 (A) -2 of evaporator temperature and pressure sensor and GND from the rear end.

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the left domain control unit.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of evaporator outlet pressure & temperature sensor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B55 (A) of evaporator outlet pressure & temperature sensor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of evaporator outlet pressure & temperature sensor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BG64(E) from the left domain control unit.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of left domain control unit is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the circuit of evaporator outlet pressure & temperature sensor is short-circuited to power supply.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between wiring harness connector B55 (A)-4 of evaporator outlet pressure & temperature sensor and GND.
