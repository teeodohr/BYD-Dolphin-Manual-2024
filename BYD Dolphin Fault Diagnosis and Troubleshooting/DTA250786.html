P056024 UBVR(PIN46) power supply circuit open-circuited

DTC description

P056024 UBVR(PIN46) power supply circuit open-circuited
Fault symptoms The IPB system partially fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Internal fault of IPB controller

  2. Front compartment fuse box fault.

  3. Wiring harness or connector fault.
Fault setting conditions -.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of electro-hydraulic brake.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the electro-hydraulic brake interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the electro-hydraulic brake interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the fuse.
  1. Check the fuse F1/30(60A) of front compartment fuse box.
  2. Check whether the fuse is normal.
    3. Replace the fuse with one with the same specification.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of front compartment fuse box.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1C from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Check whether the connector is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of IPB controller
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B03_1 from the IPB controller.
  2. Check whether the connector is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the constant power wiring harness of IPB controller is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector B03_1-46 of IPB controller and the wiring harness connector B1C-1 of front compartment fuse box.
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check the front compartment fuse box.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Replace the front compartment fuse box with a new one and restore the vehicle.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform the test drive for functional inspection.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Replace the front compartment fuse box. Replace the IBC system assembly.
