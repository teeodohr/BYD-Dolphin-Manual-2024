P1A4200 Cathode contactor burnt-out

DTC description

P1A4200 Cathode contactor burnt-out

Fault symptoms

The instrument displays "EV function limited", and charging/discharging is prohibited.

Possible causes of the fault

Main contactor of high-voltage battery pack burnt-out

Fault setting conditions

Failure to open cathode contactor normally

Fault triggering conditions

After the vehicle is powered on, the system detects that the cathode contactor cannot be opened normally, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Read the voltage of high-voltage battery pack and check for any system software version for update.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the battery execution and sampling unit interface.
  5. Read the data flow "Current total voltage of battery pack" and record it as V0.
  6. Check for any software version available for update of the battery execution and sampling unit.
    7. Go to Step 3.Go to Step 3.
2
Replace the battery execution and sampling unit, and check the DTC.
  1. Update the battery execution and sampling unit with the VDS.
  2. Clear the DTC.
  3. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  5. Check for the same DTC.
    6. The system is normal.
3
Measure the voltage of high-voltage battery pack and check the high-voltage wiring harness and connector.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for 5 min.
  2. Wear insulating gloves and disconnect all high-voltage connectors except the high-voltage battery pack (the rear electric control bus shall also be disconnected for 4WD models).
  3. Measure the voltage between positive and negative poles of high-voltage battery with a multimeter, and record it as V1.
  4. Check the high-voltage connector for corrosion, damage, or water ingress.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Measure the voltage of high-voltage battery pack when the main contactor is closed.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Use a VDS to enter the battery execution and sampling unit interface, and then the maintenance mode interface.
  3. Tap the active control of cathode contactor to perform closing action (Do not connect the high-voltage connector during closing of contactor).
  4. Read the data information and confirm that the main contactor is closed.
  5. Measure the voltage between positive and negative poles of high-voltage battery with a multimeter, and record it as V2.
  6. Stop reading the data information and open the main contactor.
  7. Check whether V1 is slightly lower than 10% of high-voltage battery pack voltage V0, and whether V2 is approximate to V0.
    8. The cathode contactor is burnt out. Replace the high-voltage battery pack.
5
Check the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  1. Restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Enter the battery execution and sampling unit interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check for the same DTC.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault". Replace the high-voltage battery pack.
