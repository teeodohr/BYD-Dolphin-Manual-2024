P157216 Low voltage at DC side

DTC description

P157216 Low voltage at DC side
Fault symptoms The instrument reports "AC charging/discharging fails. Please check the on-board charging system."
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fault of the high voltage fuse at DC side of the on-board power assembly.

  2. Fault of wiring harness or wiring harness connector.

  3. Internal fault of on-board power supply assembly.

  4. High-voltage battery pack fault
Fault setting conditions

  1. The LLC rear end voltage is less than the specified threshold before charging.

  2. The PFC rear end voltage is less than the specified threshold after charging.
Fault triggering conditions A DTC is generated when the voltage is detected to be less than a specified threshold before or after charging.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of on-board power supply assembly.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the on-board power supply assembly, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  6. Connect the external AC charging equipment to the vehicle for charging test.
  7. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  8. Check for the same DTC.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  1. Read the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  2. Check for any DTC.
    3. Go to "Battery execution and sampling system" diagnosis.
3
Check the high voltage fuse at DC side of on-board power supply assembly.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  2. Measure whether the high voltage fuse at DC side of on-board power supply assembly is damaged with a multimeter?
    3. Go to Step 6.Go to Step 6.
4
Check the DC connector of on-board power supply assembly.
  1. Disconnect the DC connector from on-board power supply assembly.
  2. Check the DC connector for foreign matters.
  3. Check the DC connector for aging, deformation or ablation.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness and high voltage fuse.
5
Check whether the DC side circuit is short-circuited.
  1. Measure whether the high voltage wiring harness at DC side is short-circuited with a multimeter.
    2. Replace the high voltage fuse. Repair or replace the wiring harness and high voltage fuse.
6
Check whether the DC side circuit is open-circuited.
  1. Measure the DC side circuit and high-voltage connector are virtually connected or open-circuited?
    2. Repair or replace the DC side circuit. Replace the on-board power supply assembly.
