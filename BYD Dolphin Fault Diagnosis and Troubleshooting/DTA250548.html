U011000 Fault in communication with motor control unit

DTC description

U011000 Fault in communication with motor control unit

Fault symptoms

-

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Integrated front drive controller fault

  3. High-voltage battery pack fault

Fault setting conditions

Abnormal communication with motor control unit.

Fault triggering conditions

When the vehicle is powered on, communication with motor control unit is abnormal and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network of front drive motor control unit
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the front drive motor control unit passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 8.Go to Step 8.
2
Check the fuse of integrated front drive controller.
  1. Check whether the front compartment fuse box fuses F1/33(1A) and F1/09(15A) are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of integrated front drive controller.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK45(A) from the integrated front drive controller.
  3. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK45(B) from the integrated front drive controller.
  4. Check whether the connector of integrated front drive controller is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the constant power of integrated front drive controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-20 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-21 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  4. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-26 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  5. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-27 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    BK45(B)-20 GND Always 11~14 V
    BK45(B)-21
    BK45(B)-26
    BK45(B)-27
  6. Check whether the result is normal.
    7. Go to Step 6.Go to Step 6.
5
Check whether the power supply of integrated front drive controller module is open-circuited.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connectors B1C and B1D from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-20 and B1C-25 of integrated front drive controller.
  4. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-26 and B1C-25 of integrated front drive controller.
  5. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-27 and B1D-20 of integrated front drive controller.
  6. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-21 and B1D-20 of integrated front drive controller.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    BK45(B)-20 B1C-25 Always Below 1 Ω
    BK45(B)-26
    BK45(B)-21 B1D-20
    BK45(B)-27
  7. Check whether the result is normal.
    8. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the front compartment fuse box.
6
Check the GND wire of integrated front drive controller

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BK45 (B)-8 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BK45 (B)-9 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BK45 (B)-14 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  4. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BK45 (B)-15 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  5. Check whether the result is normal.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    BK45(B)-8 GND Always Below 1 Ω
    BK45(B)-9
    BK45(B)-14
    BK45(B)-15
    6. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check the CAN circuit of integrated front drive controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45 (A) -13 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45 (A) -14 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    BK45(A)-13 GND Always 1.5~2.5 V
    BK45(A)-14 2.5~3.5 V
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Go to "Gateway system" diagnosis. Replace the integrated front drive controller.
8
Check the DTC of front drive motor control unit
  1. Read the DTC of front drive motor control unit with the VDS.
  2. Check whether there are other DTCs in the result.
    3. Go to "Front drive motor control unit" diagnosis.
9
Check the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  1. Read the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit with the VDS.
  2. Check whether there are other DTCs in the result.
    3. Go to "Battery execution and sampling system" diagnosis.
10
Check the DTCs of other modules
  1. Is a DTC of loss of communication with front drive motor control unit read from other modules?
    2. Replace the integrated front drive controller. Replace the high-voltage battery pack.
