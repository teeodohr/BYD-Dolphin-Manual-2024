P1A0D00 BIC2 voltage sampling abnormal

DTC description

P1A0D00 BIC2 voltage sampling abnormal

Fault symptoms

The "High-voltage battery MIL" on the instrument illuminates, the message of "Powertrain fault" is displayed, the vehicle cannot be powered on (OK mode), and charging/discharging is prohibited.

Possible causes of the fault

Internal fault of high-voltage battery pack

Fault setting conditions

BIC working normally and voltage sampling disconnected

Fault triggering conditions

When the vehicle is powered on, the battery information collector communicates and works normally.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the battery execution and sampling unit interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  7. Check for the same DTC.
    8. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault". Replace the high-voltage battery pack.
