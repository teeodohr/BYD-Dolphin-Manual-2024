Diagnosis and testing

Inspection

  1. Operate the vehicle to allow recurrence of the fault, and confirm the problem described by the customer.

  2. Perform visual inspection to identify any obvious cause of fault.

  3. If any obvious cause is identified through visual inspection or based on the customer's problem description, eliminate the cause (if possible) before proceeding with the next step.

  4. The road test and the customer's detailed description can provide the relevant information that helps to confirm the problem and the direction for starting the diagnosis correctly.

  5. If the problem still exists after inspection, eliminate it according to the fault symptoms.

Inspection

  1. Noise and vibration usually occur in the following 4 areas:

    1. Tire

    2. Suspension

    3. Drive system

    4. Interior trims

  2. The locations of problems related to noise, vibration and comfort shall be determined as soon as possible. The simplest and fastest way is to conduct a road test, with appropriately approved noise, vibration and comfort diagnostic detectors being used to support the diagnosis and testing procedures.
