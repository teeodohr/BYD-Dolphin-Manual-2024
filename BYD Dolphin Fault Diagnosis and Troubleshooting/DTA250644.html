U111387 message lost

DTC description

U111387 0x27E message lost

Fault symptoms

 Functions of the EPS partially fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. EPS fault

  3. The CAN communication wiring harness fault.

Fault setting conditions

 120 ms checksum error scrolling counter error lasts for 120 ms.

Fault triggering conditions

 120 ms checksum error scrolling counter error lasts for 120 ms.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of EPS system
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the EPS system interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the EPS system interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check for the same DTC.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the CAN communication network
  1. Perform network detection with VDS.
  2. Check whether the CAN communication network can pass network detection.
    3. Troubleshooting.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of EPS
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connectors B22_1 and B23_1 from EPS.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of EPS for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the CAN circuit of EPS.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B22_1-5 of EPS and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B22_1-6 of EPS and GND.
