Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the intelligent brake control module passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 8.Go to Step 8.
2
Check the fuse of front compartment fuse box.
  1. Check whether the fuses F1/33(15A) and F1/09(15A) of front compartment fuse box are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of integrated controller.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connectors BK45 (A) and BK45 (B) from the integrated controller.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of integrated controller is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the power supply of the integrated controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between wiring harness connector BK45 (B) -20/21/26/27 of integrated controller and GND.

    Connector

    		 Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    BK45(B)-20

    		 GND Always 11~14 V

    BK45(B)-21

    BK45(B)-26

    BK45(B)-27
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Go to Step 6.Go to Step 6.
5
Check whether the power supply of integrated controller is open-circuited.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1C from front compartment fuse box.
  3. Check the resistance between wiring harness connector B1C-25 of front compartment fuse box and wiring harness connectors BK45 (B) -20, 26 of integrated controller.

    Connector

    		 Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)

    B1C-25

    BK45(B)-20

    		 Always Below 1 Ω

    BK45(B)-26
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check whether the power supply of integrated controller is open-circuited.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1C from front compartment fuse box.
  2. Check the resistance between wiring harness connector B1D-20 of front compartment fuse box and wiring harness connectors BK45 (B) -21, 27 of integrated controller.

    Connector

    		 Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)

    B1D-20

    BK45(B)-21

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the front compartment fuse box.
7
Check the GND of integrated controller.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector BK45 (B) -8/9/14/15 of integrated controller and GND.

    Connector

    		 Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)

    BK45(B)-9

  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
8
Check the CAN circuit of the integrated controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45 (A) -21/27 of the integrated controller and the GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    BK45(A)-21

    		 GND Always

    2.5~3.5 V

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Go to "Gateway system" diagnosis. Replace the integrated controller.
9
Check the DTC of the front drive motor control unit
  1. Read the DTC of front drive motor control unit with a diagnostic device.
  2. Check whether there is any DTC in the result.
    3. Go to "Front drive motor control unit" diagnosis.
10
Check the DTC of EPB controller.
  1. Read the DTC of EPB controller with the VDS.
  2. Check whether there is any DTC in the result.
    3. Go to "EPB system" diagnosis.
11
Check the DTCs of other modules.
  1. Is any fault in communication with MCU read from other modules?
    2. Replace the integrated controller. Replace the EPB.
