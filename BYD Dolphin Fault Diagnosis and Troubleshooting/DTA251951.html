How to deal with historical faults

A fault indeed occurs although it is not displayed in the current state. If this happens, the related faults may always exist. In case of a historical fault, the following detection steps should be performed:

  1. Check whether the customer has replaced the fuse or disconnected the connector.

  2. If so, clear the DTC and then check whether the DTC has been reset. If the DTC is not reset, end the diagnosis.

  3. If the DTC is reset, perform the relevant DTC diagnosis process. After that, check the wiring harness and connector. See "How to deal with intermittent faults" for details.
