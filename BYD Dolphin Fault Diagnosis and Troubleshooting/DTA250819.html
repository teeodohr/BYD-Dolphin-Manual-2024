Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of compressor.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position and read the DTC.
  3. Check whether the DTC is displayed.
    4. Go to "A/C system" diagnosis.
2
Read data flow of BMS.
  1. Read DC bus voltage of BMS.
  2. Check whether the high-voltage battery has a high-voltage output.
    3. Go to "Battery execution and sampling unit" diagnosis.
3
Read data flow of electric compressor.
  1. Read DC bus voltage of electric compressor.
  2. Check whether the electric compressor has a high-voltage output.
    3. Go to Step 5Step 5.
4
Check the high-voltage fuse of electric compressor.
  1. Check whether the high-voltage fuse of electric compressor is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse. Check or replace the DC bus of electric compressor.
5
Check the cooling fan.
  1. Put the Start/Stop switch to OK position and turn on the A/C.
  2. Check whether the cooling fan works normally.
    3. Diagnose "radiator fan does not work".
6
Check the refrigerant pressure of A/C system.
  1. Connect the refrigerant recovery and filling machine.
  2. Check the refrigerant pressure of A/C system.
    Pressure gauge Condition Pressure value
    System pressure Turn off the A/C refrigeration mode 0.73~0.83 MPa
    High pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 1.47~1.67 MPa
    Low pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 0.15~0.25 MPa
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Check and adjust the A/C system refrigerant.
7
Check the electric compressor fuse.
  1. Check whether the fuse F1/12(7.5A) of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
8
Check the wiring harness connector of electric compressor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the high voltage connector BA17 from the electric compressor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of electric compressor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
9
Check the power supply of electric compressor.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BA17-1 of electric compressor and GND.

    4. Go to Step 12.Go to Step 12.
10
Check the wiring harness connector of front compartment fuse box.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B1D from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
11
