U011187 Fault in communication with battery management system (BMS)

DTC description

U011187 Fault in communication with battery management system (BMS)

Fault symptoms

-

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fuse fault.

  2. 12 V battery fault.

  3. Circuit or connector fault

  4. Vehicle controller fault.

  5. BMS fault

Fault setting conditions

Fault in communication with BMS

Fault triggering conditions

The system detects a fault in communication with the BMS, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the battery execution and sampling unit passes network detection.
    5. Go to Step 10.Go to Step 10.
2
Check the wiring harness connector of high-voltage battery pack.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK51 from the high-voltage battery pack.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of high-voltage battery pack is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
