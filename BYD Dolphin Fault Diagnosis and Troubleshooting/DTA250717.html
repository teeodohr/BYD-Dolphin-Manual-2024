B2A2013 Interior temperature sensor open-circuited

DTC description

B2A2013 Interior temperature sensor open-circuited
Fault symptoms The A/C system partially fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Interior temperature sensor fault.

  3. Left domain control unit fault.
Fault setting conditions The detection sensor output voltage is higher than 4.95 V.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of interior temperature sensor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G05 from the interior temperature sensor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of interior temperature sensor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the interior temperature sensor.
  1. Measure the resistance between wiring harness connector pins of interior temperature sensor.
    Connector Condition Lower limit (kΩ) Upper limit (kΩ)

  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Replace the interior temperature sensor.
4
Check whether the interior temperature sensor GND is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector G05-1 of interior temperature sensor and GND.

  2. Check whether the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
