B1C4013 Four-door lock motor open-circuited

DTC description

B1C4013 Four-door lock motor open-circuited

Fault symptoms

Four door locks do not work at the same time.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Front right window regulator switch fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault.

Fault setting conditions

No current detected in the control circuit when the four door locks work

Fault triggering conditions

The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the left domain control unit again and read the DTC.
  8. Check for the same DTC.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of front right window regulator switch.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector U05 from the front right window regulator switch.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector TG64(I) from the left domain control unit.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the wiring harness of central locking is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector U05-14/6 of front right window regulator switch and the wiring harness connector KG64(I)-4/16 of left domain control unit.
  2. Check whether the result is normal.

5
Check the front right window regulator switch.
  1. Replace the front right window regulator switch with a new one and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position, and perform a functional inspection.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the front right window regulator switch. Replace the left domain control unit.
