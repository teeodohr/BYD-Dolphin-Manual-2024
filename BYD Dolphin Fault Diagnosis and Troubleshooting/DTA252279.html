B1C2E00 Front left door lock fault

DTC description

B1C2E00 Front left door lock fault

Fault symptoms

The front left door lock fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault

  2. Front left door lock fault

  3. Left domain control unit fault

Fault setting conditions

  1. The status error count will be increased by 1 where the front left door lock is detected to be locked 200 ms after a door unlocking action with the central door lock switch, remote switch or microswitch, and a fault will be recorded where there are 4 consecutive state errors.

  2. If the program is powered off/reset, the counter will be reset.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of the left domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the left domain control unit, read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the left domain control unit again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of front left door lock motor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to "OFF" position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector T06_1 from the front left door lock motor.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of front left door lock motor for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G64 (K) of left domain control unit.
  2. Check the wiring harness connector of left domain control unit for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the wiring harness of front left door lock motor is open-circuited.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector T06_1-3/4 of front left door lock motor and the wiring harness connector G64(K)-11/10 of left domain control unit.

Check the front left door lock motor.

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
