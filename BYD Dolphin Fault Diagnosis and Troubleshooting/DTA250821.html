B2AB997 Overload

DTC description

B2AB997 Overload
Fault symptoms A/C system function fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Excessive refrigerant in A/C system.

  2. Cooling fan fault.

  3. Electric compressor is faulty.
Fault setting conditions The electric compressor is overloaded.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position and the A/C refrigeration is enabled.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of electric compressor.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the cooling fan.
  1. Put the Start/Stop switch to OK position and turn on the A/C.
  2. Check whether the cooling fan works normally.
    3. Check "Cooling fan does not work".
3
Check the refrigerant pressure of A/C system.
  1. Connect the refrigerant recovery and filling machine.
  2. Check the refrigerant pressure of A/C system.
    Pressure gauge Condition Pressure value
    System pressure Turn off the A/C refrigeration mode 0.73~0.83 MPa
    High pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 1.47~1.67 MPa
    Low pressure Turn on the A/C refrigeration mode 0.15~0.25 MPa
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Adjust the pressure of A/C system refrigerant. Replace the electric compressor.
