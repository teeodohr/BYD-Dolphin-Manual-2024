Diagnostic instructions

Introduction

　　For the diagnosis of any fault of the IPB system, it is necessary to understand and get familiar with its operating principle. Go to description and operation overview. Before diagnosis, determine the fault symptom described by the customer, and then analyze the fault cause of the IPB system, which are helpful to determine the correct fault diagnosis steps. During the inspection and measurement of circuits and components of the IPB system, the data flow and action test functions are preferred to improve diagnosis efficiency and shorten maintenance time. After confirming the fault, understand the precautions and warnings of the IPB system and implement standard operating procedures during maintenance. After maintenance, check the IPB system and confirm its working status.

General-purpose equipment

  • Multimeter

  • VDS

Reminder

  • When measuring the voltage with a multimeter, select the range that is closest to the voltage.

  • When measuring the resistance with a multimeter, select the range that is closest to the resistance.

Precautions

The IPB system is a safety-related component. Therefore, in addition to general safety and preventive measures, the following diagnostic precautions must also be observed during its maintenance and diagnosis.

  1. The IPB system must be maintained by technicians who have received professional training and mastered maintenance skills, and only original parts are allowed to be used for replacement.

  2. Before diagnosis of the IPB system, any fault of the basic brake system must be eliminated, such as:

    1. Brake system noise.

    2. Excessively hard brake pedal.

    3. Brake pedal or vehicle vibration during normal braking.

    4. Vehicle off-tracking during braking.

    5. EPB system fault.

  3. The IPB system assembly (referring to the electronic control unit and hydraulic adjuster assembly of the IPB system, excluding auxiliary devices such as brake pipes and sensors) can only be replaced as a whole and cannot be disassembled for inspection or partially replaced/interchanged.

  4. Both of the following conditions indicate that a fault of the IPB system is detected:

    1. The MIL remains illuminating after the vehicle is powered ON.

    2. The MIL remains illuminating during driving.

  5. Note the following when connecting the IPB system and sensor wiring harness:

    1. Before unplugging the IPB system and sensor wiring harness, be sure to power OFF the vehicle.

    2. Make sure that the connectors are dry and clean and avoid ingression of any foreign matters.

    3. Fit the wiring harness connector of IPB system in place horizontally and vertically to avoid connector damage.

  6. Noise will be generated in the IPB system in the following conditions:

    1. After the vehicle is powered on, it is normal to hear a short "buzzing" sound, which is generated by the self-inspection of the IPB system.

    2. When the vehicle accelerates to about 15 km/h, it is normal to hear a short "buzzing" sound, which is generated by the dynamic self-inspection of the IPB system.

    3. Action sound produced by the motor, solenoid valve and reflux pump in the hydraulic unit of IPB system.

    4. Sound caused by brake pedal rebound.

    5. Sound caused by the impact between suspension and vehicle body due to emergency braking.
