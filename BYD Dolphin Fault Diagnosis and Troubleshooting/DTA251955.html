Operation tips

  1. Clothing

    1. Please wear clean work clothes that meet the workshop standards.

    2. Be sure to wear personal protective equipment, and wear a safety helmet when working under the vehicle.

  2. Vehicle protection

    1. Prepare radiator grille cover, fender cover, seat cover, floor mat, etc. before starting the operation.

  3. Safe operation

    1. When two or more technicians work together, they must check each other for safety.

    2. Be sure to wear necessary protective equipment and be careful not to hurt yourself or others when working in a high-temperature and high-pressure environment or performing operations on rotating, moving or vibrating parts.

    3. When jacking the vehicle with a jack, be sure to support the designated par with a safety bracket.

    4. Use appropriate safety equipment to lift the vehicle.

  4. Preparation of tools and measuring equipment

    1. Prepare tool supports, special tools, measuring tools, oil/fluid and parts for replacement before starting the operation.

  5. Removal and installation, disassembly and assembly operations

    1. Perform diagnosis after fully understanding the correct steps and the problems reported.

    2. Check the general condition, deformation and damage of the assembly before disassembling its parts.

    3. When the assembly structure is complex, keep records. For example, record the total number of all electrical connectors and bolts, or the total number of hoses disassembled. Apply mating marks for reassembling the components in place, and if necessary, temporarily mark the hoses and their joints.

    4. If necessary, clean the disassembled parts, and thoroughly inspect them before installation.

  6. Parts taken out

    1. Place the removed parts in separate boxes to avoid confusion with or contaminating new parts.

    2. Replace all removed parts that cannot be reused, such as gaskets, O-rings and self-locking nuts.

    3. Keep the removed parts for inspection if required by the customer.

  7. Inspection after completion of work

    1. Ensure that the parts for replacement are properly installed and fastened.

    2. Ensure that the tools and materials used are not left inside the front compartment, vehicle or trunk.

    3. Check and confirm for no liquid leakage.

    Caution

    After completing the work, be sure to carry out the above-mentioned inspections correctly; Failure to correctly perform these inspections may lead to serious accidents or injuries.

  8. Pre-coated parts

    1. Pre-coated parts refer to bolts and nuts coated with locking sealant in the plant.

    2. If a pre-coated part is retightened, loosened or moved by any means, it must be recoated with the specified locking sealant.

    3. Before using a pre-coated part again, be sure to remove the old locking sealant, dry the part with compressed air, and apply a proper amount of new locking sealant to the part.

    4. Some locking sealants cure slowly and may require waiting for a certain time.

  9. Pads

    1. If necessary, apply sealant to pads to prevent leakage.

  10. Bolts, nuts and screws

    1. Strictly abide by all torque requirements for fasteners, and always use a torque wrench.

    2. Make sure that no foreign matters (burrs, paint, etc.) are deposited under the bolts and nuts when tightening them.

    3. Please follow the following basic principles when removing nuts or bolts: diagonally loosen them several times from outside to inside; in case of any special regulations, please operate according to such regulations.

    4. Please follow the following basic principles when tightening nuts or bolts: diagonally pre-tighten them several times from inside to outside; in case of any special regulations, please operate according to such regulations.

  11. Fuse

    1. For fuse inspection, check and confirm that the fuse wire is not blown.

    2. For fuse replacement, carry out a short-circuit test first, and then replace the fuse after confirming that there is no short-circuit fault in the circuit.

    3. Fuses with the same rated current must be used.
