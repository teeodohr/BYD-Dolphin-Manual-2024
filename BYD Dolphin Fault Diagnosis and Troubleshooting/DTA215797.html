B2FD016 Low supply voltage alarm

DTC description

B2FD016 Low supply voltage alarm
Fault symptoms Low-voltage power supply system fault.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. 12 V battery fault.

  2. Blown fuse.

  3. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  4. Charging system fault.

  5. Internal fault of wireless phone charging module.
Fault setting conditions

The supply voltage is lower than 9 V for ≥ 2 s.
Fault triggering conditions

The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of wireless phone charging module.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the charging system.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OK position.
  2. Check whether the MIL of the instrument charging system illuminates.
    3. Go to "DC-DC assembly" diagnosis.
3
Test the status of 12 V battery.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Test the status of 12 V battery.
  3. Does the 12 V battery pass the test?
    4. Replace the 12 V battery.
4
Check the fuse of instrument fuse box.
  1. Check whether the fuse F2/20(10A) of wireless phone charging module in the instrument fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
5
Check the wiring harness connector of wireless phone charging module.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G91 from the wireless phone charging module.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of wireless phone charging module is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check the GND circuit of wireless phone charging module.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector G91-8 of wireless phone charging module and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    G91-8 GND Always Below 1 Ω
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check the power supply of wireless phone charging module.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector G91-1 of wireless phone charging module and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    G91-1 GND Always 11~14 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the wireless phone charging module.
8
Check the wiring harness connector of instrument fuse box.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector G82 from the instrument fuse box.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of instrument fuse box is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
9
Check whether the power circuit of wireless phone charging module is open-circuited.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector G92-1 of wireless phone charging module and the wiring harness connector G82-21 of instrument fuse box.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    G92-1 G82-21 Always Below 1 Ω
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the instrument fuse box.
