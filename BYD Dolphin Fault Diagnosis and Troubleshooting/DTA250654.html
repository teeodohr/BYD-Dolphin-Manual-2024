Conventional fault diagnosis

Visual inspection

  1. Confirm the customer's problems.

  2. Visually inspect for obvious signs of mechanical damage and collision deformation.

  3. If the problem observed or raised is obvious and the cause has been found, the cause must be corrected before proceeding to the next step.

  4. If the visual inspection is passed, identify the fault and refer to the Fault Symptoms Table.

Conventional Fault Symptoms Table

Symptoms Possible causes Recommended maintenance measures
Heavy steering

  1. Insufficient tire pressure

  2. Front wheel alignment incorrect.

  3. Steering knuckle worn.

  4. Steering gear assembly fault.

  5. Electric power steering column assembly fault.

  1. Check the tire pressure, and adjust it if abnormal.

  2. Perform four-wheel alignment.

  3. Replace the steering knuckle.

  4. Replace the steering gear assembly.

  5. Replace the electric power steering column assembly.
Failure to return in place

  1. Insufficient tire pressure

  2. Front wheel alignment incorrect.

  3. Steering gear assembly fault.

  4. Electric power steering column assembly fault.

  1. Check the tire pressure, and adjust it if abnormal.

  2. Perform four-wheel alignment.

  3. Replace the steering gear assembly.

  4. Replace the electric power steering column assembly.
Too large clearance

  1. Steering knuckle worn.

  2. Universal joint worn.

  3. Steering gear assembly fault.

  1. Replace the steering gear assembly.

  2. Replace the universal joint.

  3. Replace the electric power steering column assembly.
Abnormal noise

  1. Speed reduction mechanism worn.

  2. Universal joint worn.

  3. Electric power steering column assembly fault.

  1. Replace the electric power steering column assembly.

  2. Replace the universal joint.

  3. Replace the electric power steering column assembly.
Shaking in steering wheel

  1. Dynamic balance of wheel incorrect.

  2. Electric power steering column assembly fault.

  1. Check and adjust the dynamic balance of wheel.

  2. Replace the electric power steering column assembly.
