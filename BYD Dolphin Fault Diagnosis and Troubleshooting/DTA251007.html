Diagnostic instructions

When diagnosing the fault of the taillight cluster, understand and get familiar with its control logic. Before diagnosis, determine the fault symptom described by the customer, and then analyze the fault cause of the taillight cluster, which are helpful to determine the correct fault diagnosis steps. During the inspection and measurement of the taillight cluster wiring harness and the taillight cluster, the data flow and action test functions of VDS are preferred to improve diagnosis efficiency and shorten maintenance time. Implement standard operating procedures after confirming the fault, and check the operation of the taillight cluster after maintenance.

It is more difficult to diagnose intermittent faults than continuous faults. Most intermittent faults are caused by poor electrical connections or faulty circuits. Therefore, an inspection of the suspected faulty circuit can help avoid replacing a functioning part.

General-purpose equipment

  • Socket wrench set

  • Screwdriver

  • Pry plate for interior trimming

  • Torque wrench

  • VDS

  • Multimeter

Reminder

  • When using a multimeter to measure the voltage, select the closest range.

  • When using a multimeter to measure the resistance, select the closest range.

Precautions

Warning

  • Never pull the wiring harness when disconnecting the connector for fear of damage to the wiring harness.

  • Never scratch the surface of the light cover when removing/installing the combination headlight assembly.

Caution

  • Never check the electrical signal of the system by piercing the wiring harness with a fine needle.

  • After the installation, check whether the specified torques are applied to the fasteners.
