U029D00 Loss of communication with ESP

DTC description

U029D00 Loss of communication with ESP

Fault symptoms

The intelligent power brake system fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fuse fault.

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  3. IPB controller fault.

Fault setting conditions

Any monitoring message is lost 10 times consecutively.

Fault triggering conditions

  1. The controller voltage ranges from 9 V to 16 V.

  2. The vehicle is powered on and initialized for 3 s.

  3. The private CAN is not busoff.

  4. The factory mode is disabled.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the communication network
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Does the intelligent integrated brake controller module pass network detection?
    5. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the fuse of IPB controller
  1. Check whether the fuses F1/30(60A), F1/42(5A) and F1/44(60A) of front compartment fuse box are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of IPB controller
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector B03_1 from the IPB controller.
  3. Check the wiring harness connector of IPB controller for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the power supply of IPB controller

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B03_1-36 of IPB controller and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B03_1-46 of IPB controller and GND.
  4. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector B03_1-14 of IPB controller and GND.
