Diagnosis steps

1
Check the wiring harness connector of NFC module.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector T21 from the NFC module.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of NFC module is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
2
Check the GND wiring harness of NFC module.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector T21-2 of NFC module and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    T21-2 GND Always Below 1 Ω
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the power supply of NFC module.
  1. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector T21-1 of NFC module and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    T21-1 GND Always 11~14 V
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Go to Step 6.Go to Step 6.
4
Check the wiring harness connector of the left domain control unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector TG64(I) from the left domain control unit.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the power wiring harness of NFC module is open-circuited.
  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector T21-1 of NFC module and the wiring harness connector TG64(I)-28 of left domain control unit.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    T21-1 TG64(I)-28 Always Below 1 Ω
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the left domain control unit.
6
Check the CAN circuit of NFC module.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector T21-3/4 of NFC module and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)

    T21-3

    		 GND Always

    2.5~3.5 V

    T21-4

    1.5~2.5 V
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Replace the NFC module. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
