U025387 Loss of communication with compressor

DTC description

U025387 Loss of communication with compressor
Fault symptoms A/C system function fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Blown fuse.

  2. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  3. CAN network communication is faulty.

  4. Electric compressor is faulty.
Fault setting conditions If the left domain control unit does not receive the electric compressor message within a certain period of time, this DTC will be generated.
Fault triggering conditions The Start/Stop switch is turned to ON position.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the electric compressor communication network.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Perform network detection with VDS.
  4. Check whether the electric compressor passes network detection.
    5. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the electric compressor fuse.
  1. Check whether the fuse F1/12(7.5A) of front compartment fuse box is normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of electric compressor.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the high voltage connector BA17 from the electric compressor.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of electric compressor is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check the power supply of electric compressor.
