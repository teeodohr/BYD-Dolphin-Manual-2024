B163A1A First row right side airbag resistance = 0

DTC description

B163A1A First row right side airbag resistance = 0

Fault symptoms

The SRS partially fails.

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. Driver side airbag fault

  3. Airbag controller fault.

Fault setting conditions

Driver side airbag resistance = 0

Fault triggering conditions

The airbag controller receives the signal that the resistance of the driver side airbag is 0, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the airbag DTC
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the airbag interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the airbag interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check for the same DTC.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of driver's seat.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K03(B) from the driver's seat.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of driver's seat is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of airbag controller
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG10 from the airbag controller.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness and connector of airbag controller are normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the driver's seat connector circuit is short-circuited to GND.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K03(B)-1 of driver's seat and GND.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector K03(B)-2 of driver's seat and GND.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    K03(B)-1 GND Always Above 10 kΩ
    K03(B)-2
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the driver's seat connector circuits are short-circuited to each other.

  1. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors K03(B)-1 and K03(B)-2 of driver's seat.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    K03(B)-1 K03(B)-2 Always Above 10 kΩ
  2. Check whether the result is normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check whether the driver's seat connector circuit is short-circuited to power supply.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K03(B)-1 of driver's seat and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector K03(B)-2 of driver's seat and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    K03(B)-1 GND Always Not present
    K03(B)-2
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Check whether the circuit from airbag controller to driver's seat connector is open-circuited.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector KG10-4 of airbag controller and the wiring harness connector K03(B)-2 of driver's seat.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connector KG10-50 of airbag controller and the wiring harness connector K03(B)-1 of driver's seat.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    KG10–4 K03(B)-2 Always Below 1 Ω
    KG10–50 K03(B)-1
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
8
Replace the driver's seat assembly and check the DTC.
  1. Replace the driver's seat assembly and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Read the DTC of SRS.
  4. Clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC of SRS.
  7. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    8. The system is normal. Replace the airbag controller.
