Conventional fault diagnosis

Symptoms

Possible causes

Recommended maintenance measures

Abnormal sound during window regulation

Sundries in the glass run channel

Clean and lubricate the glass run channel

Sundries in the weather strip

Clean and lubricate the weather strip

Deformation of glass guide rail

Replace the glass guide rail

Lack of lubrication for window regulator steel wire

Apply lubricating grease or replace the door module

Failure of the whole power window system to work

Low supply voltage of the vehicle

Start the vehicle by connecting external power, make DC-DC work, charge the 12 V battery and replace it if necessary

Blown fuse

Check that there is no short circuit in relevant wiring harnesses, and then replace the fuse with one of the same specification

Wiring harness or connector fault

Check and repair the wiring harness and connectors according to the circuit diagram

CAN communication system fault

Check and repair the CAN communication system

Failure of one power window to work

Wiring harness short-circuited or open-circuited

Repair or replace the faulty wiring harness

Loose connectors, poor contact and corrosion of terminals

Repair or replace the wiring harness connector

Fault of window regulator motor at a corresponding side

Replace the door module

Fault of window regulator switch at a corresponding side

Replace the window regulator switch set

Left domain control unit fault

Replace the left domain control unit

Right domain control unit fault

Replace the right domain control unit

Main controller switch fault

Replace the main controller switch
