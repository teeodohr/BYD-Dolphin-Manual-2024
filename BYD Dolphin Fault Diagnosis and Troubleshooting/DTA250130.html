P1B2517 Low-voltage supply voltage too high

DTC description

P1B2517 Low-voltage supply voltage too high
Fault symptoms -
Possible causes of the fault

  1. Fuse fault.

  2. 12 V battery fault.

  3. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  4. Charging system fault.

  5. Motor control unit internal fault
Fault setting conditions Low voltage detected to be greater than 18 V
Fault triggering conditions After the vehicle is powered on, the system detects that the low voltage is greater than 18 V, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of front drive motor control unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  7. Check for the same DTC.
    8. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Test the status of 12 V battery.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Test the status of 12 V battery.
  3. Does the 12 V battery pass the test?
    4. Replace the 12 V battery.
3
Check the DTC of DC-DC system.
  1. Read the DTC of DC-DC system with the VDS.
  2. Check for any DTC.
    3. Go to "DC-DC assembly" diagnosis.
4
Check the fuse of integrated front drive controller.
  1. Check whether the front compartment fuse box fuses F1/33(1A) and F1/09(15A) are normal.
    2. Replace the fuse.
5
Check the wiring harness and connector of integrated front drive controller.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK45(A) from the integrated front drive controller.
  3. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK45(B) from the integrated front drive controller.
  4. Check whether the connector of integrated front drive controller is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check the constant power of integrated front drive controller.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-20 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-21 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  4. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-26 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
  5. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK45(B)-27 of integrated front drive controller and GND.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    BK45(B)-20 GND Always 11~14 V
    BK45(B)-21
    BK45(B)-26

  6. Check whether the result is normal.
    7. Replace the integrated front drive controller.
7
Check whether the power supply of integrated front drive controller module is open-circuited.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connectors B1C and B1D from the front compartment fuse box.
  3. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-20 and B1C-25 of integrated front drive controller.
  4. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-26 and B1C-25 of integrated front drive controller.
  5. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-27 and B1D-20 of integrated front drive controller.
  6. Measure the resistance between the wiring harness connectors BK45(B)-21 and B1D-20 of integrated front drive controller.
    Connector Condition Resistance

    BK45(B)-27
  7. Check whether the result is normal.
    8. Repair or replace the wiring harness. Replace the front compartment fuse box.
