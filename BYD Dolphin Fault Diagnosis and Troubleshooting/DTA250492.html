P2B8500 Abnormal HVSU power supply

DTC description

P2B8500 Abnormal HVSU power supply

Fault symptoms

The instrument displays "Powertrain Fault".

Possible causes of the fault

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault.

  2. High-voltage battery pack fault

  3. Integrated front drive controller fault

Fault setting conditions

The voltage is less than the specified threshold.

Fault triggering conditions

When the vehicle is powered on, if the voltage is less than the specified threshold, a DTC will be generated.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of battery execution and sampling unit.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the battery execution and sampling unit and read the DTC.
  5. Check for other DTCs.
    6. Prioritize troubleshooting of other DTCs.
2
Check other system DTCs.
  1. Enter other systems and read DTCs.
  2. Check for any DTC.
    3. Troubleshoot other system DTCs.
3
Check the wiring harness connector of high-voltage battery pack.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector BK51 from the high-voltage battery pack.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of high-voltage battery pack is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the integrated front drive controller outputs HVSU power supply signal.

  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  2. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK51-5 of high-voltage battery pack and GND.
  3. Measure the voltage between the wiring harness connector BK51-6 of high-voltage battery pack and GND.
