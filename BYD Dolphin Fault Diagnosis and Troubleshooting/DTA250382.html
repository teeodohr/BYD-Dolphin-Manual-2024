U110017 Voltage too high

DTC description

U110017 Voltage too high
Fault symptoms The BSD system fails.
Possible causes of the fault

  1. On-board power supply system fault

  2. False alarm caused by other faults

  3. Rear left corner radar fault
Fault setting conditions Power supply voltage higher than 16 V for 1000 ms
Fault triggering conditions The vehicle is powered on and initialized for 3 s.

Diagnosis steps

1
Check the DTC of rear left corner radar.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the BSD interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the BSD interface again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the on-board power supply system.
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OK position.
  2. Measure the voltage of on-board power supply system.

    12 V battery

  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Discharge the 12 V battery; if the fault still exists, replace it.
3
Check all systems for any DTCs.
  1. Are there any DTCs in other systems?
    2. Diagnose other DTCs first. Replace the rear left corner radar.
