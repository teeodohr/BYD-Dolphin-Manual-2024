Structural characteristics of the body

Collision force transferring route

  1. Energy absorption design

    • In order to better protect the personal safety of passengers in a collision, the body is designed with the orientation of impact force through stress change.

    • After the energy-absorbing structure is stressed, it absorbs huge impact force, resulting in large deformation.

    • The passenger compartment is not easy to deform after being buffered by the front and rear energy-absorbing structures of the body. In addition, due to the energy absorption and buffering in the front, the inertial impact force in the rear is small, so as to effectively protect the safety of passengers.

    • This design will enable a transfer of the impact force as designed.

    • This design is available in the front and rear of the body.

  2. Collision force transferring route of passenger compartment

    • It has an integral frame and bearing performance, which maintains the bending and torsional rigidity of the vehicle, spreads energy in front and side collision, and features excellent shock absorption and sound insulation performance.

    • All plates act as an important support to protect the safety of passengers. The body pillar reinforcement plate and the inner reinforcement plate of sill panel are made of ultra-high strength steel plates.
