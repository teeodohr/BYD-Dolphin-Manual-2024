Replacement of right side wall assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the roof cover assembly. Refer toReplacement of roof cover assembly 

  2. Use a sander to grind the sealant on the inner side of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Cut with disc sander, etc.

  3. Use a sander to grind the sealant on the outer side of right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Cut with disc sander, etc.

  4. Remove 21 welding spots of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  5. Remove 30 welding spots of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  6. Remove 35 welding spots of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  7. Remove 16 welding spots of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  8. Remove 13 welding spots of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  9. Remove 12 welding spots of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  10. Remove 49 welding spots of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  11. Remove 12 welding spots and take out the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

Installation

  1. Fix the right side wall assembly to the installation position.

  2. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the right side wall assembly and front compartment parts. Refer toFront door 

  3. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the right side wall assembly and rear compartment parts. Refer toRear compartment 

  4. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the right side wall assembly and underbody parts. Refer toUnderbody 

  5. Weld 12 welding spots on the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

  11. Weld 30 welding spots on the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  12. Weld 21 welding spots on the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  13. Apply sealant to the outer side of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  14. Apply sealant to the inner side of the right side wall assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  15. After applying the external coating, apply rust inhibitor to the inner plate part of the welding spots of the closed section structure.

  16. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
