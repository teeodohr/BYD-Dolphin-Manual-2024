An introduction to the protective equipment for painting operation

Goggles

  1. Function

    • Goggles are protective devices with transparent lenses that allow operators to clearly observe the working surface while protecting their eyes.

  2. Key points

    • Easy to wear and use.

    • Prevent the splashed debris from entering the eyes during the grinding operation.

    • Prevent solvent-based liquids from coming into contact with eyes.

    • Avoid the harm of small particles to the eyes when using an air gun.

Dust mask

  1. Key points

    • A dust mask is a type of protective device used to filter dust, particles suspended in the air, and some harmful gases and dust mist.

    • Protect the respiratory system from dust generated during welding and grinding.

    Caution

    • When dry grinding, wear a anti-dust mask and replace it according to the specified frequency of use.

    • Store them properly after using to avoid secondary pollution.

    Reminder

    • Anti-dust masks are suitable for use in sheet metal spray workshops: when sanding damages the old paint surface, dry grinding putty, and when sanding and applying prime

    • Anti-dust masks that meet the quality standards can filter out more than 95% of the dust particles during the operation, but they are not suitable for painting or places where gaseous chemical hazardous substances exist.

    • Pull the adjustable bungee cords around your head and your neck.

    • Cover the upper part of the nose and pinch the upper metal strip so that it fits close to the bridge of the nose.

Filter-type respiratory protective mask

  1. Key points

    • A filter-type respiratory protective mask is a protective equipment used to filter the toxic gases volatilized when using paint products and at the same time filter air-borne dust particles.

    • Prevent inhalation of organic solvent gases, paint mist, and dust.

    Caution

    • Use carbon canisters up to standard, which are not out of date.

    • Seal and store them after use. Clean and disinfect them regularly.

    • Regularly check and replace activated carbon and the rubber seal in contact with the face.

    Reminder

    • It is suitable for short time painting operation outdoors or in places with good ventilation. Wear air supplied gas mask when spraying in large areas.

    • Wear the mask by fixing the elastic ropes on the back side of head.

    • Cover the nose and mouth. Make the mask fit.

Air-fed protective mask

  1. Key points

    • To prevent inhalation of organic solvent gases, we can wear the air-fed mask that can be classified into two types: full-face mask and half-face mask.

    Caution

    • To use the compressed air, install a filter that can filter oil and water and a refrigeration dryer to ensure the air quality.

    • Wear air supplied masks for painting in large quantities or for a long time, or in the spray booth, sealed compartment, oil tank and other closed and unventilated environment.

Protective gloves

  1. Key points

    • Protect hands from sharp steel plate edges and burrs.

    • Protect the operator's hands when handling vehicle body parts.

    • Protect the operator's hands when using basic operating tools.

    • Protect the operator's hands when grasping hard objects.

    Caution

    • Never wear cotton gloves when using the hammer.

    • Wear cotton gloves during putty grinding and new plate handling.

    • Never wear cotton gloves when using sharp objects to prevent the gloves from being penetrated and hurting fingers.

Solvent resistant gloves

  1. Key points

    • Prevent organic solvents from contacting the skin and entering the body.

    • Protect the operator's skin from solvent invasion during the use of cleaning agents.

    • Protect the skin from solvent invasion when applying sealant.

    • Protect the skin from solvent invasion during the contact with corrosive liquids.

    Caution
    During paint spraying, hands are often exposed to organic solvents, diluent and paints. Wear solvent-proof gloves whenever there is a chance to contact them.

Protective workwear

  1. Key points

    • Workwear is protective clothing worn during work and production.

    • It has various protective functions, such as waterproof, dustproof, oil-proof and radiation-proof, etc.

    • Prevent the dust, paint mist, and organic solvent generated during the spraying operation from harming your body.

    Reminder

    • Work clothes made of conductive materials can prevent static electricity.

    • Anti-static work clothes should be worn throughout the spraying construction in the paint booth.

Safety shoes

  1. Key points

    • Safety shoes are equipped with protective steel plates at the front end, which can prevent smashing and crushing harm to the feet and protect the toes from being injured by falling objects.

    • The bottom of the shoe is made of insulating rubber, which can prevent electric shock to a certain degree.

    • It has a good anti-skid function.

    Caution

    • The maintenance personnel shall wear safety boots up to standard throughout the operation.

    • Ensure the safety boots fit well.

    • Make long shoelaces fit.
