Safety protection measures for painting operation

Cleaning and degreasing process

Processes

Cleaning and degreasing

Hazardous ingredients

Organic solvents

Existing damage

Respiration, splashing, skin and eyes

Personal prevention and protection

Anti-organic gas mask, solvent-resistant gloves, and protective goggles

Basic protection

Well-ventilated environment and an operation booth equipped with an exhaust system
