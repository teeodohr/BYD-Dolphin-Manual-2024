Sheet metal shrinking process

Preparation for sheet metal shrinking

Reminder

  • Purpose of steel plate quenching The expanded steel plate fails to provide sufficient rigidity, and the expanded bulge also makes it impossible to apply the putty. Hence, it is necessary to improve the strength of steel plate through quenching and make the excessive bulge shrink.

  • Quenching is to heat the steel plate and then rapidly cool it down, so as to recover the original shape of the expanded area of the steel plate by virtue of heat expansion and cold contraction.

Grinding preparation

  1. Key points

    • Grind the paint film or other impurities on the surface of the panel.

    • Select an appropriate position to connect the ground wire.

    • Carry out a trial operation on waste plates of the same material and thickness.

    • In the shrink process, a sheet metal hammer can be used to assist in knocking repair. Only an air gun can be used for cooling, and water cooling is prohibited.

Sheet metal shrinking methods

Reminder
The methods of quenching include carbon rod quenching and copper rod quenching.

Carbon rod shrinking for sheet metal

  1. Key points

    • Carbon rod shrinking is applied to the large-area extended repair part of the body's outer panel.

    • According to the size of the raised position, a heated carbon rod can be used to draw a spiral or circle shape. The circle drawing should be conducted from large to small and from outside to inside.

  2. Operation steps

    • Adjust the welding machine mode.

    • Heating is carried out after the carbon rod contacts the panel.

    • Use an air gun for cooling.

    • Cooling time 5-6 s.

Caution

  • Never use oxyacetylene quenching which will reduce the strength and durability of the plate.

  • During the quenching process, a sheet metal hammer can be used for the repair, and an air gun shall be used for cooling, but using water for cooling is prohibited.

Copper rod shrinking

  1. Key points

    • The copper rod can only be used for spot shrinking.

    • The shrinking process is based on the principle of thermal expansion and cold contraction.

  2. Operation steps

    • Adjust the shrinking mode of the welding machine.

    • Use a copper rod to contact the panel and apply force to press the panel in the extended position.

    • Apply heat and hold for 1-2 s.

    • Quickly cool the heating area with an air gun.

    Caution
    Keep the electrode head and plate clean and free of other stains during the quenching process. The plate may be burned through.

Cases

Dented or perforated panels (bad cases)

  1. Undesirable phenomena.

    • Noticeable dents or perforations are generated on the surface of the outer plate after the shrinking operation.

  2. Causes

    • Inappropriate adjustment of welding machine parameters and excessive current.

    • The surface of the panels has dirt or impurities.

    • The power-on time is too long.

  3. Solutions.

    • Adjust the equipment parameters to shorten the power-on time.

    • Clean impurities or residual paint film on the shrinking surface.

    • In case of any hole, fill it with a welding machine before handing it over to the next process.
