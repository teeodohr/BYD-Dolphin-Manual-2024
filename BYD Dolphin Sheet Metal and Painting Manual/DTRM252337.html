Quality control standard

Items

Requirements

Flatness

  • The highest bulge of the plane after repair in the straight direction shall not be higher than the original plane.

  • The lowest point of the dent after repair shall not be 1 mm lower than the original plane. The maximum area of each dent shall not be more than 5 cm×5 cm.

  • If the thickness of poly-putty layer in the working face is greater than 3 mm or there are quality problems, remove and repair.

Radian of circular arc

  • After repair, the arc surface shall coincide with the original arc surface, and after large-area repair, it shall coincide with the overall arc surface.

  • After repair, the arc surface shall not have any bulge higher than the original arc surface.

  • After repair, the area of each dent on the arc surface shall not be more than 5 cm × 5 cm, and the lowest point shall not be 1 mm lower than the original arc surface.

Surface strength

  • With a force of 5 kg applied at any point of the repaired surface, the sag shall not exceed 1 mm.

  • There shall be no drum rebound in the repaired surface.

Line quality

  • The highest bulge of the line after repair shall not be higher than the original plane.

  • After repair, the lowest point of the line shall not be 2 mm lower than the original plane.

  • After repair, the maximum bending point of the line shall not deviate from the reference line by 1 mm.

Edge quality

  • After repair, the edge plane shall be flat in the vertical direction, with a maximum deformation of less than 1 mm.

  • After repair, the outer edge parallel to the plane shall not show obvious bending, with a maximum deformation of less than 1 mm.

  • After repair, the outer edge shall be uniform in thickness, with a maximum difference of less than 1 mm.

Edge quality

  • The gap width shall be the same as that of the original vehicle, with an error of not more than 1 mm.

  • The width of the same gap shall be consistent, and the difference between the widest and narrowest positions shall be less than 1 mm.

  • The gap shall be flat on both sides, with a maximum height difference of less than 1 mm.

Airtightness

  • The sealant shall be applied at the joint of lapped steel plates.

  • After repair, the doors and windows must be tested for water tightness, without water or air leakage.

  • All trims, sealing strips and bonding parts shall not have gaps on the joint surface after assembly.

Stability

  • All spare parts must be stable after assembly, without any looseness.

  • All screws and rubber buckles must be installed completely without omission.

Protection of non-working face

  • No scratches or abrasions are allowed on the painted and sprayed surfaces of non-working face.

  • Glass and working painted surface shall not be scalded by welding slag.

  • The excess glue after bonding must be cleaned up.

Anti-corrosion quality

  • After repairing the non-painted working face, anti-rust paint shall be sprayed.

  • The inner side of the working face of sheet metal parts removed and repaired must be sprayed with anti-corrosive wax before assembly.

Cleanliness

  • During process inspection, the assembled accessories such as door panel, interior trim panel, pressing strip and rubber strip must be cleaned.

  • During the completion inspection, the whole vehicle must be cleaned inside and outside.
