Replacement of front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam

Removal

  1. Remove the left mounting plate assembly of front bumper beam. Refer to

  2. Use a sander to grind the sealant on the inner side of the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Cut with disc sander, etc.

  3. Use a sander to grind the sealant on the outer side of the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Cut with disc sander, etc.

  4. Remove 11 welding spots and take out the lower section of rear reinforcement pillar of front left hubcap.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  5. Remove 50 welding spots and take out the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

Installation

  1. Clean the joint of front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam and apply adhesive.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  2. Fix the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam to the installation position.

  3. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam and the front compartment parts. Refer to 

  4. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam and the underbody parts. Refer to 

  5. Weld 50 welding spots on the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  6. Weld 11 welding spots on the lower section of rear reinforcement pillar of front left hubcap.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  7. Apply sealant to the outer side of the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  8. Apply sealant to the inner side of the front section assembly of front left longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  9. After applying the external coating, apply rust inhibitor to the inner plate part of the welding spots of the closed section structure.

  10. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
