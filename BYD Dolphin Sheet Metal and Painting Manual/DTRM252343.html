Basic service process for damage to the body steel plate

Manual shaping

Reminder

  • Master the fundamental using methods of tools and equipment, and get familiar with various tips of the maintenance process.

  • Basic process of manual shaping maintenance:damage judgment — accessory removal — shaping — trimming and grinding — grinding effect.

Damage determination

Reminder
There are four ways of damage judgment: visual judgment, touch judgment, ruler judgment and profile gauge judgment.

  1. Visual determination

    1. Key points

      • The damage can be quickly determined.

      • Angle and position affect the determination result.

    2. Operation steps

      • Select the appropriate light source.

      • Observe the deformed area from the front.

      • Observe the deformed area from the side.

  2. Determination by touch

    1. Key points

      • In addition to determining the dent by touch, it is also possible to check the strength change of the plate by pressing.

    2. Operation steps

      • Touch and move in a star shape in the deformed area.

      • Locate the deformed position by feeling shape change with palm.

      • Locate the deformed position by feeling shape change with palm.

    Reminder
    The touch judgment method requires that the sheet metal technician has long term work experiences to obtain good effects.

  3. Comparison with a ruler

    1. Key points

      • The damage can be quickly and intuitively determined.

    2. Operation steps

      • Select a location symmetrical to the damage for comparison.

      • Check the damage with a ruler and other tools against the deformed area, and determine the damage through comparison between both sides.

      Reminder
      There are many methods available for comparison judgment, symmetrical comparison with tools.

  4. Determination with a profile gauge

    1. Key points

      • This method enables the detection of the deformation during maintenance.

      • It can also enable the inspection of maintenance effect.

    2. Operation steps

      • Select an undeformed location symmetrical to the damage for comparison.

      • Set the profile gauge pins against the undeformed plate, so that they have the same profile.

      • Compare the obtained profile and the deformed area to determine the deformation.

Removal of accessories and preparations for repair

  1. Preparations

    1. Key points

      • Select appropriate removal and installation tools.

      • Remove the body accessories that affect service.

      • Protect the undamaged parts of the body.

    2. Operation steps

      • If shrinking is required, disconnect the negative terminal of 12 V battery.

      • Protect surrounding parts with masking materials.

      • Complete other removal and preparations.

    Caution
    Always remove the negative pole of battery when using the dent puller for maintenance, to avoid large current passing through the vehicle body and damaging lines on the vehicle.

Shaping

  1. Preparations

    1. Key points

      • Select a sheet metal hammer or crowbar according to the deformed shape for repair.

      • Perform unaligned hammering first, and then aligned hammering for finishing.

    2. Operation steps

      • Use a crowbar with small angle to pry the edges and corners.

      • Select a line chisel for the edge line according to the shape.

      • Finish the plate surface with a bucking block with smooth surface, edges and corners.

    Caution
    Observe the outward direction principle for plate repair, i.e. repair the inner structural plates before the outer ones.

Grinding after finishing

  1. Grinding the old paint film

    1. Key points

      • Select a pneumatic or electric grinder for use with sandpaper.

      • Carefully remove the minimal area of paint film in the damaged area.

    2. Operation steps

      • Remove the uneven traces left by bucking block, hammer and crowbar.

      • Select appropriate sandpaper to grind the damaged area.

      • Carry out grinding from coarse to fine.

    Caution
    Grind the damaged area only. Never grind excessively.

Effect after grinding

  1. Effect after grinding

    1. Key points

      • Good surface formation: After repair and grinding, the contact edge between the bare metal and paint is linear, which is conducive to improving the efficiency of the next process.

Cases

  1. Grinding the old paint film (bad case)

    1. Undesirable phenomena.

      • The conventional grinding disc is likely to cut steel plates, with spark splashing during grinding.

    2. Causes

      • This may cause the body steel plate to become thinner, affecting the durability of the body.

    3. Solutions.

      • Replace the black diamond grinding wheel or abrasive cloth flap wheel.

  2. Effect after grinding (bad case)

    1. Undesirable phenomena.

      • Poor surface is formed.

    2. Causes

      • After grinding, the bare metal surface of the steel plate has obvious hammer marks and shrinking marks and jagged joint with the paint, resulting in difficulties in making feather edges in the next process, prolonging the operation time and lowering the efficiency.

    3. Solutions.

      • The sheet metal technician shall grind carefully according to the marking line of the damaged area, and shall not operate beyond the marking line.

Repair with meson machine

Reminder

  • The dent puller is usually used for repairing the damaged plates with the inner area inaccessible, e.g. the external panel of door, the external board of rocker panel and the front section of the rear fender.

  • Procedure of steel plate repairing with dent puller: damage judgment — paint film removal — dent puller commissioning — welding and pulling — welding trace grinding — corrosion protection of inner cavity.

Damage determination

  1. Deformation determination

    1. Key points

      • Select the standard damage determination in the manual shaping process to determine the damage degree and scope of damaged plates.

      • Mark the damage scope with a colored pen to avoid damage expansion during service, consequently reducing working efficiency.

Removal of paint film

  1. Removal of paint film

    1. Key points

      • Use pneumatic or electric grinding tools and sandpaper to remove the old paint film and expose the steel plate.

      • Use an air gun and dust cloth to remove the dust.

    Caution

    • Never remove the paint film using the grinding tool with excessively large particles since such large particles would thin the metallic plate and reduce its strength.

    • Only remove the paint film over the area to be welded with washers.

    • The width of paint film ground shall be that of the damaged area ± 10 mm.

Adjustment of meson machine

  1. Commissioning of meson machine

    1. Key points

      • Select the working mode of meson machine.

      • Adjust the current.

      • Carry out trial welding on plates with the same material thickness.

    Caution
    Never operate the dent puller on the operating panel without commissioning to avoid burning through the plate.

Welding and drawing

  1. Repair of plate plane

    1. Key points

      • When drawing the dent on the body panel, hammer the bulge around due to extrusion with a sheet metal hammer.

      • Do not draw once to shape. Draw the plate surface by gradually increasing the force several times until it is restored to the height before deformation.

      • During drawing, use tools for measurement and inspection in time to prevent the surface from being too high due to over-drawing.

    2. Operation steps

      • Select the appropriate meson.

      • Adjust the current.

      • Carry out trial welding.

      • After grinding the plate, weld the meson or corrugated wires one by one.

      • Draw with a pull hook.

    Caution
    The recovered plate surface shall be a bit lower than the original surface, i.e. 0.5~1 mm. Make sure there is no high point or hole.

  2. Repair of the body edge line

    1. Key points

      • Draw from inside to outside in principle, and avoid drawing from the location with the least serious deformation, resulting in excessive extension or bulged points after steel plate drawing.

      • To repair large area of deformation, use a fixed pull tower to assist in drawing.

    Caution

    • Knock the area around the pulled dent with a sheet metal hammer when pulling.

    • The ridges after repair shall be about 0.5 mm lower than those original ones on the vehicle.

Grinding the welding marks

  1. Grinding the welding marks

    1. Key points

      • Use a pneumatic or electric grinder and the sandpaper to remove welding marks from the surface of the repaired plate.

      • Finish the bulged points with the tip of a finishing hammer.

    Caution
    Never remove the sharp high points directly with a grinder. Otherwise, the steel plate will be thinned or ground through.
    Reminder

    • Press the repaired plate to inspect its strength.

    • Quenching is required if the strength is decreased.

    • Further pulling is required in case of any undesirable dents.

Anti-corrosion treatment of cavity

  1. Anti-corrosion treatment of inner side

    1. Key points

      • The inner side of the ablated plate must be treated with anti-corrosion wax during welding with a meson machine.

      • For positions inaccessible, anti-corrosion wax can be sprayed by inserting a hose into the cavity.

      • The area with damaged sealant shall be treated and reapplied with sealant.

    Reminder
    Corrosion protection is a necessary process after plate maintenance.

Cases

  1. Excessive grinding after repair (bad case)

    1. Undesirable phenomena.

      • After repair, the paint film is ground beyond the damage scope.

      • The contact between the exposed metal surface of the plate and the paint is messy.

    2. Causes

      • Grinding the paint film beyond the marked damage scope may artificially expand the damage area, resulting in increased workload of the next process.

    3. Solutions.

      • The operator shall use the grinder carefully.

      • The joint between exposed metal and paint film shall be linear.

      • The area of paint film ground shall not exceed the scope indicated in damage assessment.
