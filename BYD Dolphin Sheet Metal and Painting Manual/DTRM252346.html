Operation and maintenance of shaping tools

Introduction to manual shaping tools

Reminder
Manual shaping tool and maintenance method: Manual sheet metal shaping (manual shaping in short) is a fundamental skill that all sheet metal operators shall possess. Proper use of manual shaping tools can improve maintenance efficiency and quality.

Sheet metal hammer

  1. Function

    • The sheet metal hammer is used to repair the smooth steel plate surface and basic edge line.

    • The pick-end hammer is used to knock folds or bulges on the plate.

    • The surface-compressing hammer is used to restore the lightly stretched metal parts to original strength, with the serrated surface "clamping" the metal. When the hammer knocks down a high bump (always supported by a bucking block), the metal will be compressed to its original shape.

    • The rubber hammer is used for large-area repair or relatively soft materials. For example, when repairing aluminum plates, the use of rubber hammer can avoid hammer marks on the plates.

  2. Key points

    • The hammer has an arc surface and a horizontal plane in the top end.

    • It usually features a wooden hammer handle.

    • Sheet metal hammers include pick-end hammer, pick hammer, surface-compressing hammer, rubber hammer, etc.

    • The pick-end hammer has a conical passivated chisel at one end and a circular impact surface at the other end.

    • The surface-compressing hammer has a circular smooth surface at one end, and a square serrated surface at the other end.

    • The rubber hammer has a head made of soft and hard rubber, which is light and easy to use.

  3. How to use

    • Slight hammering requires only a wrist swing.

    • Elbow swing can assist in slight increase of force.

    • Do not rely on shoulder swing during manual shaping repair.

    • Hold the rear of the handle 1-2 cm from the end and apply force with your thumb and index finger.

    • When hammering, keep the hammer surface parallel to the plate surface, and hammer slightly using the rebounding.

    Caution

    • Check the head and handle of the hammer to see whether they are secure. Never knock sharp objects.

    • Use the sheet metal hammer on the vehicle body discreetly. Never use it for maintenance of body structural members with large force, or use it as a common hammer.

    • Check the hammer surface regularly, and take proper measures for repair in case of obvious traces discovered. Otherwise, the surface of repaired plate will be scratched.

    • Trim with sand paper #120, #180, #320 and #1000 in order till the surface becomes smooth.

Jacking block

  1. Function

    • The bucking block is used in conjunction with the body hammer to repair the plate damage so that the plate can return to its original surface.

  2. Key points

    • Secure the bucking block by hand against the back of the dent, and hammer the bulge on the other side with a hammer.

    • There are various shapes of bucking blocks to adapt to various profiles of the plate. If the original profile needs to be restored, the radius of the bucking block shall be smaller than that of the original profile of the plate.

    • The bucking block is made of high-strength steel and has a combination of high, medium, low, flat or multi-shape working faces.

  3. How to use

    • The operator takes the palm as the force application point.

    • Hold the edge of the bucking block tightly with five fingers to prevent it from slipping.

    • For hammering with aligned bucking, the bucking block is vertical to the hammer, corresponding to the principle of aligned hammering.

    • For hammering with unaligned bucking, the bucking block is not vertical to the hammer, and the bucking point is not the hammering point, corresponding to the principle of unaligned hammering.

    Caution

    • Never hold the sharp tip of jacking block against the internal plane of the steel plate to avoidf unexpected deformation of steel plate surface.

    • It is generally recommended that the radian of the surface of jacking block is about 80% of the original radian of the steel plate. Never use the contact face with the radian of surface too small.

    • Never support in the vertical direction where possible because the plate is liable to expand when supported in such a manner.

    • Check and service the jacking block regularly in order to keep the work face in good conditions.

Line chisel

  1. Function

    • It is used with a hammer for the deformed parts that need to be repaired at edges and corners.

  2. Key points

    • It can repair the edge lines of different lengths and shapes.

Profile gauge

  1. Function

    • The profile gauge is an auxiliary inspection tool for repair of the body outer panel.

    • It features a retractable pin structure.

    • It is used for measurement before repair of plate damage.

    • It is used for inspection during plate repair.

    • It can measure the flatness and radian of plates after repair.

  2. Key points

    • It can repair the edge lines of different lengths and shapes.

  3. How to use

    • Before service, place the profile gauge on a standard reference object or plate, preset the shape in advance and then place it in the damaged area. Measure the deformation through comparison in terms of shape and clearance.

    • During the repair, compare it with the repaired surface many times to know the repair of the damaged area, so as to measure the repair of the repaired plate and achieve the best effect.

    Caution
    After the un-deformed plate is shaped, never press hard when comparing it with the deformed area. Otherwise the judgment result would be affected due to change of the profile gauge.

Crowbar

  1. Function

    • It is used in positions that are not easily accessible by hands or where the space is relatively narrow.

  2. Key points

    • The crowbar has different forms and can be used according to steel plates of different shapes.

    • It is usually made of carbon steel, which has good toughness and resistance to bending and deformation.

    • The crowbar consists of shank, force application end and contact end.

  3. How to use

    • When choosing a crowbar for repair, it is necessary to consider that the shape of its front end is close to the size and shape of the plate dent, and the angle of the crowbar is slightly smaller than that of the dent.

    • Select a suitable support point to avoid secondary damage at the support position.

    • Apply the force from small to large, so as to avoid secondary tension of the plate due to overexertion.

    • When using a crowbar to pry the dent, hammer around the dent with a sheet metal hammer. Like unaligned hammering, the hammer shall not be in the same straight line as the contact end of the crowbar.

    Caution

    • When using the crowbar, pay attention to parts and components at the supporting point to avoid secondary damage of the vehicle artificially.

    • Never use the contact face with a small angle. Otherwise, small protruding point difficult to maintain will be formed.

Body file

  1. Function

    • The file is a special tool for removing bumps in sheet metal repair. The unevenness of plates during manual shaping can be determined by the filing traces.

  2. Key points

    • The body file is used to finish the bumps left by tools such as hammer, bucking block and leveling blade after operation.

    • It can be used according to the dent and bulge of the repaired area, and also can assist in measurement.

  3. How to use

    • When using the file, try to keep an angle of 30~45° for pushing and pulling. Do not exert too much force.

    Caution
    When using the body file for the plate repaired by the dent puller, do not exert force excessively. Otherwise, the plate will become thinner and the strength be reduced.

Hand grinder

  1. Function

    • The hand grinder is used for large-area grinding.

  2. Key points

    • Hand grinders include pneumatic and electric types.

    • The grinder speed can be adjusted.

    Caution
    Hold the handle tightly, adjust the revolving speed properly and use the protective equipment.

Scriber

  1. Function

    • It, as a marking tool, can scribe the position and shape of the edge line.

Ruler

  1. Function

    • It is mainly used for restoring the edge line, and also for comparison with undeformed plates.

    Caution
    Never scratch the paint coat when comparing with the undamaged reference plate.

Manual shaping methods

Reminder
Manual shaping includes solid knocking and virtual knocking.

Aligned hammering

  1. Key points

    • The hammer surface is parallel to the plate.

    • The hammering point is the same as the bucking point of the bucking block.

    • The force is applied gradually from small to large.

    • Positions to note for aligned hammering: hammering position, steel plate position and bucking block position.

    Caution
    Excessive solid knocking will result in expansion deformation of the plate, and more severe solid knocking will cause uneven stress distribution on the plate, thus making the plate unusable.

Unaligned hammering

  1. Key points

    • The hammer surface is parallel to the plate.

    • The hammering point is not the same as the bucking point of the bucking block.

    • The force is applied gradually from small to large.

    • Positions to note for unaligned hammering: hammering position, steel plate position and bucking block position.

    Caution
    Exert force increasingly for virtual knocking, i.e. knock tentative before increasing the force gradually.

Introduction to shaping equipment

Meson machine

  1. Function

    • Meson machine is used to repair the body panel. With low voltage and instantaneous large current, it enables the washer to adhere to the metal outer panel of the body, so as to carry out drawing and shaping.

  2. Key points

    • The meson machine is easy to operate, with no need to remove or install too many accessories, and features improved efficiency, small heat affected area and good quality.

    Caution

    • Do a check for electricity leakage before using.

    • Remove the negative pole of the vehicle before using the dent puller.

    • Test welding is required. Otherwise, the plate may be burnt through.

Meson machine accessories

  1. Function

    • The accessories of the meson machine are used for drawing and shaping.

  2. Key points

    • Drawing hammer

    • Suction cup

    • Corrugated wires

    • Washer (round)

    • Washer (triangle)

    • Drawing hook

    • Carbon rod, copper rod

Steps for use of shaping equipment

Reminder
Steps of using shaping machine: power connection — adjustment of equipment parameters — test plate commissioning — multi point pulling — single point pulling.

  1. Power-on

    • Select the appropriate power supply according to the instructions.

    • Turn on the working switch.

    Caution
    Check the wiring of dent puller before using, in order to avoid the electric shock caused by electric leakage.

  2. Adjustment of the equipment parameters

    • After the meson machine is in normal operation, adjust it according to the instructions.

    • First select the operation function mode, and then adjust the current.

    Caution
    Select a correct working mode of the dent puller.

  3. Welding commissioning

    • Carry out trial welding on plates with the same material thickness.

    • Adjust the meson machine again according to the trial welding results, and then weld the repaired plate after the current is appropriate.

  4. Multi-point drawing

    • Use washers or corrugated wires in linear arrangement for welding. Insert a steel rod in the middle of the washers and use a single-hook pulling hammer to draw. Use a multi-hook pulling hammer to draw the corrugated wires.

    • The pulling force direction shall be perpendicular to the plate surface during drawing. Use a sheet metal hammer to knock the bulge around the dent until the dent is leveled.

    • After drawing, remove the welded washers or other mesons welded on the plate in turn, and then grind the repaired position.

    Caution
    Small dents will be left on the plate surface after pulling. Fill them with primer or putty after grinding.

  5. Single-point drawing

    • Select the appropriate meson according to the damage type, and perform welding and drawing at a single point.

    • Adjust the welding current from small to large, so as to avoid burning through the plate due to excessive current.

    • During drawing, keep the welding gun perpendicular to the plate surface, and use a sheet metal hammer to knock the surrounding area.

    • Grind the weld marks after drawing.

    Caution

    • Perform test welding before repair to avoid excessive current burning through the plate.

    • Observe the operation instructions of the equipment. Over load is forbidden.

    • Faults shall be repaired by professional technicians. Never repair by yourself.

    • Service the dent puller and its accessories regularly. Always cut off the power before servicing.
