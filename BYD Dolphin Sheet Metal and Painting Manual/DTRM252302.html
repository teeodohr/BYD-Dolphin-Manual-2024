Overview

  1. Sheet metal painting is one of the most basic services of the after-sales service department of a BYD authorized dealer. It reflects the technical level of in-store after-sales maintenance and the specialization of after-sales service process, and is also a key business to improve customer satisfaction.

  2. Sheet metal painting personnel need to know that various services of sheet metal painting are closely related and indispensable. The sheet metal painting consists of two parts: "sheet metal business process" and "painting business process". These two processes should be interlocked. Otherwise, the overall efficiency and maintenance quality of sheet metal painting will be directly affected. Workshop management personnel are required to timely monitor the collaboration between the two processes and give full play to the team efficiency among shift members, thus finally improving the operating benefits of sheet metal painting business.

Evaluation of damage

  1. Key points

    • Evaluate the damaged position of the vehicle to confirm the damage and the necessity for replacing the damaged parts.

  2. Operation steps

    • Perform a walk-around inspection at reception.

    • Assist in insurance reporting and loss assessment.

    • Confirm the maintenance position and replacement of spare parts.

    • Estimate cost and delivery time.
