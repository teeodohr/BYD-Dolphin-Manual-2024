Substrate treatment process

Types and functions of substrate treatment materials

Types of poly-putty

There are mainly three types of poly-putty according to the resin classification, and the commonly used types are polyester poly-putty and Nitrocellulose poly-putty. The types of poly-putty are as follows:

  • Polyester poly-putty: It is prepared by unsaturated polyester resin, filler, a small amount of pigment and styrene. It uses organic peroxide as curing agent and should be mixed with the curing agent before use.

  • Epoxy poly-putty: It is composed of epoxy resin and filler, etc. It uses amine as curing agent, which is mostly used to repair resin (plastic) materials and is not commonly used.

  • Nitrocellulose poly-putty: It is made of nitrocellulose, alkyd resin, pigments, body pigments, and solvents. After drying, it can be polished easily. It is commonly used for filling small pinholes and sanding marks after applying intermediate primer.

  1. Components of poly-putty

    • Main components: pigment, resin and solvent.

    Reminder

    • It is used to fill the dent when the steel plate is damaged or maintained.

    • Pigment: tinting pigment and extender pigment. Providing fillibility.

    • Resin: mixed with pigments to provide hardness, adhesion and durability.

    • Solvent: dissolving the resin, adjusting the viscosity and facilitating the mixing of components

  2. Alloy poly-putty

    • Also known as sheet metal poly-putty, it is a two-component type that needs to be mixed with a curing agent before use. It is suitable for filling depressions on materials such as regular steel plates, galvanized steel plates, and aluminum.

    Reminder

    • Thick coating type: fill deep dents (more than 3mm).

    • Medium coating type: fill the shallow dents (less than 3mm).

    • Thin coating type: fill pinholes, small dents and bulges.

  3. Plastic poly-putty.

    • Plastic poly-putty is a two-component product, which needs to be mixed with curing agent and is suitable for filling depressions on the surface of plastic parts.

    Reminder
    The plastic putty has the advantages of flexibility and good adhesion. Applying plastic putties onto plastic parts will avoid putty cracking.

  4. Nitrocellulose poly-putty.

    • Nitrocellulose poly-putty is a one-component type, which can be used without mixing with curing agent. It is suitable for filling pinholes and sandpaper marks left after application of intermediate primer.

    Reminder
    As nitro putty is very easy to shrink, do not use it to repair deep scratches or for large-area scraping.

Overview of Primers

Reminder

  • The epoxy primer is mainly applied on bare metal, which can make the paint film obtain good adhesion, fill in minor defects, and play a role in anti-corrosion for bare metal plates. The primer coating is the basis of the whole spraying. Select the primer correctly according to the operation process and the type of substrate. Improper use will affect the quality of the topcoat.

  • The intermediate primer is the coating under the topcoat, which enhances the adhesion between coatings, strengthens the sealing of the primer layer and fills in the fine scratches. The intermediate primer shall have certain adhesion, solvent resistance and fillibility to provide a perfect surface for the topcoat and highlight the decorative property of the topcoat.

  1. Epoxy primer.

    • Two-component epoxy primer is mainly used to prevent rust and provide adhesion for subsequent coatings. It can be directly applied on metal substrate as anti-corrosion primer, such as steel plate, aluminum-magnesium alloy, galvanized sheet and glass fiberglass.

    • The epoxy primer shall be stirred evenly before use. Special curing agent shall be used for the epoxy primer. Attention shall be paid to selecting matching products and mixing them in proportion.

    • The epoxy primer coating is slow to dry, so make sure that a thin layer is applied on the bare metal area during operation.

    Reminder
    The ideal construction conditions are: 20°C, humidity lower than 80%, if the ambient temperature is lower than 15°C, the drying is slow and it is not recommended to use

  2. Polyurethane intermediate primer.

    • Two-component polyurethane filled intermediate primer. Reliable operation performance, good fillibility and easy polishing.

    • Some paint brands offer intermediate primers that can be tinted to match the primer color or adjust the primer's grayness, allowing for better coverage and reducing the consumption of paint required.

    • The primer shall be mixed with curing agent and thoroughly stirred before use. Infrared heating lamps can be used for speeding up the drying process after the primer is applied.

    Reminder

    • Apply the intermediate primer to the appropriate film thickness to fill the sand holes or sandpaper marks on the putty surface. High-performance intermediate primer shall be applied for 2-3 layers, and the film thickness can reach 120-150 μm.

    • Appropriate solvent evaporation time should be reserved between coats when the intermediate primer is applied to avoid pinholes.

    Reminder

    • Apply the intermediate primer to the appropriate film thickness to fill the sand holes or sandpaper marks on the putty surface. High-performance intermediate primer shall be applied for 2-3 layers, and the film thickness can reach 120-150 μm.

    • Appropriate solvent evaporation time should be reserved between coats when the intermediate primer is applied to avoid pinholes.

  3. Nitrocellulose intermediate primer.

    • One-component product, mainly composed of nitrocellulose resin.

    Reminder

    • Nitro intermediate primer dries quickly, is easy to sand, and has good construction operability, but its adhesion, coating thickness, filling and isolation performance are slightly worse than that of polyurethane intermediate primer.

    • Nitrogen primer is suitable for the maintenance of small-area panel damage. In order to ensure the maintenance quality of large-area panel damage, it is recommended to choose a two-component primer for application.

Pre-treatment of plates

Reminder
During the paint repair operation, the pretreatment of the substrate should be carried out first, and the silicon wax, rust, old coating in the damaged area and old old paint film attached to the plate to be sprayed should be removed to improve the adhesion of the subsequent coating,focus. Substrate processing operations include: cleaning the board, confirming the damage range of the board, removing the old coating, grinding the feather edge, applying the primer, and anti-rust treatment.

Assess the extent of damage

  1. Evaluate the extent of damage.

    • Before applying poly-putty, workshop technicians need to make an initial assessment of the damaged panel to assess the type and extent of the damage. The main purpose is to understand the type and severity of the damage.

    • Typically, there are several methods used in workshops to assess the extent of damage: visual inspection, tactile examination, pressing, and measurements with a ruler or measuring tool.

    Reminder
    Common items that need to be used in the process of judging board damage are: ruler, marker pen and cotton gauze gloves.

  2. Cleaning of plate

    • The pollutants in the damaged parts of the vehicle (such as oil stains, silicone wax and asphalt) or other pollutants on the surface of the workpiece due to sheet metal repair can be brought into the sandpaper during grinding, resulting in poor adhesion, fish eyes, and other defects during spraying.

    • Before entering the spraying workshop, the vehicle shall be cleaned to remove dust and dirt on its surface, so as to avoid dust entering the spraying workshop, and facilitate the next step of work.

    • Spray the degreaser onto the surface of the workpiece with a solvent spray can, and then dry it with a clean cleaning cloth. Or clean with two pieces of wiping cloth. First, wet a cleaning cloth with the cleaning agent and wipe the surface of the workpiece. Then immediately dry with another dry cleaning cloth.

    Reminder

    • Reasonable board cleaning can avoid spray defects, such as fisheye defects.

    • It can reduce the problem of dirt on the board clogging the sandpaper and reduce the waste of sandpaper.

    • Degreaser cleaner is a mixture of various organic solvents that can dissolve components such as silicon, wax, and grease on the body surface.

    • Safety protective equipment should be used reasonably during construction operations, and organic solvent-proof masks, solvent-resistant gloves, and goggles should be worn.

  3. Visual evaluation

    • Observe surface distortion based on light reflection and determine damage scope.

    • If the old paint film of the damaged plate has been polished off or there is insufficient light, this method cannot be used.

    Reminder

    • When observing, you need to observe from different angles and a large area, and do not ignore any damaged parts.

    • If the assessment of the damage site is not accurate, it may cause process rework.

    • Clean the board before inspection to avoid missing damaged parts.

  4. Touch evaluation

    • Thoroughly check the damage scope of the plate and the surrounding dents and protrusions. Touch the steel plate with hands from all directions as much as possible.

    Reminder

    • For touch assessment, wear cotton gloves to touch the surface of the board.

    • Touchpad surface, including damaged and undamaged areas

    • When confirming by touch, focus on the palm of your hand.

    • Touch the surface of the pad with your palm straight, but do not apply any pressure to the surface.

    • Touch the undamaged board surface first, and then touch the damaged surface area. This makes it easier to spot tiny depressions or protrusions.

  5. Check by pressing

    • Check the strength change of the plate by pressing to ensure that the tension between the damaged surface and the undamaged surface is the same.

    Reminder

    • Exert sufficient force on the surface till your nail tip of the finger on the surface goes white.

    • The tension of the damaged area is evaluated by comparing the tension of the damaged area with the undamaged area.

  6. Check with a ruler

    • Ensure that there are no areas higher than the undamaged surface.

    Reminder

    • Put a ruler on both undamaged and damaged surfaces. The damage is then judged by the gap between the ruler and the surface and the movement of the ruler.

    • If the entire plate is completely damaged, use the other side of the plate for comparison.

    • The straightedge inspection method is suitable for plates with a large damage range, and the use of the straightedge inspection method helps to improve the flatness of the repaired plate.

  7. Determination of damaged area

    • Mark the damaged areas that have been checked with a marker.

    Reminder

    • It is necessary to check whether there are high points in the deformed area. If there are high points, the sheet metal work needs to be re-worked with suitable sheet metal tools until it is qualified.

    • The damaged part is marked with a circle, and the damaged outer line is generally in a ring shape, so it is necessary to draw a circle when marking the steel plate.

    • If the error of marking this range is too large, it will affect the workload of putty shaping and reduce the efficiency. If the limit of marking is smaller than the actual damage range, it will be difficult to restore the original shape of putty after repairing.

Surface treatment

Reminder
Before applying the anti-rust primer and putty, do relevant preparatory work.The adequacy of the preparation work is directly related to the quality of the putty application. The preliminary work is mainly to conduct the surface treatment on the board, and the treatment content mainly includes : Remove old coatings and rust, create feather edges, clean and degrease, apply anti-rust primer, etc.

  1. Remove old coating

    1. Key points

      • Remove all old coatings and rust within the damaged area.

      • Grind the old coatings within the damaged area with a single-action grinder equipped with sandpaper #60 or #80.

    2. Operation steps

      • Protect plates or assembly parts that have not been disassembled adjacent to the grinding area with masking tape.

      • Remove the coating along the same range as the marked circle, and the damaged area is usually circular. Remove the paint film and grind within this area.

    Caution

    • The correct grinding method is to make the grinder contact the plate first, and then start the grinder. When grinding the steel plate, you should hold the grinder at the correct angle.

    • If the grinding time is too long in the same part, overheating and deformation will occur.

    • When grinding a coating, if the grinder is moving from the steel plate to the coating, it will be difficult to remove wrinkles and the coating will be overground, increasing the width of the feather.

    Reminder

    • The difference in adhesion of the old coating causes the paint film to peel off or bubble on the steel plate. The old coating on the damaged area needs to be completely removed.

    • Damaged areas, dented areas of old coating can be removed by hand sanding or using a belt sander.

  2. Make feather-shaped edges

    1. Key points

      • Grind with a double-action grinder, with a #5/7 grinding head and sandpaper #120-180, to remove the steps between the plate and the old coating, forming a slope.

      • Ensure a smooth transition between the steel plate and the old coating, and grind a surface with a width of approximately 30 mm on the outer side of the feather edge.

    2. Operation points

      • Adjust the angle of the double-action grinder and move the grinder 30 mm around the outside of the circle.

      • Move the grinder while visually checking the feather edge.

      Caution

      • If the feathering edge prepared is not smooth, putty marks will form when the sprayed paint coat is dried.

      • Make the polisher contact with the steel plate before switching it on to avoid deep scratches.

      • The qualified standard of the feather edge is that the width must reach 20--30mm. When checking by hand, it should ensure that the transition between the coating and the steel plate is smooth.

      Reminder
      For deep scratches that do not cause metal deformation and the paint coating is cracked, you can use a double-action sander with P240-320 sandpaper to remove the scratches and sand out the feathered edge, and then apply a primer to fill up, no need to conduct the Putty application.

  3. Clean and degrease

    1. Key points

      • Remove all dirt, grease, etc.

      • Method of degreasing: Clean the plate with two pieces of degreasing cloth in a wet-dry manner. Or operate in conjunction with a solvent spray can.

    2. Operation points

      • After removing all dirt or dust on the surface of the plate with a piece of wiping paper, blow air to the surface of the plate to remove dust.

      • And remove surface oil stains with detergent.

      Caution
      Inadequate cleaning can result in peeling or blistering of the coating.

Application of primer

  1. Apply epoxy primer

    1. Key points

      • Apply a thin layer of epoxy primer to the exposed metal area.

    2. Operation steps

      • Epoxy primer can be applied using a brush or spray gun.

      • Epoxy primer provides rust prevention and improves adhesion in coatings. Since epoxy primer dries slowly, ensure that a thin layer is applied to completely cover the exposed metal area.

      • After epoxy primer is completely dry, apply poly-putty. To improve work efficiency, a short-wave infrared heating lamp can be used to heat the epoxy primer. For specific time requirements, see the product manual.

      • Poly-putty can be directly applied after epoxy primer is dry. If the interval is long, grind with scouring pad before applying.

      Reminder

      • Some alloy putty has good adhesion to a variety of metal substrates, and the putty also has anti-rust properties. Therefore, it is possible to directly scrape putty on the metal surface without applying epoxy primer.

      • Whether it is necessary to apply an epoxy primer depends on the type of putty used or the type of substrate for which the putty is suitable.

Application of poly-putty

Reminder

  • The application of putty needs to be carried out in strict accordance with the corresponding process. Adhering to the standard construction process steps can not only improve work efficiency, but also is of great importance to the later application quality control. Generally, in the painting workshop, the steps of applying putty Mainly include: stirring putty, mixing putty, thin coating putty, filling and scraping putty, trimming putty surface.

  • Items commonly used in putty application work include: goggles, gas masks, solvent-resistant gloves, putty, curing agent, stir bars, putty mixing boards, putty scrapers, and drying equipment.

  1. Stir poly-putty

    1. Key points

      • Before use, thoroughly mix poly-putty and curing agent to ensure that the ingredients are evenly mixed.

    2. Operation points

      • While rotating the poly-putty tank, move the stirring rod up and down to stir poly-putty.

      • When stirring, be careful not to allow poly-putty to adhere to the edge of the tank. Poly-putty that adheres to the edge or poly-putty cover can be cleaned with solvent.

      • Tighten the curing agent cover and knead the entire curing agent tube to mix the curing agent.

      Caution

      • If the putty adhering to the can dries, it can form particles or residues when applied.

      • If the putty and curing agent are not mixed well before mixing, the putty will not cure properly

      Reminder
      When mixing the curing agent, care should be taken to fully knead the curing agent to ensure that the components in the curing agent are uniform.

  2. Mix poly-putty

    1. Key points

      • Take out an appropriate amount of poly-putty and curing agent, and place them separately on clean mixing plates.

      • Take the curing agent with the edge of a scraper, and then draw circles with the scraper to mix curing agent and poly-putty to a mixing rate of 70%~80%.

      • Shovel up about half of poly-putty, scrape it onto the remaining poly-putty, and then press the poly-putty to spread it evenly.

      • Repeat the previous step until the color is uniform.

    2. Operation steps

      • Follow the manufacturer's instructions on the mixing ratio.

      • Squeeze the curing agent slightly away from poly-putty on the mixing plate, avoiding direct contact.

      • Squeeze and scrape poly-putty thin to remove any air, in order to prevent pinholes and improve the adhesion of poly-putty.

      • When starting to mix poly-putty and curing agent, mix the ingredients within 1 minute, as the poly-putty will start to cure and become too hard to apply after 5 minutes.

      Caution

      • After removing the putty and curing agent from the container, cover the lid immediately to prevent the solvent from evaporating.

      • Inadequate mixing of the ingredients will result in poor cure.

      • Air in the putty can cause the surface to blister and form pinholes.

  3. Apply poly-putty thinly

    1. Key points

      • Apply a thin layer of poly-putty to the area where epoxy primer is applied.

      • Fill in small scratches or dents caused by welding.

    2. Operation points

      • Ensure that poly-putty is applied to the grinding area.

      • Hold the scraper with palm facing down, gently support the scraper with index and middle fingers in a V-shape, and shovel a small amount of poly-putty onto the edge of the scraper.

      • When holding the scraper, only make the edge of the scraper touch the surface of the plate, and then press firmly to apply a thin layer of poly-putty.

  4. Apply poly-putty

    1. Key points

      • Scrape each layer of poly-putty thinly and apply it multiple times. The application height shall be higher than the undamaged reference surface.

    2. Operation points

      • First, stand in a position where poly-putty can be easily applied.

      • Hold the scraper with palm facing down and gently support the scraper with index and middle fingers in a V-shape to apply poly-putty. Each time overlap the previous layer of poly-putty by 1/3. Erect the scraper to apply a thin layer, and lower the scraper to apply a thick layer.

      • Press the scraper onto the steel plate, scrape the surrounding poly-putty layer thin, and move the scraper along the shape of the feather edge.

      Caution

      • Apply putty thinly first, build up and finish it, and remove excess putty around after application.

      • If a large amount of putty is applied at one time, air will be trapped in the putty, causing pinholes.

      Reminder
      If the putty cannot be applied at one time, wait for the edge of the applied putty to dry to a touchable level, and then proceed to the next application of the putty.

  5. Trim poly-putty

    1. Key points

      • Trim all steps around the edge of poly-putty and remove all poly-putty spreading beyond the grinding area.

    2. Operation points

      • Ensure that there are no steps between the poly-putty application area and the old coating.

      • Ensure that there is no residual poly-putty on the surface beyond the grinding area.

      • Press the scraper onto the coating side and move it along the shape of the feather edge. The coating of the edge shall be scraped into a thin coating.

      Caution

      • If there are steps around the place where the putty is applied, the front end of the lapping plate will touch the undamaged surface during the putty grinding, resulting in scratches on the coating surface.

      • After the putty is applied, the scraper should be cleaned directly with thinner.

  6. Drying of poly-putty

    1. Key points

      • Force dry poly-putty to ensure that it can be ground.

    2. Operation points

      • Once poly-putty dries to a touchable level, force it dry with a heating lamp. Avoid rapidly heating poly-putty.

      • Gently place sandpaper on the thinner part of the coating, and observe whether poly-putty becomes powder during grinding to determine whether the poly-putty is dry.

      • Or use fingernail to test the dry state of poly-putty by scraping the thinner edge. Light scratches on the surface with white marks indicate that poly-putty is completely dry.

      Caution
      If the putty is heated rapidly during the drying process, the putty may crack or fall off.
      Reminder

      • The heat generated when the putty dries will accelerate the solidification of the putty, and the thin edge parts of the putty area will dry slowly. To judge whether the putty is completely dry, it should be confirmed in the edge area.

      • The use of forced drying of the putty will reduce the curing time of the putty and improve work efficiency.

  7. Grind poly-putty - rough grinding

    1. Key points

      • Grind the rough poly-putty area with sandpaper #80 or #120 to form a relatively smooth surface. Ensure that the poly-putty surface is free from steps, large dents, and protrusions.

    2. Operation points

      • Before grinding, it is necessary to confirm that poly-putty is completely dry.

      • Applying a carbon powder guide layer to the poly-putty application area helps to determine the grinding range and flatness.

      • Stand where the grinding plate can easily reach the entire area of poly-putty to grind. Select a suitable grinding head and paste sandpaper, hold the grinding head from below, gently press it onto the poly-putty surface, and grind it to a distance of 10 mm within the edge of poly-putty. Grind only the poly-putty surface, and do not leave sandpaper marks on undamaged areas.

      • After grinding the higher part, grind the entire surface, taking care to avoid dent areas ground in local poly-putty area.

      • Check whether the dents and bulges are ground evenly through visual check and hand contact.

      Caution

      • Make the polisher contact with the steel plate before switching it on to avoid deep scratches.

      • If ground to the original old coated surface, it will leave a deep sandpaper mark.

      Reminder
      The steel plate that is still hot after drying with a baking light will expand, and the radian of the entire steel plate will be different. Before grinding, confirm that the temperature of the plate is normal.

  8. Grind poly-putty - fine grinding

    1. Key points

      • Apply carbon powder to the poly-putty surface, and then grind the poly-putty surface to 80% surface flatness with a grinding block or grinder equipped with sandpaper #180, so as to make a natural and smooth transition between the old coating and the surface applied with poly-putty.

    2. Standard

      • Ensure that the entire feather edge is faintly visible at the edge of poly-putty.

    3. Operation points

      • Stand in a suitable position to grind the poly-putty surface only, and do not leave sandpaper marks on the undamaged surface.

      • Touch the surface with hands to check for dents and protrusions.

      • If necessary, check the flatness of the surface with a ruler.

      • Check for small dents and protrusions with a grinding indicator.

      Reminder

      • The grinding direction should be multi-angle or multi-directional cross-grinding method to ensure the smooth surface of the putty after grinding.

      • During the grinding process, the smoothness effect of the entire surface should be checked visually and by hand.

      • During the grinding process, both manual and machine methods can be used for grinding.

  9. Grind poly-putty - fine trimming and grinding

    1. Key points

      • Grind the entire poly-putty surface with a manual grinding plate equipped with sandpaper #240, allowing for a natural and smooth transition between the old coating and the surface applied with poly-putty.

      • Grind the poly-putty surface to 90% surface smoothness, ensuring that there is a thin layer of poly-putty left on the feather edge.

    2. Operation points

      • Grind the entire poly-putty application range and 5 cm beyond the grinding area

      • to ensure a smooth surface. Check for coarse sandpaper marks after grinding with a grinding indicator.

      • After grinding, blow compressed air onto the poly-putty surface to check for pinholes.

      • If there are pinholes on poly-putty, apply a thin layer of poly-putty with the same method for thin application and re-grind.

      • Check the surface for dents or protrusions at any time. Check the transition between the poly-putty surface and the undamaged surface by touching it with hands or using a ruler.

  10. Grind poly-putty - final grinding

    1. Key points

      • Grind the surface for applying intermediate paint. Grind with a grinding head/plate equipped with sandpaper #320 to remove all sandpaper marks left during surface trimming.

    2. Operation points

      • Grind the surface exceeding the poly-putty edge by about 10 cm, ensuring that the area to be applied with primer has been ground and the surface is matte. Be careful not to go too far beyond the area that needs to be repainted.

      • Adjust the double-action grinder to the appropriate speed to achieve the best grinding operation.

      Reminder

      • Sandpaper marks left by sanding with grit smaller than 240 may not be fully filled with the primer, causing the sandpaper mark problem to occur.

      • Grinding is the act of sanding a surface with sandpaper or similar material. Increase the paint contact area, thereby improving paint adhesion.

      Caution
      To prevent deep scratches on the steel plate, the grinder should touch the steel plate before starting the grinder. A still glossy surface indicates insufficient grinding, which may cause paint peeling problems.

  11. Repair defects in excessively high areas

    • Defect description: The steel plate is above the undamaged surface and exposed.

    • Solution: Use tools such as a pointed hammer to repair the higher parts, so that they are below the undamaged surface. Remove poly-putty around the repaired part with sandpaper #120 and then trim the part.

  12. Repair defects in lower areas

    • Defect description: a lower area formed due to excessive grinding

    • Solution: After cleaning and degreasing, apply a thin layer of poly-putty to the entire surface. After applying the remaining poly-putty to the lower area, apply poly-putty to the entire surface.

    Caution
    Applying the putty only in the lower part makes it difficult to make the natural transition of the putty and the surrounding parts.

  13. Repair pinhole defects

    • Defect description: There are pinholes on the surface that cannot be filled with intermediate paint or nitrocellulose poly-putty.

    • Solution: After cleaning and degreasing, apply a thin layer of poly-putty to the entire surface, and fill the pinholes with the same method of thin application only. Then dry the surface with a heating lamp.

Application of intermediate paint

  1. Overview

    1. Basic knowledge of intermediate paint

      • Application of intermediate paint refers to the process of applying the intermediate paint coating before the top coat is sprayed and preparing for the top coat application.

    2. Purpose of applying intermediate paint:

      • To protect the metal substrate, prevent the substrate metal from being exposed, and improve its rust resistance.

      • To improve adhesion by grinding the surface of the steel plate to improve paint adhesion.

      • To restore surface shape, repair slight deformation, and repair surface shape.

      • To prevent poly-putty from absorbing the top coat and affecting the brightness of paint surface.

  2. Cleaning of plate

    1. Key points

      • Remove all dust, dirt, grease, etc. from the plate to be applied.

      • Method of degreasing: Clean the plate with two pieces of degreasing cloth in a wet-dry manner. Or operate in conjunction with a solvent spray can.

    2. Operation steps

      • Place the air blowing gun close to the poly-putty surface and blow compressed air to the surface to remove grinding dust from the pinholes.

      • After removing all dirt or dust on the surface of the plate with a piece of wiping paper, blow air to the surface to remove dust.

      Caution

      • Inadequate cleaning can result in abrasive particles or dust remaining

      • Insufficient degreasing will cause the paint to peel or blister.

  3. Masking

    1. Key points

      • Ensure that all masking grinding areas are ground.

    2. Operation points

      • Use reverse covering method to avoid forming steps at the edges of the spraying area.

      • If there is a body line in the spraying area, using reverse masking on the body line can reduce the application area of the intermediate paint.

  4. Mixing of intermediate paint

    1. Key points

      • Mix and stir the primer coating, curing agent, and diluent.

    2. Standard

      • Ensure that all ingredients are thoroughly mixed and stirred evenly.

    3. Operation steps

      • The pigments in the main components of the intermediate paint will sink to the bottom, so it is necessary to thoroughly stir the intermediate paint before use.

      • Choose a diluent suitable for the spraying environment.

      • Mix according to the mixing ratio provided by the paint manufacturer.

      • Repeat to mix the stirred primer, filter it with a paper funnel and pour it into the spray gun.

      Caution
      Improper mixing and insufficient agitation can result in insufficient curing of the base coat.
      Reminder

      • A measuring balance (by weight) can be used to formulate the ingredients.

      • It is also possible to use a paint mixing ruler (by volume) to mix.

  5. Application of intermediate paint

    1. Key points

      • Apply the intermediate paint in several layers, ensuring that the primer is sprayed in the ground area and the poly-putty is completely covered.

    2. Operation points

      • Adjust the spray gun settings for spray amplitude, paint output, and spray pressure, and test whether the spray amplitude is normal.

      • Apply the first layer of intermediate paint to the area ground with sandpaper #320 until a wet coating is formed in the area.

      • The area applied with intermediate paint shall be reduced layer by layer during each subsequent application. Reserve sufficient flash drying time during application.

      • Confirm the effect after the intermediate paint is applied. If pinholes or sandpaper marks are found, they can be filled with local spray.

      Caution

      • Steps on the edges of the spray masking paper can result in a sharp line between the base coat and the old coat.

      • Primer in application outside the abrasive area can cause the coating to peel off.

      • The primer should be sprayed to the largest area for the first time and then become smaller layer by layer. Since the mist of the two-component primer dries faster than the wet coating, it is easy to form small particles. If it continues to cover the application, it will cause adhesion problems

      • Insufficient flash-off time can easily cause sagging or pinholes.

  6. Drying of intermediate paint

    1. Key points

      • Forcibly dry the intermediate paint, and start grinding after ensuring that the intermediate paint is completely dry.

    2. Operation points

      • Before drying the intermediate paint, a curing time shall be reserved. The distance between the infrared heating lamp and the plate shall not be less than 80 cm to avoid excessive temperature of the plate.

      • Follow the instructions in the paint product manual to dry the intermediate paint.

      Caution
      Forced drying without allowing time for curing will result in pinhole defects.
      Reminder
      Curing time: Reserve 10 to 20 minutes before forced drying to allow the solvent in the primer to evaporate naturally.

  7. Apply nitrocellulose poly-putty and check the surface.

    Reminder

    • The defect inspection of the primer layer of the primer should be carried out in a brightly lit environment.

    • If there are defects such as trachoma or sandpaper marks in the primer area, nitro putty needs to be applied to fill.

    • If there are no pinholes or sandpaper marks, you can skip the step of patching the putty

  8. Filling of pinholes and sandpaper marks

    • Apply the nitrocellulose poly-putty into the pinholes and sandpaper marks, ensuring that all pinholes and sandpaper marks are filled.

    Reminder
    Scraping: with a vertical scraper, press firmly and scrape along the surface to apply a thin layer of putty.

  9. Drying

    • Ensure that the nitrocellulose poly-putty is completely dry before starting grinding.

    • Follow the drying conditions of the paint manufacturer. Since the poly-putty for repair is nitroresin, it dries quickly.

  10. Intermediate paint grinding - manual grinding

    1. Key points

      • Grind with a grinding block equipped with sandpaper #320.

    2. Standard

      • Grinding the intermediate paint surface and the nitrocellulose poly-putty application area with a grinding plate can better trim the flatness of the plate after application.

      • In the initial stage of grinding, slightly coarse sandpaper can be used for initial grinding, and then fine sandpaper can be used for grinding to ensure a smooth surface and no steps around the edges and surroundings of the intermediate paint.

      • Ensure that all pinholes and sandpaper marks in primer coating are filled.

      • Ensure that after grinding the intermediate paint area, the poly-putty is not exposed.

    3. Operation steps

      • Protect the area around the repaired part with masking tape.

      • Grind from the intermediate paint to the old coating.

      • To prevent leaving coarse sandpaper marks, the grinding block shall be at an angle of 45° to the grinding direction.

      • Use a vacuum type hand planer to grind the intermediate paint surface. When the sandpaper is blocked, use a brush to remove the grinding dust.

      • Do not expand the grinding area when spraying locally.

      Reminder
      If using a water mill primer, water sandpaper 800# should be used. The related operation steps and key points are similar.

  11. Intermediate paint grinding - grinding with double-action grinder

    • Grind with a #3 grinding head equipped with #400-500 sandpaper.

    • Ensure a smooth surface and no steps around the edges and surroundings of the intermediate paint.

    • Ensure that all pinholes and sandpaper marks are filled.

    • Ensure poly-putty is not exposed.

    1. Operation points

      • Before grinding, adjust and test the speed of the grinder. The grinder with a higher speed will be difficult to operate and easy to wear the intermediate paint coating.

      • Grind from the intermediate paint to the old coating.

      • Manually grind areas where the double-action grinder cannot be used.

      • Do not expand the grinding area during local repair spraying.

      Caution

      • When paint is applied, exposed putty can absorb paint and cause defect problems.

      • If the inspection surface is smooth, the primer of the primer is ground, and if it is not smooth, it needs to be ground by hand.

      Reminder

      • When sanding the exposed putty, you need to apply the primer again.

      • A sanding indicator should be used to check that the surface is flat during sanding.

Spraying method before top coat

  1. Repainting of whole plate

    • Grind the whole steel plate to ensure a matte finish.

    • Protect the area around the repaired part with masking tape.

    • Use sandpaper of the same model as that used to grind the intermediate paint.

    • Manually grind areas where the double-action grinder cannot be used.

    • Gently grind the surface to remove all the texture of the old coating.

    Caution

    • Inadequate cleaning can result in peeling or blistering of the coating.

    • Use sand paper for grinding the whole bumper surface, and scouring pads for edges and corners.

  2. Transitional spraying of primer junction

    • Grind the whole steel plate to ensure a matte finish.

    Reminder

    • Protect the area around the maintained part with masking tape.

    • Ensure adequate sanding of the medium coat application area.

    • Sand the applied area with sandpaper 2000# or grey scouring pad abrasive paste.

    • Ensure the undamaged areas are ground to a matte finish.

    Caution

    • Protect the area around the maintained part with masking tape.

    • Ensure adequate sanding of the medium coat application area.

    • Sand the applied area with sandpaper 2000# or grey scouring pad abrasive paste.

    • Ensure the undamaged areas are ground to a matte finish.

  3. Local repair spraying

    • Ensure the undamaged areas are ground to a matte finish.
