Replacement of front left hubcap assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the rear section assembly of front gutter channel. Refer toReplacement of rear section assembly of front gutter channel 

  2. Use a sander to grind the sealant of front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Cut with disc sander, etc.

  3. Remove 24 welding spots of the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  4. Remove 7 welding spots of the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  5. Remove 3 welding spots of the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  6. Remove 14 welding spots of the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  7. Remove 8 welding spots and take out the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

Installation

  1. Clean the joint of front left hubcap assembly and apply adhesive.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  2. Fix the front left hubcap assembly to the installation position.

  3. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of front left hubcap assembly and front compartment parts. Refer to 

  4. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of front left hubcap assembly and underbody parts. Refer toUnderbody 

  5. Weld 8 welding spots on the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  6. Weld 14 welding spots on the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  7. Weld 3 welding spots on the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  8. Weld 7 welding spots on the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  9. Weld 24 welding spots on the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  10. Apply sealant to the front left hubcap assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  11. After applying the external coating, apply rust inhibitor to the inner plate part of the welding spots of the closed section structure.

  12. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
