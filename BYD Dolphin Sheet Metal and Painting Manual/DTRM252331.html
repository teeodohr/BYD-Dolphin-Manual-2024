Outer panel repair standards

No bulged points on the surface of repaired area

The surface is flat and slightly lower than the original surface by 0.5-1 mm

Ensure the strength of the repair areas, and there shall be no bounce after a force of 5 kg is applied with the thumb

The maintenance scope is correct, and there is no artificial expansion of the damaged areas

The overall appearance and ridgelines are neat, and the joint surface between the paint film and the exposed steel plate is linear after grinding

The steel plate surface shall be free of perforation and excessive hammer marks

Reapply the damaged sealant

Application of anti-corrosive wax on the inner side of repaired areas
