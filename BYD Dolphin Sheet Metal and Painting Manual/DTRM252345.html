Removal and installation of sheet metal parts

Basic process of removal and installation

Reminder
In the removal, installation and maintenance work, a well-organized work procedure can largely shorten the work time, reduce the work load and improve the work efficiency. On the contrary, unfamiliarity of the removal and installation procedures and precautions will result in low work efficiency and even the danger of damaging tools, parts and components on the vehicle body.

Preparations

  1. Data collection

    1. Collect the information about removal and installation in the maintenance manual.

    2. Look up the dimension information about the repaired part.

    3. Print the relevant data of repaired parts for reference.

  2. Protection of body components

    1. Protect relevant parts of the body.

    2. Use special protective equipment to avoid damage to components.

  3. Tool preparation

    1. Prepare data query tools (such as paper or electronic service manuals), etc.

    2. Take out the tools corresponding to the components to be removed and installed from the universal toolkit.

    3. Take out other tools corresponding to the components to be removed and installed.

Removal and storage

  1. Removal of accessories

    1. Disconnect the lines or water pipe in advance.

    2. Remove clips and other connections with special tools to avoid unnecessary damage.

    3. Remove the trim panel or strip without damaging the paint surface.

    4. Refer to the vehicle service manual at any time for removal of relevant accessories.

  2. Removal of main body

    1. Carefully handle and place parts that do not need painting or repair to prevent scratches.

    2. For parts to be replaced, remove all small accessories such as clips and marks.

    Reminder
    See the maintenance manual and the removal and installation process.

  3. Storage of parts

    1. Do not store interior trims together with sharp objects. Store them carefully and mark "Do Not Move".

    2. Install the screws on parts properly if they do not affect installation, so as to avoid missing.

    3. Seal spare parts (such as interior trims, seats, etc.) not resistant to dirt, and store them with shielding items.

    Caution
    Never place parts removed from the accident vehicle on the carpet and seats of the customer's vehicle, because the interior trims would be damaged and contaminated by sharp and dirty parts.

Installation detection

Reminder

  • Preparations before the installation.

  • The installation process is the opposite of the removal process.

  • Inspect the removed and installed locations as required after the installation.

  1. Installation of parts

    1. The preparations are the same as those for the removal.

    2. It is in reverse order of the removal. That is, the main body is installed first and then accessories.

    3. Follow the sequence from inside to outside as far as possible.

    4. Refer to the service manual for dimension data and torque parameters during operation.

  2. Adjustment detection

    1. Check the clearance and flushness between parts through visual inspection and hand touch. Use tools for some items.

    2. Tools for inspection:

      • Vernier caliper

      • Clearance gauge

      • Torque measuring wrench

Precautions for removal and installation

Reminder
Precautions of removal and installation: safety matters, preparations, marking, storage, prohibitions and installation.

  1. Safety

    1. Before removal and installation, check for potential safety hazards. For example, when removing or installing accessories to lift the vehicle, observe the state of the lifter or jack at any time.

    2. For removal and installation of high-voltage parts of the vehicle, strictly observe the service precautions for new energy vehicles.

    3. Use tools properly and pay attention to working methods to avoid unnecessary injuries.

  2. Preparations

    1. Before removal of parts, for removal of important line connectors of the engine and airbag sensor, for example, remove the negative GND circuit of 12 V battery in advance.

    2. Store auxiliary materials such as liquid to be recycled properly in clean containers.

    3. Before service of new energy vehicles, please refer to the safety precautions for service to avoid electric shock.

  3. Marking

    1. Observe the specific installation structure before removal and installation of components.

    2. Remove generally from outside to inside, and from accessories to the main body.

    3. During removal, in order that the components are not omitted during installation, make some marks or signs appropriately without affecting other performances of the components. Wipe off the marks after installation.

    4. For long-term storage of the removed parts, pay attention to prevention against dust and moisture, attach corresponding labels and indicate the vehicle information.

  4. Storage

    1. Store the removed components, bolts and nuts in categories.

    2. Store components susceptible to scratch and abrasions separately.

    3. Keep written records for removal and installation with heavy workload to avoid misplacement and loss.

  5. Prohibitions

    1. Use the bolt loosening agent when removing seriously rusted bolts.

    2. Use special removal tools in case of fracture.

    3. Do not remove parts that are unable to be removed by force, but remove them in other ways.

  6. Installation

    1. When installing components, strictly implement the OEM quality standards, so that the dimensions, torque and other parameters after installation meet the manufacturer's guidance requirements.
