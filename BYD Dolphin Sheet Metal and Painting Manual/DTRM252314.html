Process flow standard

A vehicle body that has undergone sheet metal work, internal rust prevention treatment, and spraying

Check the vehicle body condition and the planned operation requirements

Degreasing and dewaxing

(AC960)

Use AC960 together with a cleaning cloth to clean it by combining wetting and drying (AC960 can only be used for cleaning before grinding)

Grind and trim the beveled edges

Use Audurra sandpaper #P80/P120-180 to polish the areas of the vehicle body that have undergone sheet metal operation and need to be filled with poly-putty (if necessary, remove excessively thick or defective paint from the car body)

Dust removal and cleaning

(AC960)

Use a pressure gun and degreaser AC960 to remove dust and stains on the vehicle body

Protective film

If necessary, please cover and protect the areas on the vehicle body that do not need to be repaired

Putty filling

(AC510)

Mix 2K sheet metal putty (with hardener AC520 added at a ratio of 100:1-3%) and apply it to the dented areas on the car body. Subsequently, dry it by baking or allow it to air dry for 10-15 minutes at a temperature of 25℃.

Grind the poly-putty

Use Audurra sandpaper #P80/P120-P180-P240 to grind the dried putty, and use hand feeling or guide coat to check the flatness of poly-putty, pinholes, and sandpaper marks

Thin puttying

(AC510)

If necessary, apply a thin layer of AC510 putty to fill the pinholes, sandpaper marks, and small dents (note: only on primer/putty)

Grinding

Use Audurra sandpaper #P280/P320 to thoroughly polish the old paint that needs to be sprayed with intermediate paint on the vehicle body

Dust removal and cleaning

(AC960)

Use a pressure gun and degreaser AC960 to remove dust and stains on the vehicle body

Protective film

If necessary, paste the masking paper with reverse application techniques.

Spray intermediate paint

(AC710)

Mix 2K high-solid quick-drying intermediate paint

Grinding process: Add curing agent AC810/AC811 and diluent AC910/AC911) at a ratio of 4:1:5-10%. Spray 1-2 layers with an interval of 10-15 minutes between each layer, and then dry them at 60 °C for 30 minutes or at 20 °C for 1-2 hours

Spray the guide coat

Spray a thin layer of guide coat before grinding to check the grinding effect during grinding.

Grind the intermediate paint

Use Audurra sandpaper #P400-500 to polish the dried intermediate paint. If necessary, use a gray scouring pad or sponge sandpaper P600-800 for small area grinding

Inspection, dust removal, and cleaning

(AC960)

Check the polishing effect. If necessary, perform micro-filling and grinding. Use a pressure gun and degreaser AC960 to remove dust and stains on the vehicle body

Protective film

Cover the vehicle body with protective film.

Dust removal and cleaning

Blow out the dust on the vehicle body with a pressure gun, and remove stains on the vehicle body with silicone removal detergent AC960, then blow out the dust in the gap of the vehicle body with a pressure gun, and use a sticky cloth to remove the fine dust on the vehicle body

Double-process colored paint

(Anvanc double-process primer)

Mix Anvanc double-process primer with a mixing ratio of 1:0.6-0.8 (AC920/AC921), and spray 2-3 layers. Note: After the previous colored paint layer flashes dry to matte, the next colored paint can be sprayed

Spray the varnish series after drying with a blower gun

Varnish spraying

(AC610/AC660/AC680)

Mix and spray 2 layers of AC610 standard varnish/AC660 high-efficiency varnish (add curing agent AC810 (fast) /AC811 (standard) at a ratio of 2:1, with an interval of 5 minutes between each layer

Parching

After standing for 5 minutes, dry at 20 °C for 15-30 minutes or at 60 °C for 20-30 minutes according to the product requirements

Waxing and polishing

If necessary, use polishing sandpaper #P1200-1500 to grind small sagging or dust spots, and then use Audurra polishing wax A8 to remove sandpaper marks and restore luster.

Operation flow chart of Axalta water-based paint Syrox series

Items

Process flow standard

A vehicle body that has undergone sheet metal work, internal rust prevention treatment, and spraying

Check the vehicle body condition and the planned operation requirements

Degreasing and dewaxing

Use detergent together with a cleaning cloth to clean in a one wet and one dry manner alternately.

Grind and trim the beveled edges

Use Audurra sandpaper #P80/P120-180 to polish the areas of the vehicle body that have undergone sheet metal operation and need to be filled with poly-putty (if necessary, remove excessively thick or defective paint from the car body)

Dust removal and cleaning

Use a pressure gun and a degreaser to remove fine dust and stains on the vehicle body.

Protective film

If necessary, please cover and protect the areas on the vehicle body that do not need to be repaired

Putty filling

(S1100)

Mix 2K sheet metal putty (add hardener H-100 at a ratio of 100:2-3%) to fill the dents on the vehicle body, and then dry it naturally at 20 °C for 10-15 minutes or bake it with a short-wave infrared heating lamp for 2-3 minutes (half power).

Grind the poly-putty

Use Audurra sandpaper #P80/P120-P180-P240 to grind the dried putty, and use hand feeling or guide coat to check the flatness of poly-putty, pinholes, and sandpaper marks

Thin puttying

(S1100)

If necessary, apply a thin layer of S1100 putty to fill the pinholes, sandpaper marks, and small dents (note: only on primer/putty)

Grinding

Use sandpaper #P280/P320 to thoroughly grind the old paint that needs intermediate paint on the vehicle body.

Dust removal and cleaning

Use a pressure gun and degreaser AC960 to remove dust and stains on the vehicle body

Protective film

If necessary, paste the masking paper with reverse application techniques.

Spray intermediate paint

(S3020/S3060)

Mix S3020 environment-friendly light gray intermediate paint or S3060 environment-friendly dark gray intermediate paint

Grinding process: Add hardener S6000 (slow drying) /S6010 (standard) /S6020 (quick drying) and S8060 (ultra-slow drying) /S8080 (standard) /S8090 (quick drying) thinner at a ratio of 4:1:0-10%, spray 1-3 layers with an interval of 5-10 minutes between each layer. Then dry for 12-16 hours (20 °C) and 25-30 minutes (60°C-65°C).

Spray the guide coat

Spray a thin layer of guide coat before grinding to check the grinding effect during grinding.

Grind the intermediate paint

Use Audurra sandpaper #P400-500 to polish the dried intermediate paint. If necessary, use a gray scouring pad or sponge sandpaper P600-800 for small area grinding

Inspection, dust removal, and cleaning

Check the polishing effect. If necessary, perform micro-filling and grinding. Use a pressure gun and degreaser AC960 to remove dust and stains on the vehicle body

Protective film

Cover the vehicle body with protective film.

Dust removal and cleaning

Blow out the dust particles on the vehicle body with a pressure gun; remove stains on the vehicle body with silicone removal detergent; blow out the dust in the gap of the vehicle body with a pressure gun; and use a sticky cloth to remove the fine dust on the vehicle body.

Double-process colored paint

(S100-S940 water-based paint)

Add 20% S940 water-based viscosity regulator, and apply 2.5 layers of wet to dry spraying.

Note: After the previous colored paint layer flashes dry to matte, spray the next colored paint or proceed with the baking process.

Varnish spraying

(S5000/S5800)

Mix and spray 2 layers of S5000 environment-friendly universal varnish/S5800 bright varnish (add hardener S6020 (quick) /S6010 (standard) at a ratio of 2:1) at an interval of 5-10 minutes between each layer.

Parching

After standing for 5 minutes, dry at 25 °C for 30 minutes or 60 °C for 30 minutes according to the product requirements.

Waxing and polishing

If necessary, use polishing sandpaper #P1200-1500 to grind small sagging or dust spots, and then use polishing wax A8 to remove sandpaper marks and restore luster.
