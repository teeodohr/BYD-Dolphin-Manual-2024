Poly-putty shaping

  1. Key points

    • Fill the damaged plate surface with poly-putty to restore its shape and flatness.

  2. Operation steps

    • Make feather-shaped edges

    • Apply epoxy primer

    • Apply poly-putty

    • Grind the poly-putty.

  3. Remarks

    • The efficiency can be improved by forcibly drying the poly-putty with an infrared heating lamp.

    • Dry grinding shall be used throughout the poly-putty grinding to avoid quality problems.
