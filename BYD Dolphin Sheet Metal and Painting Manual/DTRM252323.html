Body paint type

Paints mainly used for new vehicle manufacturing

  1. Thermal polymerization paint

    • It is mainly used for paint types on the original production line of automobiles. After the body is coated, it is heated to about 140 °C to cure the paint.

    • During the heating process, a chemical reaction will occur to combine resin molecules to form large particles, thus forming a cured coating on the vehicle body. Even if it encounters solvents, this coating will not be easily dissolved.

Paints mainly used for automobile repair and repainting

Two-component polymeric paint

  1. Key points

    • For two-component products, paint must be mixed with a curing agent.

    • The commonly used paint type is acrylic polyurethane paint.

    • The curing agent and resin components will react chemically to form a coating that does not dissolve easily even when exposed to solvents.

    Reminder

    • To mix the paints, read the paint product manual, select the matching curing agent or diluent. And mix them according to the standard proportion.

    • During construction, select painting materials reasonably according to the requirements of the operation process. Improper selection of paint products will lead to painting defects.

Solvent volatile paint

  1. Key points

    • One-component paint, directly mixed with a diluent before use.

    • After spraying, the solvent will volatilize and the resin will form a coating.

    • The plate shall be dried quickly after paint application.

    • Nitrocellulose paint coatings are easily dissolved by solvents, so care should be taken to prevent shrinkage when repainting nitrocellulose paint plates.

Water-based paint

  1. Key points

    • It is composed of water-based polyurethane resin, pigment, and additives, with water as the solvent.

    • Improve the working environment and operation conditions and eliminate fire hazards during the painting operation. This has a significant effect on reducing pollution and saving resources.

    • Painting tools can be cleaned with "water" to reduce the consumption of cleaning solvents and effectively reduce harm to the working personnel.

    Reminder

    • Mix the water-based paint with reference to the proportion in the product manual. Apply varnish coating when the water-based primer is dried.

Curing types of paint

Paint curing types

  1. Key points

    • According to the film formation mode of resin, paints can be divided into solvent volatilization type, oxidative polymerization type, baking polymerization type, and two-component polymerization type. According to the drying type, paints can also be divided into evaporation drying type and reaction drying type.

Solvent evaporation drying

  1. Key points

    • After the solvent in the wet coating film evaporates, a film is formed without resin molecular binding (reticular). Therefore, as long as it is wiped with a diluent, the coating film will dissolve (nitrocellulose paint and air-dried primer belong to this type).

Thermal polymerization drying

  1. Key points

    • When the coating is heated to a certain temperature (generally above 120 °C), the resin polymerizes and forms a dense network structure. After being completely dried, the coating film will have an excellent performance.

    • After drying, the coating is no longer soluble in organic solvents. Nowadays, almost all new vehicle coatings are of this type.

Two-component polymerization reaction drying

  1. Key points

    • By mixing paint and curing agent, the resin reacts (polymerizes) to form a network structure. This reaction can also take place at normal temperatures and will accelerate when the temperature rises. For the sake of operation efficiency, forced drying operation is usually carried out at 60 °C.

    • Its network structure is as dense as that of thermal polymerization drying, and the paint film performance is excellent. The commonly used polyurethane coatings are of this type.

    • During the operation, it is necessary to mix the curing agent with reference to the proportion instructions carried in the product manual. The two-component paint products shall be used with the matching curing agents; otherwise, poor adhesion, low coating hardness, and poor brightness may occur due to differences in chemical composition ratios.
