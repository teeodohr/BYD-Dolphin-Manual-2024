The repair process with hammer and bucking block

  1. Key points

    • Depending on the damage, aligned or unaligned knocking techniques shall be used.

    • Most of the time, the unaligned knocking technique is used.

    • First, use a wooden hammer or rubber hammer for initial repair.

    • Repair sequence: ridge line-curved surface-plane.

    • When repairing a dent, press hard with the bucking block; when repairing a protrusion, use the bucking block in a way that it can just support the steel plate.

    • Determine the position of the bucking block by the knocking sound from the hammer.

    Caution

    • Excessive solid knocking is easy to cause the steel plate to expand, which requires quenching.

    • Always check for depressions and protrusions on the surface of the plate.

    • Push out the depression on the inside of the plate with the top iron with your hand, and knock down the high point with a hand hammer.

    • The top iron is always placed at the lowest point of the depression inside the plate.

The repair process with a meson machine

  1. Key points

    • The width of the polished paint film is about 10 mm above and below the deepest concave part of the panel.

    • The clearance of the welding washer is about 10 mm, and the welding angle is 90° with the welding surface.

    • The pulling amount is 2–3 mm higher than the original plane of the panel; the pulling angle is 90° from the damaged surface; the plastic deformation part should be knocked while the pulling operation is going on.

    • When removing the washer, it is necessary to use a rotating technique to twist off the washer.

    Caution

    • When using triangular mesons or electrode heads for single-point drawing of the board, pay attention to multi-point light drawing to prevent high points from being generated.

    • In order to prevent holes in the plate during drawing, the current of the welding machine must be adjusted correctly before welding the gasket.

The shrinking repair process

  1. Key points

    • There are two shrinking methods: the copper electrode and the carbon electrode.

    • Shrinking shall be carried out first at the position with low tension or high point.

    • The copper electrode shrinking technique is used for areas with high tension; the carbon electrode shrinking technique is used for areas with low tension or large maintenance area.

    • The continuous carbon electrode shrinking is divided into spiral form and line drawing form, which can be selected according to the conditions of the damaged surface.

    Caution

    • Before quenching, use the material equivalent to the thickness of the damaged steel plate to debug the quenching equipment.

    • The cooling method is to use the air gun to cool down for 5--6 seconds.

    • After repairing, spray anti-corrosion wax on the back of the board.

Rapid repair process of plastic components

Reminder

  • If the bumper and the plastic parts of the body are punctured or cracked due to impact or scraping, it is necessary to maintain the damaged bumper and other plastic parts quickly because of the untimely supply of spare parts or from the economic point of view, thus reducing the time spent on vehicle maintenance.

  • There are two process methods for the repair of body plastic parts: plastic gluing process and plastic welding process.

Plastic adhesive process

  1. Grind and clean damaged areas.

    • Use a special ball-type pneumatic grinder to polish the inside and outside of the damaged area of spare parts to remove paint and impurities.

    • Requirements for the grinding range: 10 mm on the left and right sides of the crack, 10 mm at the end of the crack, with a depth of 1 mm.

    • Use a special plastic impurity removal spray.

    • Spray and wipe inside and outside the damaged area with a wiping cloth.

    • Cleaning requirements: The detergent shall be used together with the wiping cloth to clean twice; use an air gun to blow dry the damaged areas inside and outside to keep them dry.

  2. Bonding operation.

    • Select appropriate special soft or hard two-component glue according to the different materials of plastic parts to be repaired.

    • Spray the area to be glued with a special plastic primer, so as to improve the adhesion of adhesive.

    • Apply two-component filler glue to the damaged area from the inner side and use a special outer cloth to support the outer side of the damaged area.

    • Cut a special inner mesh adhesive film of the appropriate size and stick it to the inner side of the glue.

    • Dry naturally for 30 minutes or dry with an infrared heating lamp for 15 minutes, and then trim and polish the exterior of plastic parts.

Plastic welding process

  1. Grind and clean damaged areas.

    • Use a special ball-type pneumatic grinder to polish the inside and outside of the damaged area of spare parts to remove paint and impurities.

    • Requirements for the grinding range: 10 mm on the left and right sides of the crack, 10 mm at the end of the crack, with a depth of 1 mm.

    • Use a special plastic impurity removal spray.

    • Spray and wipe inside and outside the damaged area with a wiping cloth.

    • Cleaning requirements: The detergent shall be used together with the wiping cloth to clean twice; use an air gun to blow dry the damaged areas inside and outside to keep them dry.

  2. Welding operation.

    • Use a special plastic welding gun.

    • Select the appropriate plastic welding wire.

    • Adjust the temperature control regulator of the welding gun to a position matching the plastic material.

    • Weld the inside of the damaged area first, and then use reinforcing wire mesh to reinforce the welding to a depth of 1-2 mm.

    • Then weld the cracks on the surface that need to be welded.

    • After the welding is cooled, place a ruler at the outer weld.

    • The weld shall not be higher than the base metal surface by 1 mm.

    • Use a pneumatic grinder for exterior trimming.

Repair process with hammer and bucking block

  1. Key points

    • The hammering method for aluminum shaping is the same as that for steel plate repair.

    • The repair condition is that there is no bending deformation at the corner of aluminum alloy plate.

    • If the damage affects the deformation of inner panel, repair it after heating with flame coating.

    • Use a thermometer or temperature measuring paper to control the heating temperature at 150 °C-200 °C.

    Caution

    • Anti-corrosion: Equipped with a set of hand-shaping tools specially used for aluminum plate maintenances, and tools for maintaining steel preparations can be used after removing iron filings.

    • Prevent Oxidation: Treat base metal immediately after sanding and degreasing.

    • Prevent work hardening and cracking: Use heated tools to heat the repaired area to improve repairability.

The repair process with a meson machine

  1. Key points

    • Remove the paint film in the damaged area with a single-action grinder and sandpaper #80.

    • After adjusting the relevant parameters of the aluminum welding machine, weld the aluminum drawing meson on the damaged surface when it is appropriate.

    • Use a puller or drawing bracket to draw the damaged area, and use a special hammer for aluminum maintenance to knock on the plastic deformation area.

    • If the repaired surface shows panel ductility, use the carbon electrode shrinking method to shrink the ductile area.

    Caution

    • If the aluminum alloy plate has the following conditions, it cannot be maintained:

    • Surface cracks and openings.

    • The depressions on the ridges are wrinkled.

    • Deformation of inner panels with buckling and warping.

Non-destructive repair of the paint surface

Reminder

  • If an impact or external force causes pits and scratches on the sheet metal parts on the exterior of the body, the traditional sheet metal repair is completed through the steps of disassembly, drawing by a meson, grinding and painting, and the repair will damage the antirust function of the original paint and panels.

  • The non-destructive repair of the body paint surface is to complete the non-destructive repair of the body paint surface through advanced tools and technologies such as adhesive drawing and inner side prying of steel plates.

  • There are two methods for non-destructive repair of paint surface: adhesive drawing method and inner prying method.

Adhesive drawing method

  1. Repair conditions of adhesive drawing method

    • If the vehicle paint in the damaged area has been damaged or peeled off, it is impossible to complete non-destructive repair of the paint surface.

    Reminder

    • The working temperature of hot melt adhesive is 0-40 degrees. If the temperature is lower than 0 degrees, the hot melt adhesive will solidify quickly and cannot complete the bonding.

    • Solvent is a flammable and explosive alcohol product, pay attention to safety in use.

    • The gun body of the hot melt glue gun will generate high temperature during use, and the operator needs to prevent burns.

Gluing Steps

  1. Clean the dented surface area of the panel with a cleaning cloth, and mark the damaged point with a marker.

  2. Connect the power supply and preheat the heat melt glue gun.

  3. Squeeze the sol of hot melt adhesive gun on the surface of drawn suction cup, quickly bond it to the damaged area of vehicle body, and wait for drying for 20 s.

Caution
The hot melt adhesive will generate high temperature when heated or used, and the operator needs to prevent the danger of scalding.

Pull-out repair procedure

  1. Use a special pulling sliding hammer to pull the bonded drawing gasket, gently slide the hammer, and at the same time, use a colloidal hammer to knock on the plastic deformation area at the damaged position.

  2. To remove the bonded pulling pad, spray adhesive solvent onto the position where the pulling pad is attached.

  3. Clean with detergent the surface of the panel that has been just pulled.

Reminder

  • Sufficient light is required when pulling and repairing, and auxiliary lights for repairing are required when necessary.

  • If there are still unrepaired dents on the surface of the panel after inspection, repeat the above steps.

Caution
Never use too much force when pulling, which will easily lead to excessive pulling, resulting in high spots and failure of non-destructive repair of the paint surface.

Repair tools for inner side prying

  1. High-strength steel crowbar set.

  2. Auxiliary repair lamp.

  3. The rubber stick for knocking the high points.

  4. Rubber hammer for sheet metal work.

  5. Prying.

    Caution

    • When selecting the crowbar, the end of the crowbar in contact with the plate should be smooth.

    • When prying, control the force, operate at multiple points, or use other auxiliary accessories as the fulcrum.

    Caution
    The end of the crowbar must be smooth enough and free from other impurities. The sunken plate may be damaged, resulting in failure of non-destructive repair of the paint surface.

Repair steps for the inner side prying method

  1. Clean the dented surface of the panel with a wiping cloth, and mark the damaged point with a marker.

  2. Install the auxiliary repair lamp.

  3. Select a steel crowbar of appropriate length.

  4. Select a suitable fulcrum for prying. If there is no proper hole, take other auxiliary tools as the fulcrum.

  5. Check the damaged surface after prying using the light source of an auxiliary lamp.

  6. In case of bumps, use a special rubber rod for knocking.
