Overview of painting

Meaning of painting

Reminder
During vehicle painting and maintenance, the construction personnel will use paint materials, painting equipment and tools. They will inevitably come into contact with toxic and harmful substances. If the personnel do not fully understand the basic knowledge of painting, the composition of paint, properly use the equipment, and take protective measures, it may lead to coating defects, sudden work accidents and serious occupational diseases.

  1. Painting

    • Painting refers to a process, in which a thin layer of liquid coating is applied to the surface of a workpiece, and then the coating is dried and cured, forming a hard coating film.

Purpose of painting

  1. Key points

    • To protect materials: The surface of iron, aluminum, wood, or plastic can be protected by painting to prevent rust and corrosion of the materials and prolong their service life.

    • To improve appearance: Painting can improve the visual effect of objects by giving them color and luster.

    • Identification: Spraying specific colors on the vehicle's body can give the vehicle a specific identity, such as patrol police cars and emergency vehicles.
