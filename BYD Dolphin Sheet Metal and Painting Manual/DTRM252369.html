Replacement of left cover panel of rear floor

Removal

  1. Remove the left side wall assembly. Refer to

  2. Remove 7 welding spots.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  3. Remove 2 welding spots and take out the left cover panel of rear floor.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

Installation

  1. Fix the left cover panel of rear floor to the installation position.

  2. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the left cover panel assembly of rear floor and surrounding parts. Refer to

  3. Weld 2 welding spots on the left cover panel assembly of rear floor.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  4. Weld 7 welding spots.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  5. Apply the body sealant and internal coating at corresponding positions.

  6. After applying the external coating, apply rust inhibitor to the inner plate part of the welding spots of the closed section structure.

  7. Install the left side wall assembly. Refer to
