Types and repair of body damage

Damage to outer plates of the body

In the event of an accident, there are two ways to recover the damaged outer plates of the body: repairing the outer steel plate and replacing the outer steel plate assembly.

Repair of outer steel plate

  1. Repair with the hammer and jacking block.

    • Remove some spare parts, so that the hand holding the jacking block can reach the inside of the plate.

    • First, use a wooden hammer or rubber hammer to knock.

    • It is preferable to repair the plates through unaligned hammering to avoid extension.

    Caution

    Never wear cotton gloves when holding the hammer to prevent the hammer from slipping.

  2. Drawing repair with a meson machine

    • Use a pneumatic grinder with sandpaper #80 or black diamond grinding wheel to remove the paint film in the welded area.

    • Adjust the parameters of the welding machine before welding the washer.

    • Repair the dented and damaged part by gradually applying force and drawing many times.

    Caution

    • Never wear cotton gloves when holding the hammer to prevent the hammer from slipping.

    • Pull along the direction at an angle of 90° from the plate surface.

    • Pull the plate to an extent 2 mm above the original plate surface, and knock with a hammer to release the stress.

  3. Shrinking of steel plate

    • There are two shrinking methods for steel plates: copper electrode shrinking and carbon electrode shrinking.

    • Select different shrinking methods according to the extension of steel plate.

    • Before shrinking, adjust the parameters of the equipment to prevent excessive current from burning through the steel plate.

    • Carry out shrinking at the highest point of steel plate extension, and repeat.

    • Use a single-action grinder and sandpaper #80 to remove the shrinking marks on the welded plate.

    Caution

    • Cool down with the air gun after quenching.

    • Never use oxyacetylene for quenching of the steel plate.

    • Spray anti-corrosion wax on the back surface of steel plate for corrosion protection after repair.

Replacement of the outer steel plate assembly

  1. Replacement of the outer steel plate assembly

    • The outer panels connected to the vehicle are generally fixed and installed with bolts.

    Caution

    • Grasp the tightening torque of bolt correctly.

    • Apply strip sealant in absence of vehicle body sealant at steel plate junctions of the replaced external panel (e.g. doors, covered edges of front and rear covers).

    • Spray anti-corrosion wax on the back surface of steel plate for corrosion protection after repair.

Damage to structural plates of the body

The replacement of damaged body structural plates after an accident involve outer coverings and inner structural parts.

Damaged outer coverings

  1. Replacement of damaged rear fender

    • In case of bending or rupture of the rear fender after collision, replace it with a spare assembly.

    • In order to reduce the damage to the body and workload during replacement, generally replace the rear fender by cutting and jointing.

    • Refer to the sheet metal maintenance manual of corresponding model for the cutting position, and select a position with short welding length as far as possible for cutting.

    • Ensure the cutting position away from the sound insulation and noise reduction device inside the cavity as far as possible.

  2. Replacement of damaged roof

    • The roof is dented and bent in a large area due to collision.

    • Replace the roof with a spare assembly.

    • When replacing the roof, ensure the welding strength and good waterproof and rust resistance.

Damaged internal structural parts

  1. Damaged front part of the body

    • The collision force from the front leads to deformation such as distortion or bending of the front compartment.

    • Such accident damage is considered as common deformation.

    • In the body design, several energy-absorbing zones are designed in this part to effectively absorb the frontal impact force.

    • There are many functional components in the front compartment, so alignment and repair must be carried out with reference to relevant data in the process.

    • The length, width and diagonal dimensions are involved for the upper part of the front compartment.

    • The length, width, diagonal and height dimensions are involved for the lower part of the front compartment.

    • The front side beam assembly can be replaced according to the instructions in the sheet metal maintenance manual.

  2. Damaged middle part of the body

    • In case of an accident in the middle part, the middle part will not be deformed or will be less deformed generally, so as to better protect the passengers in the passenger compartment.

    • Such accident damage is considered as common deformation.

    • In the body manufacturing, multi-layer ultra-high strength steel plates are mostly used in this part for the safety protection of passengers in the passenger compartment.

    • Since the service of this part involves the replacement of multi-layer steel plates, plate cutting shall be carried out in strict accordance with the cutting guidance.

  3. Damaged trunk

    • The collision force from the rear leads to deformation such as distortion or bending of the trunk.

    • Such accident damage is considered as common deformation.

    • In the body design, several energy-absorbing zones are designed in this part to effectively absorb the rear impact force.

    • The arched energy-absorbing zones of the rear longitudinal beam are designed to protect the automobile fuel tank.

    • The length, width, diagonal and height dimensions are involved for the trunk.

    • The rear part of the body can be repaired or replaced according to the instructions.

Formulation of service plan

The service plan is formulated based on principles of repair and replacement of non-structural parts and structural parts of the body.

Repair and replacement of non-structural parts of the body

  1. Damaged non-structural parts of the body

    • Non-structural parts of the body are also called exterior coverings, including removable hood, front fender, doors, trunk lid, non-removable rear fender and roof.

  2. Repair criteria

    • The damaged plate is bent or deformed.

    • The damaged area is in smooth transition and continuity with undamaged areas.

    • It can be restored to its original state through sheet metal repair.

    • The damage area does not reach 50% of the component area.

  3. Replacement criteria

    • The damaged plate is bent, deformed, seriously twisted and broken.

    • The damaged plate is partially missing, or is subject to a rupture of more than 20 cm.

    • The damaged plate has lost its basic shape.

    • The damaged area accounts for more than 50% of the area of the damaged plate.

    • It is difficult to repair.

Repair and replacement of structural parts of the body

  1. Damaged structural parts of the body

    • The structural parts of the body include front and rear side beams, coolant tank frame, body pillars and sill panels.

  2. Repair criteria

    • The plate is bent or deformed.

    • The damaged area is in smooth transition and continuity with undamaged areas.

    • It can be restored to its original state by repair.

    • The damage area does not reach 30% of the component area.

  3. Replacement criteria

    • The damaged structural plate is bent and deformed.

    • The structural plate is seriously twisted and broken.

    • The structural plate is partially missing, or is subject to a rupture of more than 20 cm.

    • The plate has lost its basic shape and the energy-absorbing zone has taken effect.

    • The damaged area accounts for more than 30%.

    Caution

    • Front and rear longitudinal members of the vehicle body are essential for installation of chassis parts. Replace the damaged plate if the threaded hole in the damaged area is torn, or the damaged area affects safety and durability of the vehicle after repair.

    • The front longitudinal member is replaced in the manner of assembly replacement.

Service of typical parts

Service of front damage

  1. Characteristics of front damage

    • In case of small frontal impact force, the bumper will be extruded inward and the force is transmitted to the longitudinal beam through the bumper beam. The folded area is deformed and crumpled, absorbing some energy. The fender and its inner panel and the hood are deformed.

    • In case of large impact force, the gap between the fender and the front door will be eliminated, and the hood hinge will be deformed or ruptured.

    • The front compartment is designed with rigid connection. Under severe impact force, it will be affected by extrusion and deformation force, resulting in deformation of longitudinal beam at the other side.

  2. Diagnostic analysis

    • Open the hood, check the deformation inside the front compartment, and focus on the crumple of the side beam impact energy-absorbing zones.

    • Repeatedly open the front and rear doors, check the gap and flushness, and determine whether the body pillar moves backward.

    • Remove and record the damaged components. Remove spare parts that are not damaged but will affect the body repair, and store them properly.

  3. Body fixing

    • Move the vehicle to the alignment rack, clamp it with the alignment rack accessories, and fix the designated positions in the lower part of the sill panels.

    • Fix four fulcrums in the lower part of the sill panels of the body usually. If a fulcrum is damaged, move the rack fixture forward or backward for temporary support.

    • Use auxiliary tools to adjust the level of the vehicle and alignment rack, so as to make preparations for measurement.

    Caution

    Firmly secure the correction platform support fixture at the designated fixed position under the body to avoid the secondary damage.

  4. Body measurement

    • Remove the underbody parts that affect measurement.

    • Measure the data of relevant measuring blocks with a special measuring tool with reference to the body dimension drawing.

    • Three sets of data are involved for the upper part of the front compartment (length, width and diagonal) and four sets of data for the lower part of the front compartment (length, width, diagonal and height).

    • Record the measured data, compare the data information and obtain the deformation after analysis.

    Reminder

    Calculate the height under the engine compartment according to the vehicle body data provided so as to work out the specific difference.

  5. Body alignment and drawing

    • Use the body alignment rack fixture or other auxiliary drawing tools to draw the damaged part. If the spare part of this part need to be replaced, carry out initial drawing on this part and knock the folded area with a hammer for stress relief.

    • Use special tools to remove any body structural part that needs to be replaced. If this part meets the requirements of partial cutting and jointing, scribe a line with the measuring ruler and then cut it off partially.

    • Assemble a new structural part, temporarily fix it with clamps or self-tapping bolts, and measure the corresponding body data.

    • Weld the temporarily fixed body structural part, and pay attention to the welding strength.

    • After welding, measure the body data again, and check for any change in the body data due to stress deformation during welding.

    • Install the spare parts of body coverings, and adjust the clearance, height difference, flushness, etc.

    • Remove the body coverings and grind the welded area.

    Caution

    • Set effective auxiliary support for pulling to avoid secondary damage. Repeat puling, measuring and comparison.

    • Use safety cables for pulling because the clamping tool or chain may slide and fall off during pulling, causing personal injuries to the operator.

    • Pay attention to the pulling amount and direction, and pull in multiple directions.

    • If the front suspension is involved in the damage, perform four wheel alignment to facilitate correction result inspection.

  6. Anti-corrosion treatment

    • Clean and degrease the original anti-corrosion position damaged during repair first, and then spray with appropriate anti-corrosive products.

    Caution

    • Anti-corrosion products include spot welding paint, anti-corrosion wax and brush type sealant.

    • Never apply sealant on exposed metal surface directly, which is prone to rusting.

Service of rear damage

  1. Characteristics of rear damage

    • In case of small impact force, only the rear bumper, trunk lid and rear wall are deformed to absorb impact energy, and arching may also occur at the rear fender trim.

    • In case of large impact force, the lower end of the rear fender and the roof will be deformed. In some cases, the B-pillar may also be deformed and the door cannot be opened/closed normally.

  2. Diagnostic analysis

    • Open the trunk lid, check the deformation inside the trunk, and focus on the crumple of the area with stress concentration of the rear longitudinal beam.

    • Open the rear door repeatedly to determine whether the rear pillar of body moves forward.

Service of side damage

  1. Characteristics of rear damage

    • In order to improve the deformation resistance, interior reinforcement is designed for the front pillar, B-pillar and door sill of the body.

    • In case of side collision, the door will first be extruded inward and the outer panel will be deformed.

    • In case of large impact force, the front pillar, B-pillar and door sill will be deformed, which may affect the bottom plate.

    • In case of very large impact force, the other side of the body will be deformed.

  2. Diagnostic analysis

    • Open the door on the damaged side, remove the seats and related trims in the vehicle, check the deformation inside the passenger compartment, and observe the deformation of the body pillar.

    • Open the front and rear doors repeatedly to determine whether the body pillar is deformed.

    • Remove and record the damaged components. Remove parts that are not damaged but will affect the body repair, and store them properly.

  3. Body fixing

    • Move the vehicle to the alignment rack, clamp it with the alignment rack accessories, and fix the designated positions in the lower part of the sill panels.

    • Fix four fulcrums in the lower part of the sill panels of the body usually. If a body side is seriously damaged and drawing both from the front and rear is required, leave the fixture on the damaged side unfastened temporarily.

    Caution

    Firmly secure the correction platform support fixture at the designated fixed position under the body to avoid the secondary damage.

  4. Body measurement

    • Remove the door and body interior trims that affect measurement.

    • Measure the data of relevant measuring blocks with a special measuring tool with reference to the body dimension drawing.

    • Measure the internal and external dimensions of the body pillar generally by determining a central point at a certain position in the middle of the passenger compartment through left-right comparison.

    • Record the measured data, compare the data information and obtain the deformation after analysis.

    Reminder

    Special measuring tools are required for measurement in the middle section of the vehicle body.

  5. Body alignment and drawing

    • Use the body alignment rack fixture or other auxiliary drawing tools to draw the damaged part. If the spare part of this part need to be replaced, carry out initial drawing on this part and knock the deformed area with a hammer for stress relief.

    • Use special tools to remove any body structural part that needs to be replaced, scribe a line with the measuring ruler and then cut it off partially.

    • Assemble a new structural part, temporarily fix it with clamps or bolts, and measure the corresponding body data.

    • Install the door assembly, and adjust the clearance, flushness and height difference.

    • Remove the door, weld the temporarily fixed body structural part, and pay attention to the welding strength.

    • After welding, install the door assembly again, and check for any change in the body data due to stress deformation during welding.

    • Apply body sealant, install interior trims, and check for normal jointing.

    • Remove the body coverings and grind the welded area.

    Caution

    • Set effective auxiliary support for pulling to avoid secondary damage. Repeat puling, measuring and comparison.

    • Use safety cables for pulling because the clamping tool or chain may slide and fall off during pulling, causing personal injuries to the operator.

    • Pay attention to the pulling amount and direction, and pull in multiple directions.

    • Pillars of the vehicle body are commonly composed of multiple high strength plates. Spot welding is not recommended for this area in the sheet metal workshop, and plug welding is usually adopted.

  6. Anti-corrosion treatment

    • Clean and degrease the original anti-corrosion position damaged during repair first, and then spray with appropriate anti-corrosive products.

    Caution

    • Anti-corrosion products include spot welding paint, anti-corrosion wax and brush type sealant.

    • Never apply sealant on exposed metal surface directly, which is prone to rusting.

Service of roof damage

  1. Characteristics of roof damage

    • The damage to the roof is generally caused by the impact force from the front, rear or side of the vehicle, and in some cases, it is deformed under a single external force.

    • Due to the large area of the roof cover plate, it is difficult to restore it to its original state through shaping. Therefore, it is recommended to replace the assembly if damaged.

  2. Diagnostic analysis

    • Observe the degree of roof damage and determine to repair the steel plate or replace the roof panel assembly.

    • Remove the seats and all interior trims, and check the deformation of the interior sunroof or internal reinforcements of the passenger compartment.

    • Open the front and rear doors repeatedly to determine whether the body pillar is deformed.

    • To replace the roof panel assembly, remove the front and rear windshields first.

    • Remove and record the damaged components. Remove parts that are not damaged but will affect the body repair, and store them properly.

  3. Body fixing

    • If the body pillar is deformed, move the vehicle to the alignment rack, clamp it with the alignment rack accessories, and fix the designated positions in the lower part of the sill panels.

    • If only the outer roof panel is to be repaired or replaced and the inner frame and pillars of the body are not deformed, the body alignment rack is not required.

    Caution

    • Firmly secure the correction platform support fixture at the designated fixed position under the body to avoid the secondary damage.

    • Welding is involved in roof correction. Make sure there is no combustible article inside the vehicle, and mask the un-detachable interiors with the fire blanket.

  4. Body measurement

    • Measure the data of front and rear windshield frames with a special measuring tool with reference to the body dimension drawing.

    • Measure the internal and external dimensions of the body pillar generally by determining a central point at a certain position in the middle of the passenger compartment through left-right comparison.

    • Record the measured data, compare the data information and obtain the deformation after analysis.

    Reminder

    Special measuring tools are required for measurement in the middle section of the vehicle body.

  5. Body alignment and drawing

    • Use the body alignment rack fixture or other auxiliary drawing tools to draw the damaged part. If the spare part of this part need to be replaced, carry out initial drawing on this part and knock the deformed area with a hammer for stress relief.

    • Remove the roof steel plate and its inner frame parts to be replaced with special tools, and replace the roof assembly.

    • Assemble a new structural part, temporarily fix it with clamps or bolts, and measure the corresponding body data, such as the dimensions of front and rear windshield frames.

    • Use the front and rear windshields to align their frames. Install the sunroof (if any) for adjustment.

    • Remove the front and rear windshields, weld the temporarily fixed roof and its structural parts, and pay attention to the welding strength.

    • After welding, compare the front and rear windshields again to check whether their frames change due to stress deformation during welding.

    • Remove the glass or other trims and grind the welded area.

    Caution

    • Set effective auxiliary support for pulling to avoid secondary damage. Repeat puling, measuring and comparison.

    • Use safety cables for pulling because the clamping tool or chain may slide and fall off during pulling, causing personal injuries to the operator.

    • Pay attention to the pulling amount and direction, and pull in multiple directions.

    • Plug welding applies to the roof (bore diameter 6 mm).

  6. Anti-corrosion treatment

    • Clean and degrease the original anti-corrosion position damaged during repair first, and then spray with appropriate anti-corrosive products.

    Caution

    • Anti-corrosion products include spot welding paint, anti-corrosion wax and strip sealant.

    • Never apply sealant on exposed metal surface directly, which is prone to rust.
