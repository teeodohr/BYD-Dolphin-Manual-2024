Quick repair of painting

Principle of quick repair

What is quick repair of painting?

Reminder

  • Quick service refers to the small area paint recovery job without sheet metal removal and installation and welding, which can be completed in 2 - 4.5 hours after simple price evaluation, for small exterior damages of the paint coating.

  1. Key points

    • Complex sheet metal and welding operations are not required.

    • Use quick-drying products with high performance.

    • Use a standardized management process.

    • Use standardized equipment and tools.

    • Complete quick repair of vehicle paintwork in 2-4.5 hours.

Analysis on current situation of sheet metal painting workshop

Reminder

  • In after-sales sheet metal painting service, there are more jobs of small area painting. Many maintenance stations regard small area maintenance, middle area maintenance and large area maintenance as the same job, and include all of them into the normal sheet metal painting maintenance procedure. That is to say, after the vehicle of small area maintenance is received by the receptionist and enters the painting workshop, it will be handled for sheet metal repair — putty operation — grinding — masking — intermediate primer grinding — masking — topcoat and varnish — drying — polishing — overall inspection — delivery. Each step means a relocation of the vehicle. The whole maintenance process includes many steps, and the vehicle is maintained by different technicians at different work stations. It takes 1 to 2 days for the maintenance of common damages.

  1. Key points

    • Extended maintenance time - Assuming that each vehicle movement takes 5 minutes, it will take at least 30 minutes to move one vehicle, which in turn extends the overall maintenance time.

    • Higher cost per vehicle - Man-hour profit per vehicle will decrease due to extended maintenance time.

    • Bottleneck of workshop - It is caused as vehicles requiring small-area maintenance cannot be delivered in a short time. This is directly reflected in the difficulty to move vehicles in a workshop crowded with vehicles.

    • Decreased customer satisfaction - Due to the inclusion of a normal maintenance process, the charge cannot be reduced, and the vehicle cannot be delivered in a short time. All these lead to decreased customer satisfaction for after-sales maintenance and loss of many potential customers.

    • Lower profit - The sheet metal painting workshop is very busy. A large number of vehicles to be repaired (mainly small-area maintenance) are parked in the workshop but cannot be delivered in time, resulting in an increase in the number of maintenance units at the maintenance station, but a sharp drop in the overall profit.

Principle of quick repair of painting

Reminder

  • In view that no plate replacement is involved in maintenance of vehicle with minor damage, no large sheet metal removal and correction is required. Hence, all maintenance jobs can be done at the same work station. Neither vehicle movement or technician change is required, thus allowing for continuous and quick maintenance. The whole maintenance period can be reduced to a few hours. The quick service of painting is developed according to this feature.

Scope and key points of quick repair standard

  1. Key points

    • Quick repair of painting is characterized by that sheet metal, painting and other maintenance operations are completed in the same maintenance station. The vehicle and tools are not moved, and the technician is required to complete the maintenance of one vehicle before taking over the next vehicle.

  2. Scope

    • The damaged area is 30 cm2.

    • The damaged area of sheet metal after repair is within 30% of the plate.

    • It is not necessary to remove body trims, lights and other parts.

    • It is recommended to spray varnish on the whole body plate.

    • The quick repair process is not recommended for horizontal surfaces of the vehicle body.

  3. Key points

    • Vehicle reception and maintenance process

    • Process of quick repair of painting

    • Personnel allocation for quick repair of painting

    • Independently divided operation areas and supporting equipment

    • Use of quick-drying paint products

Reception process for quick repair

  1. Key points

    • Difference from traditional maintenance process of affected vehicles: reception - electromechanical part - sheet metal - painting - assembly.

    • Quick repair and claims consultant responsible for vehicles requiring quick repair of painting: insurance claims - appointment - reception - dispatching progress control - quality inspection and vehicle delivery.

    • As the vehicles requiring quick repair of painting are all with minor damage to plates, direct dispatching from reception to workshop maintenance can simplify the workflow, improve the efficiency and avoid a long waiting period during repair.

    • The full-time quick repair and claims consultant can control the vehicle maintenance progress and delivery time management more effectively by using the progress board.

Process of quick repair of painting

  1. Key points

    • Throughout the quick repair of painting: High-efficiency dry grinding process, quick-drying products, and infrared drying equipment are used in combination with the process of efficient painting and spraying repair. Maintenance technicians repair the vehicles at the quick repair station to reduce waiting time in the process.

    • Key points for quick repair of painting: dry grinding process, quick-drying products, auxiliary drying equipment and transition area painting process.

Personnel allocation for quick repair of painting

  1. Key points

    • Quick repair of painting realizes the integrated repair of sheet metal and painting. Vehicles with moderate and minor damages can be directly repaired at the quick repair station without entering the sheet metal workshop. It is very important for the claims consultant at reception to judge whether quick repair is applicable to the vehicle from a professional perspective and make reasonable overall planning and vehicle diversion.

    • The BYD authorized dealer is equipped with full-time quick repair reception and maintenance resources, so as to smoothly establish a green channel for quick repair of painting. Optimal allocation of vehicles requiring maintenance is a key factor.

Vehicles suitable for quick repair of painting

  1. Key points

    • For the vehicle body panel, the exterior damage with an area of A4 paper does not require a large amount of sheet metal work during repair. It can be properly removed and shaped without a large amount of poly-putty application.

Requirements for damage location

  1. Key points

    • Not all positions on the vehicle body are suitable for quick repair, and there are requirements for repair, as shown in the figure.

    • Red - Not suitable for quick repair of painting. It is recommended that the whole panel be paint-sprayed if the horizontal area is subject to major damage.

    • Yellow - Optional. The color of this area is easy to repair, and attention should be paid to the color transition between plates.

    • Green - Quick repair of painting is recommended. The damage in this area is easy to repair, and the varnish can be sprayed in the blend area.

Requirements for color type of vehicle body

  1. Key points

    • Colors suitable for quick repair: Tianshan White, Ceramic White, Crystal White, Delan Black, Silver Sand Black, French Red, Hera Red, Purple Red Pearl, Athenian Silver, Rhea Silver, Mecca Gold, Streamer Brown, Dream Blue, Ice Sea Blue, Sky Blue and Vibrant Orange.

    • Colors not suitable for quick repair: Three-process White Pearl, Three-process Red Pearl, and Champagne Gold.

Analysis of type - minor damage

  1. Key points

    • The damage area is of A4 paper size.

    • There are slight scratches, with the varnish layer slightly scratched.

    • No spraying is required after treatment by dry grinder.

    • The top coat can be sprayed directly.

Analysis of type - moderate damage

  1. Key points

    • The damage area is of A4 paper size.

    • The scratch reaches the base coat, the primer can be seen, and the bottom metal surface is not deformed.

    • Spraying primer is required after treatment by dry grinder.

    • There are gravel impact marks.

Analysis of type - major damage

  1. Key points

    • The damage area is of A4 paper size.

    • The scratch reaches the substrate, with slight deformation.

    • No complicated sheet metal treatment is required.

    • It can be restored to the original state by applying 1-2 layers of poly-putty thinly.

Whole plate repair

  1. Key points

    • Spray the whole plate with colored paint.

    • It requires high color accuracy. Painting can be carried out only when the paint color is very close to the original vehicle color.

Transition area repair

  1. Key points

    • In case of a large color difference, using this method can ensure that the color is acceptable.

    • During repair, expanding the spraying scope to adjacent plates is required.

    • Spraying scope of varnish should be expanded to the adjacent workpiece or the three plates as a whole.

Whole plate repair

  1. Key points

    • Minor damage can be repaired within one day.

    • A small amount of sheet metal and puttying may be required.

    • Transitional spraying is required for the colored paint.

    • The whole plate should be varnished.

Local repair

  1. Key points

    • It is only suitable for repairing minor damages.

    • Generally, sheet metal and puttying are not required.

    • The finished area of the colored paint is about A4 paper size.

    • Local repair is also used for the varnish.

    • Major repair is required on obvious horizontal surfaces.

    • The repair can be completed within about 2 hours.

Minor damage

  1. Key points

    • The damaged part is within 30%, and the damage depth does not exceed 1 cm. Scratches are generally between 5 cm and 15 cm, and the damage at lines and corners is within 7 cm.

    • One plate: The vehicle can be delivered within 3 hours.

    • Two plates: The vehicle can be delivered within 4.5 hours.

    • Three plates: The vehicle can be delivered within 6 hours.

Major damage

  1. Key points

    • The damage part exceeds 50%, the damage depth exceeds 3 cm, and the damage at lines and corners is more than 25 cm.

    • Two plates: The vehicle can be delivered within 9 hours.

    • Three plates: The vehicle can be delivered within 12 hours.

New parts for replacement

  1. Key points

    • Two plates: The vehicle can be delivered within 6 hours.

    • Three plates: The vehicle can be delivered within 9 hours.

Sheet metal repair

  1. Steps

    • Grind off the old coating in the damaged area.

    • Weld gaskets at the concave positions of plates, and trim them with a sheet metal hammer.

    • Check the repair quality at the grinding and trimming positions.

    • Transfer to the painting process.

  2. Spot check

    • Adjust the welding parameters of meson machine.

    • Check the flatness of the repaired plate.

    • Remove the old coating and welding marks from the damaged area.

Application of poly-putty

  1. Cleaning of plate

    1. Steps

      • Spray the degreaser evenly on the damaged plate with a degreaser spray can.

      • Wipe back and forth with a wiping cloth. Before the degreaser volatilizes, wipe it in one direction with another wiping cloth and wipe off the degreaser to clean the whole plate.

    2. Purpose

      • Clean the silicon wax components and sundries adhered to the plate.

    3. Spot check

      • Spray the degreaser evenly on the damaged plate.

      • Clean with a wet wiping cloth and then dry with a dry wiping cloth.

      • Wipe in the same direction in turn and orderly.

  2. Grinding of feather edge

    1. Steps

      • Grind with #5/7 grinding head and #P80-120 sandpaper, and slightly tilt the grinding pad to the plate to shorten the grinding time.

      • Move the grinding pad along the feather edge until the width of the feather edge of each layer of the old paint film reaches about 30 mm.

      • Grind the whole part to be coated with poly-putty with #P180 sandpaper to ensure that it exceeds the coating range.

    2. Spot check

      • Ensure the grit jump of sandpaper not exceeding 100 (like P80-P120-P180).

      • Touch the ground feather edge with hands to check if they are smooth.

      • Check if the width of the feather edge is 3 cm.

  3. Cleaning and degreasing

    1. Steps

      • Spray the degreaser evenly in the damaged plate area.

      • Clean with a wet wiping cloth and then dry with a dry wiping cloth.

    2. Spot check

      • Wipe in the same direction in turn and orderly to avoid repeated contamination.

      • Clean with a wet wiping cloth and then dry with a dry wiping cloth.

  4. Application of poly-putty

    1. Steps

      • Open the poly-putty tank to stir the poly-putty base stock evenly.

      • Mix the poly-putty and curing agent in a ratio of 100:2-3.

      • Apply the first thin coat of poly-putty to fill the small depressions or sandpaper marks after repair, avoiding pinholes and bubbles caused by air mixing.

      • Apply the second coat of poly-putty which can be thicker as appropriate and pay attention to trimming to reduce poly-putty marks.

      • Use an alternating method of lateral and longitudinal application in the subsequent process to ensure better filling.

    2. Spot check

      • The ratio of poly-putty to curing agent should be 100:2-3.

      • The poly-putty must be mixed evenly, and the color must be consistent after mixing.

      • The first coat is compacted thinly without pinholes and bubbles.

      • Subsequent layers should be thickened layer by layer. Note that the edge should not be too thick.

  5. Drying of poly-putty

    1. Steps

      • Forcibly dry it for 5-10 minutes with an infrared heating lamp.

    2. Spot check

      • Check if the heating lamp is located about 50 cm from the plate.

      • Check if it is dry by touching the edge of poly-putty with fingernails.

  6. Grinding of poly-putty

    1. Steps

      • Apply carbon powder indicator on the poly-putty to better judge grinding quality of poly-putty.

      • Grind the poly-putty and shape it with a suitable grinder with sandpaper #+P80-120.

      • After the poly-putty is basically ground flat, trim and polish it with a manual grinding plate #+P180.

      • Remove coarse sandpaper marks with a fine grinder with sandpaper #+P240.

      • Use #3 grinder with sandpaper #+P320 to grind the surrounding paint film beyond the range of intermediate paint, and use a dust gun to remove dust in small holes on the poly-putty surface.

    2. Spot check

      • During grinding, apply carbon powder to determine the defective part of poly-putty.

      • Ensure that the grit jump of sandpaper does not exceed 100, the grinding range gradually transitions from the middle to the edge, and the sandpaper marks from previous grinding are removed.

      • Clean the repair area, check pinholes and reapply poly-putty if necessary.

Process of intermediate paint application

  1. Preparation before application

    1. Steps

      • Reversely mask the periphery of the part to be sprayed within the grinding range and then degrease and clean it.

      • Clean the area around the repair area before pasting the masking paper.

      • Do not wipe the poly-putty area during cleaning.

    2. Spot check

      • The poly-putty surface is free of dust before intermediate paint spraying.

      • The spraying area is reversely masked.

      • The spraying area is cleaned before spraying.

  2. Spraying of intermediate paint

    1. Steps

      • Prepare the paint according to the mixing proportion recommended by the paint manufacturer.

      • Spray the first thin coat of intermediate paint with a special spray gun to avoid paint lifting. The spraying range should not be too close to the masking paper. Otherwise, the paint edge may be too thick for grinding.

      • Apply the second coat by wet spraying and keep the spraying range within the first coat as far as possible.

      • Start applying the third coat by wet spraying after flash drying of the second coat to avoid pinholes and keep the spraying range within the second coat as far as possible.

    2. Spot check

      • The first coat is applied throughout the grinding range.

      • Flash drying time is taken into account between coats.

      • The spraying range of intermediate paint is reduced layer by layer.

  3. Drying of intermediate paint

    1. Steps

      • Dry the paint surface for 20 minutes with an infrared heating lamp about 50 cm away.

    2. Spot check

      • The heating lamp is located about 50 cm from the plate.

      • The plate is 60 °C and dried with an infrared heating lamp for 20 minutes.

  4. Grinding of intermediate paint

    1. Steps

      • Apply carbon powder indicator on the primer surface first.

      • If necessary, manually grind the poly-putty area with a hand planer and sandpaper #P320.

      • Use #3 grinding head + sandpaper #P400 to grind the intermediate paint area.

      • Use sandpaper #P600 to grind the intermediate paint area.

    2. Spot check

      • Every time the sandpaper is replaced, it is necessary to apply carbon powder on the edge to confirm the defective part.

      • The grinding pad is closely attached to the plate, and the radian of the plate should be taken into account.

      • During grinding, it is crucial to grind from different directions and continuously check the flatness by hand.

  5. Grinding of blend area

    1. Steps

      • Grind with #3 grinding head + sandpaper #P1000, or grind with gray scouring pad and abrasive paste.

    2. Spot check

      • The grit jump of sandpaper is as required for grinding the transition paint surface.

      • It is recommended that the edge of the plate be treated with scouring pad + abrasive paste.

Process of top coat application

  1. Preparation

    1. Steps

      • Clean and degrease the plate to be sprayed.

      • Mask the area required to be masked with the masking paper.

      • Use a blower gun and tack cloth to clean the dust from top to bottom.

      • Prepare an appropriate amount of paint according to the mixing proportion recommended in the paint manual.

      • Select a filter funnel with more than 120 meshes for filtration of primer.

    2. Spot check

      • During cleaning, carefully check the defective parts of intermediate paint and repair them in time.

      • The transition area can be reversely masked.

      • Paint is prepared with a paint mixing ruler or measuring cup.

      • Paint is prepared as required to avoid waste.

      • Ensure correct mixing proportion of the two-component colored paint, curing agent and thinner.

      • Store the paint properly and do not leave paint cans open.

  2. Spraying

    1. Steps

      • Adjust the parameters of the spray gun such as the spray pattern, paint output, and pressure.

      • Spray the first coat of colored paint on the intermediate paint surface with a gun distance of about 20 cm. After flash drying of the painted surface, spray the second coat while ensuring a proper gun distance, constant speed, overlapping range, vertical angle, parallel distance, trigger retraction and release, and wrist angle.

      • Spray the third coat after flash drying of the second coat.

    2. Spot check of 2K plain paint spraying

      • The plain paint reaches 80% gloss in the first semi-wet spraying and 100% gloss in the second full wet spraying.

      • The flash drying time of the first coat is about 15 minutes.

    3. Spot check of 1K metallic paint spraying

      • For metallic paint, mist spraying, wet spraying, and semi-wet spraying are used for the first, second, and third coats, respectively.

      • The flash drying time of each coat is 5-10 minutes.

      • It is required to remove the paint mist with tack cloth before proceeding to the next coat.

      • The water-based primer should be applied properly based on the spraying method.

  3. Transitional spraying of metallic primer

    1. Steps

      • Use transitional spraying to adjacent plates if the local repair position is close to the edge of adjacent plates.

      • Use transitional spraying to adjacent plates on both sides and then varnish all areas in case of whole plate spraying.

      • In case of single-process top coat spraying, use transitional spraying, spray the transition area with blender, and dry the area before polishing.

  4. Spraying of varnish

    1. Steps

      • Adjust the spray gun and apply a thin coat of varnish on an area larger than the primer spraying area.

      • Spray the second coat of varnish after appropriate flash drying to ensure fullness of the varnish while paying attention to details such as gun distance, moving speed of spray gun, vertical angle, and overlapping of spray pattern.

      • Ensure sound coating texture and paint film brightness after spraying.

    2. Spot check

      • Paint must be prepared with a paint mixing ruler or measuring cup.

      • Curing agent and thinner should be added according to the product manual.

      • Paint should be prepared as required to avoid waste.

      • Flash drying time is taken into account between coats.

  5. Drying

    1. Steps

      • Forcibly dry the paint after spraying.

      • Tear off the covered adhesive tape after drying and before cooling down of the plate. Otherwise, it will be hard to tear it off after the plate cools down.

    2. Spot check

      • After the top coat is sprayed, it is required to wait for 10 minutes before heating and baking it.

      • It can be baked for 30 minutes by heating the spray booth to 60 °C, or baked for 20 minutes by using an infrared heating lamp, or let it dry naturally.

Polishing process

  1. Steps

    • Mark defects such as dust spots on the paint surface with a marker pen. Grind the dust spots with a point grinder + sandpaper #P1500.

    • Or manually grind the dust spots with grinding block and waterproof sandpaper.

    • Use an appropriate grinding head and fine sandpaper #P2000 for large-area grinding.

    • Polish with coarse wool wheel or sponge wheel and polishing wax.

    • Finally, polish with fine polishing wax and fine polishing wheel to restore the gloss of paint film coating.

  2. Spot check

    • The paint film defects should be ground with sandpaper #P1500-2000.

    • Suitable polishing wheel and wax should be used to restore the gloss of the coating.

Quality inspection and vehicle delivery

  1. Steps

    • Clean the repaired vehicle before the completion inspection.

    • Arrange full-time personnel to carry out the completion inspection, who shall record the defects using a completion inspection list to gradually reduce the repair rate. In this way, the quick repair of painting is completed.

    • Arrange the reception claims consultant to contact the customer and make an appointment for vehicle pickup. When the customer arrives for pickup, the reception claims consultant checks the repair quality to ensure customer satisfaction.

  2. Spot check

    • Make sure that the vehicle is cleaned.

    • Make sure that all maintenance items are completed.

    • Make all necessary preparations before delivery.

    • Make sure that the body color, plate flatness and plate gap meet the quality standards after maintenance.
