Inspection method

Reference

Any bulge on the corrected surface

Gently place the palm on the plate surface, slowly move the palm to feel and judge. Move the hand from the undamaged surface to the damaged surface, and then move to the undamaged surface.

Observe the damaged part laterally at different angles, and judge according to the scene around the vehicle reflected on the plate surface. When it is not easy to observe, move your body slightly and stay away from the vehicle for observation.

Use the sandpaper #80~120, and gently place it on the finishing surface with a hand planer and rub to generate marks. The dent does not have sandpaper marks, while the bulge has.

Select a scale and move it from the undeformed surface to the finishing surface, and judge the unevenness according to the gap between the scale and the plate.

Press the finishing surface with fingers to distribute the force on the whole plate and confirm whether there is any soft or dented part.

Welding defects

Welding cracking occurs, with small holes.

There are burrs at the welding position, and the welding melting point is too small.

There is too much welding slag inside the welding spot.

The steel plates have excessive clearance at the joint.

The welding positions are evenly spaced.

The number of welding spots is more than 1.3 times that of the original vehicle.

Non-destructive inspection: Drive the wedge or chisel into the welding side, without the weld falling off. Stop driving the wedge until the welding position can be seen. Open 3 mm - 4 mm for the same plate thickness and 1.5 mm - 2 mm for different plate thickness.

Welding inspection standard

Inspection items

Requirements

Welding standard

Plug welding

Surface

No cracks between the bonding panels

There is no holes on the surface of the welding spot

Fill the entire hole completely

The weld bead height shall not exceed 2 mm

The weld bead width: diameter of drilled hole + 2 mm

The back side

No burn-through

There is obvious penetration depth, and the height of the penetration welding spot is 1 mm

Butt welding

Surface

There is no obvious thermal deformation on the surface of the bonding panels

There are no holes on the surface of the panels

There is no undercut at the joint edges of the panels

The overlap between each bead surface in the weld pass should be 1/3

The weld pass is completely filled

The weld pass height shall not exceed 2 mm

The weld pass width shall not exceed 5 mm

The back side

No burn-through

There is obvious penetration depth, and the height of the penetration welding spot is 1 mm

There should be no cracks on the back and no deviation in the weld pass
