Masking process

Overview of masking process

  1. Description of masking

    • The so-called masking refers to the work of using masking paper or tape to mask non-coated areas to prevent the foggy paint sprayed beyond the spraying area from adhering to glass, tires, and other non-coated areas.

    1. Coating

      • The spraying range of the paint sprayed by the spray gun varies depending on the air pressure and the sensitivity of the spray gun. During top coat spraying, the paint can be sprayed approximately 1-2 m beyond the plate. In addition, paint mist can also enter the gaps between the plates. Therefore, to prevent paint mist from being sprayed to non-coated areas and to prevent paint mist from entering the gaps between plates, masking needs to be carried out.

    2. Masking

      • Cover irrelevant areas of the vehicle according to the requirements of painting.

      • During masking, different materials shall be selected according to different operation requirements.

      • The masking operation shall be standardized and rigorous to avoid damage and looseness after masking.

      • Masking boundary shall be emphasized in work.

  2. Purpose of masking

    • Masking is a protective method for vehicle body spraying, which uses tape and paper to cover the surface of plates that do not need to be sprayed. It can also be used during grinding, applying poly-putty or polishing to protect the surface of surrounding plates and provide effective protection. This section introduces the main masking method used before spraying intermediate paint and applying top coat.

    1. Purpose of masking process

      • To prevent paint from sticking to areas where the plates do not need to be sprayed.

      • To prevent damage to undamaged parts during construction.

  3. Requirements for masking materials

    • After spraying, the paint and solvent will not penetrate.

    • Adhesive tape has good heat resistance and will not fall off on its own.

    • After the tape is removed, the adhesive will not remain on the surface of the plate.

    • The tape will have no effect on the adhesive surface and will not damage the coating of undamaged areas.

  4. Basic requirements for masking operation

    • The masking operation is mainly divided into two steps. Based on the different situations and positions of the vehicle under maintenance, appropriate masking materials and methods are selected, and the masking of corresponding plate is reasonably done according to the condition of the vehicle under maintenance.

    1. Preparation before masking

      • Prior to the masking operation, it is necessary to prepare well, otherwise it will not only affect the quality of masking process, but also the spraying effect. Therefore, corresponding preparations must be made before masking.

      • Selection of masking material: Use designated masking paper or masking film.

      • Cleaning of plate: Remove dust, compounds, and moisture from the surface to prevent the masking tape from falling off. While cleaning the refilling paint part, it is necessary to clean and degrease the paste part.

      Reminder

      • If the old newspaper is used, the garbage will stick to the fiber of the newspaper, and the newspaper is easy to be soaked by the solvent, and the covered part will be polluted, resulting in dust spots.

    2. Range of masking pasted

      • The masking range varies depending on the coating location and conditions, as well as the paint type. The surroundings of the coating area shall be carefully masked. If paint mist may be sprayed to the roof, hood, and other areas, it is more convenient to use a special coating masking film.

      • Masking of interior trim First mask the inner plate of the opening area and then the position to be masked when the door is opened.

      • Masking of outer panel Mask the surroundings of the repaired area with masking paper, and then mask the whole vehicle with masking film.

      • Masking of boundary Since the masking around the boundary needs to be removed before the paint is completely dry, the boundary area is finally masked to facilitate the removal of the tape.

      • Masking of glass and tires The masking of windshield and tire areas needs to be carried out after the vehicle enters the spray booth.

    3. Masking material removal

      • Generally speaking, masking materials shall be removed after polishing. The masking tape along the boundary shall be carefully removed after coating and when the coating is still soft, as once the coating becomes dry and brittle, it will not evenly separate, resulting in unsatisfactory edge.

      Reminder

      • The reason for stripping the masking material after polishing is to protect the surrounding parts from the influence of polishing agent.

      • If the masking material of window edge tape is used, remove it when the seal strip is still hot after it has just dried. If the seal strip is removed after it is cold, it will be deformed and difficult to restore to its original status.

Masking materials and device

Introduction to masking material

Reminder

  • Before masking operation, understand the characteristics and application scope of commonly used masking materials. Proper use of masking materials and related supporting equipment will effectively improve the efficiency and quality of masking.

  1. Masking paper

    • Special masking paper is widely used because it is free from dust or fiber falling, resistant to paint solvents, easy to use, broadly applicable and efficient.

    • It is used to mask areas where plates do not need to be sprayed.

    • With treated masking paper, the solvent cannot penetrate. The masking paper will not generate dust, and the paint adhered to the paper will not fall off.

    Reminder

    • There are various sizes and thicknesses of masking paper for selection. Some masking papers have tapes on one side.

    1. Use of masking paper

      • When taking the masking paper, make it slightly larger to cover the entire steel plate.

      • When covering around the plate, ensure that there are no steps after pasting the masking paper.

      Reminder

      • Avoid supplementing small pieces of masking paper, which will increase the cost and reduce the quality.

      • Paint mist and dust will gather in the steps formed by the masking papers. They will be blown away by the air pressure of the spray gun during the spraying process and adhere to the sprayed surface, affecting the spraying quality.

  2. Masking tape

    1. Introduction to masking tape

      • The masking tape is used to fix masking paper, film, or masking boundary, which is easy to remove and use.

      • The treated masking tape will not break when pulled out from the reel and is easy to remove from the surface of the tape.

      Reminder

      • The masking tape falls into different types according to the type of substrate and heat resistance limit.

    2. Method of using masking tape

      • Insert the middle finger into the hole in the reel, pull out the required length of tape, and then pinch the tape with index finger and thumb, pull and remove it.

      • Do not pull the tape when pasting it to the surface of the plate. Press the tape to paste to the surface. Excessive pulling of the tape will cause edge warping.

      • To avoid leaving clear boundary when removing the masking tape, remove the masking tape from the sprayed surface at an angle of 15-30°.

      Reminder

      • Clean the edges and gaps of the plates and then paste the masking tape, so as to prevent dirt and dust from gathering around the tape.

      • If the tape is pulled after being stuck to the surface, it will return to its original shape and cause the edge to tilt, and may fall off.

      • Never put masking tape in your pocket or on the floor. Dust or dirt will adhere to the masking tape.

  3. Masking film

    • The advantage of ethylene film is that its width is wider than that of masking paper, so it is suitable for covering the surface of body with a relatively large workpiece surface.

    Reminder

    • The vinyl film, wider than the masking paper, can cover a larger area. Improve masking efficiency and reduce costs.

    • There are masking films of various sizes and thicknesses. Some masking films can be folded for many times with tape on one side for easy use.

    • After special treatment, the masking film has static electricity, which is convenient to stick to the vehicle body and absorb paint dust.

  4. Gap tape

    • Gap tape is used at the hood or door to prevent paint from entering the gap. The gap tape is made of polyurethane foam with adhesive, which simplifies the masking process of the area with gap. It is cylindrical in shape, thus preventing the formation of coating steps and making the coated surface easier to grind.

    Reminder

    • It is mainly used for the gaps around the engine cover, doors and trunk lid. Prevent paint mist from passing through the gap and polluting the interior of the plate.

  5. Window edge masking tape

    • It is inserted between the steel plate and the sealing strip or glass protective strip.

    Reminder

    • Use window edge masking tapes to mask the weather strips. Insert the masking tape into the adhesive strip for reverse masking. Check the entire masking contour and ensure that there is no omission. Avoid hard paint edges here.

Introduction to masking device

  1. Masking paper holder

    • The masking paper holder is a device for installing masking paper for masking operations. It is used to enable operators to easily and quickly control and use masking paper, and to stick adhesive tape while pulling the masking paper.

    Reminder

    • During the masking operation, cut the masking paper quickly according to the required length.

  2. Auxiliary tools

    • Other auxiliary tools such as plastic scrapers, scissors, and art knives are used to facilitate the pasting operation.

Masking method

Masking of opening area

Reminder

  • The opening masking method refers to masking the opening from the inside or outside after removing the body parts, so as to avoid the paint entering the vehicle.

  1. Body opening area

    • Ensure that the masking tape is not peeled off by air pressure.

    • Ensure that paint mist sprayed does not enter the opening area.

    • The masking tape can be applied from the inside or from the outside.

  2. Internal masking of opening

    • Internal masking is to adhere the masking material to the inside of the opening.

    • Ensure that the masking tape can withstand external air pressure.

  3. External masking of opening

    • External masking is to adhere the masking material to the outside of the opening. The adhesion of masking materials must be tight and firm.

Gap masking process

Reminder

  • The gap masking refers to effectively masking the gaps between body steel plates. Select appropriate masking materials according to the situation and ensure the quality of masking.

  1. Gap tape masking

    • First clean the gap to ensure firm adhesion.

    • After adhesion, ensure that paint mist does not enter the gap.

    • When using gap tape, ensure that there is no gap between the tape and the steel plate, and pay special attention to the position of the upper and lower edges when masking the gap at the front and rear doors.

  2. Masking gap with masking paper

    • First clean the gap effectively to ensure firm adhesion.

    • Cut appropriate length of masking paper and adhere it along the front door sealant and the rear door edge steps to ensure good edge masking. Pay attention to the treatment of gap edge during the masking process.

    • Ensure that the moisture inside the gap has been removed before masking the trim strip.

Masking of assembly parts

  1. Masking of assembly parts

    • Mask the assembly parts that cannot be disassembled to ensure that the paint will not adhere to the assembly parts.

    Reminder

    • Reserve a gap with the same coating thickness between the steel plate surface and the masking tape.

    • Never pull the masking tape when sticking it to the bend.

    • Finally, mask the boundary of the assembly with masking tape.

    Caution

    • The gap between the masking tape and the steel plate surface is too large, and the masked parts will be the polluted in the spraying operation.

    • The reserved gap is too small, which will lead to incomplete paint masking or no paint adhesion after the edge is sprayed.

Masking of glass protective strip

Reminder

  • The glass seal masking method refers to masking the glass seal that directly contacts with the sprayed surface.

  1. Masking of glass sealing strip

    • To ensure that there is a gap between the glass sealing strip and the steel plate, insert the window edge masking tape between the two and pull it back, so that the sealing strip will be pulled away from the steel plate.

    • For curved areas, cut a few notches on the tape with scissors before adhering the window edge masking tape. Bend the window edge tape along the edge, and use tape to supplement the missing parts.

    • After spraying and drying, when there is still residual heat, remove the window edge tape and the edge sealing strip will recover to its original shape.

Reverse masking process

Reminder

  • The reverse masking method refers to the reverse folding of masking paper when pasting. The purpose is to minimize the steps generated by spraying.

  1. Reverse masking

    • After spraying, ensure that the masked boundary surface does not produce significant coating step difference.

    • If there is body edge line in the masking area, reverse masking can be performed on the body edge line.

    • It is difficult to clean and degrease after reverse masking, so it is necessary to prepare for degreasing and cleaning before masking.

    • Reverse masking method is often used when applying intermediate paint.

Masking process for transition area

Reminder

  • Transition area masking: it refers to masking the area where transition operation is to be carried out, such as rear fender or small area touch-up.

  1. Masking of transition area

    • After spraying, ensure that the masked boundary surface does not produce step or color difference.

    • It is difficult to degrease and clean the transition area after masking, so it is necessary to degrease and clean well before masking.

Masking case

Masking method for primer coating

Reminder

  • As the air pressure used for primer application is lower than that for topcoat application (the paint mist overflow range is relatively small), the masking process of workpiece surface is relatively simple. Generally, reverse patching is adopted to prevent spraying steps.

  1. Mask before applying intermediate paint

    • When spraying intermediate paint, perform reverse masking in the grinding range.

    • When spraying locally, perform reverse covering in the poly-putty application area.

    • When spraying the entire area, follow the "masking of top coat coating" for treatment.

Masking of top coat coating

Reminder

  • To re-spray a large area, such as the fender or door plates, cover them separately. If there are holes on the plate (such as the opening of the trim or the gap between the plates), cover them to prevent paint mist from entering these areas and polluting the interiors of the plates Examples of specific repair parts are listed below to illustrate the masking methods.

  1. Masking when coating rear door and fender

    1. Taking spraying rear door and fender of vehicle as an example, explain the key points of masking.

    2. Before masking, clean the gaps and assembly parts of the plate to remove moisture and dust from the gaps, and clean with a degreaser.

    3. Adhere masking paper along the edge of the inner plate of the rear fender to avoid paint contamination on the inner side of the plate during spraying.

    4. For the inner part of the door, masking paper shall be adhered along the sealant.

    5. Center fold and adhere masking tape, adhere it to the edge of the rear fender on the upper side of the door, close the door, and adhere the remaining tape to the door frame, leaving a suitable gap at the edge of the plate.

    6. Adhere the trunk lid section and the front door section separately.

    7. Adhere masking paper along the edge of the plate separately, carefully adhering and avoiding any omissions or excessive parts.

    8. During the masking process, efforts shall be made to ensure that the surface is flat after masking, avoiding steps or wrinkles.

    9. Cut a suitable length of masking film and adhere the masking film to the body part.

    10. During the operation, parts such as tires or windshields can be covered after the vehicle is moved to the spray booth.

    11. The transition area of the junction needs to be degreased and cleaned first, and then covered with reverse masking method to avoid excessive paint mist pollution.

  2. Masking when coating the front door

    1. Clean the plate and adjacent parts to be adhered to remove dust on the plate and moisture in the gap.

    2. Adhere the edge of the plate, and adhere the trim cover on the front fender and the lower wheel arch area to prevent paint mist from contaminating the inside during spraying.

    3. Adhere masking paper slightly longer than the edge of the plate stick to adjacent plate, and adhere along the edge of the plate to avoid wrinkles.

    4. Reserve the body trim strip area for final adhering, ensuring that appropriate gaps are reserved during adhering.

    5. After moving the vehicle to the spray booth, adhere masking film, cut the masking film reasonably according to the adhering situation of the masking paper, and ensure that there are no missing parts.

    6. Adhere the body trim strip that is not removed, and reserve appropriate gaps to avoid paint adhering to the trim strip during spraying or paint and tape adhering together after spraying. Reserve gaps with the same thickness as the coating between the steel plate surface and the masking tape.

    7. Adhere masking tape to the disassembled door handle hole to avoid paint contamination of interior trims and other areas.

    8. Check the masking quality to avoid paint mist contamination during coating construction.

Analysis of defects in masking process

Reminder

  • Improper masking will cause various spraying defects. In actual work, ensure that the correct masking processes, materials and methods are used.

  1. Paint adhesion

    1. Causes

      • The area covered by the masking material before spraying is too small.

      • There are gaps at the edge or gap of the plate during masking.

    2. Measures

      • Polish the surface after removing adhesive paint.

      • Wipe with solvent before the paint is completely dry.

  2. Coating steps

    1. Causes

      • During the application of intermediate paint, reverse covering is not perform in the area, resulting in coating steps after spraying.

      • The spraying range is not reasonably controlled during spraying operations, resulting in an excessively large spraying area.

    2. Measures

      • Reasonably plan the application range before spraying the primer.

      • After thorough grinding, repaint the primer application area.

  3. Masking material adhesion

    1. Causes

      • During the application of intermediate paint, reverse covering is not perform in the area, resulting in coating steps after spraying.

      • The spraying range is not reasonably controlled during spraying operations, resulting in an excessively large spraying area.

    2. Measures

      • Reasonably plan the application range before spraying the primer.

      • After thorough grinding, repaint the primer application area.

  4. Dirt or dust adhered

    1. Causes

      • The paint and dirt gathered at the folding of the masking material adhere to the surface.

      • The dirt and dust remaining in the gaps between the plates adhere to the surface.

    2. Measures

      • Ensure that there are no steps or dents and bulges during masking.

      • Mask gaps between the plates.

      • Grind the area with sandpaper to eliminate defects, and then perform polishing treatment.

  5. Tape marks

    1. Causes

      • The residue of adhesive tape affects the paint film.

      • The surface temperature of the plate is too high or the masking tape comes into contact with solvent, causing the adhesive to fall off.

    2. Measures

      • Select appropriate masking materials for adhering and ensure that the masking tape is solvent and high-temperature resistant.

      • Remove the tape before the surface dries.

      • Polish the surface for repair.

  6. Boundary paint falling off

    1. Causes

      • After forced drying, the masking tape is removed after the surface cools. Improper operation method during the cleaning process.

      • The surface of the substrate was not properly ground and cleaned before spraying the top coat.

      • The paint coating is sprayed too thick, and the method of removing the masking tape is incorrect.

    2. Measures

      • After drying, remove the masking tape when there is still residual heat on the plate.

      • Pay attention to the angle when removing the tape.

      • Grind defects and repaint.
