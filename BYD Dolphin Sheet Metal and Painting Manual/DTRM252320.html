Coating equipment and tools

Spray booth

  1. Key points

    • Low-temperature paint is used for the maintenance of affected vehicles, and the baking temperature is generally 60-80 °C.

    • Based on the structure, spray booths can be divided into two types: room type and tunnel type.

    • The room-type spray booth is an integrated spraying and baking booth.

    • Tunnel type means that the vehicle enters from one side to complete spraying, and drives out from the baking assembly line from the other side.

    • Based on the type of energy used, they can be divided into oil, gas, and electric heating ones.

    • Based on the type of filtration, they can be divided into wet filtration and dry filtration.

  2. Operation points

    • Positive pressure air supply is generally used in the spray booth, and the indoor air supply pressure is generally kept at 4-12 bar higher than outdoors. The positive pressure air supply can ensure no flow of outdoor air into the spray booth and keep the booth clean. The pressure is adjusted by the damper.

    • The air supply rate should reach 0.2-0.6 m/s during spraying, and the airflow rate is generally 0.05 m/s during heating and baking.

    • The lighting in the paint baking booth is required to be above 800 lx. Generally, multiple groups of fluorescent lamps are used on both sides of the roof. In addition, the noise during the operation of the paint baking booth shall not exceed 80dB.

    • The best spraying environment for water-based paint is one with a temperature of 25 °C and a relative humidity of less than 70%.

    Caution

    • All spraying personnel shall be familiar with the operation process of the spray booth. Untrained personnel shall not work in the spray booth. Start the spray booth according to the specification.

    • Smoking and open fire are prohibited in the spray booth. Non workers are prohibited from entering the booth. Operators shall wear anti-static work clothes, activated carbon protective masks, protective goggles, safety boots and other protective equipment.

    • The vehicle must be cleaned before entering the spray booth. Never stick, grind or polish in the spray booth, which will pollute the spray booth.

    • During spraying operation, close the doors of the spray booth. Never enter and exit the spray booth at will. After the operation, take out all the remaining paints in the spray booth. Never heat up and bake the paints in the spray booth.

    • After spraying operation in the spray booth, clean away all sundries, such as masking paper and residual waste. Coil the compressed air supply hose and hang it on the spray gun bracket.

    • The use of short wave infrared or other thermal radiation equipment will greatly improve the drying speed of the paint film, and has the advantages of thorough drying and consistency inside and outside. Check the circuits of the thermal radiation drying equipment every year and determine whether they need to be replaced. The power circuit of the thermal radiation equipment has the problem of rapid aging.

    • Clean the spray booth once a day. Remove all sundries. Vacuum the floor, wall, grille, etc. And ensure the booth is clean and tidy.

    Reminder

    • For the maintenance and regular service of the spray booth, the main power switch of the spray booth must be cut off.

    • Clean and replace the filter cotton at the air inlet regularly according to the manufacturer's requirements, where the general service life is 60-80h.

    • Replace the top filter cotton regularly according to the manufacturer's requirements, where the general service life is 350-400h.

    • Replace the bottle filter cotton regularly according to the manufacturer's requirements, where the general service life is 80-400h.

    • Check the furnace of the spray booth regularly for carbon residue inside according to the manufacturer's requirements. Remove the carbon residue inside if necessary.

    • Check the activated carbon filter screen in the activated carbon cabinet regularly every quarter. If the surface is covered with paint, replace it in time to ensure smooth ventilation.

    • Check all wires and cables in the spray booth every six months, and replace them if they are aging.

    • Clean the whole spray booth every six months. Check the circulating air valve, air inlet and exhaust fan bearings. Check the smoke exhaust channel of the burner. Clean the sediment in the oil tank. Clean the water-based protective film in the spray booth and spray it again.

Compressed air supply system

Air compressor

  1. Key points

    • Air compressors currently in use can be divided into two types according to their modes of mechanical movement: reciprocating piston air compressor and screw air compressor.

    • The reciprocating piston air compressor makes use of the reciprocating motion of the piston to compress air. It is characterized by medium air volume, rapid decline in performance with service time, and possible entrance of oil or oil vapor into the compressed air pipeline.

    • The screw air compressor generates pressure through the high-speed movement of two uneven rotors. It is characterized by constant wind pressure and air volume, low noise, large air output, clean air, energy saving and high efficiency. It is suitable for maintenance workshops with large air consumption.

  2. Usage and daily maintenance.

    • The air compressor shall be installed in a room with good ventilation and heat dissipation, and clean air shall be sucked in. The temperature should not exceed 40 °C after normal operation.

    • The air compressor shall be installed at the position of at least 30 cm away from the wall to facilitate inspection, maintenance, and heat dissipation.

    • The air compressor shall be provided with a drainage pipeline to effectively discharge the moisture discharged from the air compressor, air storage tank and freeze dryer, so as to ensure the cleanliness of the air compressor room.

  3. Daily maintenance of the air compressor.

    • Drain the water from air storage tank, oil-water separator, and freeze dryer.

    • Check the oil level of crankcase to confirm whether it is between the maximum and minimum marks on the dipstick.

    • Clean the dust on the air compressor.

  4. Monthly maintenance of air compressor.

    • Clean the air filter element.

    • Check and add oil.

    • Check whether the air pressure gauge is normal.

    • Check whether the safety valve of air storage tank is normal.

    • Check the V-belt for tightness and make adjustment if necessary.

    • Check the air compressor accessories and air supply pipes for oil leakage and air leakage.

Supporting equipment of air compression system

  1. Key points

    • The compressed air output from the air compressor needs to enter the storage tank for temporary storage. The storage tank serves as an energy storage device that stabilizes pressure and ensures a standard supply of air.

    • Generally, the air storage tank used in the after-sales workshop is 1-2 m³ in capacity with a 1 MPa working pressure. The specific selection of the storage tank depends on the quantity of pneumatic tools and their frequency of use, that is, on the consumption of compressed air.

Freeze dryer

  1. Key points

    • The temperature of the air compressed by the air compressor is as high as 100-150 °C, and only when the temperature of the compressed air drops below the dew point can the oil and water mixed in the compressed air become oil droplets and water droplets, so that they can be filtered and discharged.

    • The specifications for the freeze dryer used in the workshop, which repairs around 400 vehicles per month, are as follows: processing capacity: 3.5-4.0 m³/min; working pressure: 1.3 Mpa.

Precision filter

  1. Key points

    • The precision filter has various grades, and the coarse filtration can generally remove dust down to 1 μm and oil down to 1X10-6. Generally, the fine filter can remove dust down to 0.01 μm and oil down to 0.1X10-6. The ultra-fine filter can remove oil down to 0.003X10-6.

    • Generally, the treatment capacity of precision filters used in maintenance shops that repair 400 vehicles per month needs to reach 3.5-4 m³/min.

Oil-water separator

  1. Key points

    • In order to ensure a high-quality spraying effect, it is necessary to ensure that the compressed air is dust-free and dry. Therefore, an oil-water separator shall be installed on the air supply pipeline before the compressed air enters the spray booth.

    • The oil and water can be separated from the compressed air by the action of drainage plates, centrifuges, expansion chambers, vibration plates, and filters inside the separator and discharged through manual or automatic drain valves to ensure an output of clean and dry air.

Compressed air supply system

  1. Key points

    • The air supply system refers to the combination of equipment and various devices and pipelines from the air compressor to the compressed air supply points at each station in the workshop. In addition to the above-mentioned equipment, it also includes air supply pipes, joints, and valves.

    • The main air supply pipe shall be arranged in a ring shape above the workshop to ensure that the pressure is equal and stable everywhere in the workshop; the pipe diameter shall be calculated and determined according to the compressed air consumption. Generally, the inner diameter shall reach 2-3 inches (1 in=25.4 mm). The main air supply pipeline shall be able to withstand a pressure of 1.2-1.6 MPa. It is recommended to use: galvanized pipe, stainless steel pipe, modified PVC pipe, or aluminium alloy pipe.

    • The main air supply pipeline should be gradually inclined towards the discharge end with a gradient of 1/100, and an automatic drain valve should be installed at the discharge end.

    • The length of the air supply pipeline shall be shortened as much as possible. The pipeline layout shall be in a straight line to reduce the use of elbows and valves, which will cause pressure loss.

    • The branch air supply pipe should branch off in an inverted U shape from the upper part of the main air supply pipe and hang down to the height required by the working station. Generally, the inner diameter of the pipe should be 1-2 inches (1 inch = 25.4 mm).

    • An oil-water separator shall be installed between the branch pipe and the rubber hose to separate oil and water again from the compressed air, so as to ensure the polishing and spraying quality. Generally, a single-stage oil-water separator can be installed in common stations for polishing and dust removal; double-stage or three-stage oil-water separators can be installed in stations for spraying.

Air supply hose

  1. Key points

    • The inner diameter of the rubber hose shall reach 8-10 mm. It is recommended to use a rubber hose with an inner diameter of 10 mm for HVLP spray guns to ensure enough compressed air supply. The length of the rubber hose shall not exceed 10 m to avoid output pressure drop.

    • Avoid stepping on or bending the hose during spraying operation. Do not allow the hose to touch the surface of the to-be-painted plate either.

Dry grinding system

Grinding equipment and tools

  1. Key points

    • In the automotive repair and coating process, polishing operations include removing old paint films that have aged or are too thick on the surface of workpieces to ensure the coating quality.

    • Grind the poly-putty to achieve the effect of shaping and trimming.

    • Grind the smooth coating surface to increase adhesion between coatings.

Dust-free dry grinding system

  1. Key points

    • The dust-free dry grinding system is composed of grinding tools, air supply and suction pipes, suction equipment, grinding pad sandpaper and auxiliary systems.

    • According to the driving mode, grinding tools can be divided into pneumatic and electric ones. Pneumatic tools are widely adopted due to their long lifespan, light weight, easy maintenance, and good safety performance.

    • According to the movement modes of grinding tools, they can be classified into unidirectional rotary grinders, orbital grinders, and eccentric vibration grinders.

    • One-way rotary grinder is also known as single-action grinder. The grinding pad makes one-way circular motion, which is mainly used to remove rust and old coatings.

    • Orbital grinder: The grinding pad reciprocates along an elliptical track, ensuring relatively uniform grinding and cutting forces in all areas. This helps prevent uneven grinding defects, making it ideal for poly-putty shaping and grinding.

    • Eccentric vibration grinder is also known as double-action grinder. The grinding pad rotates along the rotating shaft, which is an eccentric shaft, so there is a double circular movement of the grinding pad. The double-action grinder distinguishes use types based on the size of eccentricity. The larger the eccentricity, the more suitable for coarse grinding (poly-putty); the smaller the eccentricity, the more suitable for fine grinding (intermediate paint).

Drying equipment

Drying equipment

  1. Key points

    • The principle of radiation drying is to radiate heat energy directly on the heated object in the form of electromagnetic waves through thermal radiation, and dry the object by heating it with radiant heat. Infrared and far-infrared heating equipment is usually used.

    • The advantage of short-wave infrared ray is that the infrared radiation can penetrate through the paint film surface to the metal surface and then reflect out, so that the bottom layer of the paint absorbs energy first for heating up, and the paint film can be dried from inside to outside.

  2. Operation points

    • The distance between the radiation source and the heated surface, specifically the one between the heating lamp and the workpiece surface usually needs to be greater than 50 cm. Being too close can cause the temperature to rise too quickly, resulting in phenomena such as poly-putty blistering, pinholes, and miliaria-like surface on the plates.

    • Different reflectance and absorption colors of the irradiated surface will lead to different infrared absorption rates, resulting in different drying speeds. For example, dark colors dry faster than light colors.

Blow dryer

  1. Key points

    • It is used to dry the paint surface after the application of water-based paint.

    • By utilizing the Venturi effect, in addition to supplying compressed air to the system, the air blower also draws in a large amount of surrounding air and blows out a large quantity of air, which can rapidly and effectively dry the water-based paint.

    Reminder

    • Generally, the air consumption is 270 L/min, and the airflow of the blower is about 3000 L/min.

    • The blowing direction of the blower must be consistent with that of the spray booth.

    • Blowing distance: 30-80cm.

Blower bracket

  1. Key points

    • The movable blower system is suitable for places such as spray booths and workshop stations. It can directly point to the workpiece, turn on the air source, and complete the drying of water-based paint independently.

    Reminder

    • Appropriate air flow rate blowing to the plate will take away more moisture in the paint film, ensure that the inner layer of water-based paint is dried more evenly and avoid too dry paint film surface.

    • The blowing direction of the blower must be consistent with that of the spray booth. Place the blower above the top of most of the plates.

Spraying equipment

Spray gun

  1. Key points

    • It is used to apply paint. It is an important tool for coating repair and a precisely designed and manufactured device. The atomization effect of the spray gun has a significant impact on the final result of painting and spraying repair.

    • There are many types and specifications of spray guns, so the spray gun for the spraying operation shall be selected wisely according to the paint type and spraying process.

  2. Operation points

    • An appropriate type of spray gun shall be selected according to the type of paint: primer spray guns, water-based or solvent-based spray guns, and topcoat spray guns.

Gun tail gauge

  1. Key points

    • It is used to adjust the air supply pressure of spray guns. Reasonable spraying pressure during the spraying operation is an important condition for the atomization of the spray gun. A pressure gauge can be installed at the tail of the spray gun to measure and monitor the stability of air pressure.

Auxiliary tools and materials

Tack cloth

  1. Key points

    • It is used to remove foreign matters or dust from the sprayed surface.

  2. Operation points

    • Tack cloth is a kind of gauze immersed in sticky remover.

    Reminder

    • To use a piece of new tack cloth, spread it flat, and then fold it again to make it soft.

Measuring cup

  1. Key points

    • It is used to mix paint. Before mixing the paint, a paint mixing cup with appropriate volume shall be selected. Metal containers shall be avoided for water-based paint, and plastic containers or paint mixing cups with internal coating shall be selected when the paint is mixed or stored.

    Reminder

    • Disposable plastic cups are usually used.

Paint mixing ruler

  1. Key points

    • It is used to mix paint. An appropriate paint mixing ruler should be selected according to the amount of paint to be mixed. There are different scales on the side surface of the paint mixing ruler, and the paint can be mixed according to the volume ratio.

Electronic scale

  1. Key points

    • It is used for mixing and metering of paint. The electronic scale shall be calibrated regularly to ensure accuracy. Attention shall be paid to avoid overload during use. Frequent overload and overweight will reduce the accuracy of the electronic scale.

    • The electronic scale should be placed horizontally on the paint mixing table. When using it, avoid any air movement in the vicinity, otherwise it may lead to inaccurate measurements.

Filter funnel

  1. Key points

    • Filter the paint and remove impurities from the paint when pouring the paint into the spray gun.

    Reminder

    • The paper funnel has a variety of meshes. Select the appropriate mesh size according to the paint type.

    • For water-based paints, a filter funnel of about 125㎛ is recommended.

Air blowing gun

  1. Key points

    • Remove dirt and dust by air pressure.

Spare part bracket

  1. Key points

    • It is used for spraying the removed or newly replaced parts. The supporting point of the spare part bracket can be adjusted, which is convenient for spraying and polishing plates.
