Body measurement and correction

There are mainly two types of tools for body calibration and measurement: conventional measuring tools (such as tape and body dimension gauge) and special calibration and measurement equipment (such as mold-type measuring equipment and electronic measuring equipment).

Measuring tools and equipment

  1. Measuring tape

    1. Key points

      • The tape is a simple measuring tool, which is small in size and portable. It is mainly suitable for measurement of the straight lines and plane of the body.

  2. Ruler

    1. Key points

      • It is mainly used for restoring the edge line, and also for comparison with undeformed plates.

      • For comparison with the undeformed reference plate, do not scratch the painted surface.

  3. Universal measuring ruler

    1. Key points

      • It is mainly used to measure the body curve and the distance between two points with obstacles.

      • Before use, check the dimensions and handle with care.

  4. Electronic measurement

    1. Key points

      • It is easy and simple to operate. Bluetooth and other functions are available for output and recording of the measured dimensions in time, so as to accurately judge the change of data.

      • It can measure multiple three-dimensional size data between the upper, lower and components of the body.

      • It has a large database to store the body data of various models, and accurate measured data, with very small error.

Measurement methods

  1. Comparison

    1. Key points

      • The comparison is to first measure the dimension of the undamaged side and then the dimension of the damaged side, and compare the two dimensions to determine the drawing amplitude for maintenance.

  2. Bilateral symmetry

    1. Key points

      • The bilateral symmetry is to measure the two diagonals of the body frame structure and compare whether they are the same, so as to judge whether the body is deformed.

  3. Body centerline method

    1. Key points

      • The body centerline method is to hang an appropriate number of body rulers at the correct position on the underbody, and judge whether the body is deformed by observing whether the center points of all body rulers are in the same straight line.

Calibration tools and equipment

If the outer plate and inner structure are deformed due to severe impact of the vehicle, it is necessary to use tools and equipment for body correction and measurement to draw, correct, measure, cut, joint or replace the affected vehicle so as to restore to its original appearance and quality.

  1. Lift-type correction bench

    1. Key points

      • It is easy and simple to operate, usually in conjunction with an electronic measuring system.

      • Bluetooth is available for output and recording of the measured dimensions in time, so as to accurately judge the change of data.

      • It can measure multiple three-dimensional size data between the upper, lower and components of the body.

      • It has a large database to store the body data of various models, and accurate measured data, with very small error.

  2. Removable correction bench

    1. Key points

      • It can be moved as required to maintain the vehicle.

      • It is convenient for correction and maintenance of the body side damage.

  3. Small damage repair and correction bench

    1. Key points

      • It can also be called the ground trigram bench, which are buried below the ground and have a surface flush with the ground.

      • With the movable pulling tower, it is convenient and quick to repair the affected vehicle with small damage.

  4. Platform-type correction bench

    1. Key points

      • It is convenient and quick to install the underbody fixtures in multiple positions at the same time.

  5. Ground-cage correction bench

    1. Key points

      • It is available for all-round correction and drawing for the affected vehicle.

  6. Pulling tower

    1. Key points

      • It is fixed on the side of the correction bench.

      • It can draw the body with damage and deformation at different positions and angles.

      • Please refer to the equipment instructions before use, and get familiar with installation requirements, operation methods and maintenance methods.

  7. Body fixtures

    1. Key points

      • There are usually four fixtures.

      • They are used to fix the bottom of the middle section of the body, providing a fixed support for drawing correction.

      • Check the clearance between the body and fixture at any time during drawing correction.

      • In case of any abnormality, reinstall and fix it and tighten the bolts.

  8. Body chassis fixing bracket

    1. Key points

      • It is used in conjunction with fixtures.

      • It is used for the body fixing and height adjustment.

      • It can adjust the positions of front, rear, left and right supports for flexible use.

  9. Drawing fixture

    1. Key points

      • It is necessary for drawing correction.

      • According to different damages and positions, select the correct fixture before operation and use correctly.

  10. Edge-clamped drawing fixture

    1. Key points

      • It is mainly used for fixing at the edge of body.

  11. Flat drawing fixture

    1. Key points

      • It is mainly used for fixing the barrier-free edge of the body.

  12. Hook-type drawing fixture

    1. Key points

      • It is mainly used for fixing the edge of body.

  13. Two-way drawing fixture

    1. Key points

      • It is mainly used to install two drawing chains and draw at two angles simultaneously.

  14. Safety cable

    1. Key points

      • It must be installed before drawing correction to prevent potential safety hazards caused by fixture slipping and chain breakage during drawing.

Correction methods

Body correction principle

  1. Vector drawing

    • In terms of the direction and angle of multiple forces to be combined or divided, the vector can be divided into resultant force and component force.

    • As shown in the figure, the effect of drawing F1 and F2 simultaneously is the same as that of drawing F3 alone.

    • In some special positions, the vector drawing of force decomposition principle must be adopted.

  2. Multi-point drawing

    • Reverse single-point drawing may be used for slight deformation.

    • Serious or large-scale deformation of the body in a collision can be corrected by multi-point reverse drawing to avoid rupture and uneven tension.

    • In case of multi-point drawing, the fixtures do not need to clamp in one position. Multiple points with severe deformation can be selected to secure the fixtures, and multiple pulling towers or steel chains can be used together.

  3. Drawing angle

    • The angle between the chain and the hydraulic cylinder is 90° generally, exceeding which uneven stress will occur, resulting in damage to the chain.

  4. No auxiliary support for drawing

    • Before drawing correction, it is necessary to confirm whether auxiliary support is required. Otherwise, secondary damage will be caused.

  5. Use of auxiliary support for drawing

    • Proper use of auxiliary support will not cause secondary damage, but improves the drawing efficiency.

  6. No auxiliary fixation for drawing

    • Before drawing correction, it is necessary to confirm whether auxiliary fixation is required. Otherwise, secondary damage will be caused.

  7. Use of auxiliary fixation for drawing

    • Proper use of auxiliary fixing support will not cause secondary damage, but improves the drawing efficiency.

  8. Fixture installation and drawing angle

    • Before drawing correction, install the fixture correctly and pay attention to the drawing angle to avoid the fixture from slipping.

  9. Correct drawing point

    • Before drawing, select the correct drawing points.

  10. Damage and drawing analysis

    • Before drawing correction, analyze the damaged workpiece and adopt the correct drawing methods.

  11. Drawing technique of front longitudinal beam

    • In order to improve the drawing efficiency, auxiliary drawing is required for such deformation.

    • Carry out drawing in both directions to improve efficiency.

  12. Sill panel drawing technique

    • If the sill panel is subject to large-area deformation and damage, it can be drawn by inserting a round stick.

  13. Rear fender drawing technique

    • In order to improve the drawing efficiency, before removal, the rear fender with large-area damage can be drawn by welding auxiliary drawing points.

Body correction process

  1. Check the body data chart before correction.

    • Check the body data according to the model.

    • Use a measuring tool to measure the deformation of damaged area in the body.

  2. Install the drawing equipment.

    • Select appropriate drawing correction equipment.

    • Install the pulling tower in place and adjust the drawing angle.

  3. Check the body fixture.

    • Confirm whether the body fixture is fastened before drawing correction.

  4. Install the drawing tools.

    • Select the appropriate drawing point, and then install the drawing tools.

  5. Install the safety cable.

    • Before vehicle correction, install the safety cable to prevent personal injury or other damages due to breakage and falling of drawing tools.

    • Connect one end of the safety cable to the steel chain close to the fixture, and the other end to the body position to be corrected.

    • Do not knock the steel chain during drawing to prevent breakage.

  6. Install the air source joint.

    • Before drawing correction, ensure that the air source pressure is sufficient and there is no air leakage at the joint.

  7. Safe positions for operators

    • During drawing, for sake of the safety, the operators must stand on the side of the drawing direction during operation and pay attention to the safety of surrounding vehicles and personnel.

  8. Drawing angle

    • Pay attention to keeping the chain at a drawing angle of 90° with the drawn part during drawing.

  9. Measure after drawing.

    • During or after drawing, it is necessary to confirm the amount of this drawing so as to be aware of the amount required for the next drawing.

  10. Check the change of the vehicle during drawing.

    • During drawing correction and maintenance, check the overall change of the body at any time to judge the drawing effect and prevent secondary damage to the vehicle caused by excessive stretching.

    • During drawing, pay attention to the change of gaps between doors, between the door and front fender and other components.

    • Do not draw excessively. Measure and draw many times to reach the final state.

  11. Eliminate the stress after drawing.

    • During drawing correction and maintenance, the plate in the corrected area will generate a certain internal stress. In this case, it is necessary to knock the corresponding parts of the plate to eliminate the internal stress.

  12. Measure after secondary drawing.

    • After drawing, it is necessary to measure the relevant dimensions of the corrected and drawn area, check the changes of relevant data, and measure whether the values at each point reach the ideal value.

  13. Correct the value after drawing.

    • Carry out accurate drawing correction until the data of damaged area is close to the original data of body.

    • Error is ≤ ±2 mm.
