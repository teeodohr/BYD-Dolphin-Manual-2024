Anti-rust treatment

  1. Key points

    • After painting, spray anti-rust wax and granular paint in inner cavity and bond damping rubber plates before component installation.

  2. Operation steps

    • Cover the applied part.

    • Apply wax or epoxy primer to the cavity.

    • Apply sealant after drying.

  3. Remarks

    • Bare metal areas must be painted with epoxy primer.
