Replacement of rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam

Removal

  1. Remove the front section assembly of front right longitudinal beam. Refer to

  2. Use a sander to grind the sealant on the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam and related accessories.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Cut with disc sander, etc.

  3. Remove 35 welding spots and take out the right connecting plate of lower cross beam of front wall panel.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  4. Remove 19 welding spots and take out the front right connecting plate of the lower longitudinal beam on the front floor.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  5. Remove 62 welding spots and take out the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

Installation

  1. Clean the joint of rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam and apply adhesive.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  2. Fix the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam to the installation position.

  3. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam and the front compartment parts. Refer to 

  4. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam and the underbody parts. Refer toUnderbody 

  5. Weld 62 welding spots on the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  6. Clean the joint of relevant accessories of the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam and apply adhesive.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  7. Fix the front right connecting plate of lower longitudinal beam on the front floor to the installation position.

  8. Weld 19 welding spots on the front right connecting plate of lower longitudinal beam on the front floor.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  9. Fix the right connecting plate of the lower cross beam of front wall panel to the installation position.

  10. Weld 35 welding spots on the right connecting plate of the lower cross beam of front wall panel.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  11. Apply sealant to the rear section assembly of front right longitudinal beam and related accessories.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Body sealant

  12. After applying the external coating, apply rust inhibitor to the inner plate part of the welding spots of the closed section structure.

  13. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
