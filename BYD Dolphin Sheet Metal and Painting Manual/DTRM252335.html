Anti-corrosion treatment

Anti-corrosion materials

Purpose of anti-corrosion treatment after steel plate maintenance: Galvanized steel plates are used by the OEM and phosphated to prevent rust. During the maintenance of vehicle steel plates, their anti-rust function is damaged after high-temperature operations such as welding and grinding. Therefore, anti-corrosion treatment must be carried out after maintenance to prevent the corrosion of steel plates and the decrease in durability.

Anti-rust primer

  1. Function

    • The anti-rust primer can prevent panels from rusting, and shall be sprayed on joints and ground areas.

  2. How to use

    • Apply anti-rust primer after sheet metal repair.

    • Remove water, grease, and other affected substances.

    • Spray or brush on exposed metal panels.

    Caution
    Remove moisture and stains from the surface before application.

Sealant

  1. Function

    • The sealant has good sealing and waterproofing properties.

  2. Key points

    • The sealant has a short hardening time and can be delivered to the next process more quickly.

  3. How to use

    • Remove moisture and stains from the panel surface.

    • Apply anti-rust primer.

    • After the anti-rust primer is dried, apply the sealant with the sealant gun.

    • After the repair operation, sealant must be reapplied to the damaged positions of the OEM sealing.

    Caution
    The sealant must be applied after the anti-corrosion paint on the panels has dried.

Chassis sealant

  1. Function

    • It can prevent the paint surface from being damaged and rusted by flying stones on the road and has the effects of sound insulation and vibration absorption.

  2. Key points

    • The chassis sealant still has a certain degree of softness after drying.

    • It is mainly used at the bottom of door sill or at the base plate.

  3. How to use

    • Remove moisture and stains from the panel surface.

    • Wait until the surface of the panel is completely dry.

    • Use a special chassis adhesive gun to spray the chassis adhesive.

Caution
The chassis glue can only be used on the lower part of the sill plate and the bottom plate, and cannot be sprayed on other positions.

Special cleaning degreaser

  1. Function

    • Oil stains on the panel surface can be removed.

  2. Key points

    • Anti-corrosion cannot be guaranteed without the use of a degreaser.

    • Before carrying out most anti-corrosion work, it is necessary to clean the panel surface with a degreaser.

Caution
Degreasing agent is generally an organic solvent liquid, and personal protection must be done when using it.

Cavity anti-corrosive wax

  1. Function

    • Corrosion and rust prevention treatment shall be carried out for inaccessible positions.

    • It is used for spraying wax oil based internal rust inhibitor.

    • It is used for the anti-corrosion treatment of the inner cavities of door pillars and door sills.

    • It is used for other damaged areas of sheet metal repair (where painting cannot be carried out inside).

  2. Key points

    • Provide rust preventive treatment to all surfaces including inaccessible locations as per process specification.

Caution
Failure to take cavity anti-corrosion measures may result in steel plate corrosion, rust and other quality problems.

Anti-corrosion tools

Spray gun

  1. Function

    • It is used to spray anti-corrosive paint.

  2. Key points

    • It is suitable for large-area spraying operations.

    • The spraying effect is relatively uniform.

Glue gun

  1. Function

    • As a special tool for applying cylindrical glue, it is used to apply various sheet metal sealants.

Caution
The glue gun is divided into pneumatic, manual and electric type. If you use a pneumatic or electric glue gun, the pressure should not be too large, and the pressure should be gradually adjusted in order from small to large.

Cavity anti-corrosive spray gun

  1. Function

    • Corrosion and rust prevention treatment shall be carried out for inaccessible positions.

    • It is used for spraying wax oil based internal rust inhibitor.

    • It is used for the anti-corrosion treatment of the inner cavities of door pillars and door sills.

    • It is used for other damaged areas of sheet metal repair (where painting cannot be carried out inside).

  2. Key points

    • Provide rust preventive treatment to all surfaces including inaccessible locations as per process specification.

    • If the cavity anti-corrosion measures are not strictly carried out, quality problems such as steel plate corrosion and rusting may occur after maintenance.

    • In inaccessible cavities inside the components, a spray gun with a hose is required to apply cavity anti-corrosive wax.

Caution
The spray gun with the hose has a large spraying angle, and only needs to change the angle to meet the surface coverage of the inner cavity in almost all positions.

Brush

  1. Function

    • Liquid primer and sealant can be applied by brush.

    Caution
    Soak and wash after using.
