Replacement of lower cross beam assembly of rear floor

Removal

  1. Remove the rear floor panel assembly. Refer to

  2. Remove 12 welding spots of the lower cross beam assembly of rear floor, and take out the lower cross beam assembly of rear floor.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

Installation

  1. Fix the lower cross beam assembly of rear floor.

  2. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the lower cross beam assembly of rear floor and underbody parts. Refer to

  3. Weld 12 welding spots on the lower cross beam assembly of rear floor.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the outer side)

  4. Apply the body sealant and internal coating at corresponding positions.

  5. After applying the external coating, apply rust inhibitor to the inner plate part of the welding spots of the closed section structure.

  6. Install the rear floor panel assembly. Refer to
