Cutting principles of plates

Principle of cutting position

  1. Standard cutting plan.

    • The cutting position shall be set as possible at the area without reinforcement on the inner side.

    • There should be no stress concentration area (fold design) at the cutting position.

    • The welding bonding area at the cutting position is small, which is convenient for spraying operation

    • Select a position with few spare parts installed, or a position with installed spare parts that covers the welding joint area.

    • The front longitudinal beam must be replaced in the form of an assembly.

  2. Reasons for non-cutting.

    • If there is a reinforced structural part inside the plate being cut, it will not only affect the normal cutting stroke of the cutting tool, but also have the risk of damaging the inner reinforced structural part during cutting.

    • After the cutting and welding in the fold design area are completed, when a frontal collision occurs again at this part of the vehicle, the fold design area will not collapse or its collapse function will decrease, which will affect the absorption of impact energy and thus reduce the safety performance of the body.

    • The cutting and welding position shall be selected in a small area so that the thermal deformation of plates is small due to the small heat generated by welding, which is conducive to improving the efficiency of spraying process.

    • The cutting and welding area shall be selected at a position covered by the exterior spare parts to reduce the time for fine treatment after welding at this position and improve the operation efficiency (such as the installation positions of rear fender quarter window and rear trim strip of quarter window of Song model).

    • The front longitudinal beam of the integral body not only ensures the torsional rigidity of the frame and bears longitudinal load, but also supports the main components of the vehicle. Therefore, it must be replaced in the form of an assembly provided as a spare part in case of bending deformation after a collision. And cutting replacement is not allowed.
