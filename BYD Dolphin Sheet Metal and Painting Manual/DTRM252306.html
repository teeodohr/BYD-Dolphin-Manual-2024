Quality inspection standard for painting maintenance

  1. Flatness

    • The vehicle surface is smooth, without unevenness when viewed from the front. The vehicle is free of unevenness when viewed from any direction outdoors.

    • The lines are straight and smooth. Waves and unevenness are not allowed.

  2. Paint quality

    • The paint surface should be smooth without defects such as flow marks, fish eyes or blistering.

    • No defects such as dust spots and pinholes should be observed at a distance of 1 m.

  3. Color quality

    • If the whole vehicle is refurbished, its color must be consistent without any color difference.

    • The color must be the same as that of the adjacent parts for paint touch-up, and the color must be consistent when observed within 1 m and be free of obvious color difference when observed from a long distance and multiple directions.

  4. Paint film brightness

    • The paint surface should be glossy enough, with uniform reflections. Misty surface or uneven texture of paint film is not allowed.

    • Except for special requirements, obvious orange peel texture is not allowed on the ordinary paint surface.

  5. Primer quality

    • The particle size, density and reflectivity of metallic paint must be the same as those of the original paint surface.

    • Sandpaper marks are not allowed on the primer.

  6. Painting quality of inner side of the plate

    • Before assembling the sheet metal, it is required to observe the inner surface. The paint must be consistent with that of the original vehicle.

    • The uncovered surfaces in the interior and boxes shall be painted the same as the outer surfaces.

    • The covered surfaces in the interior or boxes shall be puttied and polished. The paint requirement may be slightly lower than that of the outer surface.

    • The tire cover, wiper arm, water spray nozzle and door handle of the refurbished vehicle shall be painted, and the interior and exterior of chassis shall be painted.

  7. Color bar quality

    • The color bar shall be properly pasted with a deviation not exceeding 1 mm.

    • The color bar lines shall be straight and free of waves.

    • In the color bar, obvious bubbles are not allowed, with a diameter of no more than 1 mm, and no more than 2 bubbles in each color bar.

  8. Protection of non-processed surfaces

    • Sandpaper marks are not allowed in the electroplated and glass surfaces.

    • Defects such as abrasion and paint mist are not allowed in non-painted surfaces.

    • The exhaust pipe, trim panel and rubber strip shall not be sprayed with paint.

  9. Cleaning quality

    • Before the delivery of sheet metal assembly, the silt and water inside the vehicle must be cleaned up.

    • During process inspection, the painted surface must be clean and free of dust, water droplets, residual wax, etc.

    • During the completion inspection, the whole vehicle must be cleaned inside and outside.
