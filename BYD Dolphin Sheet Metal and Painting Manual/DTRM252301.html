Polishing method

Overview of polishing

Purpose of polishing

  1. Key points

    • After painting, there may be some defects on the new coating surface, such as sagging, dust spots, orange peel, gloss loss, and poor film fullness, as well as paint dust falling on the old paint surface during local spraying and transition areas at the junction of new and old paint films. Such defects can be removed by polishing.

  2. Purpose

    • Polishing is carried out mainly to increase the glossiness and smoothness of paint films, and eliminate fine defects on film surfaces such as particles, slight flow marks, orange peel, fine sandpaper marks, and scratches. Polishing is applicable to both the renovation of old paint surfaces and the defect removal of repainted paint surfaces.

Function of polishing

  1. Key points

    • The gloss of a paint coating is generated by the reflection of light, so it is closely related to the roughness of the coating surface.

    • If the coating surface is rough and uneven, light will diffuse and reflect on the surface, resulting in the appearance of numerous small scratches and a lack of gloss.

    • Polishing will make the surface roughness uniform, reduce the diffuse reflection on the surface and produce uniform brightness on the coating surface.

  2. Function

    • Removal of particles and sagging: Particles and sagging on the sprayed surface can be removed by polishing.

    • Adjustment of texture and glossiness of top coat: By polishing, the texture and glossiness can be adjusted to match the original surface.

    • Polishing of transition area: By polishing, the rough surface of the transition area can be trimmed to match the texture and glossiness of the surrounding surface.

Key points of polishing

  1. Key points

    • Pressure, particle size and speed are three key factors affecting polishing, so it is very important to balance all these factors in polishing operation.

    Reminder

    • The "friction heat" and "static electricity" generated by friction of the polishing pad on the film are also factors that adversely affect the polishing.

    • Quality requirement: make the textures and gloss of the re-coated surface consistent with those of the original surface.

    • Operation environment: the work place in the workshop shall be well lighted for convenience of checking the paint film. Besides, a clean operation environment is required for avoid surface damage caused by the dust.

About coating drying

  1. Key points

    • Drying is to fully cure the coating surface for polishing. Both temperature and coating thickness affect the drying duration of the coating.

    • Classification of coating drying stages: ready-to-touch; ready-to-polish, completely dry.

    • Ready-to-touch: The coating is not tacky when pressed with fingers.

    • Ready-to-polish: Polishing can be performed with polishing wax.

    • Completely dry: The solvent is completely volatilized, and the coating has been cross-linked.

Drying methods

  1. Key points

    • Methods fall under natural and forced drying.

    • Natural drying: It refers to the process that plates are placed after painting in the workshop to allow the painting to cure naturally. The drying duration varies with the surrounding environment and temperature.

    • Forced drying: It refers to the process in which auxiliary drying equipment is used to speed up the drying. To avoid pinholes, sufficient curing time should be reserved before drying.

Common drying methods

  1. Key points

    • Common drying methods: drying through hot air circulation with a heat exchanger and drying by heat radiation emitted by a heated lamp tube.

    • Method 1: It is safe for use since the air containing volatile solvent is not in direct contact with the heat source, but the heating efficiency is low and the equipment is large.

    • Method 2: The short-wave infrared heating lamp is fast in heating and convenient for use. However, the heating range is affected by the radiation range of the lamp.

Introduction to polishing equipment and materials

Grinding tools

  1. Key points

    • Common grinding tools: dual-action grinding head, rubber grinding block, and wet grinding pad. These tools need to be selected based on the operation process.

  2. Operation steps

    • During operation, use grinding pads or dual-action grinders together with sandpaper to remove particles and sagging.

    • When grinding with rubber grinding block and sandpaper, move in as small circular motions as possible to grind the defect.

    • Use a small amount of water when grinding.

Polishing machine

  1. Key points

    • A polishing machine is a device used to trim and process the paint film surface using polishing pads. It can be classified based on power source into electric polishing machine and pneumatic polishing machine.

    Caution

    • When operating, hold the polisher firmly with both hands while keeping the cord or air hose behind you over your shoulders to prevent the cord or air hose from tangling.

    • During the polishing operation, completely press the polishing pad on the surface of the workpiece before starting the polisher (otherwise, the polisher will contact the surface of the workpiece after rotating at a high speed, which will easily scratch the paint film). Start rotating the polisher at a low speed, let the polishing agent adhere to the entire surface of the polishing wheel, and adjust the speed appropriately.

    Reminder

    • The driving torque of the electric polisher is large, which can ensure the rotation stability under load. The advantage of an electric polisher is that it is not affected by insufficiency or absence of air supply.

    • Pneumatic polishers are relatively light and have a longer lifespan, and are relatively safer than models with the electrical power as the power. Ensure stable air source supply when using the pneumatic polisher.

Polishing pad

  1. Key points

    • Polishing pads can be classified based on the material into wool and sponge ones. The former features strong grinding and cutting force and is generally used for rough polishing. The latter is generally used for fine polishing, and its grinding and cutting force varies with the material.

    • Dark polishing pads are generally used for fine polishing.

    • Light-colored polishing pads are generally used for rough polishing.

    Caution

    • Never directly coat the polishing agent on the body panels for polishing. The correct way to use it is to spread the polishing paste evenly on the polishing pad before polishing.

Basic method of moving polishing wheel

  1. Key points

    • The polishing is to "uniformly" grind the paint film. Therefore, it is necessary to "keep the force on the polishing wheel constant" during operation.

    • Pressure: In principle, the pressure is provided by the weight of the polishing machine itself at the polished surface. The pressure applied to the side polished surface should also be the same as that on the horizontal surface, and the force can be judged according to the deformation of the polishing wheel.

    • Grinding range: Due to the complex and varied shapes of the vehicle body, the range cannot be generalized. The range where a constant pressure can be maintained is about equal to the shoulder width of a person.

    • Moving speed: To maintain a constant pressure throughout the polishing, a relatively slow moving speed should be maintained. Too low speed may result in "sintering of the polishing wheel" under heating while too high speed may lead to unstable cutting and "uneven polishing".

    • The pressure distribution varies with the angle of the polishing pad. A constant pressure can be applied by maximizing the contact between the polishing wheel and the plate, depending on the plate shape.

    Reminder

    • The polishing wheels vary in shapes and sizes based the type of material, and are generally round. Use the polishing wheel in a rational manner according to the structural characteristics of the polishing pad.

    • The state with the polishing wheel pressed in parallel on the plate and that with the polishing wheel include for a certain angle.

    • When the polisher is pressed on the plate in parallel, the polishing pad can press and polish the surface of the paint film substantially uniformly within the range of the tray.

    • When pressing on the plate at a large angle, the contact area between the polishing wheel and the steel plate becomes smaller, which is easy to make the local temperature of the plate coating too high.

Cleaning of polishing wheel

  1. Key points

    • During polishing, some powder of the polishing agent and stains will adhere to the inside of the polishing wheel. If the operation continues, the paint film will be scratched.

  2. Requirements for cleaning

    • Blowing: blow once every 5-6 times of polishing agent replenishment.

    • Cleaning: brush once every 2-3 times of blowing cleaning.

    Caution

    • Wash with a relatively hard scrubbing brush or toothbrush. Hard objects such as metal brushes can peel off wool fibers or cause scratches on the surface of the sponge wheel, resulting in efficiency reduction and impact on polishing quality.

Polishing wax

  1. Key points

    • It is used to remove particles, polish the surface in case of paint sagging, and adjust the texture of the paint film.

    • Main components: friction particles, solvents, moisture and additives.

    • Classification of polishing wax: rough wax, medium wax, fine wax, mirror wax, handmade wax, etc.

    Caution

    • Only use a little polishing paste for polishing.

    • Pay close attention to the surface temperature of plate during polishing.

Application method of polishing wax

  1. Key points

    • In polishing operation, the particle of polishing agent gradually becomes smaller and circular as polishing proceeds. During the operation, the moisture in the polishing agent begins to lose, the mist on the coating surface starts to disappear, and the coating becomes glossy.

    • To proficiently use a polishing agent, it is necessary to fully understand its characteristics.

  2. Operation steps

    • Dosage of polishing wax: The dosage should be proportional to the polished area. Overdosage can only make the polished surface more slippery without additional polishing and grinding effects, but prolongs the operation time. On the contrary, underdosage will cause the agent to dry quickly, resulting in insufficient polishing.

    • Timing for replenishment of polishing agent: During polishing, it is required to spread the polishing agent on the coating surface into a thin layer. After the polishing agent is completely dried, it becomes powdery and loses its polishing and grinding force. Therefore, it is necessary to replenish an appropriate amount of polishing agent before it becomes powdery. It is not appropriate to replenish any polishing agent if it still contains sufficient solvent and moisture. Otherwise, the grinding performance of the polishing wax will be affected.

Sandpaper

  1. Key points

    • It is used to remove particles and paint sagging on the surface.

    • It is used to adjust the texture and rectify the defects of paint film surface.

    • It is used to grind the dust spots.

    • It can be used for the whole plate grinding to adjust the texture and deal with paint mist.

    • Generally, sandpaper #P1500-2000 is selected.

    Caution

    • Evenly grind the paint film with water and sand paper during surface grinding.

Fine fiber cloth

  1. Key points

    • It is used to clean the plate surface. The fine fiber cloth is soft and does not leave scratches when used to wipe the surface.

    • It is used for manual polishing in narrow areas unaccessible to a polishing machine.

    Caution

    • Never use strong cloth (e.g. towel) for wiping the plate since it will form scratches on the paint surface.

Light source for polishing effect inspection

  1. Key points

    • It is used for polishing effect inspection.

    Caution

    • Make an inspection with a suitable fluorescent light source (above 1500 lux) at a proper angle from the incident light (about 25° angle of view).

    • Alternatively, check the washed paint film surface under the sunlight.

Polishing technology

Evaluation of coating surface

Reminder

  • If the texture on the painted surface differ from those of the original surface after painting and paint film drying, remove the defects of bulge, fish eye, sagging and others on the paint film surface by grinding or cutting and polishing, in order to obtain surface similar to the original coating.

Paint film drying before polishing

  1. Key points

    • The spray-painted surface should be dried through natural or forced drying.

    • The drying temperature and allowable polishing time should be determined according to the parameters of the supplier for the drying time.

    Reminder

    • “Natural drying” means drying of paint under the ambient temperature.

    • “Forced drying” means a process with a equipment for heating in order to accelerate drying and curing.

Evaluation of surface dryness

  1. Key points

    • Press the coating with fingers to determine if the surface is sufficiently dry for polishing.

    • Forced drying is required if the surface is not sufficiently dry.

    Reminder

    • Select a zone that will not influence the overall maintenance quality.

    • Exert sufficient force on the surface till your nail tip of the finger on the surface goes white.

Evaluation of spray-painted surface

  1. Key points

    • Prior to polishing, check the coating for particles or paint sagging and texture similar to adjacent original surfaces.

    • Ensure all defects are marked.

    • Determine whether defects can be rectified or repainting is required.

    Caution

    • Select a well-lighted place to check the surface with the fluorescent light.

    • Re-paint the surface in case of failure in trimming the dent at the fish eye bottom of the coating.

Rectify defects on coating surface - particles

  1. Key points

    • Check the paint film surface for particles. If any, determine an appropriate grinding method.

  2. Operation steps

    • Grind the dust spots with a rubber grinding block complete with sandpaper #1500-2000.

    Caution

    • Spray water on the sand paper as appropriate in order to avoid any scratches left on the paint film surface by the cushion block.

    • Hold the lower part of the cushion block for a better stability and reduction of damages to the paint film surface.

Rectify defects on coating surface - sagging

  1. Key points

    • Remove the sagging marks with a polishing point scraper.

    • After removing the obvious sagging, use sandpaper for fine grinding.

  2. Operation steps

    • Once the surface is roughly leveled with the scraper, completely remove the sagging marks with sandpaper #1000-1500.

    Caution

    • Check whether the paint has been thoroughly dried before blade coating or not. If not, the whole coating may be wiped off.

Adjust the texture of paint film

  1. Key points

    • After drying, the surface texture is slightly rougher than that of the original paint film. In this case, grind the surface with a wet grinding pad or a dual-action grinder equipped with sandpaper #1500-2000.

  2. Operation steps

    • Wipe off the dirt and dust on the steel plate with clean wiping paper.

    • To prevent scratches during manual grinding, keep the grinding block at an angle of 45° to the grinding direction.

    • To prevent sandpaper from being blocked, use water for grinding.

    Caution

    • It is unnecessary to adjust the texture if there is no visible difference between textures of the re-coated paint film and the neighboring plates.

Polishing technology and application

Reminder

  • Polishing refers to trimming of the work piece surface with flexible polishing material and abrasive particles, or other polishing media. Instead of improving the dimensional accuracy or geometric shape accuracy of the work piece, polishing aims to obtain a smooth surface or specular gloss.

Masking before polishing

  1. Key points

    • To prevent accidents and damage to adjacent workpieces or other body components during grinding and polishing, trim parts should be masked from being damaged by the polishing pad.

    • The edge with thin paint film should not be excessively polished.

    Caution

    • To prevent inhalation of fine dust and particles, wear anti-dust mask, protective glasses and safety boots during polishing.

    Reminder

    • Make full use of the masking of topcoat painting. That is to say, reserve the usable masking after painting of topcoat for polishing.

Selection of polishing pad

  1. Key points

    • Select the polishing pad based on the polishing range of the plate and install it in the center of the polishing machine to prevent the machine from shaking.

    • Before use, clean the polishing pad to remove all dirt.

    Caution

    • Excessive dried paste or dirts on the polishing pad can cause scratches in paint film polishing.

    • Spot griding is used because the body surface is curved and the defective area is usually small. The combination of small polisher and polishing pad is efficient and cost effective.

Contact position of polishing pad

  1. Key points

    • When polishing, do not make the whole polishing pad contact the surface. During operation, slightly tilt the polishing machine so that the polishing pad lightly contacts the surface.

    Caution

    • Make the polisher contact with the surface before switching it to avoid deep scratches.

    Reminder

    • After use of the polisher, place it upward to prevent deformation and adhesion of dirts.

Movement range for polishing

  1. Key points

    • Move the polishing machine so that its movement path overlaps 2/3 of the previous path.

    • When polishing a large area, polish the area in sections (area of each section: about 30 cm×30 mm).

    • To avoid deformation of the steel plate or condensation of polishing wax, spray water on the steel plate during operation.

    Caution

    • Polishing at the same zone for an extended period will result in heat generation, steel plate deformation or polishing paste coagulation.

    • In case of excessive polishing paste coagulated on the polishing pad, do not continue polishing. Otherwise, the film surface will be damaged.

    Reminder

    • If the polishing paste is coagulated and adheres on the plate surface, wipe it off using wipe paper with water or polishing paste.

Rough polishing

  1. Key points

    • Rough polishing: remove sandpaper marks generated during texture adjustment or removal of particles and paint sagging.

    • Use a polishing machine equipped with wool pad or white sponge wheel.

    • Ensure that all sandpaper marks generated during repair have been eliminated and the surface has the same texture as the adjacent paint coating.

  2. Operation steps

    • Clean the plate surface, adjust the rotating speed of the polishing machine to 1,000-1,500r/min, install the rough polishing pad, and evenly apply polishing wax on the polishing pad.

    • Place the polishing pad flat on the paint surface and start the polishing machine to move back and forth regularly along the horizontal direction for polishing.

    Caution

    • After trimming with sand paper, select polishing paste suitable to conditions of the surface. Polish the surface with polishing paste in the order from low fineness to high fineness.

    • Pay special attention to ridges, edges, corners and zones above the plane. Those locations are easily exposed to polishing, and the paint film there is liable to be polished through.

Fine polishing

  1. Key points

    • Fine polishing: remove swirl marks generated during rough polishing to create gloss.

    • Use a polishing machine equipped with fine polishing sponge pad.

    • Ensure that all swirl marks generated during rough polishing are removed and the surface has the same texture and glossiness as the adjacent steel plate.

    Caution

    • Select suitable ultra-fine polishing paste.

    • Use the auxiliary toning light to check whether the vortex trace still exists. Glazing is needed if yor can still see the vortex trace.

Polishing of transition area

  1. Key points

    • Polish the blend area to ensure no roughness or coating delamination at its edges.

    • Use a fine polishing pad and polishing wax.

    • If the edges of the transition area are significantly rough, use a wool pad and ultra-fine polishing wax.

    • When polishing, move the polishing machine from the repainted surface to the original surface, and properly adjust the angle of the polishing machine so that the rotation direction of the polishing pad rotates from the repainted surface to the original surface.

    Caution

    • The coating interface may be polished through since the coating is thin if it is polished with sand paper or coarse polishing paste or the polisher is moved from the original surface to the re-coated surface.

Lustering

  1. Key points

    • The plate coating is lustered to remove swirl marks (halos) generated during fine polishing to create gloss.

    • Use a dual-action polishing machine equipped with sponge pad.

    • Ensure that all swirl marks generated during fine polishing have been eliminated and the surface has the same texture and glossiness as the adjacent steel plate.

    Caution

    • Wipe the plate surface with a piece of fine fiber cloth, and make an evaluation using the toning light to make sure no vortex trace is omitted.

Analysis of polishing defects

Low glossiness

  1. Causes

    • Surface polishing before sufficient drying of paint coating.

    • Use of inferior polishing wax.

  2. Solutions

    • Adjust the drying environment and dry it under appropriate drying conditions.

    • Select an appropriate polishing material according to the type of paint.

  3. Measures

    • Allow the surface to dry sufficiently before polishing it.

Polishing marks

  1. Causes

    • Surface polishing before sufficient drying.

  2. Solutions

    • Adjust the drying environment and dry it under appropriate drying conditions.

  3. Measures

    • Allow the surface to dry sufficiently and then repolish it.

Sandpaper mark

  1. Causes

    • Insufficient rough polishing or too deep sandpaper marks.

  2. Solutions

    • Re-select fine polishing sandpaper to grind the defective area.

  3. Measures

    • Grind the surface and then polish it again.

Rough texture

  1. Causes

    • Inadequate surface polishing.

    • Excessive surface polishing.

  2. Solutions

    • Carefully compare the repainted area with the undamaged area, and ensure even sanding while trimming the texture of the coating with sandpaper.

  3. Measures

    • Further polish it if the polishing is insufficient.

Exposure due to polishing

  1. Causes

    • Excessive polishing of the plate edge surface.

    • Excessive polishing at the body contour feature line.

  2. Solutions

    • Before polishing, protect the plate edge surface with masking paper or other proper means.

    • Adopt appropriate methods for polishing.

  3. Measures

    • Repaint the plate surface.

Coating delamination

  1. Causes

    • Excessive polishing of coating surface in the transition area, resulting in coating cracking.

    • Movement of the polishing machine from the undamaged surface to the repainted surface, which causes the rotation direction of the polishing pad to change from the undamaged surface to the repainted surface.

  2. Solutions

    • Pay attention to the movement direction of polishing machine and rotation direction of polishing pad.

  3. Measures

    • Repaint the plate surface.
