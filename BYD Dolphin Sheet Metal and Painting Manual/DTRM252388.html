Replacement of roof cover assembly

Removal

  1. Remove 33 welding spots of the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  2. Remove 12 welding spots of the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  3. Remove 12 welding spots of the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  4. Remove 35 welding spots of the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Remove the welding spots (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  5. Take out the roof cover assembly.

Installation

  1. Fix the roof cover assembly to the installation position.

  2. Check and adjust the relative positions and sizes of the roof cover assembly and side wall parts.

  3. Weld 35 welding spots on the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  4. Weld 12 welding spots on the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  5. Weld 12 welding spots on the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  6. Weld 33 welding spots on the roof cover assembly.

    Maintenance method indicator

    Descriptions

    Spot welding (the replaced panel is on the inner side)

  7. Apply the body sealant and internal coating at corresponding positions.

  8. After applying the external coating, apply rust inhibitor to the inner plate part of the welding spots of the closed section structure.
