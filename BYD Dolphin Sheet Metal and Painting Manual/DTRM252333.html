Body welding

An introduction to welding tools and equipment

Resistance spot welder

Reminder

  • Resistance spot welding: it refers to applying pressure through the electrode after the workpiece is assembled, and welding is carried out by using the resistance heat generated by the current passing through the contact surface of the electrode and the adjacent area; and the resistance heat effect generated by the current flowing through the contact surface and adjacent areas of the workpiece is used to heat it to the melting or plastic status, so as to form metal bonding.

  1. Function

    • Resistance spot welding is a process in which two or three layers of steel plates are clamped between electrodes, and the pressed parts are melted by resistance heating by means of low voltage and high current. And at the same time, pressure is maintained at the welding points, thus the plates are welded together.

  2. Key points

    • When it is necessary to join the body panels, spot welding is the most reliable method, which is the same as the original manufacturing process.

    • Filler metals such as welding wires and welding rods, as well as welding materials such as oxygen, acetylene and hydrogen are not required, so the welding cost is low.

    • There will be no residual internal stress and deformation at the welding position.

    • The heat penetrated into the steel plate during welding is low, the thermal deformation is small, and the unwelded areas will not harden.

    • The operation time is short and the efficiency is improved.

    • It has high productivity and is free of noise and harmful gases, but sometimes sparks splashing may occur, which calls for isolation.

  3. How to use

    • Apply anti-rust primer after sheet metal repair.

    • Remove water, grease, and other affected substances.

    • Spray or brush on exposed metal panels.

  4. Selection of electrode arm.

    • Select an appropriate electrode arm according to the welding position of vehicle body.

    • The longer the length of the electrode arm, the larger the operating space and the greater the control difficulty.

Gas shielded welding machine

Reminder

  • Gas shielded arc welding: the arc welding that uses the mixture of carbon dioxide and argon as the shielding gas, which protects the welding zone against environmental influence is called carbon dioxide gas shielded arc welding, or gas shielded arc welding for short.

  1. Function

    • The welding wire is sent out in a semi-automatic manner, and the arc is generated through low voltage and high current in cooperation with the ground wire. In addition, high heat is generated by the short circuit displacement method to melt the welding wire, so as to achieve the purpose of welding.

  2. Key points

    • The visibility of gas shielded arc welding and weld pool is good, and the welding parameters can be adjusted according to the conditions of the weld pool during welding.

    • The welding operation is easy, without or with little welding slag, and there is basically no need for slag removal after welding.

    • With the compression of the protective gas flow, the arc heat is concentrated; the welding speed is faster; the weld pool is smaller; the heat-affected zone is narrow; the weldment deformation after welding is small.

    • It can be used to weld magnesium, aluminum, and their alloys with strong chemical activity and easy formation of high melting point oxide film.

    • It can be used to weld sheets.

    • When working outdoors, windshield devices shall be set up; otherwise, the effect of gas shielded welding is not good or even very poor.

    • The light radiation of the arc is strong.

    • The welding equipment is relatively complex and more expensive than the shielded metal arc welding equipment.

  3. An introduction to the welding wire transmission system.

    • The welding wire is sent out through the electric motor.

    • The tightness of the pinch roller of wire feed motor shall be adjusted moderately.

    • Choose different grooves for the pinch roller according to the size of welding wire.

  4. Shielding gas.

    • The shielding gas is ejected from the welding gun nozzle to isolate oxygen in the air and protect the welding area.

    • After the welding switch is pressed, the shielding gas will be ejected first and then the welding will proceed.

    • The shielding gas is called MIG when an inert gas (such as argon) is used and MAG when a reactive gas (such as CO2) is used.

    • The mixed gas of 80% argon and 20% carbon dioxide is most suitable for welding high-strength steel plates of the vehicle body.

    • Adjust the gas flow to 10-15 L/min during welding.

  5. Conduit.

    • Conduit metal spiral construction.

    • The conduit can ensure that the welding wire can smoothly reach the nozzle position.

    Caution

    • Never bend the wire tube during welding. The uniform output of welding wire may be affected.

  6. Conductive nozzle and welding gun nozzle.

    • The inside of the welding gun nozzle must be kept clean and free from excessive welding slag.

    • As one end of the electrode, the conductive nozzle is used to conduct current to the welding wire, so the inner wall of the conductive nozzle must be kept in contact with the welding wire.

    • The models of the conductive nozzle are 0.6 mm, 0.8 mm and 1.0 mm aperture. Pay attention to matching with the welding wire during replacement.

    Caution

    • Once the welding wire is heated, it will expand. The diameter of the contact tube should be slightly larger than that of the welding wire. However, if the gap is too large, it will lead to unstable wire discharge and arc.

  7. An introduction to the maintenance of welding equipment.

    • Perform daily cleaning on the welding machine.

    • Check the power connector of welding machine before startup.

    • Check whether the shielding gas leaks.

    • Use welding equipment correctly.

    • Clean the dust on the cooling fan.

    • Clear welding slag from the inside of the welding gun nozzle.

Caution

  • Keep the grounding clamp of the welding machine clean. Never use it for debugging welding.

Steel body welding technology

Influence of resistance spot welding conditions

Adjustment of spot welding machine

  1. Effect of welding current.

    • If the current is too small, the heat will be too low to completely melt the joint position of the welded plate, resulting in insufficient welding strength.

    • If the current is too large, the heat will be too high, which will deform the welding position of the welded plate. At the same time, the area affected by heat will be too large, and the strength will be reduced.

  2. Effect of welding time.

    • Excessive welding time will result in an excessively large heat-affected range around the welded area, but will not increase the weld strength.

    • The welding power-on time is too long and the heat is too large, resulting in weldment overfusion or cracks at welding spots.

    • The welding power-on time is too short and the heat is insufficient, resulting in no nugget or a too small welding spot.

    • If the welding time is too short, the fusion and strength of welding spots will be insufficient.

    Reminder

    • Reasonable welding time will ensure moderate heat, no obvious deformation at welding spots and good welding quality.

  3. Effect of welding pressure.

    • After power off, the melted part gradually cools down and solidifies, and the material structure becomes fine under pressure, thus bonding two steel plates.

    • During pressurization, attention shall be paid to maintaining the pressure. If the pressure is too small, the fusion welding will not be tight enough, resulting in strength reduction.

    • If the pressure is too large, the reduction of resistance will lead to a decrease in welding heat, resulting in smaller base metal welding spots and lower welding strength.

  4. Influence of the distance between welding spots.

    • If the distance between welding spots is too long, the overall connection strength of plates will be insufficient due to too few connecting points.

    • If the distance between welding spots is appropriate, the overall connection strength increases accordingly.

    • When the distance between the welding spots is too short, the current will flow through other completed welding spots, which is called the diversion effect. This will cause insufficient strength of the welded spots completed later.

    • Use a drawing needle to draw lines and mark the welding spot position.

    • The welding spot spacing formula is S=10 t + 10 mm (t = plate thickness).

    • The number of welding spots at the resistance spot welding position of the replaced plate is about 1.3 times that of the OEM.

  5. Influence of welding spot edge distance.

    • If the welding spot is too close to the edge, it will easily crack.

    • Mark the welding spots before welding to determine their precise locations and avoid the welding positions being too close to the edges.

    • Edge distance of welding spot: about 6 mm from the center of the welding spot to the edge of steel plate.

  6. An introduction to the spot welding of three-layer plate.

    • If the welding machine has sufficient power, three-layer steel plate welding can be carried out directly on the vehicle body.

    • When the power of the welding machine cannot meet the three-layer welding requirements, two layers of steel plates shall be welded first, and the third layer of steel plate shall be welded at the welding spots of the two layers of steel plates.

    • Failure to follow the above principles will result in the degradation of welding quality.

Operation process of resistance spot welding

  1. Operation process of resistance spot welding.

    • Plate grinding.

    • Cleaning and degreasing

    • Spray conductive paint.

    • Plate positioning.

    • Debug welding machine.

    • Welding.

  2. Plate grinding.

    • Use a grinder to remove the old paint film, grind and clean up the old paint film in the welding area to ensure the conductive effect.

    Caution

    • Using a grinding tool with excessive particles to grind the plate will reduce its strength.

  3. Cleaning and degreasing

    • Clean the welding surface with a wiping cloth dampened with detergent.

    • A clean, non-contaminated surface allows current to pass smoothly through the steel plate.

    Caution

    • During the cleaning process, wear protective equipment, such as masks and solvent-proof gloves.

  4. Spray conductive primer.

    • Spray special primer for spot welding on the steel plate welding contact surface.

  5. Plate positioning.

    • After positioning the steel plate to be replaced, fix it with a locking clamp.

    • To avoid the diversion effect, the locking clamp shall not be too close to the welding spot to be welded.

    Caution

    • Ensure that there is no gap on the contact surface of welded plates.

  6. Debug welding machine.

    • Set the welding parameters, applied pressure, welding current, and power-on time.

    • Carry out a destructive test on the welding spots after the trial welding.

  7. Plate positioning.

    • The electrode must be perpendicular to the surface of the steel plate.

    • Keep the distance between the two electrodes, the distance from the welding spots to the edges, and the cooling time appropriate.

    Caution

    • Ensure that the plate joint surfaces and other surfaces are clean.

    • Ensure that the two electrode heads are vertically aligned.

Quality check of resistance spot welding (cases)

  1. Unqualified welding spots.

    1. Undesirable phenomena.

      • Spot welding perforation (poor welding conditions, hole formed by splashing of spot welding).

      • Spot welding burrs (serious welding slag burrs formed at the welding spot).

      • Spot welding cracks (poor welding conditions cause cracks in the welding spot).

      • Spot welding dent (poor welding conditions cause deep welding spot dent).

    2. Consequences.

      • The welding strength decreases.

      • The appearance is rough and uneven.

    3. Solutions.

      • Adjust parameters such as welding current and power-on time.

      • Clean the impurities on the welding surface of plates.

      • Trim the flatness of plates to ensure the matching of contact surfaces.

      • Adjust the clamping force of the welding machine.

    4. Qualified welding spots.

      • The welding spot surface shall be smooth and free of obvious splash.

      • The diameter of the weld nugget is moderate.

      • The edge size affected by welding heat is about 20% of the weld spot.

      • There is no obvious burning mark outside the welding nugget.

  2. Non-destructive test methods.

    • Take two steel sample plates (consistent with or similar to the materials to be welded).

    • Grind and clean the steel template.

    • Try to weld a welding spot.

    • Check the appearance quality of welding spots.

    • Separate the test welding spots.

    • Measure the diameter of test welding spots.

    Reminder

    • After the two templates are separated, if a round hole with proper diameter is left on one side of the steel plate and a bump remains on one side of the steel plate, the welding quality is good.

  3. Destructive test methods.

    • Use a sheet metal hammer to knock the chisel between the welding spots, and check the connection of the welding spots. If there is no cracking in the welding spots, restore the plate to its original state with a hammer and bucking block.

    Reminder

    • This kind of test is usually carried out on the vehicle body. If the quality is good, restore the plate to its original status.

Gas shielded welding technology

Welding condition control of gas shielded welding

  1. Welding current.

    • Select the corresponding welding current according to the welding conditions (plate thickness, welding position, welding speed, material, and other parameters).

  2. Arc voltage.

    • In order to obtain a good welding effect, it is necessary to have an appropriate arc length, which depends on the arc voltage.

    • If the arc voltage is too low, the welding wire will not generate an electric arc, causing the welding wire to stick to the steel plate and producing a bang sound, resulting in deeper fusion depth, more welding slag, and higher weld bead.

    • If the arc voltage is appropriate, not only a good welding effect can be obtained, but also a smooth squeaking sound can be heard.

    • If the arc voltage is too high, the arc length will become longer. Besides, the welding defects such as wider weld pool and shallower melting depth will also be caused in addition to the increase in welding slag and noise.

  3. Wire feeding speed.

    • Wire feeding speed determines the filling amount of weld pool, and different wire feeding speeds are adjusted according to the current.

    • If the wire feed is too slow, the arc will be intermittent; If the wire is fed too quickly, there will be a bang-bang sound when welding against the gun.

  4. The welding power-on time.

    • The carbon dioxide gas shielded welding machine has the function of welding time control.

    • Turn on this switch, and the welding time is intelligently controlled by the welder itself.

    • Turn off this switch and adjust the time knob to "0". The welding power-on time is controlled by the operator.

    Caution

    • Adjust this switch to "0" during sheet metal repair and welding. And the operator controls the power-on time of welding.

  5. Gas flow.

    • The standard flow rate currently used is in the range of 10-15 L/min.

    • The flow rate shall also be adjusted in accordance with the distance from the nozzle to the base metal, the welding current, the welding speed, and the surrounding environment (wind speed).

    Caution

    • Carbon dioxide welding is not recommended to be carried out in a place with high wind speed. It may cause poor protection of welding spots.

  6. The distance between the electrode and the base metal.

    • The distance between the electrode and base metal is another important factor affecting the welding effect.

    • If the distance is too large, the welding wire will melt faster. This is because the protruding length of the welding wire is too long, and the excessively long part will generate preheating. Therefore, the current flow rate will decrease, thus reducing the penetration depth of weld beads. At the same time, excessive distance will also reduce the isolation effect of shielding gas.

    • If the distance is too small, it will be difficult for the operator to observe the welding area and this may affect the welding quality.

    Caution

    • The distance between the welding torch and the plate is about 8-15mm.

    • Never use the nozzle as a fulcrum to contact the plate during welding.

  7. The angle of the welding gun in the forward welding method.

    • It is characterized by shallow penetration and high weld beads.

    Caution

    • The angle of push welding is about 10-15 degrees from the vertical plane of the base metal.

  8. The angle of the welding gun in the backward welding method.

    • It is characterized by deep penetration and flat weld beads.

    Caution

    • The angle of push welding is about 30 degrees from the vertical plane of the base metal.

  9. Welding speed.

    • During welding, the correct welding current and speed must be adjusted according to the thickness of the base metal to obtain a good fusion depth and weld bead width.

    • If the welding current remains unchanged, accelerating the welding speed will reduce the fusion depth and bead width, causing weld beads to protrude and fail to reach the welding strength. If the welding speed is too slow, the base metal will be overheated and melted through.

    • Generally, the welding speed of a thin steel plate with a thickness of 0.8 mm is 105-115 mm/min. Usually, the thicker the welded steel plate, the slower the welding speed.

Classification of Gas Shielded Welding

  1. Butt welding.

    1. Key points

      • Butt welding is relatively simple, but prolonged high temperatures may easily cause deformation at the welding position.

      • During welding, pre-welding grooves should be reserved and fixed through tack welding. It is recommended to use segmented welding as much as possible and strictly follow the welding sequence.

      • Welding shall be carried out in a cross or skip welding sequence as much as possible.

      Caution

      • For butt welding, ensure the flatness of plates.

    2. Butt welding method.

      • Place two steel plates on the same plane, and fill the gap between the two butt-jointed plates to form a whole.

      • It is used to weld panels that are joined after cutting.

      • This welding method must be used on the exterior structural parts of the vehicle body to ensure the flatness of the exterior parts.

      Caution

      • This method is applied to the parts where overlapping welding cannot be carried out.

    3. Butt welding requirements.

      • Make tack welding as required and ensure that the plates are not deformed.

      • Continuous welding shall be carried out in sections to control the deformation as much as possible.

      • Although butt welding can be carried out for both thick and thin steel plates, in order to ensure better permeability when welding thicker steel plates, the opening must be ground into a bevel.

  2. Lap welding.

    1. Key points

      • Lap welding is relatively simple, and it is necessary to ensure that the gap between plates is kept tight before welding.

      • Resistance spot welding or gas shielded welding can be used for lap welding.

      • Seam welding with gas shielded welding allows temporary welding fixation in advance to prevent deformation. If plug welding is used, holes shall be drilled in advance and the edges of holes shall be deburred.

    2. Lap welding operation.

      • Lap welding is a welding method in which the edges of two overlapping steel plates are welded to combine them into a whole.

      • It is mostly used for parts on the body where spot welding or hole filling welding cannot be carried out.

      • This method is usually used for welding structural parts inside the vehicle body.

    3. Lap welding requirements.

      • Make sure that there is no false weld at the welding spots.

      • No obvious porosity and burn-through.

  3. Liner plate welding.

    1. Key points

      • The use of lining plates can avoid thermal deformation and improve welding strength. Resistance spot welding or plug-hole welding can be used for welding the overlapping parts of lining plates.

      • Lining plate welding is often used to weld local areas with long lengths and small shape differences and where greater weld strength is required.

      Caution

      • When welding the lining plate, avoid gaps between the upper and lower plates. The interface position may be deformed.

    2. Lining plate welding requirements.

      • The welds of lining plate welding shall have the same surface profile and be free from defects such as pores.

      • The heat influence range (discoloration area) is between 20-30 times the plate thickness.

      • There is no excessive penetration depth on the back, but obvious weld marks are required.

  4. Plug welding.

    1. Key points

      • Plug welding is the most commonly used welding method in body repair. It is applied to special areas where spot welding cannot be carried out or the strength requirements cannot be met by using spot welding.

      • Drill holes in the upper plate of two or more overlapping steel plates, and then fill the holes.

    2. Pre-drilling.

      Plug-hole welding method for small holes (about 6 mm in diameter):

      • Align the welding wire with the center of the hole and activate the switch of the welding gun.

      • Fill the hole and release the welding gun switch.

      • The diameter of the plug welding used on the vehicle body is 6 mm for the outer panel and 8 mm for the inner structural plate.

      Caution

      • The welding torch shall be perpendicular to the plate.

    3. Standard for plug welding.

      • Weld nugget diameter ≤ aperture + 2 mm.

      • There is penetration on the back.

      • It shall be free of defects such as burnt-through, incomplete filling, and porosity.

      • Plug hole height < 2 mm.

The welding process of gas shielded welding

  1. Plate positioning.

    • To prevent deformation of the panel, the locking clamp shall not be too tight when positioning the panel.

    • When selecting a locking clamp, make sure that the clamping surface is not too narrow and there should be no foreign matters on the surface.

    Caution

    • Use U-type and wide plane vise grip pliers to clamp plates and ensure that there is no gap at the joint position.

  2. Debug welding machine.

    • Adjust various functions of the welding machine according to the user's manual.

    • Test welding shall be carried out with test plates of the same material and thickness.

    • Place the nozzle end close to the steel plate.

    • Turn the welding gun switch to "ON", and contact the end of the welding wire with the steel plate to generate an arc.

    • First, confirm the welding machine is adjusted correctly, and then proceed with the panel welding.

  3. Tack welding.

    • Temporary spot welding can pre-position two steel plates.

    Caution

    • The temporary welding spacing is generally 15-30 times of the thickness of the welded plate.

  4. Welding sequence.

    • Welding in the sequence from 1 to 5 is helpful for heat dissipation of steel plates and preventing deformation.

  5. Main welding.

    • Move in a stable posture to prevent the welding gun from shaking.

    • Align the ends of tack welds.

    • Repeatedly turn the switch to "ON" and "OFF".

    • Continuously weld through the tack welding spots

  6. Connecting the welding points.

    • Welding by aligning the welding wire with the end of the previous welding spot not only produces a good welding appearance but also effectively controls welding heat and prevents deformation.

    Caution

    • Weld the last welding spot at about two-thirds of the previous welding spot.

  7. Gun holding posture.

    • Use the thumb and index finger mainly in the manipulation of the welding gun.

    • Hold the gun with one hand and hold the handle of the welding gun with another hand to control the welding point.

    Caution

    • The grasping position should not be too close to the nozzle to avoid the impact of vision and burns.

  8. Anti-corrosion treatment after welding.

    • Use a belt grinder to grind the weld pass to the level of the steel plate surface, but do not over grind the weld pass.

    • Spray or brush special anti-rust epoxy primer.

    Caution

    • Anti corrosion treatment is necessary. Ignoring the anti-corrosion treatment will not guarantee the maintenance quality.

Welding quality inspection of gas shielded welding

  1. Visual inspection.

    • Sizes of weld seams.

    • The appearance of the weld bead.

    • Height of grooves and welds.

    • Surface impurities.

    • Penetration depths.

    Caution

    • Select the position with sufficient light for multi angle observation.

  2. Non-destructive test.

    • Non-destructive test includes X-ray irradiation and defect detection agents.

    Caution

    • To use flaw detection testing agent, you should first know how to use it.

  3. Destructive test.

    • Destructive test (tension test).

    • Conduct a separation test on the joint position to check whether there is cracking.

    • Cut the welding position to check the longitudinal section of weld pool.

    Caution

    • After cutting the welding position, grind it with sandpaper 600#, and check it with magnifying glass and testing agent.

Analyses and solutions of welding defects (cases)

  1. Burn-through and excessive penetration depth

    1. Causes.

      • Excessive current and voltage.

      • The welding speed is too slow and the heat is excessively concentrated.

      • Unreasonable welding methods.

    2. Solutions.

      • Adjust to reasonable current and voltage.

      • Increase the welding speed.

      • Select the appropriate forward welding or backward welding methods.

      • Welding lining plates can be used to prevent burn-through or excessive deformation of the panel.

  2. Insufficient penetration depth.

    1. Causes.

      • The current and voltage are too small.

      • The welding speed is too fast and the heat is insufficient.

      • Unreasonable welding methods.

    2. Solutions.

      • Increase the current and voltage.

      • Reduce the welding speed.

      • Select the appropriate forward welding or backward welding methods.

  3. Weld pass porosity.

    1. Causes.

      • Insufficient shielding gas flow.

      • The welding area is not cleaned and has oil stains or other impurities.

      • There is wind during welding operation.

    2. Solutions.

      • Increase the gas flow (10-15 L/min).

      • Before welding, clean the welding area of the panel to remove oil and impurities.

      • Avoid welding in windy places.

  4. Deviation of weld pass.

    1. Causes.

      • Unstable gun movement during the welding operation.

      • The angle of the welding gun is incorrect, blocking the view.

      • The special welding mask is not used.

    2. Solutions.

      • Operators should strengthen welding training.

      • Use the special welding mask to ensure the visibility of welding.

      • Adjust the welding angle and direction.

  5. End undercut.

    1. Causes.

      • An arc that fails to produce a weld occurs at the starting position of welding.

      • An arc that fails to produce a weld occurs at the ending position of welding.

    2. Solutions.

      • Use welding guide plates at the start and end positions.

      • The length of the guide plate shall not be less than 20 mm.

      • Welding starts and ends at the position of the guide plate.

Anti-corrosion treatment of vehicle body welding

Classification of anti-rust paint and sealant

Reminder

It falls into three forms according to the use of antirust paint and sealant:

  • Spray type: chassis and body cavity.

  • Extrusion type: the joint between the door edge and the inner and outer plates of the front and rear covers.

  • Brush coating type: the joint between the interior of the trunk and the plate of the front engine compartment.

Spray-type anti-rust paint and sealant

  1. Introduction to anti-rust sealant at the joint of panels.

    • The sealant can be applied in a way to achieve the 'fish-scale' sealing and a surface with granular undulations like those produced by the OEM.

    • It can be applied on the weld as a sealant or sprayed and applied over a large area.

    • It can be applied in a way to create a normal strip appearance.

  2. Chassis anti-rust sealant.

    • After the chassis is repaired, the repaired area shall be sprayed with chassis sealant after spraying the primer.

    Caution

    • Chassis sealant can only be used on underbody.

  3. Cavity anti-corrosive wax

    • To prevent rusting, anti-rust treatment shall be carried out on all surfaces including inaccessible positions according to the OEM's process specifications.

    • If the cavity anti-corrosion measures are not strictly carried out, quality problems such as steel plate corrosion and rusting may occur after maintenance.

    • In inaccessible cavities inside the components, a spray gun with a hose is required to apply cavity anti-corrosive wax.

    Caution

    • The spray gun with the hose has a large spraying angle, and only needs to change the angle to meet the surface coverage of the inner cavity in almost all positions.

  4. Convenient anti-rust paint.

    • In automobile sheet metal operation, the surface of the metal panel is exposed due to welding, polishing, or the removal of spare parts, especially during the prolonged waiting after the sheet metal repair, it is easy for the corrosion of panels to occur. Therefore, timely anti-corrosion treatment is essential.

    • Convenient anti-rust paint can be used for timely anti-corrosion treatment. This kind of anti-rust paint can achieve the ideal effect without special treatment on the surface and can be carried out by spraying or brushing.

Extruded anti-rust sealant

  1. The application positions for the extrusion-type anti-rust sealant

    • Edge wrapping of new body panels, doors, engine hoods, etc.

    • At the water gutters on the roof.

    • At weld seams and weld edges.

    • At the joints of replaced panels

  2. Extruded anti-rust sealant treatment.

    • The tightness of the outer panel is particularly important to ensure that water does not flow into the vehicle and avoid problems such as rusting.

    • After sealant application, trim the sealant with a hard brush to make its appearance consistent with that of the original vehicle.

    Caution

    • Anti rust sealant on the plate surface will make it difficult for the paint to adhere to the plate surface. Clean up the residual anti rust sealant.

Brush-applied anti-rust primer

  1. Selection of brushing conditions for anti-rust primer

    • After welding, brush the welding slag and burnt paint film with a steel brush, blow it clean with an air gun, and then apply a certified primer on exposed steel plates.

  2. Brush-applied sealant.

    • Some parts that are easy to be wet for a long time and those with more water flow call for special treatment.

    • Before resistance spot welding, please apply conductive primer on the joint.
