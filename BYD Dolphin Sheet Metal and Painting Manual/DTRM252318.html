Plastic parts spraying

Basic knowledge of plastic parts repair

Plastic parts repair concept

  1. Key points

    • In the process of painting and maintenance, a lot of plastic parts damage can be seen. How to correctly repair these plastic parts is one of the skills we must master.

Resin type of plastic parts

Reminder
Resins used in vehicles can generally be classified into thermoplastic resins and thermosetting resins. Thermoplastic resins are widely used in vehicle applications due to their high recyclability, while thermosetting resins are decreasingly used in vehicle materials because of their poor recyclability. Let's take a look at characteristics of the two resin types.

Thermoplastic resin

  1. Key points

    • Thermoplastic resin is a material that softens when heated and solidifies when cooled, and can soften when heated, making it widely used. Although the resin is heated and cooled repeatedly without changing its properties, its performance degrades slightly. The performance of this kind of resin is similar to that of candles.

    • It softens when heated and solidifies when cooled.

    • Even after curing, the thermoplastic resin will soften when heated, so deformation can be repaired by heating.

    • Thermoplastic resin can be recycled as repeated heating and cooling has little effect on its quality.

    Reminder
    Thermoplastic resins include PP (polypropylene), PC (polycarbonate), PVC (polyvinyl chloride), ABS, and so on.

Thermosetting resin

  1. Key points

    • It is a type of synthetic resin cured under chemical reaction when heated.

    • It cannot be recycled and is thus less used than thermoplastic resin in automobiles. For strong thermal resistance, it will not soften even if heated again after curing.

    • It is like an egg that will not become fluid again once cooked.

    • Once the resin is cured, it cannot soften even when heated. Therefore, the deformation cannot be repaired by heating.

    • It has excellent strength and thermal resistance, but it is less and less used in vehicle spare parts due to its low recycling rate.

    Reminder
    For example, PUR (thermosetting polyurethane resin).

Application of resin materials on vehicles

  1. Application of plastics

    • Various plastics widely used in automobiles are made of various resin materials. Plastics are increasingly used in automobile manufacturing, and their lighter weight compared with steel plates has become the main driving force for plastic applications. Moreover, there is a desire to avoid minor damage (e.g., scratch deformation or similar damage) by using elastic resin plastics. This also drives the development of plastic applications.

  2. Symbol meaning of plastics

    • ABS-acrylonitrile butadiene styrene

    • PP-polypropylene

    • PP/EPDM-polypropylene/ethylene propylene diene monomer

    • PA-polyamide, PS-polystyrene, PC-polycarbonate

    • PU-polyurethane, PU (soft)-flexible polyurethane foam

    • PPO-polyphenylene oxide

    • PBT-polybutylene terephthalate

  3. Key points

    • Application of plastics in automobile manufacturing: bumper, spoiler and side collision protector; engine hood, trunk lid, roof panel, door and fender; vehicle lighting, side mirror and instrument panel.

    Reminder
    In the past, the application of plastics was limited to bumpers and other molded parts only. with the development of science and technology, it is also widely used in the front compartment covers and the trunk lids.

Introduction to repair materials of plastic parts

Reminder
Due to the special properties of resin materials, special maintenance materials need to be used in maintenance accordingly, generally including reinforcing tapes, resin adhesive, resin curing agent, PP primer, etc.

Resin adhesive

  1. Key points

    • The resin adhesive has a flexible agent that can be used to fill scratches or small depressions.

    • For repairing of damaged plastic parts, it is used to fill dents and cracks. To ensure the repair quality of cracked parts, it should be used together with reinforced interfacing cloth.

Reinforced fiber

  1. Key points

    • It is used to reinforce the fractured part and behind the reinforced cracked part.

    Reminder
    To use it, place it on top of the damaged area, position it and press it down into the adhesive till the web is saturated.

Soft additives

  1. Key points

    • It is mixed with paint to improve the flexibility of intermediate paint and top coat.

    • Soft additives or curing-agent-containing soft additives are used to improve the flexibility of paint film.

    Reminder
    For the mixing ratio and the drying time, observe the manufacturer's instructions.

Plastic primer

  1. Key points

    • It is used to improve the adhesion between exposed surfaces and resin adhesive or top coating.

    • After the newly replaced bare plastic surface is treated, it is required to spray 1-2 layers of plastic primer on the bumper surface first and start subsequent coating after drying of primer.

    Reminder
    For the drying time, observe the manufacturer's instructions.

Anti-static detergent

  1. Key points

    • It is used to remove static electricity on the plate surface and prevent absorbed dust under static electricity from producing dust spots during painting.

    Reminder
    To use it, clean the plate surface by alternating wet and dry wiping.

Repair tool of plastic parts

Reminder
In maintenance of plastic parts, due to the special properties of plastic parts such as easy bending and brittleness, it is necessary to use corresponding repair tools in the repair process to meet the characteristics requirements of plastic parts. The common repair tools include the hot air gun, hot light, pneumatic drill and other special tools.

Hot air gun

  1. Key points

    • It is a tool that mainly uses the core of heating resistance wire and hot air blown out to weld plastic parts and remove plates.

    • It can be used for thermal treatment of plastic parts.

    • It can be used to repair plastic deformation in narrow parts.

    Caution
    The temperature of the hot air gun is high, be careful of scalding and keep it away from the fire source; never blow the hot air to humans or animals.

Pneumatic drill

  1. Key points

    • It is mainly used for drilling plastic parts to prevent further expansion of fracture.

    Caution
    Take care when using the pneumatic driller. Never touch the revolving bit directly by hand.

Heating lamp

  1. Key points

    • It is used for heating the poly-putty or primer coating on the plate surface.

    • It is used for heating repair after damage and deformation of large-area plastic plates.

    Caution
    Keep away from combustibles and explosives when using the hot light since its very hot.

Grinder

  1. Key points

    • There are generally three kinds of grinders: single-action grinder with strong cutting force; double-action grinder with moderate grinding force; orbital grinder with small grinding force.

    • From the perspective of grinding principle, the single-action grinder mainly has strong unidirectional rotary cutting force, the orbital grinder mainly has vibration, and the double-action grinder has both rotation and vibration.

    • The single-action grinder is mainly used to remove the old paint film.

    • The double-action grinder can be used for grinding feather edges, poly-putty and paint surface.

    • The orbital grinder is mainly used for the grinding of poly-putty.

Sandpaper

  1. Key points

    • Sandpaper is mainly installed on a grinder for grinding and can be divided based on shape into round sandpaper and square sandpaper.

    • It can be divided based on grinding particles into silicon carbide type and aluminum oxide type.

    • Based on the pasting method, it is divided into grinder sandpaper (self-adhesive type and nylon hasp type) and grinding plate sandpaper (non-self-adhesive type, self-adhesive type and nylon hasp type).

    • It is mainly used to remove paint, rust and grind poly-putty.

    Reminder
    In maintenance operations, most grinding heads are of Velcro type.

Spray gun

  1. Key points

    • Based on gun structure, spray guns can be divided into three types: gravity feed, pressure feed and suction feed.

    • The spray gun is mainly used to evenly spray paint on the surface of an object.

    • The primer spray gun is used to apply plastic primer and intermediate paint on plastic parts.

    • The top coat spray gun is used to apply the top coat of plastic parts.

New bumper spraying process

New bumper

  1. Operating procedures

    • The operating procedure for a new bumper is shown in the figure.

Release agent

  1. Key points

    • New bumpers generally do not have a paint coating as polypropylene resin bumpers are manufactured by pouring the resin into concave molds and letting it cure. To facilitate removal of bumpers, it is necessary to spray release agent onto concave molds before pouring resin.

    • After the paint is applied to plastic parts, the residual release agent (if any) on the surface of plastic parts may affect the adhesion of paint and cause coating defects such as poor adhesion and paint peeling off. Therefore, the release agent must be removed from the surface before painting plastic parts.

    • Generally, the exposed bumper surface can be cleaned with hot soapy water.

    • Spraying can only be carried out after the release agent is removed from the surface. Otherwise, the residual release agent may cause coating defects such as poor adhesion.

Bumper surface grinding

  1. Key points

    • It is required to grind the exposed bumper surface before painting.

    • Gray scouring pad and abrasive paste should be used to grind the entire surface.

    Reminder
    Grind or repair the damaged area on the surface of new bumper.

Masking

  1. Key points

    • The bumper area not to be painted should be masked with masking tape and masking paper, and the edge should be protected with special gap tape to avoid paint leakage at the edge of the tape.

    Reminder

    • If ordinary masking tapes are used, paint leakage will easily occur at the edge of the coating.

    • Remove the masking tapes after spraying and before forced drying of the coating.

Surface cleaning

  1. Key points

    • First, it is necessary to clean and degrease the entire bumper surface and remove static electricity on the plate surface.

    Caution
    A cleaning step for removing static electricity is required because the plastic bumper can easily produce static electricity in the operation process, and the adsorbed dust points will cause defects in the spraying process.

Application of plastic primer

  1. Key points

    • Make sure that a thin layer of plastic primer is evenly sprayed on the surface of the plastic part, and then spray the next coating after drying of the plastic primer.

    Caution

    • Application of plastic primer to the surface of residual degreaser will result in paint peeling.

    • If the plastic primer has not been applied, the paint will not adhere, resulting in coating peeling.

    • Never apply the plastic primer thickly as this will cause the paint to peel or shrink around the feathering edge.

    Reminder
    Since the plastic primer has no filling property, it only provides adhesion in paint coating. When applying, ensure that the surface of the plastic part is sprayed with a thin layer of primer without exposed plastics.

Application of intermediate paint

  1. Key points

    • Grinding-free process can be used for the intermediate paint. In case of defects, dust spots and sagging after application of intermediate paint, grinding is required after drying.

    Caution

    • Grinding is required in case of any defects after spraying of intermediate primer.

    • Use sand paper for grinding the whole bumper surface, and scouring pads for edges and corners.

    Reminder

    • You can skip the intermediate primer step and directly spray topcoat in case of a new replacement bumper without surface defect.

    • Add soft additive when applying intermediate primer, so as to avoid cracks on surface of the plastic part under stress.

Application of top coat

  1. Key points

    • The top coat should be applied according to the paint coating type on the surface of plastic parts.

  2. Operation steps

    • Remove the dust and apply the top coat.

    • Apply the coat according to the SOP, and dry the coating at the same time.

    Reminder
    Add soft additive for the plain topcoat and the varnish coating to avoid cracks.

Repair process for damaged bumper

Overview

  1. Operating procedures

    • The operating procedure for a new bumper is shown in the figure.

Damage evaluation

Reminder
The maintenance technician need to effectively analyze and evaluate the damage conditions of the damaged bumper and determine the maintenance solution based on the damage degree and area and other actual conditions of the bumper before maintaining. In actual operations, the maintenance technician evaluates the damaged plastic parts through visual inspection and touching mainly.

Visual inspection

  1. Key points

    • Visually confirm the damaged part and damage range by observing reflected light from fluorescent lamps or light sources, and evaluate the size and degree of damage.

    Reminder
    Visual inspection only allows a preliminary understanding. A detailed understanding of the damage requires further testing.

Touch

  1. Key points

    • Evaluate the dents and bulges on the surface.

    • Wear cotton gloves and touch the damaged area with hands.

    • Estimate the degree of damage by hand touching.

    Reminder

    • Touching allows more comprehensive understanding of the damage degree. It is commonly used for evaluation.

    • Mark the damage range after damage evaluation.

Surface treatment

Reminder
Proceed to the next maintenance steps based on the damage area and depth after damage evaluation of the bumper. Proper treatment is required for the damaged bumper before start of maintenance, mainly including treatment of the damaged area, preparation of feathering edge, cleaning, degreasing, and application of plastic primer.

Treatment of damaged area

  1. Key points

    • For the crack position of plastic parts, measures should be taken to prevent the crack from spreading.

    • The scratches on the bumper need to be ground off. Generally, the grinding standard is: shallow scratches removed, edges of deep scratches ground smooth, and no cracks in the substrate.

  2. Operation steps

    • Drill holes at the front end of the fracture to prevent further expansion.

    • Install a 4 mm-diameter drill bit on the pneumatic drill.

    • Place the drill on the front end of the fracture and drill holes.

    Reminder
    The round edge drilled can reduce stress concentrations and prevent further crack growth.

Making of feather edges

  1. Key points

    • The feather edge is made to trim the step difference between the exposed surface and the old coating. The surface should be ground 20 mm beyond the feather edge to prepare for applying plastic poly-putty.

  2. Operation steps

    • Install sandpaper #P120-P180 on the grinder by selecting the sandpaper based on the damage.

    • Cautiously grind off shallow scratches and trim the edges of residual deep scratches to avoid surface deformation.

    • Blow the dust with an air gun to check and confirm that there is no crack.

    • Finally, use sandpaper #240 for grinding to ensure that the grinding range is greater than the damage range of bumper.

    • To ensure good adhesion when applying adhesive, grind the inner side of the bumper in the damaged area with a belt grinder.

    • Grind complex surfaces by using grinding blocks or adding pads on the grinder.

    Caution
    If the feathering edge prepared is not smooth, putty marks will form when the sprayed paint coat is dried.

Cleaning of plate

  1. Key points

    • Cleaning and degreasing means cleaning the abrasive surface with degreaser and cleaning cloth to ensure that all dirt, dust and oil are removed in preparation for primer application.

    • Dust can be removed with the air gun.

    • The grinding dust can be wiped off with the cleaning cloth.

    • It is required to wipe the grinding area with a cleaning cloth to dissolve oil residue on the surface, and then wipe the surface with a clean cleaning cloth.

Application of plastic primer

  1. Key points

    • After effective cleaning of the repair surface, apply a thin layer of plastic primer evenly on the exposed repair surface to ensure good adhesion of the adhesive to be applied later.

    • Apply a thin layer of plastic primer to the grinding area.

    • Make sure that the plastic primer is evenly and thinly applied on the exposed surface, and apply adhesive after the surface completely dries.

    Caution

    • The paint will not adhere easily if the primer is insufficient.

    • Never apply the plastic primer too thickly in one time as this will cause the paint to peel or shrink around the feathering edge.

    Reminder

    • Due to the softness of the resin bumper, a special plastic primer needs to be applied.

    • For drying of the plastic primer, refer to instructions of the paint manufacturer.

Repair of the damaged part

Reminder
The plastic strength of the damaged area of the plate is generally achieved by applying resin adhesive and attaching a fiber mesh on the back. Mix the main components of adhesive completely with the curing agent, and then apply the mixture on the cracked area behind the bumper.

Application of adhesive

  1. Key points

    • The instructions in the product manual should be followed to mix the required amount of adhesive.

    • A layer of plastic part adhesive should be evenly applied to the broken bumper within the grinding area.

    • A piece of auxiliary material (such as thin iron plate or similar materials) can be fixed on the outer side of the cracking part, and it can be fixed with plastic plates by clamps. This method can eliminate the height difference caused by bumper fracture.

    Reminder

    • The mixture is mainly composed of adhesive and curing agent which are mixed in a ratio in compliance with the manufacturer's requirements.

    • This step is not required if the bumper is only scratched and not broken.

Application of reinforced fiber cloth

  1. Key points

    • Apply reinforced fiber cloth to the back of the bumper after applying adhesive to ensure the strength of the repaired plate.

    • Prepare a fiber cloth of appropriate width at the broken and damaged part.

    • Attach the fiber cloth to the adhesive application position and apply pressure with a scraper to ensure that the fiber cloth is attached to the adhesive.

    Caution
    On the area where the adhesive is applied, fix a reinforcing fiber cloth, and scrape the adhesive outside the fiber cloth onto the surface of the fiber cloth with a scraper to form a smooth coating.
    Reminder

    • This step is not required if the bumper is only scratched and not broken.

    • The back fiber cloth, which is generally made of glass fiber, is attached inside for improving the strength of maintained plastic part after it is broken

Application of resin adhesive

  1. Key points

    • Continue to apply adhesive on the surface of the reinforcing tape until it covers the entire surface.

    • Use an infrared lamp to heat the part to approximately 50 °C and allow the adhesive to solidify. After cooling, remove the auxiliary steel plate and locking plier.

    • Continue to apply plastic adhesive on the damaged area outside the bumper and forcibly dry it.

    • After the adhesive is dried, grind with a double-action grinder to roughly trim the plates in damaged areas.

    Reminder
    Observe instructions in the product manual of adhesive for the drying method of adhesive, so as to ensure thorough curing by heating.

Application of poly-putty (plastic poly-putty)

  1. Key points

    • Thoroughly mix the main components of plastic poly-putty and curing agent, and apply the mixture on the parts to be repaired.

    • Apply the first thin layer of poly-putty so that it can be squeezed into the gap in the damaged area.

    • Fill the damaged surface by applying multiple thin layers. In case of large steps on the surface, trimming is required.

    Caution

    • Use the resin putty (plastic putty). Cracking may occur if ordinary polyester putty is used because it does not have the softness of plastic parts.

    • Plastic putty is sticky, and easy to form steps after scraping. When scraping, follow the principle of thin scraping each time and repeated application.

Drying

  1. Key points

    • The applied plastic poly-putty should be forcibly dried.

    • This position should be heated to about 50 °C with an infrared lamp to solidify the poly-putty.

    Caution

    • Set the time of heating and curing according to instructions in the product manual of putty.

    • Control the temperature of drying plate to avoid excessive temperature that can cause deformation of plastic parts.

Grind the poly-putty

  1. Key points

    • Grind the poly-putty to restore the original shape of bumper.

    • After the bumper is cooled, paste sandpaper #120 on a double-action grinder or manual grinding plate to grind the surface of plastic poly-putty. Repair the external profile of the damaged area, and finally grind it with sandpaper #240 to remove coarse sandpaper marks.

    • Grind a slightly larger area with sandpaper #320, and reserve the intermediate paint application area.

    • After grinding, clean and degrease the ground part and its surroundings, and paste masking paper on the area where intermediate paint is applied.

    Reminder
    Carefully ground and correct if the damaged area contains the body contour or is at the edge of plate.

Application of intermediate paint

  1. Key points

    • Apply intermediate paint in the ground area to remove defects such as sandpaper marks or sand holes on the surface.

    • Spray a proper layer of intermediate paint and dry them for about 10 minutes.

    • Add soft additives to the intermediate paint to avoid cracking.

    Reminder

    • Dry the intermediate primer according to requirements in the product manual.

    • Add the soft additive based on softness of the plastic parts with reference to the product instructions.

Grinding of intermediate paint

  1. Key points

    • Grind the intermediate paint to remove defects on the coating surface and prepare for subsequent coating repair.

    • Apply nitrocellulose poly-putty to the part with pinholes or sandpaper scratches. After the nitrocellulose poly-putty is dried, use #400 or higher model of sandpaper to grind the intermediate paint and prepare for spraying top coat area.

    • For local coating, use sandpaper #600 to grind the intermediate paint area. Use scouring pad and abrasive paste for grinding along the periphery where intermediate paint is applied.

    Reminder

    • Due to the soft additives added in the primer, the drying time of the primer will be longer, and the primer will be slightly soft when polished, which will lead to poor polishing.

    • The substrate shall be polished to a dull surface. Insufficient polishing may cause poor adhesion and paint peeling.

Spraying of top coat

  1. Key points

    • Apply the top coat according to the coating type of repaired plastic parts.

    • Tune the color according to the repair requirements and add soft additives for the varnish coating.

    • After spraying, dry the top coat.

    Reminder
    During paint application of plastic parts, soft additive shall be used according to the product description of the coating manufacturer. If not used, it may cause cracks or poor adhesion in future.
