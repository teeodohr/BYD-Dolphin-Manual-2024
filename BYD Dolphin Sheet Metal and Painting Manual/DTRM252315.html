Spray gun operation method

Basic knowledge of spray gun

Purpose of spray gun

Reminder

  • The spray gun is used to mix the paint and air and spray the atomized paint evenly on the surface of the plate. The spray gun is a commonly used painting equipment. The atomization effect of spray gun has a great impact on the quality of painting and repair. There are many types and specifications of spray guns, which can be divided into automatic spray guns and manual spray guns based on the degree of automation.

  1. Purpose of spray gun

    • Select an appropriate spray gun according to the spraying requirements.

    • Perform correct and standardized operations.

    • Regularly clean and maintain the spray gun.

Working principle of spray gun

Reminder

  • When the compressed air with fast flow rate supplied by the air compressor is sprayed out through the central hole of the cap in the front of the spray gun, a negative pressure zone with lower pressure than atmospheric pressure will be generated in the front of the gun nozzle. Due to the pressure difference generated at the gun muzzle, the paint is sucked out of the paint tank. With the compressed air from the auxiliary holes at both ends of the central hole and the corner holes at both ends of the gun cap, the paint forms a very small fog and is in a diffusion state, and then it is sprayed on the surface of the object under the action of high-speed jet force of compressed air.

  1. Working principle of spray gun

    • The injection pressure of the spray gun comes from compressed air generated by the air compressor.

    • The paint is sucked out from the paint storage tank by the air pressure difference formed by the external environment.

    • When the paint is sprayed from the nozzle, it is sprayed on the surface of the object in the form of atomized small particles.

Classification of spray guns

  1. Classification by paint supply modes.

    Reminder

    The air spray gun is mainly used in the vehicle repair industry. The spray gun falls into different types according to the supply mode of paint. It can be divided into gravity type, updraft type and pressure type according to the delivery mode of paint or upper pot type and lower pot type according to the position of the pot. This manual mainly introduces two types of spray guns commonly used in the maintenance work of the sheet spray shop, namely, the upper pot type and the lower pot type.

    1. Pressure feed spray gun

      • Pressurize the paint with a paint pressure pot or paint pump, and deliver the pressurized paint to the nozzle of the spray gun for atomization and spraying.

      • It is suitable for medium-sized production enterprises that require efficient large-area painting operations. A pressure feed spray gun needs a long time to be cleaned before it is used to spray paint of another color, making it less suitable for small-area spraying operations.

    2. Upper cup spray gun (gravity feed)

      • The paint cup is located on the top of the spray gun. The paint flows into the nozzle by gravity and negative pressure at the nozzle. When the gravity feed spray gun is spraying, compressed air will generate negative pressure at the air cap, and the paint will enter the spray gun through the joint action of negative pressure and gravity. The paint will be atomized at the air cap and finally sprayed out from the nozzle.

      • Advantages: It minimizes fluctuations in spraying volume due to changes in paint viscosity.

      • Disadvantages: The capacity of its paint cup is too small, making it not suitable for large-area spraying.

      • Typically, the paint cup capacity of the gravity feed spray gun is around 600 ml. The vent hole on the top of the paint cup should be kept clear.

      • The gravity feed spray gun is suitable for small-area operations, such as local repairs with low paint consumption.

      • Disposable gun can be used to improve efficiency and save paint.

    3. Lower cup spray gun (suction feed)

      • The paint cup is located below the nozzle. The suction feed spray gun utilizes high-speed airflow to generate vacuum at the air cap, and then sucks the paint from the cup into the nozzle for atomization and spraying.

      • Advantages: The paint cup has a larger capacity, making the suction feed spray gun suitable for large-area spraying.

      • Disadvantages: The amount of paint sprayed is significantly affected by the paint viscosity and density. Paint output becomes lower at higher viscosity, and also it is closely related to the nozzle size of the spray gun.

      • For spraying the same kind of paint, the suction feed spray gun typically has a larger nozzle diameter.

      • The paint cup capacity of the suction feed spray gun is usually around 1L, facilitating large-area spraying.

  2. Classified according to the function of spray gun.

    Reminder

    The spray gun can also be divided into primer spray gun, finish spray gun and small repair spray gun according to the type of paint sprayed. During workshop maintenance, the painting technician shall reasonably select the spray gun according to the type of paint used and the plate to be painted.

    1. Top coat spray gun

      • Spraying requirements of top coat spray gun. Atomizing the paint into fine particles and thin paint film can ensure that the paint film texture of the sprayed top coat layer is relatively thin and uniform.

      • The top coat spray gun is mainly used for spraying single-stage topcoat, two-stage paint and varnish. The key to top coat spraying is to ensure even color distribution and good leveling performance. Therefore, the top coat spray gun differs from the primer spray gun in terms of spray pattern in that the atomization layer is wider than the middle wet layer.

      Reminder

      • Recommended spraying distance: 15-20cm.

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.0-1.5mm.

      Caution

      • Due to the small caliber of the topcoat spray gun and the high atomization fineness of the paint, spraying the primer with the topcoat spray gun will lead to thin paint film and insufficient filling. If the number of spraying passes is increased, the work efficiency will be reduced, and the workload of paint dust and surface grinding on the coating surface will be increased.

    2. Primer spray gun

      • It is used for spraying anti-rust primer and intermediate primer. The key point is to ensure good filling of the primer layer and uniform paint atomization, but its atomization fineness is not as good as that of top coat spray gun.

      • Spraying requirements of primer spray gun:

        1. Fast drying of intermediate primers.

        2. Larger nozzle size.

        3. Concentrated spray pattern with fewer atomization areas after spraying.

        4. It is suitable for spraying operations with relatively high viscosity and thick paint film such as primer spraying.

      Reminder

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.5-2.0mm.

      • The elliptical spray range of the spray gun generally has three layers, namely wet area, atomization area and excessive atomization area, which are used to ensure the filling and polishing properties of the intermediate primer. The spray range of the primer spray gun is relatively concentrated, and the central wet area is large while the surrounding atomization area is small.

    3. Comparison of spray pattern between top coat and primer spray guns.

      • The elliptical spray pattern of the spray gun is divided into three areas: innermost central wet layer, middle atomization layer, and outer transitional atomization layer.

      • The top coat is mainly used for coloring and decorating the surface of the coated object.

      • The primer is used to fill the sandpaper marks or sand holes on the surface of the coated object, providing a foundation for the top coat layer to prevent coating defects.

    4. Minor repair spray gun

      • Minor repair spray guns are specially designed for small-area repair, making local repair work easier.

      • It features light weight, smaller nozzle size, easy application of thin coatings, and effective control of spraying area. When used for aluminum paint and pearl paint repair, it reduces the occurrence of "black circles".

      Reminder

      • Use 0.7-2.0bar air pressure and adjust the amount of paint output according to the shape and scope of the local area to be repaired.

      • Spray several times thinly, and control the color and cover slowly.

      • Spraying distance: 10- 15cm

      • Diameter of spray gun: 0.8-1.2mm.

  3. Classification according to the atomization technology of spray gun.

    1. Conventional high-pressure spray gun

      • The conventional high-pressure spray gun, as a standard atomizing spray gun, features a typical spray pressure of 0.3-0.5 MPa, an air consumption of about 380 L/min and a typical spraying distance of 18-23 cm.

      • Advantages: fast air spraying speed and effective atomization.

      • Disadvantages: High-speed spraying leads to high paint rebound rate and relatively low paint transfer efficiency. This type of spray gun is gradually being replaced.

      • Paint transfer efficiency: 30-40%.

    2. Reduced pressure spray gun

      • The reduced pressure spray gun (RP spray gun) utilizes optimized pressure reduction spraying technology to appropriately reduce the air pressure of the air cap, so as to achieve balanced air consumption and spraying pressure, ensuring efficient atomization of the paint.

      • It combines the fast working speed characteristic of conventional high-pressure spray guns and the high efficiency advantage of high-volume low-pressure (HVLP) spray guns. The pressure on the spray gun cap is significantly reduced, resulting in higher transfer efficiency.

      • The spraying pressure is usually 0.2 Mpa, the air consumption is about 290 L/min, and the spraying distance is generally 15-20 cm.

      • Without deliberately changing the painting method of the coating personnel, the working efficiency can be improved, at the same time, a lot of painting costs can be saved, and the working environment can be greatly improved.

      • Paint transfer efficiency: 65%.

    3. High-volume low-pressure spray gun

      • The HVLP spray gun uses a large volume of low-pressure air to atomize the paint. In general, the atomization pressure at the air cap is 0.07 MPa or lower. Due to the lower spraying speed, paint wastage caused by rebound is reduced compared to conventional high-pressure spray guns, resulting in high paint transfer efficiency.

      • The requirements for air compressors and air supply systems are higher. Generally, the inner diameter of compressed air main pipeline shall be around 50 mm, that of branch pipeline shall be around 25 mm, and that of air supply hose shall be around 9-10 mm.

      • The spraying pressure is usually 0.2 Mpa, the air consumption is about 430 L/min, and the spraying distance is generally 10-15 cm.

      • Paint transfer efficiency: above 65%.

    4. Comparison of spray atomization technology

      • Advantages of HVLP spray gun: reduce the atomization pressure of air cap.

      • Reduce excessive paint atomization and dust.

      • Reduce paint rebound from the workpiece.

      • Reduce the consumption of paint.

      • Reduce pollution to the working environment.

      • Reduce health hazards.

Structure of spray gun

  1. Air cap

    Reminder

    The spray gun is mainly composed of body, nozzle, cap, etc. The gun body is composed of handle, air knob, paint amount knob, sector knob, pot interface, trigger, etc. Understanding the structure of the spray gun and the function of each component is helpful to the selection, use, maintenance and troubleshooting of the spray gun. No matter what kind of spray gun, its structure and principle are basically the same. This section introduces the structure of the spray gun.

    1. It is one of the components that have the greatest influence on the performance of spray gun. According to different design purposes of spray gun, there are many styles for the position, size, dimension and angle of atomizing hole.

      • In the center of the air cap, there is a circular center hole between the nozzle and the air cap, through which the paint is drawn out under the negative pressure of the nozzle and the center hole.

      • Next to the center hole, there are auxiliary atomization holes that help atomize the paint. The more auxiliary holes, the finer the atomization particles.

      • The outer periphery of the air cap has air horn holes, which are used to control the shape of the paint mist and adjust the effect of spray pattern.

      • The more auxiliary atomization holes, the better the atomization of the paint.

      • During actual operation, the air cap can be rotated to change the direction of spray pattern.

  2. Nozzle and needle

    1. Nozzle and needle

      • They are the main components that work in conjunction with the air cap for atomization. They supply air and paint to the central part in front of the air cap.

      • After the trigger is hooked, the needle leaves the nozzle, and the paint is sprayed out from the nozzle after passing through the paint channel.

      • The flow rate of the paint is determined by the stroke of the needle moving away from the nozzle.

      Reminder

      • The nozzle caliber refers to the caliber at the front of the nozzle. The topcoat spray gun uses 1.0-1.5mm nozzle, and the primer spray gun uses 1.5-2.0mm nozzle. Select the nozzle caliber based on the paint type, generally referring to the paint manufacturer's spraying standard or instructions in the product manual.

  3. Paint cup

    1. Paint cup

      • Also known as the "paint pot," it is used to hold the paint.

      • Rotate for removal.

      • Rotate for installation.

    2. Bad cases

      • Too much paint is added to the paint cup, and the paint overflows and drips onto the spraying plate during application.

      • After the spraying work is completed, the paint pot shall be cleaned in time to avoid the paint from adhering to it after drying.

  4. Flow adjustment knob

    1. Flow adjustment knob

      • The flow rate of the spray gun is determined by the stroke of the needle. When the knob is loosened, the needle stroke increases, resulting in a higher spraying flow. When the knob is tightened, the needle stroke decreases, resulting in a lower spraying flow.

    2. How to set the flow adjustment knob

      • Set the spraying air pressure to maintain a pressure of 2 bar at the gun tail.

      • Set the spray distance to 20 cm between the air cap and the test paper.

      • Turn on all the spray pattern adjustment.

      • Test the spray pattern to the best and determine the number of paint turns.

      • Tighten the flow adjustment knob to the minimum, and then release it for 1-1.5 turns. Carry out spraying test on the test paper, with 0.5 turn added each time until the spray pattern no longer increases, and observe the atomization effect of spray pattern after spraying.

      • The spray pattern requires uniform atomization of paint, and a reasonable width of the wet area in the center of the spray pattern is the best.

      • Rotate the air cap by 90° to test the horizontal spray pattern. If the streaks are longer on the sides and shorter in the center (Fig. b), it indicates that the paint output adjustment is too small or the air pressure is too high, and such a result may also occur if the fan width is too wide or the paint viscosity is too low.

      • If the streak in the center is longer than on the sides (Fig. c), it indicates that the paint output is too much or the air pressure adjustment is too small, and such a result may also occur if the fan width is too narrow or the paint viscosity is too high.

      • The standard spray pattern should be as shown in (Fig. a), with the paint sagging length roughly equal at all positions.

  5. Air pressure adjustment knob

    1. Air pressure adjustment knob

      • This knob is mainly used to adjust the air flow of compressed air.

      • When the knob is loosened, the flow of compressed air increases, resulting in higher paint atomization degree.

      • When the knob is tightened, the flow of compressed air decreases, resulting in lower paint atomization degree.

      Reminder

      • The atomization effect and spraying quality will be affected if the pressure of the spray gun is too high or too low. When spraying paints of different types onto plates of different sizes, adjust the pressure of the spray gun reasonably.

      • The best spraying pressure is to ensure the spray range and the best atomization effect.

      • Too high air pressure will lead to excessive atomization, which will produce too much paint mist and lead to an increase in the amount of paint. It will also lead to a lot of solvent volatilization before the paint reaches the plate, resulting in orange peel and other defects.

      • Too low air pressure will make the atomized particles coarser and the paint film thicker, which may lead to sagging, solvent bubble and other defects.

      • Use a gas pressure gauge for the spray gun or install a gas pressure gauge at the gun end.

  6. Spray pattern adjustment knob

    1. Spray pattern adjustment knob

      • This knob is used to adjust the range of spray pattern.

      • Release the knob to increase the spray pattern, showing an elliptical shape.

      • Tighten the knob to reduce the spray pattern, showing a round shape.

      Reminder

      • Use the sector knob to adjust the spray range (sector). Tightening the sector knob to the minimum will reduce the diameter of the paint mist sector and make it become a circle. Unscrewing the sector knob will make the paint mist shape become a wide oval.

      • The narrower sector is used for local repair, while the wider sector is used for large area painting such as entire plate painting and vehicle painting.

  7. Trigger

    1. Spray gun trigger

      • The trigger of the spray gun operates in two steps. When the trigger is pulled (Gear 1), only air is sprayed first, and it can also be used for dust blowing. If the trigger is pulled continuously (Gear 2), air and paint will be sprayed, and the paint flowing through the nozzle will be atomized by the sprayed air at the same time.

      Reminder

      • After pulling the trigger, only compressed air is sprayed at first. It can be seen from the profile of the spray gun that the air is transmitted through the compressed air (blue) channel inside the spray gun. The compressed air arrives at each hole of the gun cap through the blue channel and is ejected at a high speed.

      • Continue to pull the trigger, the nozzle opens, and the paint is sucked out from the nozzle along the red pipe and atomized.

Operation skills of spray gun

Key points of spray gun movement

Reminder

Correct adjustment of the spray gun and standardized technique and spray gun operation are the key points of high-quality spraying. This chapter focuses on how to hold the spray gun, the use of air pipes during spraying and other spraying points.

  1. How to hold the spray gun

    • During spraying, only by mastering the correct holding method and posture of the spray gun can the equipment be brought into full play. Attention should be paid to the coordination among hands, eyes and body. Only with proper coordination of the whole body can the spray gun's effectiveness be fully realized.

    • The commonly used gripping method is to use the thumb and palm in conjunction with the pinky, middle finger, and ring finger to operate the trigger, while the index finger stabilizes the spray gun. Alternatively, the spray gun can be held using the palm, thumb, pinky, and ring finger, with the middle finger and index finger used to operate the trigger.

    Reminder

    • During the spray gun operation, keep the eyes on the spraying area and observe the coating effect.

    • Body. Never bend your wrist, but instead move the whole body. The wrist should be flexible.

Air hose placement

Reminder

During the spraying process, properly place the air hose to avoid inconvenience to the operator and affecting the spraying effect. It is necessary to understand how to properly place the air hose.

  1. Placement of the air hose

    • Cross the air hose from back to shoulder, and avoid stepping on or bending the hose.

    • Use the other hand to hold the hose behind the back.

    Reminder

    • Avoid air hose entanglement during operation.

    • Stepping on the air hose or bending the hose will interrupt the flow of compressed air and the paint will spray out in the form of droplets.

Essentials of body movement

Reminder

During the operation, the spraying personnel body shall move with it as required. The standard moving mode has an important impact on the painting effect. Before painting, make a reasonable plan for the standing position and spraying range. Make the spraying range be within the reasonable extension range of the arm as far as possible.

  1. Essentials of body movement.

    • When standing, the shoulder on the side holding the gun should align with the center of the object being sprayed. Keep the feet slightly wider than shoulder width and slightly bend the knees.

    • During spraying, the entire body should move with the hip as the center, instead of just moving the wrist.

    • When moving towards the lower part of the plate, gradually lower the hip and adjust the body height accordingly.

Spraying sequence of spray gun

Reminder

Plan the spraying route reasonably for plate painting. The general spraying principles are: Spraying from high to low, from left to right, from top to bottom, and from inside to outside. Do any eaves and corners first. Work from the top down.

  1. Spraying sequence of plates.

    • When moving the spray gun, the spraying range should be about 5 to 10 cm wider than the width of the steel plate on both sides.

    • Pull the trigger before the spray gun reaches the steel plate surface and release it after leaving the steel plate surface. Do not release the trigger completely at this time, otherwise the compressed air flow will stop. Only release the trigger to the point where the paint spraying stops.

    • When spraying the upper and lower edges of the plate, start and finish spraying at positions beyond the surface of the steel plate.

    Reminder

    • Pull the trigger before the spray gun reaches the steel plate surface, and release the trigger after it leaves the surface.

Key points of spray gun operation

Reminder

For high-quality spraying, correct adjustment of spray gun is the first key point, and correct technique is the second key point. During the use of the spray gun, constantly move and adjust the position of the spray gun according to the shape of the plate. Note the operation essentials of the spray gun: Spraying distance, spraying angle, spraying overlap, spray gun moving speed.

Spray gun distance requirements

Reminder

The spraying distance generally refers to the linear length from the atomizing hole of the air cap of the spray gun to the surface of the object to be sprayed. During spraying, always make the parallel distance between the spray gun and the workpiece to be sprayed consistent. Keep the spray gun at a right angle to the plane. Ensure the spray gun moves at a stable and consistent speed along the surface of the plate. Never swing the wrist or elbow.

  1. Spray gun distance requirements.

    • The spray gun must be kept parallel to the plate surface during application.

    • The specific distance between the spray gun and the steel plate in actual work shall be determined according to the actual situation of the work, generally about 15-20 cm.

    Reminder

    • The spraying distance is too close. A large amount of paint will be applied on the plate, resulting in sagging.

    • The spraying distance is too far. The paint sprayed on the surface is reduced, resulting in rough coating, poor brightness or orange peel.

Skills for spraying transition junction area

  1. Skills for spraying transition junction area.

    • When performing junction repairs, the spray gun needs to move in an arc to ensure even coverage in the transition area or gradually thinning the paint in the junction area for color transition.

    • Pull the trigger at the beginning of spraying, while moving the gun in an arc shape and release it as it moves to the edge away from the painting area.

    Reminder

    • Swing the wrist and make arc movement in the transition area of the splice, so that the distance of the spray gun gradually increases.

    • Move the spray gun in an arc shape (like drawing an arc) to thin the paint film in the splice area.

    • During the transitional spraying, reduce the air pressure and paint output appropriately.

Spraying angle requirements

Reminder

Note the spraying angle. The angle deviation of will affect the spraying effect. Keep the spray gun perpendicular to the surface (plane, vertical plane, side plane, or slope). Incorrect spraying angle will make the angle between the spray gun and the surface change, resulting in uneven film.

  1. Spraying angle requirements.

    • Make sure that the spray gun is perpendicular to the spraying surface during painting.

    Reminder

    • The surface shapes of the body plates are complex. To avoid uneven film, ensure that the spray gun is perpendicular to the plate.

    • Otherwise, the coating thickness will be different, the coating will be rough, and the coverage will be poor.

Moving speed of spray gun

Reminder

Due to the different performance and drying speed of the paints, the moving speed of the spray gun shall vary according to the drying speed of the paint and the operation requirements. When operating the spray gun, the moving speed of the spray gun shall be uniform and stable. Or uneven coating thickness will occur. The moving speed of the spray gun affects the spraying quality. Normally, it should be kept at a speed of 30-60cm/s.

  1. Moving speed of spray gun.

    • It is the moving speed of spray gun during spraying.

    • It depends on the type of coating and spraying requirements, as well as the spraying distance and overlapping spray pattern.

    Reminder

    • The gun moves too fast. It will make the paint too dry, produce rough surface, orange peel, and poor gloss and clarity.

    • The gun moves too slowly. It is easy to cause sagging. And it may lead to accumulation and floating when spraying metallic paint.

Key points of overlapping spray pattern

Reminder

The spray range of the spray gun is usually oval, and the paint around the spray range is generally thinner. If the spray range overlaps poorly, the film thickness will be uneven and streaks will appear. There should be a reasonable overlap between the spray range of each layer and the previous layer. Each spray range should have an overlap of not less than 1/2, so as to ensure the flatness of the paint film.

  1. Overlapping spray pattern.

    • It refers to the overlap with the previously sprayed width.

    • If the spray patterns do not overlap or overlap unevenly, the coating thickness will not be uniform.

    Reminder

    • The paint sprayed by the spray gun is thick in the middle and thin at the edge. During spraying, it should overlap the previous spray range by 1/2 to 2/3, depending on the type of paint used.

Cleaning and maintenance of spray gun

Purpose of spray gun cleaning

Reminder

  • To ensure the service life and spraying quality of the spray gun, properly clean and maintain the spray gun. Clean the spray gun immediately after use, especially for two-component paint. If it is not cleaned in time, the paint and other residues will remain in the paint tank or the gun nozzle will be blocked, which will affect the next use or even lead to the damage or scrapping of the spray gun.

  1. Purpose of spray gun cleaning.

    • If the spray gun is not cleaned in time after use, the paint may harden inside the paint channel, leading to gun malfunction or failure.

    • During the cleaning process, the spray gun should not be soaked in thinner for a long time, so as to avoid the expansion of the internal sealing material and failure.

    Reminder

    • The paint sprayed by the spray gun is thick in the middle and thin at the edge. During spraying, it should overlap the previous spray range by 1/2 to 2/3, depending on the type of paint used.

Cleaning method of spray gun

Reminder

  • There are two methods for cleaning the spray gun, one is manual cleaning, and the other is cleaning with a gun washer. No matter which method is used, the key is to clean the gun pot, paint channel, cap and nozzle.

  1. Removal of components.

    • To remove the paint inside the air cap and paint cup, take off the paint cup and air cap, and clean them with a brush and solvent.

    • If it is necessary to clean the needle, unscrew the paint output adjustment knob of the built-in spring, pull out the spring and stainless steel needle, and carefully clean the needle with a brush and solvent to prevent damage, bending and deformation.

  2. Paint channel cleaning.

    • First, pour the remaining paint into a designated waste collection container. Pour the diluent into the paint cup, and spray out the residual paint in the spray gun several times.

    • Block the front end of nozzle with a piece of wiping paper, and pull the trigger to reverse the paint and thinner to clean the interior of the paint channel.

    • Repeat the above steps until the interior of paint channel is clean.

Maintenance of spray gun

Reminder

  • To maintain the effective and normal spraying of the spray gun, routine maintenance must be carried out. Poor maintenance or cleaning will cause malfunction and damage of the spray gun.

  1. Maintenance of spray gun.

    • Regularly check the needle valve, sealing washers, and air valve washers for any leaks. Replace them promptly if there are any leaks. Apply a proper amount of lubricating oil to the gun needle, air valve spring and sealing ring to facilitate sliding.

    • Do not remove the spray gun at will. When it must be removed, be careful that the cap and nozzle shall never be damaged. After reassembly, readjust them and pull the trigger to test the spraying effect of compressed air and paint.

    • The spray gun shall not collide with the coated plate or fall on the ground during use, otherwise it will be damaged and cannot be used.

    • After the cleaned spray gun is assembled, it shall be placed and stored properly. It can be placed in a spray gun holder or toolbox to protect the nozzle and air cap from damage.

    Caution

    • After cleaning the spray gun, never pour solvent into the paint tank or immerse the spray gun in the solvent as a whole, which may cause corrosion of the gun body or premature damage of the spray gun, affecting the atomization effect and spraying quality.

Analysis of spray pattern defects

Reminder

  • Faults may be caused by improper use, poor cleaning and maintenance, aging and other reasons of the spray gun. When the spray gun fails, a qualified spraying technician will analyze the reason based on the symptoms. If the fault is caused by blockage and uncleanness, clean the spray gun. If the spray gun is worn or damaged, send it to the supplier for maintenance.

  1. The bottom or top pattern of the spray pattern is large.

    1. Possible causes

      • Clogged or damaged air cap vents.

      • Clogged or damaged nozzle.

    2. Solution

      • First try to spray the pattern, then rotate it by 180° to spray again, and check the sprayed pattern.

      • If it is still in the original direction, the nozzle is blocked or damaged.

      • If the pattern direction changes, the problem is with the air cap, which needs cleaning or replacement.

  2. The spray pattern bends in a crescent shape to the left or right.

    1. Possible causes

      • Insufficient atomization pressure from the air cap.

      • Paint viscosity is too high.

      • The paint output flow is large, resulting in excessive paint.

    2. Solution

      • Increase the air pressure during spraying.

      • Add diluent to reduce viscosity.

      • Choose a nozzle with smaller diameter to reduce the liquid flow.

  3. Intermittent spray of the spray gun.

    1. Possible causes

      • Damaged sealing gaskets in the spray gun.

      • Loose or damaged nozzle during installation.

      • Insufficient paint in the paint cup or the paint cup is loose.

      • There is air in the paint channel.

    2. Solution

      • Replace the sealing washer with a new one.

      • Reinstall the nozzle to proper tightness.

      • Add paint.

      • Clean the spray gun.
