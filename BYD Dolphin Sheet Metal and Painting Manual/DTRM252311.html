Color mixing of metallic paint

Introduction to metallic paint

What is metallic paint?

  1. Metallic paint body

    • Unlike plain paints that only use tinting pigments and present the same color at different observation angles, metallic paints contain glittering pigments with special gloss under light and can exhibit different colors at different observation angles.

Coating structure of metallic paint

  1. Coating structure of metallic paint

    • The metallic gloss will glitter when the light irradiates tinting pigments and glittering pigments (such as aluminum or mica).

    • The coating process of the original vehicle usually involves 4 times of coating and 3 times of baking.

    • Colors vary depending on the viewing angle.

    • Metallic paint includes: aluminum paint, pearl paint and other two-process paint obtained by mixing special pigments. In case of three-process pearl paint, it is usually sprayed 5 times and baked 4 times.

Color change due to spraying conditions

  1. Color change of dry and wet coatings of paint

    1. Comparison of wet and dry coatings

      • Color varies with spraying conditions. Although the mixing proportion of colors is the same, the structural arrangement of aluminum pigments in metal colors during wet coating and dry coating is different, causing color changes.

      Caution

      • When applying paint on the color test plate, ensure the application conditions are stable.

      • When applying paint on the color test plate, the application conditions shall be the same as when the actual vehicle is applied.

      Reminder

      • In the actual plate spraying, the paint mixing, spray gun setting, and spraying environment will affect the arrangement effect of aluminum powder pigment in the coating, that is, they will affect the color.

  2. Arrangement rules of pigments in coatings

    • During the drying process of paint, the color masterbatches are arranged. The pigments with lighter specific gravity float up and those with greater specific gravity sink down.

    • Specific gravity:

    • White: 3.7-3.9.

    • Black: 1.8-2.0.

    • Blue: 1.5-1.6.

    Reminder

    When correcting the paint color, spray the color test plate for confirmation. Add the curing agent and diluent and spray varnish according to the standard proportion. Wait until the paint is completely dry and then confirm the color. Otherwise, the color may not be accurately determined due to the large difference between the dry and wet colors.

Common pigment types

Reminder

Pigment is used for coloring and providing color effect in paint. Pigments falls into inorganic pigments and organic pigments according to the type of components used.

  1. Inorganic pigments

    • Inorganic pigments contain processed natural materials (e.g. minerals).

    • They usually exhibit excellent weather and heat resistance and excellent covering power, but are not as vivid as organic pigments.

    Reminder

    The main components are: zinc, titanium, lead, iron, copper and other metal compounds.

  2. Organic pigments

    • Organic pigments are those synthesized from petrochemicals.

    • They are usually finer and more vivid than inorganic ones, but they have poor covering power.

  3. Metallic pigments (aluminium pigments)

    • They are made of aluminum powder and exhibit unique metallic gloss under light.

    • The difference in surface size, roughness and other properties determines various types of pigments.

    • These pigments do not allow light to pass through and reflect almost all wavelengths of light.

    • Metallic pigments (aluminium pigments) include standard type, white type and bright type.

    • Standard type: Many shadows may be formed on the surface, so this type of pigment is darker than other types.

    • White type: The surface is curved and reflects light from all angles, so this type of pigment glitters white light when viewed from all directions. Therefore, metal pigments are relatively white.

    • Bright type: The surface is flat, and the brightness from front vision and side vision is quite different. Therefore, this type of pigment has a stronger glittering effect than other types, giving off dazzling light when viewed directly. It is brighter than the other two types.

    Reminder

    The type of pigment varies according to the paint manufacturer's processing techniques.

  4. Pearl mica pigments

    • This kind of pigment consists of translucent mica wrapped in titanium dioxide or other colorants, whose surface reflects part of the light and allows the rest to pass through.

    • The reflected light color of the mica pigment varies with the thickness of titanium dioxide, and this pigment is translucent.

    • Commonly used mica pigment types include muscovite pigment, interference mica pigment, tinting mica pigment and silver mica pigment.

  5. Graphite pigments

    • The figure shows the comparison between graphite pigments and ordinary black pigments.

    • Graphite pigment is made of a kind of carbon crystal.

    • The average particle diameter is about 5 μm.

    • The covering effect is worse than that of ordinary black pigment.

  6. Milk white pigments

    • A translucent pigment using titanium dioxide approximately 1/10 of the standard white pigment.

    • The reflected light appears light yellow when the pigment is viewed directly, and light blue-green transmitted light flashes when viewed obliquely.

    • The covering effect is worse than that of ordinary white pigment.

Knowledge of vehicle color identification and mixing

Color identification process

  1. Light source - object - observer

    • The light source emits light, which strikes an object (such as a vehicle). Part of the light is reflected by the object and part absorbed. The human eye receives the signal, and the human brain subjectively interprets and identifies the color.

  2. Light and color

    • Reflection: An object striken by light reflects its own color.

    • Absorption: A striken object absorbs all kinds of light except the light of its own color.

    • Transmission: Light passes through transparent objects similar to glass windows.

    Reminder

    The energy of the absorbed light is converted into heat energy.

Process of light reflection

  1. Process of light reflection

    • Light does not stay on the paint film surface, but is reflected and absorbed between pigment particles through the paint film surface.

    • The figure shows the light scattering between pigment particles in the paint film.

    Reminder

    • Isotropy means a light source that theoretically radiates a signal with equal power density in all directions. Isotropic scattering means that the radiation of light in each different direction is the same in 3D space.

    • If the light penetrates the surface to the primer layer, the light will be affected by the base color. Therefore, colored intermediate coat can affect the color of the topcoat layer.

Color wheel and color system

  1. Color wheel and color system

    1. Color decomposition

      • When a prism is used to carry out dispersion experiment and analyze the color composition of sunlight, the color band refracted from the prism is called spectrum.

      • Visible light is dispersed into multiple colors by refraction or diffraction.

      Reminder

      • A rainbow can occur when water droplets are uniform enough so that many millions of them cause light to be scattered from all at the same angle.

      • The spectrum starts from red and ends with purple. There will be no fuchsia, because purple and red are not connected in the spectrum. However, when light encounters fuchsia objects, purple and red will be reflected, and we will perceive them as red.

    2. Color tree

      • The color tree can reflect each color clearly based on: hue, chroma and lightness. If these three are used to describe a color, the color can be precisely defined and distinguished.

      • Hue: When a color must be described, its hue is usually mentioned first. Hue is the basis to identify a color, such as red, orange, green or blue.

      • Chroma (saturation and purity): To know whether a color is dim or bright, it is based on its chroma. Chroma describes whether a color area is close to gray or pure color. For example, when comparing a tomato with a carrot, the tomato exhibits more intense red, while the carrot exhibits weaker red.

      • Lightness (value): It is a further feature of a color. Colors can be distinguished according to their lightness levels, whether they are light (bright) or dark (dim). For example, if a tomato and a carrot are placed together, the tomato is usually brighter and the carrot is less bright.

    3. Munsell color system

      • In the Munsell color system, all colors are set along the same axis according to their lightness and chroma.

      • The color system reflects the change of three characteristic attributes: hue, chroma and lightness. The hue difference between adjacent colors is the same as that in visual sense.

      • The change degree of lightness and chroma between adjacent colors gives people the same feeling. For this reason, the color difference system is an asymmetrical structure that looks like a tree.

Base color characteristics

  1. Three primary colors

    • Blue, red and yellow are the three primary colors (also base colors), and their important characteristic is that they cannot be obtained through the mixing of other colors.

  2. Secondary colors

    • A secondary color is a mixture of two adjacent primary colors. A second-order color as a result of the combination of two primary colors is called a "secondary color".

    • Secondary colors are obtained from combinations of any two of the three primary colors: orange (red + yellow), purple (red + blue) and green (yellow + blue).

  3. Tertiary colors

    • The combination of a primary color and a secondary color or of two secondary colors yields tertiary colors. Depending on the color mixing ratio, the result may be more red, blue or yellow.

    • A tertiary color contains all the elements of the three primary colors. Therefore, all the tertiary colors are broken colors with reduced value and chroma. The tertiary colors contain a large number of brown hues. Human eyes can distinguish more than 100,000 colors. In fact, among the colors of automotive paint repair, tertiary colors are the most commonly used.

  4. Complementary colors

    • Colors opposite each other on the color wheel are called complementary colors.

    • The complementary color of a specific color depends on its position on the color wheel.

    Reminder

    • Mixing a pair of complementary colors will form black. For example, magenta complements green. Mixing pure green with pure red will get black color.

Metamerism phenomenon

  1. Metamerism

    • The color of an object will change with the light source.

    • If two objects are considered to exhibit the same color under one light source, but exhibit completely different colors under another light source, this phenomenon is called "metamerism".

    Reminder

    • When two colors appear to match under one lighting condition, such as sunlight, but not when the light changes, such as incandescent lights, this is called metamerism.

    • Metamerism is common when a product is assembled using different pigments, but not ideal same pigments (for example: green pigment versus mixture of blue and yellow pigments).

  2. Color observation under different light sources

    • Compare the spaying color plate with OEM standard plate in the light source color change chamber: D65 daylight, incandescent light and sodium light.

    • Observe the color plate from different angles with eyes, and decide whether the color plate is satisfactory or not.

    Reminder

    • Methods to avoid metamerism:

    • No manual color matching.

    • Keep the lights clean.

    • Use existing color formulas whenever possible.

    • Use suitable lighting sources.

    • Make the wall of spray booth (or paint mixing room) white.

Color mixing method of metallic paint

Color mixing by measurement

Reminder

  • Personal protective equipment: goggles, respirators, solvent-proof gloves.

  • Articles required: paint mixing container, stirring rod, electronic scale, mixing ruler, and color atla information.

  1. Comparison color data

    Reminder

    • It is extremely important to confirm the mixed color by checking the information provided by the paint manufacturer.

    • Fully understanding the color information will shorten the toning time and avoid problems.

    • Before toning, confirm the color effect, flashing effect of metal particles, and coating glossiness of the color boards. In case of color aging, poor brightness, dirt on the surface of the plates, polish it to facilitate color comparison.

    1. With color data

      • Follow the order of checking vehicle body color, obtaining formula information, checking color card and vehicle body color, color mixing by measurement, trial spraying of color plate and color comparison, color fine-tuning and completion.

      Caution

      • Color comparison and color fine-tuning are the key to toning. If there are color differences, repeat this step several times.

    2. Without color data

      • Follow the order of checking vehicle body color, obtaining formula information, determining the same color, selecting ordinary color formula, preliminary color mixing, trial spraying of color plate and color comparison, final color mixing, color fine-tuning and completion.

      Caution

      • In case of uncertainty about the accuracy of color information, mix a small amount of paint to confirm the color.

  2. Determining color mixing formula

    • Determine the color formula according to the vehicle body color number and vehicle model color card.

    • If one color number corresponds to multiple color formulas, select the color that is close to the color of the target vehicle.

    Reminder

    • Prepare appropriate tools and environment for color comparison.

    • After confirming the color formula, find the formula information on the color formula query system provided by the paint brand.

  3. Masterbatch preparation

    • The color masterbatch for color mixing must be mixed for at least 15 minutes every morning and evening with the paint mixer. If a new color masterbatch is used, it can be vibrated with an oscillator for 20 minutes or stirred evenly with a paint mixing ruler before installing the slurry cover.

    Reminder

    • Store the new color master batch upside down to avoid pigment precipitation.

    • Clean the cover and opening after each use.

  4. Color mixing by measurement

    • Ensure that the paint is accurately weighed and mixed evenly.

    • Follow the corresponding color formula and pour the color masterbatch paint in the specified order.

    • Mix the paint evenly with a paint mixing ruler.

    Reminder

    • Select the appropriate size container based on the amount of paint and diluent required.

    • Accurately weigh the color master batch paint and add the color master batches one by one.

    • Repeat stirring of paint and scrape off all paints adhesive to the inner wall of the container with a mixing ruler.

    • Personal protective equipment: goggles, respirators, solvent-proof gloves.

    Caution

    • For the color master batch with small content and high concentration in the formula, ensure accurate weighing.

    • If the color master batch with a small proportion is poured first, the paint will be easy to stick to the inner wall of the container, resulting in uneven color. Ensure that the master batch paint is mixed evenly.

    • Use plastic containers or metal containers with internal coating for water-based color master batch paint. Never use metal containers directly.

Spraying color plate for color fine-tuning

Reminder

  • Personal protective equipment: goggles, respirators, solvent-proof gloves.

  • Articles required: paint mixing container, stirring rod, electronic scale, mixing ruler, and color atla information.

  1. Mixing color sample plate paint

    • Pour the required amount of color masterbatch into a container, add curing agent and thinner, and then mix them.

    • Add the water-based thinner into the water-based paint, and mix them even for varnish.

    Reminder

    • Select the diluent and curing agent according to the temperature and humidity of the spray booth.

    • Pour the paints into the containers according to the required amount, add solvent or curing agent, and mix them evenly.

    • Pour the mixed paint into the spray gun and spray the color board to confirm the color accuracy.

    • Follow the manufacturer's instructions for mixing ratios.

    • Select the solvent and curing agent according to the temperature of the spraying environment.

  2. Spraying color sample plate

    • Filter the thinned paint with a funnel and pour it into the spray gun.

    • Set the same spraying conditions as those for spraying vehicles.

    • Apply several coats of base paint and allow appropriate flash drying time.

    • Apply the varnish coating until the coated surface becomes glossy.

    • Dry the coating with an oven.

    Reminder

    • The base color coating shall ensure that the base color of the color test plate cannot be seen absolutely.

    • When spraying the first color board, use the coverage test paper to prevent poor coverage.

    Caution

    • If forced drying is carried out without sufficient curing time, pinholes will appear, which makes it difficult to compare colors accurately.

    • If the base color is required (when spraying the color with low coverage performance), spray the base color onto the whole color test plate first.

  3. Color comparison

    • Select an appropriate environment or conditions for color comparison.

    • Compare the color of the color sample plate with that of the original steel plate under sunlight or color mixing light.

    • Identify the color difference and determine the missing color.

    Reminder

    • The inspection shall be carried out from all angles, taking into account the influence of flash and other factors.

    • If the human eyes look at a color for a long time, it will produce visual error, so it is difficult to determine the difference between colors. Avoid visual fatigue during color comparison.

    • Identify the missing color according to color, hue, lightness and chroma.

    • For light colors, observe them under the condition of shady light to avoid too strong reflected light.

    • Before final color comparison, compare the colors under at least two different light sources to prevent metamer.

  4. Color difference analysis

    • Analyze the color difference of the sprayed color plate, and the characteristics of each color masterbatch in the color formula.

    Caution

    Observe the color at a proper angle and distance for color comparison, and check whether the color observed is influenced by the ambient environment.

  5. Color fine-tuning

    • Add the color masterbatch after analysis, stir it thoroughly, and then spray it on the plate again for confirmation.

    Caution

    • Never add multiple color master batches in one time. It is recommended to add the color master batch of about 10% of the formula amount every time, so as to avoid over hue toning.

    • Record the addition of color master batch for convenience of sorting out and archiving of color formulas.

  6. Use of the color masterbatch characteristic table for color fine-tuning

    • During fine-tuning, analyze the characteristics of each color masterbatch in the formula with the color masterbatch characteristic table to confirm whether the color difference analysis is correct.

    Reminder

    • The color master batch table is an effective tool guiding color difference analysis. Use the color atla of color master batch mixed with aluminum powder for observation when failing to judge the color difference.

  7. Color confirmation

    • Confirm the color plate and accuracy, and spray the color on the real vehicle after confirming its accuracy.

    • Repeat the steps: paint color sample plate, compare colors and add missing color masterbatch until the mixed color is closest to the target color.

    Caution

    • Observe the color board to avoid impact on the color by the ambient environment.

    • Keep the color board after the final toning, collect the color formula after final toning and attach it on the back of the color board for convenience of reference in future.

    • As for the color difference, it is recommended to keep the color board and color formula after color toning every time and set up a color database.

Key points of color comparison and adjustment

  1. Key points of color comparison

    1. Observation angle

      • Observe the color of the sample plate from three angles: front, middle and side.

      Caution

      Slightly move to the front position or a slightly tilted position for observation because the light is too strong when observing the aluminum powder paint in the frontal direction.

      Reminder

      • Angle of observation: the angle of color observation influences accuracy and correctness of color judgment.

      • Observe and check the chroma and hue at an angle of ±90°.

      • Observe and check the lightness at an angle of ±45°.

      • Observe and check the side lightness and color at an angle of ±15°.

    2. Observation distance

      • It refers to the vertical distance between the observer and the surface to be observed. The distance should vary with the size of the object to be observed.

      Caution

      Slightly move to the front or back position for observation because the light is too strong when observing the aluminum powder paint in the frontal direction.

      Reminder

      • Observe the metallic paint at multiple angles because it is different from the plain colored paint and flashes. Check the hue, lightness and chroma of colors.

    3. Dimensions of sample plate

      • The spraying color sample plate shall be 10*15 cm. If it is too small, it will be impossible to accurately determine the color difference.

      Caution

      • For color comparison, make sure the size of color test plate match with that of the observed zone on the steel plate.

      • Select flat zones at the edges of neighboring plates for observation in sample plate comparison.

    4. Spraying varnish coat

      • The color of the color plate depends on the applied coating thickness, so it is necessary to apply a coat of varnish and check the results when comparing the colors.

      • Although the surface may be temporarily lustered for color comparison, it is necessary to apply a coat of varnish and confirm that it is completely dry before color comparison.

  2. Key points of metallic paint color flash fine-tuning

    1. Metallic paint color flash

      • Color and gloss change according to different viewing angles, which is called "flash", also known as particle flickering.

      • For metallic paint, the flash can be adjusted in three ways, and the color should be fine-tuned while maintaining the balance between direct vision and oblique vision.

      Reminder

      • Carefully check flashing of the color at all angles in color comparison.

    2. Adjustment with spraying conditions

      • The flash can be adjusted by changing the thinner type, thinning ratio and spraying conditions of the spray gun (dry or wet spraying) and changing the arrangement of aluminum powder pigments.

    3. Use of additives

      • The arrangement of aluminum powder pigments is changed with additives to adjust the flash.

      • Two types of additives are mainly used depending on the conditions during color comparison.

      • Aluminum powder control agent: It makes the reflection of aluminum powder strong and black when directly observed.

      • Extender pigment (white masterbatch): It makes the reflection of aluminum powder turn white when observed indirectly.

      • Aluminum powder control agent: It enlarges the clearance between aluminum pigments, so that the flash pigments take on their original size and the hue of tinting pigments and other primary colors is visible.

      • Extender pigment: It is mixed with aluminum powder pigments to prevent the aluminum powder from being arranged flatly, which makes the aluminum powder pigments stand up, enhances the reflectivity when viewed obliquely, and thus brightens the surface.

    4. Adjusting aluminum powder particles

      • When using primary color particles of pigment for fine adjustment, first adjust the structure and mixing ratio of aluminum powder pigments, and then add tinting pigments to make the color consistent after glossiness adjustment under direct vision and oblique vision.

      Reminder

      The hues observed from the direct and oblique angles of view are different since the aluminum powder pigments are different from each other in structures.

    5. Selecting flash pigments

      • When fine-tuning two or more aluminum powder pigments, check the color masterbatch characteristic table to select an appropriate color masterbatch pigment, because the lightness under direct vision and oblique vision varies with different types of aluminum powder pigments.

      • The covering power varies with the roughness of aluminum powder pigments.

      Reminder

      • Check differences of colors and gloss from the direct and oblique angles of view during fine toning.

      • The tinting pigment is un-obvious since the fine aluminum powder pigment has strong hiding power.

      • The tinting pigment is obvious since the coarse aluminum powder pigment has poor hiding power.
