Removal and installation of EDR camera

Removal

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Remove the lane departure warning system protection cover II. Refer to Installation and Removal of Lane Departure Protective Cover II 

  3. Remove the lane departure warning system protection cover I. Refer to Installation and Removal of Lane Departure Protective Cover I 

  4. Remove the EDR camera.

    1. Disconnect 1 connector.

    2. Detach 2 fixing clips, and take out the EDR camera.

    Caution
    Remove with care to avoid damaging the clip.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
