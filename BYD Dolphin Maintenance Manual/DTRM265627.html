Removal and installation of rear left seat backrest assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the cushion assembly of the rear left seat. Refer to Removal and Installation of Left Rear Seat Cushion Assembly 

  2. Pull the unlocking webbing and turn over the rear left seat backrest assembly.

  3. Remove 1 fixing bolt of the rear left seat backrest assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

    Caution

    • Take out the seat assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

    • Never scratch the body guards when taking out the seat assembly.

  4. Remove 1 fixing bolt and the rear left seat backrest assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
