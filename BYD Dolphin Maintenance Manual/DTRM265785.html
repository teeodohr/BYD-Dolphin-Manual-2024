Removal and installation of left shield of the front passenger's seat

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the front passenger's seat assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front passenger's seat assembly 

  2. Remove the backrest angle adjustment handle of the front passenger's seat. Refer to Removal and installation of front passenger's seat backrest angle adjustment handle 

  3. Remove the lower anchorage end cover of the front passenger's seat belt (manual). Refer to Removal and installation of lower anchorage end cover of front passenger's manual seat belt 

  4. Remove 1 fixing screw of the left shield of the front passenger's seat.

  5. Remove the left shield of the front passenger's seat.

    1. Detach it from the front passenger's seat assembly.

    2. Take out the left shield of the front passenger's seat.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
