Removal and installation of driver's seat left shield (Electric)

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the driver's seat two-way adjustment button. Refer to Removal and installation of driver's seat backrest adjustment button (electric) 

  2. Remove the driver's seat six-way adjustment button. Refer to Removal and installation of driver's seat slideway adjustment button (electric) 

  3. Remove the driver's seat assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of driver's seat assembly (power) 

  4. Disconnect 1 connector of the driver's power seat adjustment switch body.

  5. Remove 1 fixing screw from the left shield of the driver's seat.

  6. Remove the left shield of the driver's seat.

    1. Detach it from the driver seat assembly.

    2. Take out the left shield of driver's seat.

  7. Remove 2 fixing screws and take out the driver's power seat adjustment switch body.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
