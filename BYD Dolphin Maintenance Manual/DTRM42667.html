Installation and Removal of Wheel Speed Sensor

Removal

Reminder
The removal and installation of the front and rear 4 wheel speed sensors are the same. Take the left rear side as an example.

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  2. Remove the left rear wheel assembly. Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  3. Remove the left rear wheel speed sensor.

    1. Disconnect 1 connector

    2. Remove 1 fixing bolt. Take out the left rear wheel speed sensor.

      • Tightening torque: 8N·m

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
