System descriptions

Overview

The rear suspension system of this model uses a torsion beam type non-independent suspension, which is space-efficient, light and comfortable, and is easy to control.

The torsion beam type non-independent suspension balances the up and down bounce of left and right wheels through a torsion beam to reduce vehicle shaking and keep the vehicle stable.

The shock absorber is mainly used to buffer the impact force transmitted from uneven road surface to the frame or the vehicle body, reduce the vibration caused by it, ensure the smooth running, and improve the comfort of the driver and passengers.
