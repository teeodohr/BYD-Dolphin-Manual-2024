Removal and installation of rear right brake pipe I assembly

Removal

Caution

  • The brake fluid has a strong corrosive effect on the paint. If it is accidentally splashed on the paint of the vehicle body, it must be cleaned with a rag as soon as possible.

  • Avoid oil contact with skin. If it is accidentally splashed into the eyes, immediately rinse with plenty of water and seek medical attention as soon as possible.

  • Do not discharge the oil at will, and recycle it according to local regulations.

  1. Drain the brake fluid. Refer to Draining and Filling of Brake Fluid 

  2. Remove the front subframe and the powertrain. Refer to Removal and installation of front subframe and powertrain 

  3. Disconnect the rear right brake pipe I assembly from the rear right brake pipe II assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: 18±2 N•m

    2. Remove 2 pipe clamps.

  4. Remove 1 fixing nut and take out the rear right brake pipe I assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 20±2 N•m

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation is completed, the brake system needs to be bled. Refer to Air Discharge of Brake System
