Replacement of gear oil

Caution
Before replacing the gear oil, make sure that the vehicle has been driven for more than 3 km, which is conducive to completely discharging the gear oil.
Warning
If the vehicle is overheated, wait until the vehicle cools down to near room temperature, otherwise there is a risk of scalding.

Drainage of gear oil 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Lift the vehicle and keep it in a level state.

  2. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Subframe Mudguard 

  3. Remove 1 oil filler plug.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2 N•m

    Caution

    • Place the collection container below the plug in advance.

    • Be careful when unscrewing the plug to avoid oil spilling out of the collecting container.

  4. Remove 1 oil drain plug, and install it after draining all gear oil.

    • Tightening torque: 50±3 N•m

Filling of gear oil

  1. Add gear oil to the oil filler plug hole.

    • Model: Castrol BOT384 or equivalent gear oil.

    Reminder

    For details of gear oil filling amount, please refer toOil/Fluid specifications of the vehicle 

  2. Install the oil filler plug.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2 N•m

  3. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
