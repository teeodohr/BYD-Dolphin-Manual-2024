Precautions

Warning

  • Cracked tires can be dangerous. Before removing a cracked tire, exhaust the air from the vehicle. Failure to follow this operation may cause serious personal injury.

  • When install the wheel and tire, the model must be matched. Does not match the installation may cause the tire bead to break or the tire to detach from the wheel.

Caution

  • When remove the counterweight, do not use sharp metal tools such as screwdrivers to prevent wheel damage. Counterweight remover must be used for removal.

  • Do not use wire brush or strong detergent to clean the wheel, otherwise, the wheel will be damaged.

  • The standard tire pressure has an important effect on the life of the tire. The tire pressure must be charged to the standard value after installation.
