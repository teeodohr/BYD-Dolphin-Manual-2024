Removal and installation of rear subframe assembly

Removal

Caution
It is necessary to use tools to provide stable support before its removal by multiple technicians.

  1. Remove the high-voltage battery pack. Refer to Removal and installation of high-voltage battery pack 

  2. Remove the rear left steering knuckle. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear left steering knuckle (multi-link suspension) 

  3. Remove the rear right steering knuckle. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear right steering knuckle (multi-link suspension) 

  4. Remove the rear left and right shock absorber assemblies. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear shock absorber assembly 

  5. Remove the rear stabilizer bar assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear stabilizer bar assembly 

  6. Remove the rear left and right suspension lower arm assemblies. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension lower arm assembly 

  7. Remove the rear suspension upper left arm assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension upper left arm assembly 

  8. Remove the rear suspension upper right arm assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension upper right arm assembly 

  9. Remove the rear suspension left trailing arm assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension left trailing arm assembly 

  10. Remove the rear suspension right trailing arm assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension right trailing arm assembly 

  11. Remove the rear left and right suspension control arm assemblies. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension control arm assembly 

  12. Remove 4 fixing bolts and take out the rear subframe assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 200±10 N•m

  13. To replace the rear subframe assembly, remove the following accessories:

    • Remove the front left rubber bushing assembly of rear subframe. Refer toRemoval and installation of front left rubber bushing assembly of rear subframe 

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Finish four-wheel alignment after installation. Refer toInspection and adjustment of four-wheel alignment
