EPB maintenance mode

EPB maintenance mode release

Caution

  • After release in the maintenance mode as per method 1 and method 2 as below, all the functions of the EPB will be disabled to avoid damage of the EPB drive assembly due to accidental actions during maintenance.

  • After the maintenance is completed and the vehicle state is recovered, the EPB can return to the normal state only when the state of release in the maintenance mode is deactivated.

Method 1

  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.

  2. Power on the vehicle and wait until its self-test ends.

  3. After the VDS establishes communication with the vehicle, click "Diagnostic system".

  4. Select "Passenger car".

  5. Select the corresponding vehicle series and model to scan.

  6. Select the "EPB" module.

  7. Select "Action test".

  8. Select "Maintenance mode release".

Method 2

Caution

  • The brake pedal must be depressed in the whole process;

  • If the switch is held for less than 10 s or more than 12 s when being pressed down for the first time, the system will return to the initial state automatically and must be operated again;

  • If the switch is pressed down again within 3 s or 5 s after being released for the first time, the system will return to the initial state automatically and must be operated again;

  • After activating the full release state, the system will not respond to any function. In this case, it is necessary to select “Pull-up in maintenance mode” or use the EPB switch to deactivate the state of release in the maintenance mode.

  1. Power on the vehicle or start it.

  2. Press and hold the brake pedal.

  3. Press and hold the EPB switch for more than 10 s, and the brake warning indicator on the instrument panel will flash.

  4. Release the EPB switch within 2 s after the warning indicator starts flashing.

  5. Start timing after the EPB switch is released and press the EPB switch again within the subsequent 3 ~ 5 s.

  6. Completely release the EPB, and the warning indicator turns to illuminate constantly.

  7. Release the brake pedal.

Method 3

If the above two methods fail due to electrical equipment fault, mechanical service release can be carried out as follows:

  1. Remove the wheeled EPB motor drive assembly. Refer to

  2. Use a tool to release the brake piston by rotating the spline on the caliper body clockwise, and release the brake piston until the thrust screw of the caliper body rotates freely.

EPB maintenance mode release exit

Caution

  • After release of EPB in the maintenance mode, only the state of release in the maintenance mode is deactivated, can the EPB return to the normal state and respond to all functions.

  • Once the EPB drive assembly is removed from the vehicle, it is forbidden to power on the vehicle for drive.

Method 1

  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.

  2. Power on the vehicle and wait until its self-test ends.

  3. After the VDS establishes communication with the vehicle, click "Diagnostic system".

  4. Select "Passenger car".

  5. Select the corresponding vehicle series and model to scan.

  6. Select the "EPB" module.

  7. Select "Action test".

  8. Select "Service clamping".

Method 2

Caution

  • The brake pedal must be depressed in the whole process;

  • The switch must be held for more than 10 s when being pressed down for the first time, otherwise the system will return to the initial state;

  • The switch must be pressed down again within 5 s after being released for the first time, otherwise the system will return to the initial state;

  • After the state of release in the maintenance mode is deactivated with the switch, the EPB returns to the normal state and can respond to all functions.

  1. Power on the vehicle or start it.

  2. Press and hold the brake pedal.

  3. Press and hold the EPB switch for more than 10 s, and the brake warning indicator on the instrument panel will flash.

  4. Release the EPB switch and pull it up again within the subsequent 5 s.

  5. With the EPB pulled up, the warning indicator will go out, and the EPB status light will illuminate.

  6. Release the brake pedal.
