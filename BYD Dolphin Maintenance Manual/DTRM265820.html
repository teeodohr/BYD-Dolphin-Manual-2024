Removal and installation the left shield of front passenger's seat (double-pretensioner seat belt)

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the front passenger's seat assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front passenger's seat assembly (double-pretensioner seat belt) 

  2. Remove the front passenger's seat backrest adjustment button (electric). Refer to Removal and installation of front passenger's seat backrest adjustment button (electric) 

  3. Remove the slideway adjustment button (electric). Refer to Removal and installation of front passenger's seat slideway adjustment button (electric) 

  4. Remove 1 fixing screw of the left shield of the front passenger's seat.

  5. Remove 1 connector of the left shield of front passenger's seat.

  6. Remove the trim cover of the driver's seat right shield.

  7. Detach the left outer shield of front passenger's seat.

  8. Take out the left shield of the front passenger's seat.

  9. To replace the left outer shield of the front passenger's seat, remove the accessories from the left outer shield of the front passenger's seat.

    • Remove the front passenger's power seat adjustment switch body. Refer toRemoval and installation of front passenger's power seat adjustment switch body 

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
