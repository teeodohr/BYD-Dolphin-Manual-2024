Replacement of brake fluid

Draining

Caution

  • When removing the brake tube or intelligent power braking system, the brake fluid must be drained and filled.

  • Before draining, use VDS to confirm that the braking system has no fault code.

  • The brake fluid has a strong corrosive effect on the paint. If it is accidentally splashed on the paint of the vehicle body, it must be cleaned with a rag as soon as possible.

  • Avoid oil contact with skin. If it is accidentally splashed into the eyes, immediately rinse with plenty of water and seek medical attention as soon as possible.

  • Do not discharge the oil at will, and recycle it according to local regulations.

  • After completion, use VDS to check that the braking system has no fault code.

Reminder
The method for draining the brake fluid of the front and rear wheels is the same. Take the left rear side as an example.

  1. Remove the bleed bolt cover.

  2. Connect the brake fluid collection container with the bleed screw, and loosen the bleed screw.

  3. Press the brake pedal and gradually drain all brake fluid.

Filling

Caution

  • Check whether there is dirt in the brake fluid reservoir before filling.

  • Do not refill used brake fluid, and fill brake fluid of the same specification as required.

  • Ensure that the brake fluid level is within the specified range after filling.

  1. Open the filler cap of brake fluid reservoir and add brake fluid.

  2. Loosen the bleed screw, slowly press the brake pedal to the bottom, and then release it. Repeat this operation every 2-3s until new brake fluid is discharged from the bleed screw.

  3. Then press the brake pedal and tighten the bleed screw.

  4. Carry out same operations for other 3 wheels.

  5. Detect the water content of brake fluid with a brake fluid detector. If the measured result shows that there is no water in the brake fluid, this round of brake fluid replacement is completed.

  6. Bleed the brake system. Refer to Air Discharge of Brake System 

  7. Install the bleed bolt cover.
