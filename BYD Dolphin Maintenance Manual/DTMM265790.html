System descriptions

Overview

Vehicle wiring harnesses run through the whole vehicle and connect all electrical equipment. They can transmit both current and signal, playing an important role in vehicle circuits.

Main components:

  • Electric wire: It is the main part of the wiring harness, usually called conductor. It is the main body of the wiring harness and mainly used to carry the current required by the load.

  • Connector: It is an indispensable component for connecting the wiring harness with other electrical equipment, which can be divided into a wiring harness end connector and an electrical appliance end connector.

  • Wiring harness wrapping: It is mainly used to protect the wiring harness. The application conditions and methods on the wiring harness assembly vary with the wrapping material. The wrapping should be selected based on the specific service environment, wear resistance and heat resistance of the wiring harness.

  • Wiring harness fixture: As a flexible part, the wiring harness cannot be arranged according to the set position. Therefore, it is necessary to constrain its layout path and use additional parts to fix it. These parts are called wiring harness fixtures, mainly including tie wraps, clips, rubber sheaths and wiring harness brackets. Depending on the different installation environment of wiring harness, such as installation space, temperature and waterproof requirements, the shape and material of each wiring harness fixture are also different.

  • Wiring harness accessories: such as product labels on the wiring harness assembly.
