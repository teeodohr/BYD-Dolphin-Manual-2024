Installation and Removal of Right Rear Door Assembly

Caution
Remove or install the door assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

Removal

  1. Remove the right rear door lock body assembly. See Removal and installation of rear right door lock body assembly 

  2. Remove the right rear door outer handle assembly. See Removal and installation of rear right door outer handle assembly 

  3. Remove the right rear window regulator assembly. See Removal and installation of rear right window regulator assembly 

  4. Remove the right rear door limit block. See Installation and Removal of Door Stop Block 

  5. Remove the right rear door stopper assembly. See Front Hatch Cover Rear Door Stopper Assembly 

  6. Remove the right rear door outer weatherstrip assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Rear Door Outer Weatherstrip Assembly 

  7. Remove the right rear door harness. See Installation and Removal of Right Rear Door Harness 

  8. Remove 2 fixing bolts of the right rear door.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  9. Remove 2 fixing bolts, and take out the right rear door assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
