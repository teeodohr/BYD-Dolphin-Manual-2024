Removal and installation of indoor condenser assembly

Removal

Caution

  • Remove A/C pipeline with O-ring. Replace with a new O-ring after each removal, and apply compressor lubricant to the new O-ring before installing.

  • After removing the components of the A/C system, all components need to be sealed to prevent foreign matters such as dust and impurities from entering the A/C system and causing abnormalities.

  1. Recycle the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  2. Remove the HVAC assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of HVAC assembly 

  3. Remove the buffer pad.

  4. Remove 2 fixing screws and take out the fixing cover.

  5. Remove the indoor condenser assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing screws.

    2. Take out the hatch cover of indoor condenser assembly.

    3. Take out the indoor condenser assembly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Fill refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

Caution

  • If need replace part with a new one, filling the compressor with refrigeration oil according to the specified amount.

  • Before filling the refrigerant, the A/C pipeline must be vacuumized.

  • After the above operation is completed, check whether the A/C system works properly and whether there are any leaks in the A/C pipeline.
