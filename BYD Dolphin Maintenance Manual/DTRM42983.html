Installation and Removal of Tailgate Gas Spring Ball Stud

Removal

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right tailgate gas spring ball studs are the same. Take the left as an example.

  1. Use a micro slotted screwdriver to pry up the bayonet pin and detach the tailgate left gas spring body from the tailgate gas spring ball stud.

    Caution

    • Detach the tailgate left gas spring body with another maintenance technician supporting the tailgate assembly.

    • When using a micro slotted screwdriver to pry up the bayonet pin, do not pry the bayonet pin out of the cage completely.

  2. Remove the tailgate gas spring ball stud.

    • Tightening torque: 20±2N•m

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
