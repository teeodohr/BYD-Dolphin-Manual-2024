Removal and installation of left trim panel assembly of instrument panel

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the PAD access panel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of PAD access panel assembly 

  2. Carefully pry out the left trim panel assembly of instrument panel with a plastic pry plate.

Installation

Caution

  • Before installation, check whether the clips are missing or damaged. If so, install new clips, and then install the instrument panel left trim panel assembly.

  • After installation, check whether all clips are installed in place. The installation position of the instrument panel left trim panel assembly may deviate, resulting in warping or abnormal gap.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
