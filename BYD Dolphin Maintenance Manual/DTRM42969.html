Adjustment of Rear Door Assembly

Reminder
The left and right rear door assemblies are adjusted in the same way. The left side is taken as an example below.
Caution
The door assembly side bolt has no adjustment and cannot be adjusted by loosening the door assembly side bolt.

  1. Loosen 4 fixing bolts of left rear door assembly body side.

    • Tightening torque: 28±2N•m

  1. Adjust the left rear door assembly horizontally and vertically.

  1. Loosen 2 fixing screws of side door lock ring and adjust the clearance level difference of left rear door assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 20±2N•m
