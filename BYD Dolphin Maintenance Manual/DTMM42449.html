Systems Manual

Overview

The front suspension system of this model adopts McPherson independent suspension, which occupies small space, has light weight, good comfort and is easy to handle.

The main function of the shock absorber is to buffer the impact force transmitted to the frame or body from uneven road, reduce the vibration caused thereby, ensure the smooth running and improve the comfort of the driver and passengers.
