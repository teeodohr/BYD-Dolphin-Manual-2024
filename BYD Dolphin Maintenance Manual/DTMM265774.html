System descriptions

Overview

The suspension is mainly used to alleviate the impact from uneven road on wheels, determine the positions of wheels and axles, and improve the grip performance of wheels on road. Therefore, the suspension has a certain impact on the riding comfort and handling stability of the vehicle. The rear suspension of the vehicle is a multi-link independent suspension. It has the advantage that the left and right wheels can bounce separately. The wheels do not interfere with each other, which can reduce the inclination and vibration of the vehicle body, reduce the impact on the vehicle body, and improve the comfort of the rear seat.
