Removal and installation of instrument panel upper body assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the left A-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left A-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  2. Remove the right A-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right A-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  3. Remove the left shield assembly of instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of left shield assembly of instrument panel 

  4. Remove the right shield assembly of instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of right shield assembly of instrument panel 

  5. Remove the glove box assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of glove box assembly 

  6. Remove the glove box upper trim panel assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Storage Box Upper Trim Panel Assembly 

  7. Remove the display screen bracket assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of display screen bracket assembly 

  8. Remove the instrument panel cluster assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of instrument panel cluster assembly 

  9. Remove the steering wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of steering wheel assembly 

  10. Remove 3 fixing screws on the left side of instrument panel upper body assembly.

  11. Remove 1 fixing bolt on the left side of instrument panel upper body assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 4 N•m

  12. Remove 2 fixing screws on the right side of instrument panel upper body assembly.

  13. Remove 1 fixing bolt on the right side of instrument panel upper body assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 4 N•m

  14. Disconnect the front passenger airbag module.

    1. Disconnect 1 connector.

    2. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

      • Tightening torque: 20 N•m

  15. Remove the instrument panel upper body assembly.

    Caution

    • Remove the instrument panel upper body assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

    • When taking out the instrument panel upper body assembly, take care to avoid scratching the interior trim panel.

  16. To replace the instrument panel upper body assembly, remove the following accessories from it:

    • Remove the front passenger airbag module. Refer to Removal and installation of front passenger airbag module 

    • Remove the light intensity sensor wire. Refer to Removal and installation of light intensity sensor wire 

    • Remove the PAB wiring harness. Refer to Removal and Installation of PAB Harness 

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
