Removal and installation of rear bumper assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the left taillight cluster assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Combination Rear Light Assembly 

  2. Remove the right taillight cluster assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Combination Rear Light Assembly 

  3. Remove the rear left wheel arch trim panel assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Rear Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly 

  4. Remove the rear right wheel arch trim panel assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Rear Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly 

  5. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the rear bumper assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 3±0.5 N•m

  6. Remove 4 fixing screws on the left side of rear bumper assembly.

  7. Remove 4 fixing screws on the right side of rear bumper assembly.

  8. Remove 4 fixing bolts on the lower side of rear bumper assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 3±0.5 N•m

  9. Detach the rear bumper assembly.

    Caution
    Do not exert excessive force when detaching the bumper assembly to prevent the connector being damaged.

  10. Disconnect 3 connectors of the small probe.

    Caution
    The removal and installation of the left, right and middle small probes are the same. Take the middle part as an example in the figure below.

  11. Disconnect 2 connectors of license plate light.

  12. Disconnect 1 connector of the rear-view camera assembly and detach the wiring harness clip.

  13. Take out the rear bumper assembly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
