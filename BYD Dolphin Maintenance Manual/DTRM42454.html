Removal and installation of front right shock absorber strut assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the front right wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove the vent cover assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of the Ventilation Cover Assembly 

  3. Remove the front wiper drive system assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Wiper Drive System Assembly 

  4. Detach the front compartment wiring harness from the front right shock absorber strut assembly.

  5. Detach the front right brake hose assembly from the front right shock absorber strut assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 30 N•m

    2. Detach the front right brake hose assembly.

  6. Remove 1 fixing nut, and detach the front right stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly from the front right shock absorber strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 90 N•m

    Caution
    Please secure bolt with a suitable tool and then remove the fixing nut when removing the front stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly.

  7. Remove 2 fixing nuts, take out 2 fixing bolts, and detach the front right steering knuckle from the front right shock absorber strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 200 N•m

  8. Remove 3 fixing nuts and take out the front right shock absorber strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 55 N•m

    Caution
    Take out the right front shock absorber strut assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Finish four-wheel alignment after installation. Refer to Inspection and adjustment of four-wheel alignment
