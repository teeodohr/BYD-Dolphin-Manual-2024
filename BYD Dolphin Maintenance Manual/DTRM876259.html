Visual inspection of lower control arm ball stud dust cover and rubber support

Reminder

The inspection methods of left and right supports are the same. Take the left one as an example.

Front suspension

  1. Check that the dust cover of the lower control arm ball stud is free of damage or aging.

    Reminder

    • If dirt or moisture enters for a long time due to damage of the dust cover, the ball stud may be damaged and must be replaced.

  2. Check that the rubber of the lower control arm rubber support is free of cracks, aging or disconnection.

  3. Obtain a new work order for repair or replacement.

Rear suspension

  1. Check that the rubber of all rubber supports on the rear suspension is free of aging or cracking.

  2. Check that the rubber of all rubber supports on the rear subframe is free of aging or cracking.

  3. Check whether the rubber support is damaged as follows:

    • The connection between the rubber molded parts and metal is completely cracked.

    • There is a large clearance between the rubber support and subframe components, which has an obvious negative impact on the function of the rubber support.

    Reminder

    Surface cracks have no substantial impact on the function of elastic motion rubber support, so they are not covered in the scope of fault.

  4. Obtain a new work order for repair or replacement.
