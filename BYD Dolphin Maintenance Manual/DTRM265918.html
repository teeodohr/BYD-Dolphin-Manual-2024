Removal and installation of front compartment heat insulator

Removal

  1. Remove the front subframe and the powertrain. Refer to Removal and installation of front subframe and powertrain 

  2. Remove the compressor suction pipe assembly 3. Refer to Removal and installation of compressor suction pipe assembly 3 

  3. Remove the compressor discharge pipe assembly 1. Refer to Removal and installation of compressor discharge pipe assembly 1 

  4. Remove the compressor discharge pipe assembly 2. Refer to Removal and installation of compressor discharge pipe assembly 2 

  5. Remove the compressor suction pipe assembly 2. Refer to Removal and installation of compressor suction pipe assembly 2 

  6. Remove the refrigeration pipe assembly 3. Refer to Removal and installation of refrigeration pipe assembly 3 

  7. Remove the front right brake pipe assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front right brake pipe assembly 

  8. Remove the rear right brake pipe I assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of rear right brake pipe I assembly 

  9. Remove the front left brake pipe assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of front left brake pipe assembly 

  10. Detach 9 clips from the front compartment wiring harness.

  11. Remove 7 plastic nuts and take out the front compartment heat insulator.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
