Precautions

Warning
Before removing the wiper system, low voltage electrical system must be powered off to prevent injury caused by sudden operation of the wiper system.
Caution

  • Switch the wiper system to the maintenance gear before removing.

  • When removing the wiper arm assembly, pay attention to prevent the front windshield glass from being damaged due to the rebound of the wiper arm assembly.

  • After installation, check whether the wiper arm assembly is in the initial position.

  • After installation, run the front wiper system and check to see if it is working properly.
