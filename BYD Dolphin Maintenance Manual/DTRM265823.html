Removal and installation of rear right door wiring harness

Removal

Caution
Before removing the door harness, lift the glass to the highest point.

  1. Remove the rear right door low-pitch speaker. Refer to Installation and Removal of Rear Door Low-Pitch Speaker 

  2. Remove the right B-pillar lower shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right B-Pillar Lower Guard Assembly 

  3. Remove the rear right door sealing plug I.

  4. Disconnect 1 connector from the floor wiring harness.

  5. Disconnect 1 connector from the rear right door lock body assembly.

  6. Disconnect 1 connector from the rear right window regulator assembly.

  7. Disconnect the rear right door wiring harness from the rear right door assembly.

    1. Detach 11 wiring harness clips.

    2. Remove 1 wiring harness sealant sleeve.

  8. Remove the rear right door wiring harness.

    1. Remove 1 wiring harness sealant sleeve.

    2. Remove 1 wiring harness sealant sleeve and take out the rear right door wiring harness.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
