Removal and installation of the front left brake hose assembly

Removal

Caution

  • The brake fluid has a strong corrosive effect on the paint. If it is accidentally splashed on the paint of the vehicle body, it must be cleaned with a rag as soon as possible.

  • Avoid oil contact with skin. If it is accidentally splashed into the eyes, immediately rinse with plenty of water and seek medical attention as soon as possible.

  • Do not discharge the oil at will. It must be recycled according to local regulations.

  1. Remove the front left wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the front left brake hose assembly from the front left shock absorber strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 25±2 N•m

  3. Remove 1 fixing bolt and detach the front left brake hose assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 30±3 N•m

    Caution
    Place a collection container under the brake hose.

  4. Remove the front left brake hose assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: 18±2 N•m

    2. Remove 1 E-clip and take out the front left brake hose assembly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation, bleed the brake system of front left wheel. Refer to Air Discharge of Brake System
