Safety description

This manual cannot anticipate all possible variations or provide comprehensive advice or warnings. Anyone who does not follow the instructions in this manual must first make sure that the selected methods, tools or spare parts will neither endanger their own safety and those of others nor endanger the overall performance and environment of the vehicle/equipment.

This manual categorizes four types of symbols to represent specific meanings based on severity levels and usage scenarios. Each symbol represents a specific type of prompt.

Reminder
This type of content provides supplementary information that helps to complete a specific procedure, or better results can be achieved by following the prompts.
Caution
Such content indicates that ignoring the reminder may result in the failure to complete the repair or to accomplish certain tasks.
Warning
Such content indicates that ignoring the warning may result in general personal injury, damage to vehicles/parts, or environmental damage.
Danger
Such content indicates that neglect of this warning may result in serious personal injury or death.

This manual contains many above symbols. A symbol applying to multiple steps is placed before these steps. A symbol only applying to one step is placed after this step.
