Installation and Removal of Tailgate Left Hinge Assembly

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right tailgate hinge assemblies are the same. Take the left side as an example.

Removal

  1. Remove the left C-pillar upper guard assembly. See Installation and removal of left C-pillar upper guard assembly .

  2. Remove 2 fixing clips from the ceiling assembly

  3. Remove part of the ceiling assembly.

  4. Remove the tailgate left hinge assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

      • Tightening torque: 25±3N•m

    2. Remove 1 fixing nut and take out the tailgate left hinge assembly.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

      Caution

      • Before removing the fixing nut, detach the ceiling assembly from the roof (only lift as the fixing nut can be removed).

      • Care must be taken when removing the tailgate left hinge assembly to avoid scratching the surface material.

      • Remove or install the tailgate left hinge assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

Installation

Caution
Adjust the tailgate assembly after the replacement of the tailgate hinge assembly.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Adjust the tailgate assembly. See “Installation and removal of tailgate assembly” .
