Removal and installation of battery cooling plate heat exchanger assembly

Removal

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer toPower-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Discharge refrigerant. Refer toRefrigerant recovery and filling 

  3. Drain the coolant. Refer toReplacement of coolant 

  4. Remove the front right wheel mudguard assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Right Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  5. Remove the washer fluid reservoir and motor assembly. Refer to 

  6. Remove 2 fixing bolts and move aside the brake fluid reservoir assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 10±2 N•m

  7. Disconnect the wiring harness of battery cooling plate heat exchanger assembly.

    1. Disconnect 1 connector.

    2. Detach 1 wiring harness fixing clip.

  8. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and detach the coolant pipe assembly 5 from the battery cooling plate heat exchanger assembly.

  9. Remove 2 fixing bolts and detach the refrigeration pipe assembly 5 from the battery cooling plate heat exchanger assembly.

    Caution
    Replace the O-ring with new one when removing the A/C refrigeration pipeline.

  10. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and detach the plate heat exchanger coolant inlet pipe assembly from the electric coolant pump assembly.

  11. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and detach the plate heat exchanger coolant outlet pipe assembly from the electric three-way coolant valve assembly.

  12. Remove the battery cooling plate heat exchanger assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    2. Remove 1 fixing nut and take out the battery cooling plate heat exchanger assembly.

  13. To replace the battery cooling plate heat exchanger assembly, remove the following accessories:

    • Remove the plate heat exchanger coolant inlet pipe assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of plate heat exchanger coolant inlet pipe assembly 

    • Remove the plate heat exchanger coolant outlet pipe assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of plate heat exchanger coolant outlet pipe assembly 

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Add coolant. Refer toReplacement of coolant 

  3. Vacuumize the A/C system. Refer toRefrigerant recovery and filling 

  4. Fill refrigerant. Refer toRefrigerant recovery and filling
