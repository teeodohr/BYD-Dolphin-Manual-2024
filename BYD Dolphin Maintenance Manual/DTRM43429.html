Installation and Removal of Left C-Pillar Outer Trim Panel Assembly

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the left C-pillar exterior trim panel assembly carefully with a plastic crow plate.

    Caution
    Before removal, open the left rear door assembly and tailgate assembly at the same time.

Installation

Caution

  • Before installation, check whether the clips are missing or damaged. If so, install new clips, and then install left C-pillar exterior trim panel assembly;

  • After installation, check whether all clips are installed in place. The installation position of the left C-pillar exterior trim panel assembly may deviate, resulting in warping or abnormal gap.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
