Inspection of wheel bearing axial clearance

  1. Remove the rear wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Install the dial indicator, set its reading to zero, and measure the axial clearance of the wheel bearing by moving the brake disc axially inward and outward.

    • Standard axial clearance of rear wheel bearing: -0.01-0.04 mm

    Caution
    Replace wheel bearings if the axial clearance of bearings is greater than the standard value.
