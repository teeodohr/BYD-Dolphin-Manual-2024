Removal and installation of EPS column & universal joint assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the steering wheel assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Steering Wheel Assembly 

  2. Remove the multi-function switch. Refer to Removal and installation of multi-function switch 

  3. Remove the multi-function display screen bracket assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of multi-function display screen bracket assembly 

  4. Remove the lower right shield assembly of instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of lower right shield assembly of instrument panel 

  5. Remove 2 fixing screws and take out the lower right reinforcement plate of instrument panel.

  6. Detach 2 wiring harness clips from the instrument panel wiring harness.

  7. Disconnect the instrument panel wiring harness.

    1. Detach 1 fixing clip from the instrument panel wiring harness.

    2. Disconnect 2 connectors.

  8. Remove 1 fixing bolt from the EPS column & universal joint assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 50 N•m

  9. Remove the EPS column & universal joint assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the EPS column & universal joint assembly from the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 70 N•m

      Reminder

      Before removing the EPS column & universal joint assembly, mark the removal/installation position at the connection between the EPS column & universal joint assembly and the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly with a marker.

    2. Remove 2 fixing nuts and take out the EPS column & universal joint assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 25 N•m

    Caution
    Remove it with the assistance of another maintenance technician to prevent injury caused by sudden falling.

Installation

Caution

  • When installing the steering column and steering gear bolts, screw them in from the unthreaded side. Screwing in from the other side will lead to poor tightening, or even an accident. The side of the universal joint yoke without threads has a milled plane. Screw in bolts from this side.

  • After replacing the EPS column & universal joint assembly, use a VDS to calibrate the new one.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
