System descriptions

Overview

The cooling system consists of motor coolant pump, motor radiator assembly I, electronic fan assembly I, expansion tank assembly I, coolant pipeline and other components.

The cooling system mainly relies on the motor coolant pump to drive the coolant to circulate in the coolant pipeline, take away the heat generated by the integrated front drive assembly, and cool down the system through physical processes such as heat exchange in the radiator. When the coolant reaches a certain temperature, the electronic fan starts to work to maintain the integrated front drive assembly at the optimal temperature for operation.
