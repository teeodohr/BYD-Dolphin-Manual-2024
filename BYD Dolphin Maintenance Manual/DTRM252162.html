Removal and installation of rear coil spring

Reminder
For the rear coil spring, the removal and installation methods of the left one are the same as those of the right one. Take the left one as an example.

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the rear left wheel assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove the rear coil spring.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut from the rear stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly.

      • 90±5 N•m

    2. Unscrew 1 lock nut of the rear suspension lower arm assembly.

      • 200±10 N•m

    3. Remove 1 fixing nut/bolt, detach the rear suspension lower arm assembly from the rear left steering knuckle, and take out the rear coil spring.

      • 200±10 N•m

      Caution
      When removing the connecting bolt of the rear suspension lower arm assembly, support the lower arm with a lifter first, and clamp the coil spring with an auxiliary equipment, and then slowly lower the arm to prevent the spring from popping out.

Installation

  1. During installation, pay attention to whether the rear coil spring and the upper buffer pad are installed in place.

  2. During installation, pay attention to whether the rear coil spring and the lower buffer pad are installed in place.

  3. Install other components in the reverse order of removal.

  4. Finish four-wheel alignment after installation. Refer toInspection and adjustment of four-wheel alignment
