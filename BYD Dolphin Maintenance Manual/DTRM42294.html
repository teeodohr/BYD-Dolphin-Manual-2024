Removal and installation of high-voltage battery pack

Warning

  • When repairing the high voltage system (the high voltage system harnesses are identified by orange on the vehicle), wear insulation protection equipment, including insulating gloves, insulating shoes and masks, and power down the high voltage system of the vehicle.

  • To avoid the personal injury, only the professionals may remove the power battery.

  • Do not touch or operate the power battery without appropriate protective equipment.

  • Please switch the vehicle power supply to OFF position before operation.

  • Remove the battery according to the process.

  • During the removal, pay attention to the high voltage warning signs on the power battery and the vehicle.

  • During the removal, do not damage the parts with locking function.

  • During the removal, properly protect the power battery.

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Adjust the driver's seat and front passenger's seat assemblies to the foremost positions.

  2. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  3. Power off the high-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  4. Drain the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  5. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Subframe Mudguard 

  6. Remove the front subframe rear anti-collision bar. Refer toRemoval and installation of front subframe rear anti-collision bar 

  7. Disconnect 1 connector from the front compartment wiring harness.

  8. Disconnect 2 connectors from the front compartment wiring harness.

  9. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the compressor suction pipe assembly 3 from the high-voltage battery pack.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

    Caution

    • Remove the A/C pipeline involving the O-rings. Install new O-rings after each removal. Apply compressor lubricating oil to the new O-rings before installation.

    • After removing the components of the A/C system, all components need to be sealed to prevent foreign matters such as dust and impurities from entering the A/C system and causing abnormalities.

  10. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the high-voltage wiring harness bracket.

    • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

  11. Remove 2 fixing bolts, and disconnect the high-voltage connector from the high-voltage battery pack.

    • Tightening torque: 5 N•m

    Caution
    After the high voltage components are disassembled, they must be sealed and insulated to prevent dust and impurities from entering or accidentally touching the high voltage terminals.

  12. Remove 1 fixing bolt and move the battery pack GND wiring harness aside.

    • Tightening torque: 18±2 N•m

  13. Support the high-voltage battery using a lifting device equipped with an adjustable platform.

    1. Place the lifting device equipped with a lifting platform right below the high-voltage battery pack.

    2. Slowly raise the lifting platform and ensure that the high-voltage battery pack is securely supported by the lifting device.

    3. Do not let the lifting platform cover the fixing bolts of the high-voltage battery pack.

    Warning

    The power battery assembly must be firmly supported to prevent personal injury and damage to the power battery assembly caused by falling power battery assembly.

    Caution
    Place soft supports materials (such as thick wooden frames, rubber pads) on the lifting equipment of the liftable platform to prevent lift platform from damaging the power battery assembly.

  14. Remove 2 carpet plug caps with a plastic pry plate.

  15. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the high-voltage battery pack.

    • Tightening torque: 60 N•m

  16. Remove 10 fixing bolts, and adjust the high-voltage battery pack removal and installation tool to slowly lower the high-voltage battery pack.

    • Tightening torque: 135±5 N•m

    Caution

    • When removing the power battery assembly, more than one people is needed to cooperate with each other. When lowering the power battery assembly, observe whether there is interference with the body.

    • After removing the power battery assembly, seal all connectors to prevent the dust and impurities from entering and causing pollution, blockage or the risk of electric shock when touching the high voltage connector by mistake.

Installation

Caution
Pay attention to standardized operation when installing the lifting power battery assembly. Slowly lift the power battery assembly and observe whether there is interference with the body. Pay attention to align the mounting hole of the adjusting bolt with the mounting hole of the vehicle body. Screw in 14 fixing bolts in sequence and tighten them according to the specified torque.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation, add refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  3. Power on the high-voltage system of the vehicle. Refer to Power-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  4. Power on the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system
