VIN position

Common VINs are located as follows:

1

On the transmission body

5

On the sheet metal surface of rear left hubcap

2

At the VIN slot of upper cover of front windshield lower beam

6

Attached to the sheet metal surface of the inner panel of the rear left door sill

3

Attached on the middle sheet metal surface above the trunk glass

7

Attached to the inner sheet metal surface of the hood

4

Attached to the sheet metal surface in the bottom left of the front left door

8

Attached on the sheet metal plane on the upper edge of the front bumper beam
