Removal and Installation of General Clip

A wide variety and quantity of clip are used in the assembly, most of which are made of plastic. Some clips are disposable parts, which need to be replaced with new clips after removal. When the clip is aged and damaged, it is also necessary to replace it with a new clip in time.

The following is the shape of the general clip and the schematics for disassembly and assembly.

Shape (example) Removal and installation (example)
Scenario 1: Remove the clip with a clip remover or a pair of pliers.
Scenario 2: Use a clip remover or a screwdriver with a protective tape wrapped around the head to remove the clip.
Scenario 3: Remove the clip with a wide scraper to avoid damage to the panel.
Scenario 4: Remove the clip by pushing in the pin shaft and prying the pin shell apart.
Scenario 5: Remove the clip by loosening the pin and prying the pin shell apart.
Scenario 6: Remove the clip by prying out the pin with a screwdriver wrapped in protective tape on the head and then prying out the pin shell.
