Removal and installation of rear washer pipeline subassembly II

Removal

  1. Remove the lower right sound insulation board assembly of instrument panel. Refer toRemoval and installation of lower right shield assembly of instrument panel 

  2. Remove the front right door sill shield assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Right Front Sill Guard Assembly 

  3. Remove the rear right door sill shield assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Right Rear Sill Guard Assembly 

  4. Remove the right B-pillar lower shield assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Right B-Pillar Lower Guard Assembly 

  5. Remove the right C-pillar lower shield assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Right B-Pillar Lower Guard Assembly 

  6. Remove the right C-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Right C-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  7. Remove the rear washer pipeline subassembly II.

    1. Detach the rear washer pipeline subassembly II from the rear washer pipeline subassembly I.

    2. Detach the rear washer pipeline subassembly II from the rear washer pipeline subassembly III.

    3. Detach 6 fixing clips and take out the rear washer pipeline subassembly II.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
