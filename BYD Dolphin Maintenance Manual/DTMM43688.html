System Overview

The lighting system provides lighting for driving at night. The exterior lighting mainly includes head lights, stop lights, license plate lights. Each light is installed at its required position and provided with respective control switch, circuit, fuse and so forth, thereby forming the lighting system. The lighting system also serves for signaling, with such optical signals as steering signal, brake signal, hazard warning signal, outline marker signal, and reversing signal generated to warn other drivers and pedestrians for the purpose of driving safety.

In addition to the traditional lighting, the auto light and head light delayed shut-off makes the use of light more convenient and user-friendly.

Automatic light

Turn the light switch group to AUTO. The light switch group will determine the state according to the external light intensity collected by the four-in-one sensor to automatically control the on and off conditions of light, and turn on the side light or head light according to the light intensity.

Head light delayed shut-off

When the head lights are turned ON and the vehicle is powered OFF, the head lights will not go out immediately. The light switch group will time to turn off the head light after 10 s.
