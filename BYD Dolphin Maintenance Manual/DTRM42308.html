Removal and installation of repair kit for drive motor control unit assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the front subframe and the powertrain. Refer to Removal and installation of the front subframe assembly 

  2. Loosen 2 fixing clamps and remove the outlet pipe from the drive motor control unit.

  3. Remove the electric compressor assembly.

    1. Disconnect 1 connector from the electric compressor assembly.

    2. Remove 3 fixing bolts and take out the electric compressor assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 22±2 N•m

  4. Remove 14 fixing bolts and take out the left cover of integrated front drive assembly.

    Caution

    • Uncover the front drive electric assembly package II side.

    • Each time the left cover of the integrated intelligent front drive assembly is removed, the operation must be standardized. The bolts must be removed from the outside to the inside.

  5. Remove 3 fixing bolts of the three-phase high-voltage wiring harness of the integrated front drive assembly.

  6. Disconnect 1 connector from the repair kit for drive motor control unit assembly.

  7. Remove 6 fixing bolts and take out the repair kit for drive motor control unit assembly.

Installation

  1. Replace the sealing gasket.

    Caution
    The sealing gasket must be replace after disassembly and assembly.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

    Caution
    When install the left cover of the integrated intelligent front drive assembly, the fixing bolts must be tightened in a cross sequence.
