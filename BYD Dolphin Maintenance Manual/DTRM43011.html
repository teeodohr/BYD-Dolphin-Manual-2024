Installation and Removal of Word Mark Assembly

Removal

Reminder
The removal and installation of the "BYD" word mark assembly, EV assembly and dolphin assembly are the same. Take the Dolphin word mark assembly as an example.

  1. Make marks before removal to ensure accurate installation to the original position.

    Caution
    Do not mark the tailgate assembly with a sharp object.

  2. Pull the wire and cut the adhesive on the back of dolphin word mark assembly.

    Caution
    Be careful not to damage the tailgate assembly and paint surface during removal.

Installation

  1. Remove the adhesive residue on the tailgate assembly and ensure that the tailgate assembly meets the requirement.

    Caution
    Be careful not to damage the tailgate assembly and lacquer during clearing.

  2. Install the Dolphin word mark assembly.

    Caution
    Clear the mark after installation.
