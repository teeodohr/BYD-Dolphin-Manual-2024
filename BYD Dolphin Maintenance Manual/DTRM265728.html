Removal and installation of front bumper lower body

Removal

  1. Remove the lower trim panel of front bumper. Refer to Removal and installation of lower trim panel of front bumper 

  2. Remove the lower left trim of front bumper. Refer to Removal and installation of lower left trim of front bumper 

  3. Remove the lower right trim of front bumper. Refer to Removal and installation of lower right trim of front bumper 

  4. Remove 7 fixing screws of the front bumper lower body.

  5. Press the clip and take out the front bumper lower body.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
