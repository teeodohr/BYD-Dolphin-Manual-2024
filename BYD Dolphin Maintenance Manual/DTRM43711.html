Refrigerant recovery and filling

Warning

  • Be careful when repairing the A/C system, and strictly follow corresponding repair precautions. See Common Sense of Safety  for details. Failure to follow these instructions may result in serious personal injury or damage to vehicle components.

  • Be sure to carefully read the user manual of refrigerant recovery and filling machine, and operate in strict accordance with its guidelines.

Refrigerant recovery

  1. Turn on the A/C and set it as follows:

    • A/C switch: ON

    • Temperature setting: Lo

    • Blower speed: level 7

    • Running time: more than 3 min

    Reminder

    • The above settings can concentrate most of the refrigeration oil in the A/C system into the compressor, and try to avoid the discharge of refrigeration oil.

    • If the A/C does not work due to a compressor failure, the preceding settings are not required.

  2. Power off the vehicle, and make sure that the A/C system is not functioning (e.g., the compressor and electronic fan are not running).

  3. Take out the high-pressure and low-pressure valve caps from the A/C pipeline in the front compartment.

  4. Connect the A/C system pipeline to the refrigerant recovery and filling machine.

    1. Connect the quick connector at the high-pressure end of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine to the high-pressure connector of A/C system.

    2. Connect the quick connector at the low-pressure end of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine to the low-pressure connector of A/C system.

  5. Slowly open the pressure valves on the high-pressure and low-pressure sides of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine, avoiding excessive valve opening, and continue the operation according to the instructions provided by the refrigerant recovery and filling machine for refrigerant recovery.

    Caution
    Observe and record the liquid level of the refrigeration oil bottle on the refrigerant recovery and filling machine before and after the refrigerant recovery, and calculate and record the difference.

  6. Disconnect the refrigerant recovery and filling machine from the vehicle in reverse order.

    Caution
    The high pressure bonnet and low pressure bonnet must be installed to avoid contamination of the joint by impurities.
    Warning
    Do not turn on the A/C switch before filling the A/C system with refrigerant, otherwise the compressor may work without refrigerant, resulting in compressor damage.

System vacuumizing

Caution
Make sure that the A/C system is free of refrigerant and then vacuumize. If you are unable to confirm the presence of refrigerant in the A/C system, please follow the "refrigerant recovery" procedure described above.

  1. Connect the A/C system pipeline to the refrigerant recovery and filling machine.

    1. Connect the quick connector at the high-pressure end of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine to the high-pressure connector of A/C system.

    2. Connect the quick connector at the low-pressure end of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine to the low-pressure connector of A/C system.

  2. Open the pressure valves on the high-pressure and low-pressure sides of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine, and proceed with vacuumizing according to the operating instructions of the machine.

  3. After the vacuumizing process is complete, maintain the pressure for 5 minutes and check for any changes.

    • If the pressure remains unchanged, you can proceed to replenish refrigerant oil or refrigerant.

    • If the pressure increases, it indicates a leakage in the cooling system, and you need to address the leakage issue before replenishing refrigerant oil or refrigerant.

  4. After completing the vacuumizing process, if you need to replenish refrigerant oil, please continue with the following "Filling refrigerant oil" procedure.

  5. Continue with the process of "Filling refrigerant"

Filling refrigerant oil

Caution

  • Before filling the refrigerant, it is necessary to confirm the A/C system must be filled with refrigeration oil: according to the discharge amount of recycling refrigerant (observe and record the refrigerant recovery and filling machine refrigeration oil bottle level, before and after the refrigerant recovery is discharge amount), A/C some refrigeration oil (if replaced), or in the case of refrigerant leakage, the total refrigeration oil to be filled.

  • The filling refrigeration oil must be carried out according to the above-mentioned value of calculation.

Caution
Must use refrigeration oil that pass the requirements and asks, otherwise the A/C system may be damaged.

  1. The discharge amount A of refrigeration oil during refrigerant recover = refrigerant oil level after refrigerant recovery - refrigerant oil level before refrigerant recovery.

  2. Based on the replacement of A/C system components or refrigerant leakage, select the appropriate replenishment amount B of refrigerant oil:

    Components to be replaced Replenishment amount B of refrigeration oil
    When the A/C system is completely free of refrigerant oil 450 ml
    Replacement of evaporator assembly 20~30 ml
    Replacement of evaporator box assembly 20~30 ml
    Replacement of condenser assembly 20~30 ml
    Replacement of interior condenser assembly 20~30 ml

  3. The total amount of refrigerant oil to be filled C = A+B.

  4. Use the refrigerant recovery and filling machine to fill refrigerant oil according to its instructions.

    • Filling amount of refrigeration oil: C (fill the oil according to the above calculated amount)

  5. Continue with the process of "Filling refrigerant"

Filling refrigerant

Reminder
When using a refrigerant recovery device, make sure that the refrigerant is sufficient for filling of the system. The refrigerant recovery device cannot recover 100% of the refrigerant in the A/C system.
Warning
Never use refrigerant of different models, and only the refrigerant of the applicable model can be used. Serious system failures may occur if the refrigerant is mixed or misused.

  1. Before filling refrigerant, vacuumize the A/C pipeline with a refrigerant recovery and filling machine for 15 min.

  2. Use the refrigerant recovery and filling machine to fill refrigerant following its instructions.

    • Refrigerant model: R134a

    • Filling amount of refrigerant: 1100 g

  3. After filling, close the pressure valves on the high-pressure and low-pressure sides of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine.

  4. Remove the quick connectors at high-pressure and low-pressure ends of the refrigerant recovery and filling machine.

  5. Install the high-pressure and low-pressure valve caps.

  6. Turn on the A/C system and inspect its operation to ensure it functions properly. Check for smooth operation without any unusual noises and ensure that the cooling and heating functions are working normally.
