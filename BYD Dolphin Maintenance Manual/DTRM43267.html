Removal and Installation of Rear Seat Cushion Support Plate

Removal

  1. Remove the left rear seat cushion assembly. See Removal and Installation of Left Rear Seat Cushion Assembly 

  2. Remove the right rear seat belt buckle assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Rear Seat Belt Buckle Assembly 

  3. Remove the left rear seat belt buckle assembly. See Installation and Removal of Left Rear Seat Belt Buckle Assembly 

  4. Remove the rear seat cushion holder assembly. See Removal and Installation of Rear Seat Cushion Holder Assembly 

  5. Remove 1 fixing bolt of left rear seat belt.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  6. Remove 1 fixing bolt of right rear seat belt.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  7. Disconnect the floor harness 1 connector clip.

  8. Remove the rear seat cushion support plate.

    1. Remove the 6 fixing bolts.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    2. Remove 5 clips and take down the rear seat cushion support plate.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
