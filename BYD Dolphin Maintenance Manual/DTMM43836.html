Removal and Installation of Other Connecting Parts

Some parts of the vehicle can be removed and installed using common methods, such as typical hinges, conduits, claw and pins. If the parts are damaged after disassembly and assembly, or the installation is not firm due to aging, cracks, etc., they must be replaced with new ones.

The following are examples of the general shapes and removal and installation of several typical components.

Shape (example) Removal and installation (example)
Scenario 1: Pull out the component from the pin to separate it.
Scenario 2: Separate the pin by pulling.
Scenario 3: Remove the claw with pliers.
Scenario 4: Separate the pin by pulling.
