Removal and installation of brake fluid reservoir assembly

Removal

  1. Open the hood assembly.

  2. Drain the brake fluid from the reservoir.

  3. Remove 2 fixing bolts of the brake fluid reservoir assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 10±2 N•m

  4. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and take out the brake fluid reservoir assembly.

    Caution
    Place a rag under the brake fluid reservoir to prevent the residual brake fluid in the pot from dripping onto the surface of the parts.

Installation

Caution

  • After installation, add brake fluid between the brake fluid reservoir assembly level markings MAX and MIN.

  • After installation, check that there is no oil leakage at the connection of the pipeline. If there is any, it needs to be overhauled.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
