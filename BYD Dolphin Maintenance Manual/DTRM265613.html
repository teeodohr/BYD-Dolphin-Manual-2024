Removal and installation of left lock assembly

Removal

Reminder

For the lock assembly, the removal and installation methods of the left one are the same as those of the right one. Take the left one as an example.

  1. Remove the rear seat headrest assembly. Refer to Removal and Installation of Rear Headrest Assembly 

  2. Remove the rear left seat backrest assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear left seat backrest assembly 

  3. Lift the cover of the rear seat backrest assembly.

  4. Remove the rear seat backrest panel.

  5. Remove the left lock assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

      • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

    2. Detach the unlocking webbing and take out the left lock assembly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
