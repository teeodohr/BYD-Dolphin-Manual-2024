Removal and installation of front left door wiring harness

Removal

Caution
Before removing the door harness, lift the glass to the highest point.

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Remove the front left door shield assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front left door shield assembly 

  3. Remove the front left door sealing plug.

    1. Remove the front left door sealing plug III.

    2. Remove the front left door sealing plug II.

  4. Remove the front left door low-pitch speaker. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Door Low-Pitch Speaker 

  5. Disconnect the front left door wiring harness from the front left door.

    1. Disconnect 5 connectors.

    2. Disconnect 16 wiring harness clips.

    3. Press the rubber plug cap of wiring harness to push the branch wiring harness into the door.

    4. Press the rubber plug cap of wiring harness and carefully take out the front left door wiring harness from the door.

  6. Disconnect the indoor connection of front left door harness.

    1. Disconnect 3 connectors.

    2. Remove 1 fixing bolt of GND point.

      • 9±1 N•m

  7. Press the rubber plug cap of wiring harness and take out the front left door wiring harness.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
