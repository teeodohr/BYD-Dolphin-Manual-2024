Battery Safety

Risk Identification

Risky vehicle

The power battery is risky if the vehicle is in the following states:

  1. The wading depth of the vehicle reaches door sills and there is no water inside, with wading time more than 30 min.

  2. The high and low voltage connectors of the power battery are soaked in water.

  3. The power battery suffers deformation, puncture and electrolyte leakage after a collision.

  4. A fire breaks out in the vehicle.

Risk judgment

When a risky vehicle is to be transferred or enters the service store, a portable infrared thermal imager or infrared thermometer must be used to detect the highest temperature point of the power battery at the first time;

Portable infrared thermal imager/infrared thermometer: the measuring range must be -50°C~380°C.

  1. If the temperature of the damaged part of the battery pack is higher than the ambient temperature by 10°C or there is an obvious temperature rise (2°C/minute) in the ambient temperature.

    Note: In this case, it is required to spray a large amount of water to cool the battery pack until the temperature drops below the ambient temperature;

  2. The battery tray is seriously deformed (with depression depth of the bearing area more than 10mm) and damaged.

  3. The battery has been ablated by flame or baked for a long time (flame distance > 10cm, duration > 10 min).

  4. The battery has been ablated by flame, resulting in ablation marks, and baked at close distance (flame distance < 10cm, duration ＞ 3 min).

  5. Electrical leakage is reported after wading and wading time exceeds 30 min.

  6. The battery tray is damaged or leaks electrolyte.

    The vehicle is highly risky if any of the above conditions is met.

Risk Handling

Transportation of risky vehicle

For vehicles that need to be transferred to the service store for maintenance, the risk level must be judged according to the risk identification contents, and corresponding pre-transportation treatment must be carried out according to the risk level:

  1. For risky vehicles, a portable infrared thermal imager or infrared thermometer must be used to detect the highest temperature point of the power battery. Vehicles with no abnormal temperature and no high risk can be normally transferred to the service store for further maintenance.

  2. For vehicles with abnormal temperature or high risk, the battery pack must be cooled by spraying a large amount of water. After the fire is extinguished, the battery pack must be continuously cooled with water for more than 30 min until the temperature drops below the ambient temperature before transportation.

For batteries with abnormal temperature, it is required to re-measure the temperature every 5min during transportation to judge whether cooling is needed. For high-risk vehicles with no abnormal temperature, it is required to re-measure the temperature every 10 min during transportation to judge whether cooling is needed.

Note: It is recommended that when possible, a temperature monitoring system must be used to monitor the temperature of the battery pack throughout the transportation, with not less than 8 temperature monitoring points.

Handling of risky vehicle

After a risky vehicle arrives at the service store, the risk level must be judged according to the risk identification contents, and corresponding treatment must be carried out according to the risk level:

  1. For risky vehicles, a portable infrared thermal imager or infrared thermometer must be used to detect the highest temperature point of the power battery. Vehicles with no abnormal temperature and no high risk must be overhauled normally according to the Service Manual, and the battery temperature must be measured every 1 hour during maintenance. It can be judged that the battery is risk-free if there is no abnormality over 12 hours.

  2. For vehicles with abnormal temperature or high risk, the battery pack must be cooled by spraying a large amount of water until the temperature drops below the ambient temperature before the next operation can be carried out. The specific operation is as follows:

    1. When a vehicle with abnormal temperature enters the service store, it must be cooled immediately (at the same time, the low-voltage power supply of the vehicle must be switched off) until the temperature drops below the ambient temperature before the next operation is carried out;

    2. After the vehicle with abnormal temperature cools down or for the high-risk vehicle, it is required to remove the battery pack at the first time. The removal process must follow Chapter III Safe Maintenance. At the same time, the temperature of the power battery must be monitored throughout the process. For abnormal temperature rise, a large amount of water is needed for cooling;

    3. The power battery must be transferred to a safe area immediately after removal and the seal cover must be removed (for a glued seal cover, cut it off directly with a knife, and pay attention to avoiding scratches). and the connecting pieces and BIC connectors between all modules must be quickly disconnected with electric insulating tools;

    4. All deformed and overheating modules must be taken out separately and soaked in 3.6% brine. During the soaking process, a large amount of hydrogen and oxygen will be produced by electrolysis. The soaking location must be far away from fire sources and well ventilated. Operators must not wear any metal ornaments to avoid sparks during operation. Pay attention to avoiding static electricity in dry environment, which can be eliminated by spraying water. After immersion in 3.6% brine for 12 hours, the measured voltage of the module must be lower than 1/2 of the nominal voltage, and then the module can be dried with an air gun and then moved to the high-risk battery storage area.

      All other modules must be hoisted out of the tray synchronously, dried with an air gun, loaded back to the tray and transferred to the high-risk battery storage area, and each module must be discharged to 5% SOC by using discharging equipment.

      Note: When there is no water inside the power battery, only the deformed or overheating modules need to be hoisted out, and the rest modules can remain in the tray. The connecting pieces and BIC connectors between modules must be disconnected and then these modules can be stored after discharging.

    5. In case of flooding, breakage and overheating of sampling lines, it is required to open the side covers of each module and cut off the sampling lines of all conductors (cutting off 1cm length close to the conductor to prevent re-contact). This operation is only applicable to batteries with wires as sampling lines in centralized BMS, instead of batteries with FPC sampling lines in distributed BMS;

    6. For maintenance after battery disassembly, seeSafe Maintenance 

Storage and Transportation of High-Risk Batteries

Storage condition

The high-risk battery storage area must meet the following requirements:

  1. The stored high-risk batteries must be discharged and dried. At the same time, the batteries of various risk levels must be classified and separately placed in the storage area, and relevant identification (vehicle information, fault conditions, etc.) must be made. Leaking batteries must be stored after anti-corrosion and anti-leakage packaging;

  2. The storage site must be a separate storage area for risky batteries. It must be far away from the production area, and have warning signs such as High Voltage and Danger. Other materials or personnel are prohibited from approaching. The site must have basic monitoring facilities and be inspected regularly.

  3. c. Stacking is prohibited. Steel multi-layer shelves can be used (the bearing weight of each layer must be greater than twice the specified weight of stored batteries, and the shelf must be clearly marked with specified bearing capacity). At the same time, the batteries must be placed horizontally, rather than obliquely, upside down or horizontally, and no items must be placed on the batteries to avoid battery damage caused by compression.

  4. The storage environment must be kept ventilated and dry, avoid direct sunlight, exposure to sunlight or rain, with favorable dust prevention, waterproof and anti-static protection. The storage temperature of the battery must be controlled within - 20 ℃ - 45 ℃ and the humidity must be 5-95%.

  5. The storage area must be equipped with fire-fighting facilities such as smoke alarm, automatic sprinkler system and automatic smoke removal system (infrared temperature monitoring system is recommended at the same time to achieve remote alarm of abnormal temperature).

Transportation conditions

The transportation of high-risk batteries must meet the following requirements:

  1. High-risk batteries to be transported must have been discharged and dried, and have no abnormal temperature rise over 24 hours.

  2. Protective packaging (with waterproof, moisture-proof, anti-static and anti-corrosion measures) must be used for high-risk batteries to be transported;

  3. The road must be unblocked. Direct sunlight must be avoided within the scope of loading activities, and flammable, explosive and hazardous chemicals must not exist. Water and other liquids must not enter the battery. Outdoor loading must be suspended in case of bad weather such as rain.

  4. Requirements for transport equipment Professional equipment and tools such as electric forklift and manual forklift with proper carrying capacity must be used for handling and placing traction batteries, so as to avoid damage to traction batteries during handling.

  5. Requirements for transport vehicles A closed compartment must be used for transporting high-risk batteries to prevent rainwater from entering the batteries during transportation. Traction batteries must be transported separately. If other items need to be loaded on the transport vehicle together, liquid, flammable, explosive and hazardous chemicals are prohibited;

  6. During handling of traction batteries with equipment, the equipment must be operated by professionals and careful handling is required; throwing, rolling and heavy pressure are prohibited. Packaged traction batteries must be loaded from the warehouse, rather than loaded halfway or stacked on the transport vehicle, to prevent the traction batteries from heavy pressure.

  7. Requirements for transportation process Land transportation: during transportation, squeeze and bumps between batteries or between battery and the transport vehicle body due to sudden braking must be avoided; during transportation, the storage temperature of the power battery must be -20°C to 45°C, the humidity be 5-95%, and the transport time controlled within 72 hours. If the time is exceeded, special treatment must be done to control the temperature and humidity to ensure that the product is not wet or exposed to the sun; shipping and air transport: Special packaging certified must be used for shipping and air transport, and shipping and air transport are not allowed in case of ordinary packaging; air transport must meet MH/T1020-2009 and pass UN38.3 test. The ambient temperature around the power battery packing box must not exceed 45°C, and it must be ensured that the product is not wet or exposed to the sun.

  8. Emergency handling in land transportation.

    1. In case of electrolyte leakage of traction batteries, stop transportation and contact relevant BYD after-sales personnel for handling. If the electrolyte of the power battery splashes on the skin and eyes, please rinse the skin and eyes with plenty of water immediately for more than 15 min, and then seek medical treatment.

    2. When the power battery catches fire, please use a non-water-based fire extinguisher to extinguish the fire. If the fire is large, it can be extinguished with a large amount of water. Afterwards, the power battery needs to be cooled with water for 30 min.

    3. During transportation, if the battery packing box is accidentally wet by water, the packing box must be removed immediately to avoid the wet packing box becoming a conductor causing battery discharge or burning battery terminals;

    4. In case of bumps and severe squeeze during transportation of the traction batteries, the transportation must be stopped, and can continue until professionals eliminate potential safety hazards.

  9. Requirements for unloading The unloading environment and equipment are the same as the loading and the unloading and storage place must avoid direct sunlight, and be not less than 2m from heat sources; before unloading, check whether the power battery packing box is damaged on the transport vehicle. Pay attention to the height of the compartment when unloading, and prevent the battery from being damaged due to the collision between the forklift truck and the compartment.
