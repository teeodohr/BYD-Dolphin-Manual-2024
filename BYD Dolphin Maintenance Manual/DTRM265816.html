Removal and installation of rear left brake caliper subassembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Caution

  • The brake fluid has a strong corrosive effect on the paint. If it is accidentally splashed on the paint of the vehicle body, it must be cleaned with a rag as soon as possible.

  • Avoid oil contact with skin. If it is accidentally splashed into the eyes, immediately rinse with plenty of water and seek medical attention as soon as possible.

  • Do not discharge the oil at will, and recycle it according to local regulations.

  1. Remove the rear left wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Connect the vehicle with VDS and enter the "Maintenance Mode Release State" of rear brake interface.

  3. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  4. Remove 1 fixing bolt and detach the rear left assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 30±3 N•m

    Caution
    Place a collection container under the brake hose.

  5. Disconnect 1 connector of the wheeled EPB motor drive assembly.

  6. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the wheeled EPB motor drive assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 8±2 N•m

  7. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the rear left brake caliper subassembly.

    • Tightening torque: 30±2 N•m

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation is completed, the brake system needs to be bled. Refer to Air Discharge of Brake System
