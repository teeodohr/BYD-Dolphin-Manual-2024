Installation and Removal of Left Rear Brake Hose Assembly

Removal

Caution

  • The brake fluid has a strong corrosive effect on the paint. If it is accidentally splashed on the paint of the vehicle body, it must be cleaned with a rag as soon as possible.

  • Avoid oil contact with skin. If it is accidentally splashed into the eyes, immediately rinse with plenty of water and seek medical attention as soon as possible.

  • Do not discharge the oil at will, and recycle it according to local regulations.

  1. Remove the left rear wheel assembly. See Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove 1 fixing bolt and detach the left rear brake hose assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    Caution
    Place a collection container under the brake hose.

  3. Remove the left rear brake hose assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    2. Remove one E-clip and take out the left rear brake hose assembly.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation, the brake system of the left rear wheel must be drained. See Air Discharge of Brake System
