System function principle

System overview

This vehicle is equipped with a single/double-evaporator automatic A/C that is applied to medium- and high-grade SUVs. It is mainly composed of a compressor, condenser, HVAC assembly, refrigeration pipeline, air duct and A/C controller, featuring such functions as refrigeration, heating, defrosting, defogging and ventilation. The system utilizes the low-voltage PTC air heater for heating and the vapor compression cycle for refrigeration, with the refrigerant of R134a. The system is touch controlled. The mode damper, cooling/heating damper and recirculation/fresh air damper of automatic air handling unit (AHU) are motor controlled.

Principle of the refrigeration system

This vehicle is equipped with a scroll compressor, which pumps the gaseous refrigerant out of the evaporator and compresses it into the condenser. The high-pressure gaseous refrigerant is liquefied by the condenser where heat exchange occurs (heat is released) and the heat is taken away by outside air. The high-pressure liquid refrigerant is routed through an expansion valve where it undergoes reduction in pressure. The low-pressure liquid refrigerant is vaporized in the evaporator where heat exchange occurs (heat is absorbed). The cooled air near the evaporator is blown by the blower into the compartment. The gaseous refrigerant is pumped by the compressor into the condenser. In this way, the refrigerant circulates in a closed area and the heat in the compartment is steadily rejected to the outside, bringing the temperature in the compartment down to a suitable degree.

Principle of heating system

The heating system utilizes a heat pump or PTC air heater. The heating system is mainly composed of a PTC air heater, compressor, gas-liquid separator, thermal management integrated module, heat exchanger and blower, air duct and control mechanism. The shell of the heating unit is integrated with the evaporator shell. The blower and air duct are shared between the heating and refrigeration systems.

When the compressor works, the high-temperature and high-pressure gaseous refrigerant discharged from the compressor discharge pipe flows into the indoor condenser through the heating pipe. The air from the blower brings the heat emitted by the high-temperature and high-pressure refrigerant into the passenger compartment or onto the front windshield to increase the interior temperature and perform defrosting. After being dissipated in the indoor condenser, the low-temperature and low-pressure liquid refrigerant absorbs heat from the environment (or motor controller coolant) through the thermal management integrated module and then becomes gaseous refrigerant. Then, it flows into the compressor through a gas-liquid separator and circulates in this way, so that heating with hot air is achieved.

Thermal management function of the battery

The battery has thermal management functions such as battery cooling, battery heating and internal circulation of the battery. They are the main functions of the heat pump A/C system.

The heat pump A/C system is characterized by battery direct cooling and direct heating, decrease of A/C energy consumption in non-extremely low temperature, and inefficient heat generation in extremely low temperature environment supplemented by the motor reactive and low-voltage PTC. Common functions include: heat pump heating, A/C refrigeration, battery cooling, battery heating, simultaneous operation of refrigeration and battery cooling, simultaneous operation of heating and battery heating, dehumidification and other working modes.
