Removal and installation of front left door assembly

Removal

Caution
Remove or install the door assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

  1. Remove the front left door frame trim strip assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Door Trim Strip Assembly 

  2. Remove the left side mirror assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Outside Rearview Mirror Assembly 

  3. Remove the front left door outer handle assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front left door outer handle assembly 

  4. Remove the front left door run channel assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Door Window Runchannel Assembly 

  5. Remove the front left window regulator assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front left window regulator assembly 

  6. Remove the rear movable guide rail assembly of front left door. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Door Rear Guide Rail Assembly 

  7. Remove the front left door stopper block. Refer to Installation and Removal of Door Stop Block 

  8. Remove the front left door brake assembly. Refer to Front Hatch Cover Front Door Stopper Assembly 

  9. Remove the outer weather strip of front left door. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Door Outer Weatherstrip Assembly 

  10. Remove the front left door wiring harness. Refer to Removal and installation of front left door wiring harness 

  11. Remove 2 fixing bolts of the front left door assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 28±2 N•m

  12. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the front left door assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 28±2 N•m

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
