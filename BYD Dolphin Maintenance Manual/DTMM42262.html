Lifting the vehicle

Supporting points for vehicle lifting

Danger

  • During lifting, ensure that the lifting equipment is not in contact with the power battery, high voltage harness or any other high voltage component. Damage to high voltage component can seriously endanger personal safety.

  • Be sure to ensure that all support point are aligned properly. If any deviation occurs, the support point may be deformed or even slid during lifting, resulting in serious personal injury.

  • When lifting the vehicle, it is strictly forbidden to stand or get close to the underside of the vehicle.

Warning

  • The vehicle lifting support point is specially designed to bear the weight of the vehicle, while other parts cannot, and can easily be deformed and damaged when overstressed.

  • Do not use the power battery assembly housing as a support point, otherwise the power battery may be damaged.

Confirmations before vehicle lifting

Warning

  • The jack supplied with the vehicle is used to replace the leaking tire in case of emergency. To avoid damage to the vehicle, do not jack up the vehicle with jack for other purposes, otherwise personal injury may be caused. See the user manual for the use of the jack supplied with the vehicle.

  • Before jacking the vehicle with a jack, make sure that the vehicle is parked on a stable and level ground.

  • Do not use the jack to support the vehicle completely. Be sure to lift the vehicle with appropriate equipment such as a vehicle lifter to provide rigid support.

  • When working under the vehicle, you must use a vehicle lift, not just a jack or bracket to lift the vehicle.

  • Check the relevant parameters of the lifting equipment to ensure that the lifting equipment has sufficient size and load carrying capacity.

  • Ensure that the lifting equipment has been properly maintained as required and is in good condition.

  • Whether the lifting equipment can work normally during the test run, it is strictly prohibited to use the lifting equipment with faults or potential safety risks.

  • When lifting the vehicle, do not sit in the vehicle unless necessary.

  • Before lifting the vehicle, make sure that no person is close to the vehicle directly below it.

Lifting the vehicle with a double-post lift

Caution
Please operate the lifting vehicle as follows. Please see the instructions of the lift if special operation is required.

  1. Drive the vehicle to the center of the lift to ensure that the distance from the vehicle to the front supporting point of the lift and the distance from the vehicle to the rear supporting points of the lift are equal.

  2. Place the supporting points at 4 positions of the lift right below the vehicle lifting supporting points, and adjust the height between the lift supporting points and the vehicle lifting supporting points to be consistent.

  3. Operate the lift slowly, so that its supporting points just come into contact with the vehicle lifting supporting points and are slightly stressed.

  4. Check again whether the supporting points at 4 positions of the lift are aligned with the vehicle lifting supporting points. In case of any misalignment, slightly lower the lift and repeat steps 2 and 3 until all supporting points are aligned.

  5. Operate the lift to rise to a proper height. When lifting the vehicle, always pay attention to whether there are objects around and under the vehicle or people approaching.

  6. Lock the lift to ensure that the safety mechanism of the lift works correctly.

  7. When lowering the vehicle, make sure again that there are no personnel or equipment directly under the vehicle.

  8. Deactivate the safety mechanism of lift and operate the lift to slowly lower the vehicle. When lowering the vehicle, always pay attention to whether there are objects around and under the vehicle or people approaching.

  9. Lower the lift to the lowest position, retract its support arm and drive the vehicle out.

Lifting the vehicle with a platform lift

Caution
Please operate the lifting vehicle as follows. Please see the instructions of the lift if special operation is required.

  1. Drive the vehicle to the center of the lift platform to ensure that the distances in all directions are equal.

  2. Apply the parking brake to ensure that the vehicle is firmly parked on the lifting platform.

  3. Slowly operate the lift to raise the vehicle to a proper height. When lifting the vehicle, always pay attention to whether there are objects around and under the vehicle or people approaching.

  4. Lock the lift to ensure that the safety mechanism of the lift works correctly.

  5. When lowering the vehicle, make sure again that there are no personnel or equipment directly under the vehicle.

  6. Deactivate the safety mechanism of lift and operate the lift to slowly lower the vehicle. When lowering the vehicle, always pay attention to whether there are objects around and under the vehicle or people approaching.

  7. Lower the lift to the lowest position and drive the vehicle out.
