Replacement of coolant

Caution
If the coolant is hot, wait for the coolant to cool down before operating. Otherwise, the high temperature and high pressure coolant will be sprayed out, resulting in serious personal injury.

Drainage 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the front bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front bumper assembly 

  2. Open the expansion tank cover.

  3. Loosen 1 fixing clamp, detach coolant pipe assembly 1 and motor radiator assembly I, and drain the coolant.

    Caution
    Collect the coolant in the collecting container at the junction between the cooling pipe assembly 1 and the motor radiator assembly I until the coolant is drained.

Filling

  1. Install the coolant pipe assembly 1 to its original position.

  2. Fill coolant to a level between the "MAX" and "MIN" marks on the expansion tank assembly I, and put on the expansion tank cover.

    Caution

    • Observe the coolant level in the expansion tank assembly I. If the coolant is lower than the "Min" mark, add coolant until it reaches between the "Max" and "Min" marks.

    • Do not dilute the coolant at will, coolant do not reuse, mixed use, do not allow to replace different colors of coolant.

    • Coolant protects against frost, corrosion and scaling, and also increases boiling point, so coolant must be filled standardly.

    • Coolant must be recovered in accordance with the relevant national regulations.
