Removal and installation of rear spoiler assembly

Removal

  1. Open the trunk lid assembly.

  2. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  3. Remove 4 bolt caps of the trunk lid assembly.

  4. Remove 4 fixing bolts of the rear spoiler assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 5±1 N•m

  5. Remove the rear spoiler assembly.

    1. Detach the rear spoiler assembly.

    2. Detach 1 wiring harness rubber sleeve.

  6. Detach the rear washer pipeline subassembly III from the rear spoiler assembly.

  7. Disconnect 1 connector and take out the rear spoiler assembly.

  8. To replace the rear spoiler assembly, remove the following accessories from the rear spoiler:

    • Remove the high-mount stop light. Refer to Installation and Removal of High-Mounted Brake Light 

    • Remove the rear washer pipeline and nozzle assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear washer pipeline and nozzle assembly 

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
