Removal and installation of driver's seat assembly (power)

Removal

  1. Remove the lower anchorage end cover of the driver's seat belt. Refer to Removal and installation of lower anchorage end cover of driver's seat belt (electric) 

  2. Remove 1 fixing bolt of the driver's seat assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

  3. Remove 2 fixing bolts at the front of the driver's seat assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

    Caution
    Before removing the front fixing bolt of the seat assembly, the seat assembly must be adjusted to the rearmost position.

  4. Remove 2 fixing bolts at the rear of driver's seat assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

    Caution
    Please adjust the seat assembly to the front end before removing the fixing bolt at the rear of the seat assembly.

  5. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  6. Detach the floor wiring harness from the driver's seat assembly.

    1. Detach 1 wiring harness clip.

    2. Disconnect 3 connectors.

  7. Take out the driver's seat assembly.

    Caution

    • Take out the seat assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

    • Never scratch the body guards when taking out the seat assembly.

Installation

Caution
After the electric seat assembly is installed, operate the seat adjustment button to check whether the seat assembly can work normally.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
