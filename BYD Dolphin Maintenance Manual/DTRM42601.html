Removal and installation of front brake pad assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right front brake pad assemblies are the same. Take the left as an example.
Caution

  • The brake pad is made of materials that meet the requirements of national standards, but frequent inhalation of its dust is harmful to personal heath.

  • Do not clean the brake assembly with inhalation hose or bristle brush; be sure to use the vacuum cleaner.

  1. Remove the front left wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove 1 fixing bolt from the front left brake caliper body subassembly.

    • Tightening torque: 35±3 N•m

  3. Remove the front brake pad assembly.

    1. Turn the front left brake caliper body subassembly upward.

      Caution
      After turning upwards, it must be fixed with a tie to avoid injury caused by the left front brake caliper body sub-assembly turning downwards suddenly.

    2. Remove the front brake pad assembly.

Installation

Caution
If it is necessary to replace the brake pad assembly, replace the brake pad fixing support with a new one.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
