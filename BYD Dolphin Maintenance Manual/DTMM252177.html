System descriptions

Overview

The TPMS directly measures parameters such as tire pressure and acceleration with the pressure sensor in each tire and sends the collected data through wireless RF to the receiving module which demodulates, analyzes and processes the RF data as CAN signals. Finally, the tire-related information is displayed digitally through instrument panel cluster in a real time manner. When the pressure of any tire exceeds the set normal threshold, the system will send alarms to driver in audible and visual form to ensure driving safety.
