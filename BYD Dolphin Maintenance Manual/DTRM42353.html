Removal and installation of integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Power off the high-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  3. Remove the front bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front bumper assembly 

  4. Remove the right fender assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Fender Assembly 

  5. Remove the front right wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  6. Remove the front right wheel mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  7. Remove 3 plastic nuts and take out the acoustic package V of front drive electric powertrain.

  8. Remove 7 fixing bolts and take out the power domain control unit repair cover.

    • Tightening torque: 3.5 N•m

  9. Remove 4 fixing bolts of integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

  10. Disconnect 1 connector from the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

  11. Disconnect the wiring harness of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly from the integrated front drive assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

    2. Detach the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

      1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

        • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

      2. Detach it from the integrated front drive assembly.

  12. Disconnect the wiring harness of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly from the front compartment wiring harness.

    1. Detach 1 wiring harness clip.

    2. Disconnect 1 connector.

  13. Remove 2 fixing nuts and move the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly aside.

    • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

  14. Remove 4 fixing nuts from the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

  15. Detach the sealing rubber plug of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

  16. Remove the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

    1. Detach 4 wiring harness clips.

    2. Remove 2 GND bolts.

      • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

    3. Remove 1 GND bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 20 N•m

    4. Remove 4 fixing bolts and take out the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

      • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
