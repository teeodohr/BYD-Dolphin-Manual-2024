Removal and installation of front subframe and powertrain

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Power off the high-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  3. Remove the front bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front bumper assembly 

  4. Drain the coolant. Refer to Replacement of coolant 

  5. Remove the fully-enclosed deflector assembly. Removal and installation of fully-enclosed deflector assembly 

  6. Drain the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  7. Remove the condenser assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of condenser assembly 

  8. Remove the motor radiator assembly I. Refer to Removal and installation of motor radiator assembly I 

  9. Remove the front left wheel. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  10. Remove the front right wheel. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  11. Remove the front right wheel mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  12. Remove the left/right front stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Tie Rod and Ball Assembly of Front Transverse Stabilizer Bar 

  13. Remove the front left lower control arm assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Lower Swing Arm Assembly 

  14. Remove the front right lower control arm assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Front Lower Swing Arm Assembly 

  15. Remove the front left CV joint assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front left CV joint assembly 

  16. Remove the front right CV joint assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front right CV joint assembly 

  17. Remove 3 fixing clips and take out the acoustic package V of front drive electric powertrain.

  18. Remove 7 fixing bolts and take out the power domain control unit repair cover.

    • Tightening torque: 3.5 N•m

  19. Remove 4 fixing bolts from the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

  20. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly from the power domain control unit.

    • Tightening torque: 9 N•m

  21. Disconnect 1 high-voltage connector of the integrated AC/DC charging vehicle socket subassembly.

  22. Detach the positive terminal insulation cover.

  23. Remove 1 fixing nut, and detach the front compartment wiring harness from the power domain control unit.

    • Tightening torque: 13±1 N•m

  24. Detach 4 fixing clips from the front compartment wiring harness.

  25. Disconnect 4 low-voltage connectors and move the front compartment wiring harness aside.

  26. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the compressor suction pipe assembly 1 from the electric compressor assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 22±2 N•m

    Caution
    To replace the A/C pipeline involves the O-ring of the component, you need to replace the O-ring with a new one every time.

  27. Remove 1 fixing bolt of the electric compressor assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 22±2 N•m

  28. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 1 from the electric compressor assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 22±2 N•m

    Caution
    To replace the A/C pipeline involves the O-ring of the component, you need to replace the O-ring with a new one every time.

  29. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and move the GND wiring harness of the front motor assembly aside.

    • Tightening torque: 18±2 N•m

  30. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and move the GND wiring harness of the main motor controller housing aside.

    • Tightening torque: 18±2 N•m

  31. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and detach the coolant pipe assembly 5 from the coolant inlet pipe tee.

  32. Detach the wiring harness clip of the coolant pipe assembly 5.

  33. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and detach the motor coolant pump inlet pipe assembly from the coolant inlet pipe tee.

  34. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and detach the coolant pipe assembly 2 from the integrated front drive assembly.

  35. Remove 2 fixing bolts and move aside the electric coolant pump assembly I.

    • Tightening torque: 10 N•m

  36. Remove 1 cotter pin from the left outer ball joint of the steering gear.

  37. Remove 1 fixing nut, and use a tool to detach the left outer ball joint of the steering gear from the front left steering knuckle.

    • Tightening torque: 50 N•m

  38. Remove 1 cotter pin from the right outer ball joint of the steering gear.

  39. Remove 1 fixing nut, and use a tool to detach the outer right ball joint of the steering gear from the front right steering knuckle.

    • Tightening torque: 50 N•m

  40. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the EPS column & universal joint assembly from the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 60 N•m

  41. Remove the front subframe and the powertrain.

    1. Detach 1 fixing clip of the front drain pipe.

    2. Remove 4 fixing bolts and take out the front subframe and powertrain.

      • Tightening torque: 200±10 N•m

    Caution

    • Use the workbench to support under the front subframe and powertrain and support firmly, do not allow shaking.

    • Slowly lift the vehicle. Multiple maintenance technicians are required to assist and observe whether other accessories of the vehicle body scratch the powertrain.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation, add coolant. Refer to Replacement of coolant 

  3. After installation, add refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling
