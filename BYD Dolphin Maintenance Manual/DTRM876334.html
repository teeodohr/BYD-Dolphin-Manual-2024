Replacement of coolant

Every 48 months or 100,000 kilometres

Drainage

Tools and equipment required for repair

  • Liquid collecting container

  • Spring clamp pliers/water pipe pliers

Caution

When the vehicle is in a warm-up state, there is a high pressure in the cooling system. High-temperature steam and high-temperature coolant may cause burns. It may cause burns to hands and other parts of the body.

  • Wear protective gloves.

  • Wear protective glasses.

  • Unloading pressure: Cover the coolant sub-tank sealing cap with a rag and carefully open it.

Warning

  • Refer to the time and mileage recommended by the scheduled maintenance schedule, completely drain the coolant, and then refill a new coolant, which needs to be filled with the coolant designated by BYD or of the same level. Using a different grade of coolant or adding water directly may cause corrosion and deposits in the cooling system.

  • The drain of coolant is carried out under the vehicle. Therefore, unless you have sufficient knowledge and tools, this job should be done by a skilled technician.

Reminder

Strictly comply with local waste disposal regulations on coolant!

  1. Set the vehicle power supply to OK and let the electric coolant pump assembly I run for about 5 minutes, and then set the vehicle power supply to OFF.Repeat this step for 2-3 times.

  2. Remove the front bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front bumper assembly 

  3. Unscrew the expansion tank cover.

  4. Loosen 1 fixing clamp, detach coolant pipe assembly 1 and motor radiator assembly I, and drain the coolant.

    Caution
    Collect the coolant in the collecting container at the junction between the cooling pipe assembly 1 and the motor radiator assembly I until the coolant is drained.

  5. Install the coolant pipe assembly 1 to its original position.

Filling

  1. Fill with qualified new coolant.

  2. Install the expansion tank lid and tighten it. Set the vehicle power supply to OK and let the electric coolant pump assembly I run for about 5 minutes then set the vehicle power supply to OFF.

  3. After the coolant temperature down, open the expansion tank lid, and then fill coolant till its liquid level reach the mark of the upper limit (MAX) of the expansion tank.

  4. Repeat “Step 1” and “Step 2” till the expansion tank liquid level unchanging in the middle of “MAX” and ”MIN”,then install the expansion tank lid.

    Caution

    • Set the vehicle power supply to OK, observe the coolant level in the expansion tank assembly I. If the coolant is lower than the "Min" mark, add coolant until it reaches between the "Max" and "Min" marks.

    • The capacity of the coolant of the cooling system of this vehicle is platform A power: 3.3 ± 0.2. Platform B power: 3.1±0.2 （BYD DOLPHIN Active） 。

    • Do not dilute coolant at will, do not reuse or mix the coolant, and do not replace it with one of a different color.

    • Coolant can prevent frost, corrosion damage, and scaling. In addition, it can also raise the boiling point, so coolant must be filled according to standards.

    • The coolant must be recovered in accordance with relevant national regulations.

    • After the installation, check joints between cooling pipe assembly I and motor radiator assembly I to make sure there is no leakage. If any, the overhaul shall be carried out again.

  5. Install front bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front bumper assembly
