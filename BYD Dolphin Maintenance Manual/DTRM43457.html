Installation and Removal of Right Side Skirt Panel Assembly

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the right front wheel eyebrow trim panel assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Front Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly 

  2. Remove the right rear wheel eyebrow trim panel assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Rear Wheel Eyebrow Trim Panel Assembly 

  3. Remove 1 fixing clip at the front of the right skirt panel assembly.

  4. Remove 1 fixing clip at the rear of the right skirt panel assembly.

  5. Remove the right side skirt panel assembly.

    1. Remove 8 fixing clips.

    2. Carefully lever out the right side skirt panel assembly with a plastic crow plate.

    Caution
    Before removal, open the right front and right rear door assembly at the same time.

Installation

Caution

  • Before installation, check whether the clips are missing or damaged. If so, install new clips, and then install the right side skirt panel assembly;

  • After installation, check whether all clips are installed in place. The installation position the right side wall skirt panel assembly may deviate, resulting in warping or abnormal gap.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
