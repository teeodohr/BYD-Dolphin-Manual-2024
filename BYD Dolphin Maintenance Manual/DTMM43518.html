Precautions

Caution

  • When servicing the airbag system, failure to follow the correct operation procedure may result in serious injury due to accidental airbag deployment. Besides, incorrect service operation may result in deployment failure when deployment conditions are fulfilled. Therefore, before servicing the airbag system, be sure to read the precautions for the airbag system carefully and observe the correct operation procedure.

  • The safety protection system (SRS) can still work for a period of time after the power is disconnected. Wait at least 3 minutes before disassembling or removing any safety protection system (SRS) components.

  • Ensure that the electrical system is completely de-energized and no other power supply is connected before the removal and installation of the airbag system.

  • When installing the SRS electronic control unit, install it in the correct direction to ensure it functions properly. Before installation, check for cracks, deformation and rust.

  • If airbag or SRS electronic control unit falls down to the ground or has been subjected to vibration or strikes, or if there is any crack, dent or damage on the casing, bracket or connector, replace with a new part.

  • After a minor impact, be sure to check the airbag system even if airbags have not been deployed.

  • There are warning labels attached to the airbag components and related position in the vehicle compartment. Be sure to observe the warnings when performing any specific operation.

  • If the vehicle may be subjected to vibration during any repair to other systems or components, remove the SRS electronic control unit before the repair begins.

  • When unfavorable effect may be caused by overheating due to painting operation, remove the SRS electronic control unit, PAB or clock spring before operation and keep them properly.

  • The airbag system must be installed immediately after removed from a transport container during airbag module repairing. if a suspension is required, such module must be put back in the container rather than being unattended. The airbag module removed must be placed with the buffer facing up.

  • Do not use a test lamp or ordinary voltmeter and ohmmeter to perform airbag testing; high-impedance voltmeters and ohmmeters must be used (at least 10 kΩ/V).

  • Allow the system to perform self-diagnosis before and after any replacement of airbag component, checking if the system is normal.

  • Replace the instrument panel if it is damaged after airbag deployment.

  • The SRS electronic control unit connector, clock spring connector and PAB connector all have a protection mechanism against accidental airbag deployment.

  • After servicing the airbag system, do not connect the airbag module to the circuit before electrical inspection. First verify everything is ok, and then connect the airbag module.

  • An airbag inflator unit contains sodium azide and potassium nitrate. These materials are poisonous and extremely flammable. Contact with acid, water, or heavy metals may produce harmful and irritating gases (sodium hydroxide is formed in the presence of moisture) or combustible compounds. An airbag inflator unit may also contain pressurized argon. Do not attempt to dismantle an airbag unit or damage its inflator. Do not puncture, incinerate, or bring into contact with electricity. Do not store at temperatures exceeding 93℃.

Warning

  • Never use any airbag component removed from other vehicles. Replace with a new one as necessary.

  • Do not disassemble and repair the component of airbag system for reuse or any other reason.

  • Do not position the airbag or SRS electronic control unit in hot air or flames.

  • The airbags have a normal service life of 10 years. Do not use the airbag and replace the airbag and labels after the service life has been reached.

  • Be sure to disconnect the airbag system before performing welding operation on any position of the vehicle; otherwise, accidental airbag deployment may occur.

  • Never test any circuit of the airbag system using an electrical tester.
