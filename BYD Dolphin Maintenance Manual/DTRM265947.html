Removal and installation of thermal management integrated module

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Caution
After removing pipeline connections, properly seal all pipe fittings as soon as possible to prevent dust, impurities, and foreign objects from entering the A/C system and causing system faults.

  1. Recycle the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  2. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  3. Power off the high-voltage electrical system. Refer toPower-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  4. Remove the front gutter channel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front gutter channel assembly 

  5. Remove the fuse box bracket I. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Power Distribution Box Bracket I 

  6. Disconnect 1 connector from the thermal management integrated module and detach it from the vehicle body.

  7. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the refrigeration pipe assembly 4 from the thermal management integrated module.

  8. Disconnect the compressor discharge pipe assembly 5.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

    2. Remove 1 connecting bolt, and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 1 from the compressor discharge pipe assembly 5.

  9. Remove 1 connecting bolt, and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 5 from the thermal management integrated module.

  10. Remove 1 connecting bolt, and detach the compressor suction pipe assembly 4 from the compressor suction pipe assembly 2.

  11. Remove 1 fixing bolt and take out the compressor suction pipe assembly 2.

  12. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the refrigeration pipe assembly 3 from the thermal management integrated module.

  13. Remove 2 fixing bolt, and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 3 from the thermal management integrated module.

  14. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the refrigeration pipe assembly 7 from the thermal management integrated module.

  15. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the refrigeration pipe assembly 6 from the thermal management integrated module.

  16. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 2 from the heat pump HVAC assembly.

  17. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the compressor suction pipe assembly 3 from the heat pump HVAC assembly.

  18. Remove the thermal management integrated module and accessories assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

    2. Remove 1 fixing nut and take out the thermal management integrated module and accessories assembly.

  19. Remove the accessories from the thermal management integrated module.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt and take out the compressor suction pipe assembly 3.

    2. Remove 1 fixing bolt and take out the compressor discharge pipe assembly 2.

  20. Remove 3 fixing bolts and take out the thermal management integrated module bracket.

  21. Take out the thermal management integrated module.

Installation

Caution

  • All the O-rings on the pipe fittings must be replaced with new ones.

  • Before installing the new O-ring, make sure that the pipeline and joints are clean and free of impurities, and evenly apply refrigeration oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Vacuumize the A/C system. Refer toRefrigerant recovery and filling 

  3. Fill refrigerant. Refer toRefrigerant recovery and filling
