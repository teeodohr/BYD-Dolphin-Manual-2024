Removal and installation of electric compressor assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Caution
After removing pipeline connections, properly seal all pipe fittings as soon as possible to prevent dust, impurities, and foreign objects from entering the A/C system and causing system faults.

  1. Recycle the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  2. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  3. Power off the high-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  4. Disconnect the low-voltage wiring harness connector from the electric compressor.

    1. Disconnect 1 connector.

    2. Detach 1 wiring harness clip.

  5. Disconnect 1 high-voltage connector from the electric compressor assembly.

  6. Remove 2 fixing bolts, and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 1 from the compressor suction pipe assembly 1.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

  7. Remove 4 fixing bolts and take out the electric compressor assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 22±2 N•m

    • Filling amount of refrigerant oil of 28CC compressor: 160 g

    • Filling amount of refrigerant oil of 33CC compressor: 180 g

    Reminder

    • After replacement of the electric compressor assembly with a new one, since it contains refrigerant oil, there is no need to add refrigerant oil.

    • If the electric compressor assembly is replaced 3 times or more, it is recommended to add 20 g of refrigerant oil.

Installation

Caution

  • All the O-rings on the pipe fittings must be replaced with new ones.

  • Before installing the new O-ring, make sure that the pipeline and joints are clean and free of impurities, and evenly apply refrigeration oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal. To replace the electric compressor assembly, add refrigerant oil when filling refrigerant.
