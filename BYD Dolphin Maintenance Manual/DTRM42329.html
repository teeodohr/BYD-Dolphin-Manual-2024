Removal and installation of right differential oil seal

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Drain the differential gear oil. Refer to Gear Oil Replacement 

  2. Remove the front right CV joint assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front right CV joint assembly 

  3. Carefully remove the right differential oil seal.

    Caution

    • When removing the oil seal, special care must be taken not to scratch the reducer housing.

    • The differential oil seal is not reusable and needs to be installed with a new differential oil seal.

Installation

  1. Install the differential oil seal with a special tool (tool name: CV joint oil seal installation tool; material No.: 13461863-00). Refer to Special Tools Manual.

    Caution

    • Before installing the differential oil seal, clean the differential oil seal base and the new differential oil seal.

    • Apply a thin layer of differential lubricating oil to the outer wall of the differential oil seal before installation.

    • When installing the differential oil seal, be careful to prevent installing it at an angle.

    • After the differential oil seal is installed, it is necessary to check and ensure that it is installed in place.
