Removal and installation of condenser assembly

Caution

  • Remove the A/C pipeline involving the O-rings. Install new O-rings after each removal. Apply compressor lubricating oil to the new O-rings before installation.

  • After removing the components of the A/C system, all components need to be sealed to prevent foreign matters such as dust and impurities from entering the A/C system and causing abnormalities.

Removal

  1. Drain the coolant. Refer toReplacement of coolant 

  2. Discharge the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  3. Remove the motor radiator assembly I. Refer to Removal and installation of motor radiator assembly I 

  4. Remove 1 fixing bolt and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 4 from the condenser assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

  5. Remove 1 fixing bolt and detach the compressor discharge pipe assembly 4 from the condenser assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

  6. Remove 4 fixing bolts and take out the condenser assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Fill coolant. Refer toReplacement of coolant 

  3. Fill refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

Caution

  • Fill refrigeration oil according to the specified amount when replacing new parts.

  • Before filling the refrigerant, the A/C pipeline must be vacuumized.

  • After the above operation is completed, check whether the A/C system works properly and whether there are any leaks in the A/C pipeline.
