Installation and Removal of Right Fender Assembly

  1. Remove the right combination headlight assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Combination Headlight Assembly 

  2. Remove the right fender rear sealing plate assembly. See Removal and Installation of Right Fender Rear Sealing Plate Assembly 

  3. Remove the right bracket of front bumper, refer to Installation and Removal of Right Bracket of Front Bumper 

  4. Remove the right side skirt panel assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Side Skirt Panel Assembly 

  5. Disconnect AC/DC charging port cover assembly I connector.

  6. Remove the right fender assembly fasteners.

    1. Detach 1 clip of the wiper pipeline.

    2. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    3. Remove 1 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  7. Remove 2 fixing bolts of the right fender assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  8. Loosen the upper part of the right fender assembly for one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  9. Loosen the middle part of the right fender assembly for one fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  10. Remove the bottom two fixing bolts and take out the right fender assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  11. To replace the right fender assembly, remove the following accessories from it:

    • AC/DC charging port cover assembly I. See Installation and Removal of AC/DC Charging Port Cover Assembly 

Installation

Caution
When installing the fender assembly, confirm that the peripheral clearance is normal.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
