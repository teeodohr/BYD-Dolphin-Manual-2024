Installation and Removal of Left Rear Door Rear Guide Rail Assembly

Removal

Caution
Raise the window glass assembly to the highest position before removing the rear movable guide rail assembly from the door.

  1. Remove the left rear door guard assembly. See removal and installation of left rear door guard assembly 

  2. Remove the left rear door sealing plug I.

  3. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and take out the left rear door rear movable guide rail assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

Installation

Caution
After installing the movable guide rail assembly, check whether it is installed in place, and ensure that the window glass assembly can be lifted and lowered smoothly.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
