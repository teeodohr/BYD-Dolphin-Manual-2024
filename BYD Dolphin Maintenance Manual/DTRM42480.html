Installation and Removal of Lower Cushion of Rear Coil Spring

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right rear coil spring lower cushion are the same. Take the left as an example.

Removal

  1. Remove the rear coil spring. See Removal and installation of rear coil spring 

  2. Take out the lower cushion of rear coil spring.

Caution

  • When installing the lower cushion of the rear coil spring, the raised point must be installed into the opening of the torsion beam tray.

  • When installed, the lower cushion notches of the rear coil spring must be matched with the end of the rear coil spring.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
