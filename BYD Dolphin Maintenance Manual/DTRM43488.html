Removal and installation of roof assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the grab handle assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of grab handle assembly 

  2. Remove the glasses case assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of glasses case assembly 

  3. Remove the left sun visor assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Sun Visor Assembly 

  4. Remove the right sun visor assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Sun Visor Assembly 

  5. Remove the sun visor fixing support. Refer to Installation and Removal of Sun Visor Fixing Base 

  6. Remove the front interior light assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Interior Lamp Assembly 

  7. Remove the front left noise reduction MIC. Refer to Removal and Installation of Left Front Noise Reduction MIC 

  8. Remove the front right noise reduction MIC. Refer to Removal and Installation of Left Front Noise Reduction MIC 

  9. Remove the left A-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left A-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  10. Remove the right A-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right A-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  11. Remove the left B-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left B-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  12. Remove the right B-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right B-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  13. Remove the left C-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left C-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  14. Remove the right C-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right C-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  15. Remove 2 fixing bolts at the front of roof assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 3 N•m

  16. Remove 2 fixing clips at the rear of roof assembly.

  17. Pull the roof assembly downward to detach it from the roof.

  18. Take out the roof assembly.

    Caution

    • Take out the ceiling assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

    • When taking out the ceiling assembly, do not bend it, and place it properly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
