Removal and installation of HVAC assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Caution
After removing pipeline connections, properly seal all pipe fittings as soon as possible to prevent dust, impurities, and foreign objects from entering the A/C system and causing system faults.

  1. Recycle the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  2. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  3. Remove 4 fixing bolts of the A/C pipeline connected to the HVAC assembly in the front compartment, and detach the pipeline.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

  4. Remove the instrument panel upper body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of instrument panel upper body assembly 

  5. Remove the auxiliary instrument panel body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of auxiliary instrument panel body assembly 

  6. Remove the instrument panel lower body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of instrument panel lower body assembly  

  7. Remove the tubular beam welding assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of tubular beam welding assembly 

  8. Disconnect all low-voltage connectors from the HVAC assembly.

  9. Disconnect the right circuit of HVAC assembly.

    1. Disconnect 2 connectors.

    2. Detach 2 wiring harness clips.

  10. Lift the front end of the carpet on the front passenger's seat side.

  11. Lift the round sound insulation cotton.

  12. Detach the front drain pipe from the vehicle body upward.

  13. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the HVAC assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

Installation

Caution

  • All the O-rings on the pipe fittings must be replaced with new ones.

  • Before installing the new O-ring, make sure that the pipeline and joints are clean and free of impurities, and evenly apply refrigeration oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
