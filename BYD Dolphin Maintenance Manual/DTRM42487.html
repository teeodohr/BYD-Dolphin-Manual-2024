Installation and Removal of Rear Torsion Beam Left Mounting Bracket Assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the left rear wheel assembly. See Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove the right rear wheel assembly. See Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  3. Remove the left rear brake disc. See Removal and installation of rear brake disc 

  4. Remove the right rear brake disc. See Removal and installation of rear brake disc 

  5. Remove the rear wheel hub unit assembly. See Installation and Removal of Rear Wheel Hub Unit Assembly 

  6. Remove the upper cushion of rear coil spring. See Installation and Removal of Upper Cushion of Rear Coil Spring 

  7. Remove the lower cushion of rear coil spring. See Installation and Removal of Lower Cushion of Rear Coil Spring 

  8. Remove 2 fixing nut of rear shock absorber assembly and take out 2 fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 120N·m

    Caution
    Before taking out the fixing bolts of the rear shock absorber assembly, use equipment to support the rear torsion beam assembly.

  9. Remove 8 fixing bolts from the left and right mounting bracket assembly of the rear torsion beam.

    • Tightening torque: 120N·m

  10. Remove 1 fixing nut, take out 1 fixing bolt and the left mounting bracket assembly of the rear torsion beam.

    • Tightening torque: 160N·m

Installation

Caution
When installing the fasteners of the rear torsion beam mounting bracket assembly, tighten them by hand, install the rear torsion beam assembly on the vehicle body, use the equipment to support it to a proper position, and then tighten them to the standard torque.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
