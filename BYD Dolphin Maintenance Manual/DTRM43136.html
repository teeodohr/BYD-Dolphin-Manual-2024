Installation and Removal of Console Cup Holder Assembly

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  2. Pry out the console front shielding plate assembly.

  3. Disconnect 2 connector and take out the console front shielding plate assembly.

  4. Remove the console armrest assembly. See Installation and Removal of Console Armrest Assembly 

  5. Use a plastic crow plate to carefully pry out the console cup holder assembly.

  6. Disconnect 3 connectors and take out the console cup holder assembly.

  7. To replace the console cup holder assembly, remove the following accessories from it:

    • EPB switch See Installation and Removal of EPB Switch 

    • Wireless charging module. See Installation and Removal of Wireless Charging Module 

    • Interior detection antenna assembly. See Removal and Installation of Interior Detection Antenna (Located in the Console Cup Holder Assembly) 

Installation

Caution

  • Before installation, check whether the clips are missing or damaged. If so, install new clips, and then install the console cup holder assembly.

  • After installation, check whether all clips are installed in place. The installation position of the console cup holder assembly may deviate, resulting in warping or abnormal gaps.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
