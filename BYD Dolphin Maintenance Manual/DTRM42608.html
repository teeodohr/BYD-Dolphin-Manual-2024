Installation and Removal of Front Brake Pin Disassembly

Removal

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right front brake pins are the same. Take the left as an example.

  1. Detach the left front brake hose assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 30N·m

    2. Detach the left front brake hose assembly.

  2. Detach the brake caliper sub-assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts of the brake caliper sub-assembly.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    2. Detach the brake caliper sub-assembly from the brake disc.

    Caution
    After disengaging from the brake disc, fix the brake caliper assembly to the body with a tie to prevent damage to the hose.

  3. Take out the front brake pin.

Installation

Caution

  • Check whether the front brake pin dust boot is damaged before installation, and replace with a new front brake pin if damaged.

  • Before installing the front brake pin, apply grease to the front brake pin and the front bracket.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
