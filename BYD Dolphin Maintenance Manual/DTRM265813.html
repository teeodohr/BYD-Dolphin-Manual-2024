Calibration of EPS (Electric Power Steering)

Caution
The EPS system has an active return-to-center control function and remote driving function. After any part of the steering system (including EPS column assembly, countershaft assembly, mechanical steering gear assembly, and EPS electronic control unit assembly) is removed and replaced, four-wheel alignment should be performed, with the steering angle signals calibrated and at the same time with the ESP steering angle signals calibrated.

  1. Connect the VDS and enter the diagnostic system interface.

  2. Select the vehicle model.

  3. Power steering.

  4. Steering angle zero calibration.

  5. Calibration succeeded.
