Replacement of Left Front Quarter Window Glass Assembly

Removal

Caution

  • Must use the manufacturer's recommended glass cement, and misuse of glass cement will affect its own original adhesive property.

  • Carefully read the instructions and designations provided by the manufacturer of the glass cement used before operation, and be sure to adopt this procedure and observe all precautions throughout the operation.

  • If the coating surface is scratched or damaged, be sure to repair the damaged area; otherwise, corrosion will occur accordingly.

  1. Remove the left A-pillar upper guard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Left A-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  2. 3) Drill a hole through the adhesive agent from the inside of the vehicle at the corner of the windshield glass with a drill, and then make the piano wire pass through it, and wrap both ends of the piano wire on the wooden block.

    Caution

    • The glass seal fits the body. To prevent damage to the body surface when pulling a steel wire to cut the sealant, it is necessary to cut the edge of the sealing strip;

    • Cover the body with tape to prevent damage to the body.

  3. Carefully saw off the sealant of the corner pane by pulling the wire around the perimeter of the pane.

  4. Remove the left front corner window assembly.

Installation

  1. Clean the surfaces around the rear quarter window glass.

    Caution
    Do not touch the glass surface after cleaning.

  2. Remove the adhesive agent remaining on the contact surface between the vehicle body and the glass to ensure that the surface of the vehicle body meets the requirements.

    Caution

    • Be careful not to damage the body on its paint.

    • After cleaning, do not touch oil and water.

  3. Apply the primer to the body and window inside coated with the adhesive agent.

    Caution
    Allow the primer to dry for more than 20 minutes, remove excess primer, and do not apply too much.

  4. Apply adhesive agent with a sealant gun, align the clip on the inside of the glass with the hole on the body, and press the glass into the body.

    Caution

    • After installation, make sure that the glass clings to the body completely.

    • If the door is closed suddenly before the adhesive is completely bonded, the glass may become loose or fall off.

    • Do not drive the vehicle before the bonding part is fully bonded to ensure a thorough and sufficient curing.

    • Each glass cement has its own curing time. Be sure to check the curing time of the used glass cement with reference to manufacturer’s instruction, and take necessary measures before the bonding part is fully bonded.

    • Do not use high pressure water gun for waterproof test.

    • When drying, do not use the compressed air directly to the glued part, do not use infrared bulb or the like.

    • After the glass cement is solidified, the waterproof test must be carried out. If water seepage is found, glass cement must be used to fill the leakage part. After filling, if leakage can still be found, remove the glass and restart the installation according to the above installation steps.
