Adjustment of Front Door Assembly

Reminder
The assembly of the left and right front doors must be adjusted in the same way. Take the left side as an example.
Caution
The door assembly side bolt has no adjustment and cannot be adjusted by loosening the door assembly side bolt.

  1. Loosen 4 fixing bolts on the left front door assembly body side.

    • Tightening torque: 28±2N•m

  2. Adjust the left front door assembly horizontally and vertically.

  3. Loosen 2 fixing screws on the side door lock ring and adjust the clearance surface difference of left front door assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 20±2N•m
