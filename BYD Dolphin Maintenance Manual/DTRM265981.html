Removal and installation of lower right shield assembly of instrument panel

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the right shield assembly of instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of right shield assembly of instrument panel 

  2. Carefully pry out the lower left shield assembly of instrument panel with a plastic pry plate.

Installation

Caution

  1. Before installation, check for any missing or damaged clips. If any, install a new clip and then the lower right shield assembly of instrument panel;

  1. After installation, check whether all clips have been installed in place. If not, the installation position of the lower right shield assembly of instrument panel may deviate, resulting in tilting up or abnormal gaps.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
