Installation and Removal of Tailgate Window Assembly

Removal

Caution

  • Must use the manufacturer's recommended glass cement, and misuse of glass cement will affect its own original adhesive property.

  • Carefully read the instructions and designations provided by the manufacturer of the glass cement used before operation, and be sure to adopt this procedure and observe all precautions throughout the operation.

  • Cover the body with tape to prevent damage to the tailgate assembly.

  • If the coating surface is scratched and damaged, repair it or replace it with a new one, otherwise the damaged part will gradually expand.

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  2. Remove the rear spoiler assembly. See Installation and Removal of Rear Spoiler Assembly 

  3. Remove the tailgate lower guard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Lower Guard Assembly 

  4. Disconnect 2 connectors of the tailgate window assembly.

  5. Remove the glass seal. If the glass seal is difficult to remove, cut it off if necessary.

  6. Drill a hole through the glass adhesive from the inside of the vehicle at the corner of the windshield glass with a drill, and then make the piano wire pass through it, and wrap both ends of the piano wire on the wooden block.

    Caution

    • The glass seal fits the tailgate assembly. To prevent damage to the tailgate assembly surface when pulling a steel wire to cut the sealant, it is necessary to cut the edge of the sealing strip.

    • Cover the tailgate assembly with tape to prevent damage to the tailgate assembly.

  7. Carefully saw the sealant of the glass component by pulling the wire along the perimeter of the glass.

  8. Take out the tailgate window assembly.

    Caution
    Take out with care and with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

Installation

  1. Remove the adhesive residue on the tailgate assembly contacting the glass and ensure that the tailgate assembly meets the requirement.

    Caution

    • Be careful not to damage the tailgate assembly on its paint.

    • After cleaning, do not touch oil and water.

  2. Apply sufficient base agent around the glass surface to be bonded to the tailgate assembly with a brush, with a width of about 15 mm.

  3. Apply glass cement evenly to the installation position of the tailgate assembly and the glass.

  4. Align the clip on the inner surface of the glass with the hole on the tailgate assembly. Press the glass onto the tailgate assembly.

    Caution

    • Ensure that the glass is fully backrest to the door assembly after installation.

    • If the adhesive tape is not fully attached, press it with tape until the adhesive is completely bonded.

    • If the door is closed suddenly before the adhesive is completely bonded, the glass may become loose or fall off.

    • Do not drive the vehicle before the bonding part is fully bonded to ensure a thorough and sufficient curing.

    • Each glass cement has its own curing time. Be sure to check the curing time of the used glass cement with reference to manufacturer’s instruction, and take necessary measures before the bonding part is fully bonded.

    • Do not use high pressure water gun for waterproof test.

    • When drying, do not use the compressed air directly to the glued part, do not use infrared bulb or the like.

    • After the glass cement is solidified, the waterproof test must be carried out. If water seepage is found, glass cement must be used to fill the leakage part. After filling, if leakage can still be found, remove the glass and restart the installation according to the above installation steps.
