Installation and Removal of Expansion Tank Assembly I

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  2. Untighten the expansion tank cover.

    Reminder
    Before removing the expansion tank assembly I, the coolant in the expansion tank assembly I must be drained by equipment.

  3. Remove the expansion tank assembly Ⅰ.

    1. Loosen the fixing clamp, and detach the cooling pipe assembly 5 from the expansion tank assembly I.

    2. Loosen the fixing clamp, and detach the cooling pipe assembly 4 from the expansion tank assembly I.

    3. Remove 2 bolts and take out the expansion tank assembly I.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

Installation

Caution

  • Different models of coolant must not be mixed for use, otherwise you may damage the vehicle.

  • After the installation is completed, the coolant must be added between the level mark Max and MIN of the expansion tank assembly I.

  • After installation, check the pipeline joints to make sure there is no leakage. If any, the inspection and repair must be carried out again.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation, fill in the coolant. See Replacement of coolant
