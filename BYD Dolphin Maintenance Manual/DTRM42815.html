Adjustment of Front Compartment Cover Assembly

Adjustment

  1. Loosen the four fixing nuts of the front compartment cover assembly.

  2. Adjust the clearance between the front compartment cover assembly and the left and right fender assembly in all directions, and then tighten the fixing bolt.

    • Tightening torque: 25±3N•m

  3. Turn the buffer block of the front compartment cover clockwise or counterclockwise and adjust the height of the front end of the front compartment cover assembly so that it is not loose or difficult to close properly.

    Caution
    The two buffer blocks on the left side of the front compartment cover are the same as those on the right side. The following figure uses the two buffer blocks on the right as an example.
