Safe Maintenance

Personnel Requirements

Personnel allocation

  1. Maintenance technicians:

    Maintenance operators with qualifications for the maintenance of pure electric and hybrid models are responsible for vehicle inspection and maintenance as described below:

    1. Routine maintenance

    2. Inspection and maintenance of non-high voltage components;

    3. Inspection and maintenance of non-high voltage circuits;

  2. Supervisors:

    Maintenance technicians with qualifications for the maintenance of pure electric and hybrid models are responsible for supervising the whole process of maintenance as described below:

    1. Supervise whether the composition of maintenance personnel, use of tools, wearing of PPE, safety protection of spare parts, and the maintenance safety warning signs meet the requirements;

    2. Check the emergency maintenance switch on and off (if equipped) / check the vehicle power on and off;

    3. Check the safety maintenance operation procedures in the maintenance process, and guide the operators according to the safety maintenance operation procedures; the maintenance personnel must inform the supervisor after completing an operation, and the supervisor must make marks in the operation flow sheet;

    4. The supervisors must take responsibility seriously to ensure the safety of the maintenance process and avoid safety liability accidents.

  3. In addition to the maintenance technician, a supervisor must be provided for the inspection and maintenance of the high voltage circuits.

Personnel qualification

Maintenance technicians for new energy vehicles must have the following qualifications:

  1. Special Appliance Operation Certificate (Electrician) recognized by the state.

  2. Junior (and above) Electrician's Certificate for (vocational qualification)

  3. The corresponding maintenance qualification certificate for pure electric and hybrid models granted by BYD Auto Sales Co., Ltd.

  4. And other requirements of local regulations, if any.

Equipment Inspection

  1. Insulation equipment and safety protection equipment must be inspected for damage, metal puncture, etc. before each use, and if any, they must not be used;

  2. Insulation equipment and safety protection equipment must be tested for moisture, water and dirt before each use, and if any, they must be restored to performance before use;

  3. Set the megohmmeter to 1000VDC to detect the insulation resistance of the weakest part of the insulation equipment every month. If the resistance is less than 2MΩ, the insulation equipment must not be used.

High Voltage Identification

high voltage line:

  1. The orange harnesses of the vehicle are high voltage wires.

  2. The power battery is connected to the red voltage sampling harness of the power manager.

high voltage components:

  1. All modules connected to the high voltage harness of the vehicle.

  2. High voltage components include (but are not limited to): power battery, high voltage distribution box, OBC, solar charger (if equipped), MCU assembly, charging and distribution assembly, DC and A/C driver assembly, electric powertrain, electric compressor assembly, PTC, A/C distribution box, DC/AC charging port, etc.

Maintenance Preparation

Personnel and protection equipment

  • Insulating shoes

  • Insulating gloves

  • Acid and alkali resistant gloves

  • Insulation pad

Allocate personnel according to maintenance items and provide them with protection equipment.

  1. Maintenance and inspection of non-high voltage circuits:

    Maintenance technicians can work independently, and follow the operating specifications consistent with those for fuel vehicles, as well as the maintenance specifications as specified in the service manual.

  2. Inspection of high voltage circuits:

    1. No power battery leakage:

      Personnel allocation: Maintenance technicians + supervisors.

      Protection equipment: insulating shoes + insulating gloves + insulating pads.

    2. power battery leakage found:

      Personnel allocation: Maintenance technicians + supervisors.

      power battery busbar disconnection: insulating shoes + insulating gloves + insulating pads.

      power battery removal: insulating shoes + acid and alkali resistant gloves + protective goggles + insulating pads.

Maintenance tools

  1. Maintenance and inspection of non-high voltage circuits:

    Select basic tools + special tools according to maintenance items.

  2. Inspection of high voltage circuits

    Select basic tools + special tools + insulation tools according to the maintenance items.

Maintenance site

  1. Please take safety isolation measures (use guard fences for isolation) before maintenance operations, and set up high voltage warning signs to warn relevant personnel and avoid safety accidents.

  2. Operate at the new energy maintenance station when conditions permit, and connect the body to the protective ground wire.

Inspection of High Voltage Circuits

Non-live inspection

  1. Power off the vehicle.

    1. Set the vehicle to OFF and keep it stationary for 5min.

    2. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See  

    3. Disconnect the power maintenance switch (if equipped);

    4. Disconnect the positive and negative busbars of the power battery;

    5. Insulate and seal the positive and negative busbar connectors of the power battery and the harness connectors to prevent short circuits and foreign objects.

      Note: Operators in steps 3, 4, and 5, and e must wear insulating shoes, insulating gloves and insulating pads.

  2. Inspection of high voltage circuits other than power battery.

    1. 3 min after the completion of the power-off link of the vehicle, use a multimeter to measure the voltage between the positive and negative terminals (one-handed operation) before testing the peripheral high voltage parts, which must be less than 60V DC.

    2. When the measurement voltage is less than 60V DC, the high voltage circuits and components other than the power battery can be detected normally. When the measured voltage is greater than 60V DC, do not perform any operation until the voltage drops to within 60V DC.

    3. During removal of the high voltage connector, it is necessary to insulate and seal the high voltage connector with insulating tape to prevent short circuit and foreign objects.

  3. Inspection of high voltage battery.

    Refer to live maintenance below.

  4. Power up the vehicle.

    1. Connect the high voltage harness connectors well, and the supervisor must review it;

    2. Connect all low-voltage harness connectors well;

    3. Power on the low voltage electrical system. See  

    4. Power up the vehicle.

Live inspection

  1. Charging fault inspection.

    1. Wear protective equipment according to personnel and protective equipment.

    2. Connect the body to the protective ground wire;

    3. Refer to the service manual to measure the corresponding parameters depending on the faults. During the measurement process, the following principles must be observed:

      When using a multimeter to measure high voltage, it is necessary to select the correct range. The accuracy of the multimeter used for testing must not be lower than 0.5. It is required to set the multimeter to AC/DC voltage, the AC range must be greater than 400V AC for AC input detection of charging equipment, and the selected test range for DC output side must be greater than that when the multimeter is set to DC of the total voltage of the power battery of the vehicle under test.

      Ⅱ. The multimeter and other testing equipment used are equipped with insulated alligator clips on one probe (required with a withstand voltage of 3kV and an overcurrent capability greater than 5A). When measuring, first clip the alligator clip to one terminal of the circuit, and then connect another probe to the terminal to be measured to measure the reading. Hold the probe with only one hand at each measurement. During the measurement process, it is strictly forbidden to operate and touch the metal part of the probe with both hands.

    4. During replacement of high voltage components and harness connectors, the disconnected connectors must be insulated and sealed to prevent short circuit and foreign objects;

    5. It is strictly forbidden to disconnect the high/low-voltage connectors when the load is live, otherwise it may cause personal injury and damage to the vehicle.

    6. It is strictly forbidden to disconnect the low-voltage connectors when the power is ON, otherwise the vehicle may be damaged.

    7. For models that require tooling for high voltage detection, live measurement is strictly prohibited when there is no tooling.

  2. Inspection of high voltage circuits and components.

    1. Wear protective equipment according to personnel and protective equipment.

    2. Refer to the service manual to measure the corresponding parameters depending on the faults. During the measurement process, the following principles must be observed:

      When using a multimeter to measure high voltage, it is necessary to select the correct range. The accuracy of the multimeter used for testing must not be lower than 0.5. It is required to set the multimeter to DC voltage, and the selected range must be greater than that when the multimeter is set to DC of the total voltage of the power battery of the vehicle under test.

      Ⅱ. The multimeter and other testing equipment used are equipped with insulated alligator clips on one probe (required with a withstand voltage of 3kV and an overcurrent capability greater than 5A). When measuring, first clip the alligator clip to one terminal of the circuit, and then connect another probe to the terminal to be measured to measure the reading. Hold the probe with only one hand at each measurement. During the measurement process, it is strictly forbidden to operate and touch the metal part of the probe with both hands.

    3. During replacement of high voltage components and harness connectors, the disconnected connectors must be insulated and sealed to prevent short circuit and foreign objects;

    4. It is strictly forbidden to disconnect the high/low-voltage connectors when the load is live, otherwise it may cause personal injury and damage to the vehicle.

    5. It is strictly forbidden to disconnect the low-voltage connectors when the power is ON, otherwise the vehicle may be damaged.

    6. For models that require tooling for high voltage detection, live measurement is strictly prohibited when there is no tooling.

  3. Inspection of power battery.

    Set the vehicle to OFF and keep it stationary for 5min.

    1. Wear protective equipment according to personnel and protective equipment.

    2. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See  

    3. Disconnect the power maintenance switch (if equipped);

    4. Disconnect the positive and negative busbars of the power battery;

    5. Insulate and seal the positive and negative busbar connectors of the power battery and the harness connectors to prevent short circuits and foreign objects.

    6. Leakage, deformation, water ingress and damage of the power battery must be handled in accordance with Chapter V Battery Safety.

    7. The power battery must be lifted up and down with a forklift with a load-bearing rating 1.5 times higher than the total weight of the power battery, and placed without skew to ensure safety.

Sheet Metal Maintenance

Welder required

  1. Power off the vehicle.

    1. Set the vehicle to OFF and keep it stationary for 5min.

    2. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See  

    3. Disconnect the power maintenance switch (if equipped);

    4. Disconnect the positive and negative busbars of the power battery;

    5. Insulate and seal the positive and negative busbar connectors of the power battery and the harness connectors to prevent short circuits and foreign objects.

    Operators in steps 3, 4, and 5, and e must wear insulating shoes, insulating gloves and insulating pads.

  2. The power battery and high voltage components will be stressed during the body sheet metal calibration process. The stressed power battery or high voltage components must be removed for safe storage and correction.

No welder required

The power battery and high voltage components will be stressed during the body sheet metal calibration process. The stressed power battery or high voltage components must be removed for safe storage and correction.

Surface grinding

Surface grinding must be done by dry grinding process, and water grinding is strictly prohibited.
