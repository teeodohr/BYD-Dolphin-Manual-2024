Removal and Installation of Three-Way Valve Body Assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Front Subframe Mudguard 

  2. Remove the front bumper assembly See Installation and Removal of Front Bumper Assembly 

  3. Discharge the coolant. See Replacement of coolant 

  4. Remove the high-pitch fanfare horn. See Installation and Removal of High-Pitch Fanfare Horn 

  5. Remove the right combination headlight assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Combination Headlight Assembly 

  6. Remove 2 fixing bolts, and take out the expansion tank assembly I.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  7. Disconnect 1 connector, and take out the front compartment harness.

  8. Detach the harness connector clip of the electric three-way valve assembly.

  9. Loosen 2 fixing clamps and detach the cooling pipe assembly 3 and the motor cooling water pump inlet pipe assembly.

  10. Loosen 1 fixing clamp and detach the plate heat exchanger outlet pipe assembly.

  11. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the electric three-way valve assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  12. Remove the electric three-way valve assembly.

    1. Detach 1 clip.

    2. Remove 3 fixing screws, and take out the electric three-way valve assembly.

  13. To replace three-way valve body assembly, remove the following accessories from it:

    • Water valve actuating motor. See Removal and Installation of Water Valve Actuating Motor 

Installation

Caution
After installation, check the pipeline joints to make sure there is no leakage. If any, the inspection and repair must be carried out again.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation, fill in the coolant. See Replacement of coolant
