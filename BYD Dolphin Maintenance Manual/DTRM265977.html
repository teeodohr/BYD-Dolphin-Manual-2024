Removal and installation of front compartment wiring harness

Removal

  1. Remove the instrument panel upper body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of instrument panel upper body assembly 

  2. Remove the front door sill shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Sill Guard Assembly 

  3. Remove the front right door sill shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Front Sill Guard Assembly 

  4. Remove the accelerator pedal assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of accelerator pedal assembly 

  5. Remove the accelerator pedal bracket assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of accelerator pedal bracket assembly 

  6. Remove the front right wheel. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  7. Remove the front left wheel. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  8. Remove the front left wheel mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  9. Remove the front right wheel mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  10. Remove the front bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front bumper assembly 

  11. Remove the vent cover assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of the Ventilation Cover Assembly 

  12. Remove the front compartment fuse box body. Refer to Removal and installation of front compartment fuse box body 

  13. Remove the integrated brake control (IBC) system assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of integrated brake control (IBC) system assembly 

  14. Remove the thermal management integrated module. Refer to Removal and installation of thermal management integrated module 

  15. Disconnect all connectors of the front compartment wiring harness.

  16. Remove all GND point fixing bolts of front compartment harness.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

  17. Pry up all fixing clips and through-hole rubber sleeves of the front compartment wiring harness, and take out the front compartment wiring harness.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
