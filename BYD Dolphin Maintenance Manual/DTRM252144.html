Inspection of rear brake pad

Reminder

The brake pad is composed of toxic substances, so frequent inhalation of its dust will be harmful to your health.

  • Avoid inhaling brake pad dust.

  • Be sure to use the vacuum cleaner to clean the brake assembly, instead of a suction hose or brush.

  1. Remove the rear brake pad assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of rear brake pad assembly 

  2. Check the thickness of inner and outer brake pads.

    • Standard: 6.5 mm

    • Service limit: 2 mm

  3. If the thickness of brake pad is less than the service limit, replace the whole set of brake pad.
