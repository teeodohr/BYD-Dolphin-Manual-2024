Removal and installation of front brake disc

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right front brake discs are the same. Take the left front brake disc as an example.

  1. Remove the front left wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Detach the front left brake hose assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 25±2 N•m

    2. Detach the front left brake hose assembly.

  3. Detach the brake caliper assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the brake caliper assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 100±7 N•m

    2. Detach the brake caliper assembly from the brake disc.

    Caution
    After disengaging from the brake disc, fix the brake caliper assembly to the body with a tie to prevent damage to the hose.

  4. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the front brake disc.

    • Tightening torque: 10 N•m

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
