Removal and installation of fully-enclosed deflector assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the front bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front bumper assembly 

  2. Remove the front bumper beam assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of front bumper beam assembly 

  3. Detach 3 fixing clips of the fully-enclosed deflector assembly.

  4. Remove the fully-enclosed deflector assembly.

    1. Remove 4 fixing bolts of the fully-enclosed deflector assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 5±1 N•m

    2. Detach 3 fixing clips and take out the fully enclosed deflector assembly.

Installation

Caution
If the fixing clip is damaged, replace it with a new fixing clip.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
