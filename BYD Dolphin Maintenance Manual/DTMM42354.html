Precautions

Caution

  • To avoid personal injury, do not remove the filler cap until the coolant has cooled down completely. Otherwise, boiling liquid and steam may eject under pressure because the filler cap is opened too early.

  • Do not dilute the coolant at will. The coolant cannot be reused or mixed. Coolant of different colors cannot be changed.

  • Do not add coolant with inconsistent boiling point and freezing point at will. Instead, use coolant that conforms to the specified model of the vehicle. Alcohol or methanol coolant or water alone cannot be used as coolant liquid for cooling system. The cooling system may fail.

  • Observe the "MAX" and "MIN" markings of the coolant reservoir. If the coolant reservoir level is lower than the "MIN" marking, check the cooling system for leakage.
