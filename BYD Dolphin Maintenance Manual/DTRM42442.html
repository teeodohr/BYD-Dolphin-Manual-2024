Installation and Removal of Right Front Lower Swing Arm Assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the right front wheel assembly. See Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Front Subframe Mudguard 

  3. Remove the fastener between the right front lower swing arm assembly and the ball stud press assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: 90N·m

    2. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 90N·m

  4. Remove the right front lower swing arm assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut, and take out 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 200N·m

    2. Remove 1 fixing nut, take out 1 fixing bolt, and take out the right front lower swing arm assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 200N·m

    Caution
    Take out the right front lower swing arm assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Wheel alignment must be carried out after installation. See Wheel Alignment
