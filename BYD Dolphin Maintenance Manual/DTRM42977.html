Installation and Removal of Tailgate Assembly

Removal

Caution

  • Remove or install the tailgate assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

  • When removing and installing the tailgate assembly, be careful to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  2. Remove the rear spoiler assembly. See Installation and Removal of Rear Spoiler Assembly 

  3. Remove the tailgate lower guard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Lower Guard Assembly 

  4. Remove the middle tail lamp. See Installation and Removal of Middle Tail Light 

  5. Remove the trunk lid switch. See Installation and Removal of Trunk Lid Switch 

  6. Remove the tailgate lock body assembly. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Lock Body Assembly 

  7. Remove the tailgate handle. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Handle 

  8. Remove the tailgate harness I. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Harness I 

  9. Remove the tailgate harness II. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Harness II 

  10. Remove the tailgate left gas spring door bracket. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Left Gas Spring Door Bracket 

  11. Remove the tailgate right gas spring door bracket. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Right Gas Spring Door Bracket 

  12. Remove the tailgate lower buffer block. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Lower Buffer Block 

  13. Remove the tailgate window assembly. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Window Assembly 

  14. Remove 4 fixing bolts, and take out the tailgate assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 25±3N•m

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
