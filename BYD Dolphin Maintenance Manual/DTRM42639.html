Removal and installation of rear brake pad assembly

Caution

  • The brake pad is made of materials that meet the requirements of national standards, but frequent inhalation of its dust is harmful to personal heath.

  • Do not clean the brake assembly with inhalation hose or bristle brush; be sure to use the vacuum cleaner.

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right rear brake pad assemblies are the same. Take the left as an example.

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Connect the vehicle with the VDS and enter the "Repair mode release" interface of rear brake.

  2. Remove the rear left wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  3. Remove 1 fixing bolt from the rear left brake caliper body subassembly.

  4. Remove the front brake pad assembly.

    1. Turn the rear left brake caliper body subassembly upward.

      Caution
      After turning up, fix it with a tie. Avoid injury caused by sudden downward overturning of left rear brake caliper body sub-assembly during operation.

    2. Remove the rear brake pad assembly.

Installation

Caution
If the rear brake pad needs to be replaced, it's necessary to reset the rear brake caliper sub-assembly with suitable tools.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
