Removal and installation of front left seat belt assembly

Removal

Warning

  • Carefully read the precautions related to the airbag module before repairing.

  • Before maintenance, turn off the ignition switch, disconnect the negative electrode for more than 3 minutes, and prevent static electricity.

  • Do not operate on the front of the airbag, but on the side of the airbag.

  1. Remove the left B-pillar upper shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left B-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  2. Disconnect 1 connector of the front left seat belt assembly.

    Caution
    When disconnecting the connector, insert the small screwdriver wrapped with tape into the connector slot to pry up the striker, and then pull out the connector.

  3. Remove 1 fixing bolt at the upper part of front left seat belt assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

  4. Remove 1 bolt and take out the front left seat belt assembly.

Installation

Caution
After installation, check whether the seat belt is installed in place to ensure that there is no distortion or seizure.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
