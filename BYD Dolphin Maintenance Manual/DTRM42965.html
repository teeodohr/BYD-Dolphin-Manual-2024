Installation and Removal of Left Rear Door External Pull Rod

Removal

Caution
Raise the window glass assembly to the highest position before removing the left rear lock outer pull rod.

  1. Remove the left rear door guard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Left Rear Door Guard Assembly 

  2. Remove the left rear door sealing plug I.

  3. Remove the left rear door outside handle.

    1. Disconnect the left rear door handle assembly.

    2. Disconnect the left rear door lock body assembly and take out the left rear door lock outer pull rod.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
