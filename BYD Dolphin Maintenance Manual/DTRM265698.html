Removal and installation of hood lock body assembly

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer toPower-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the front bumper upper cover.

  3. Disconnect 1 connector of the hood lock body assembly, and detach the wiring harness connector clip.

  4. Remove 3 fixing bolts of the hood lock body assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 10 N•m

  5. Disconnect the hood lock body assembly from the hood release cable assembly.

    1. Detach the hood release cable assembly upward.

    2. Detach the hood release cable assembly downward and take out the hood lock body assembly.

Installation

Caution
After installing the front compartment cover lock body assembly, it is required to adjust the front compartment cover lock body assembly.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Adjustment of hood lock body assembly. Refer to Adjustment of the Front Compartment Cover Lock Assembly
