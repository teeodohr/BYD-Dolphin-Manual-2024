Removal and installation of blower box assembly

Removal

Caution
After removing pipeline connections, properly seal all pipe fittings as soon as possible to prevent dust, impurities, and foreign objects from entering the A/C system and causing system faults.

  1. Recycle the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  2. Remove the instrument panel upper body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of instrument panel upper body assembly 

  3. Remove the auxiliary instrument panel body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of auxiliary instrument panel body assembly 

  4. Remove the instrument panel lower body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of instrument panel lower body assembly 

  5. Remove the tubular beam welding assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Pipe Beam Weldment 

  6. Remove the HVAC assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of HVAC assembly 

  7. Remove 2 connecting screws on the front side of HVAC assembly.

  8. Remove 2 connecting screws on the rear side of HVAC assembly.

  9. Separate the evaporator box assembly from the blower box assembly.

Installation

Caution

  • All the O-rings on the pipe fittings must be replaced with new ones.

  • Before installing the new O-ring, make sure that the pipeline and joints are clean and free of impurities, and evenly apply refrigeration oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
