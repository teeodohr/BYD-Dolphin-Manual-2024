Removal and installation of front passenger's seat assembly (double-pretensioner seat belt)

Removal

  1. Open the front and rear doors.

  2. Insert 2 miniature straight screwdrivers into 2 unlocking holes, and at the same time, forcibly open 2 unlocking devices and detach the lower buckles of the seat belt upwards.

    Reminder
    Two persons are required to perform this step.

  3. Remove 2 fixing bolts at the front of the front passenger's seat assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

    Caution
    Before removing the front fixing bolt of the seat assembly, the seat assembly must be adjusted to the rearmost position.

  4. Remove 2 fixing bolts at the rear of the front passenger's seat assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 45±5 N•m

    Caution
    Please adjust the seat assembly to the front end before removing the fixing bolt at the rear of the seat assembly.

  5. Detach the floor wiring harness from the front passenger's seat assembly.

    1. Detach 1 wiring harness clip.

    2. Disconnect 3 connectors.

  6. Take out the front passenger's seat assembly.

    Caution

    • When taking out the seat assembly, do not move the seat slide rail to avoid dislocation and affect the installation of the seat assembly.

    • Take out the seat assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

    • Never scratch the body guards when taking out the seat assembly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
