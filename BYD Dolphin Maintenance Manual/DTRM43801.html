Installation and Removal of Main Motor Control Module Housing Ground Harness

Removal

  1. Power off the high voltage electrical system. See Power-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  2. Remove the right front wheel assembly. See Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  3. Remove the right front wheel mudguard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Right Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  4. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and take out the main motor control module housing ground harness.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  5. Remove the main motor control module housing ground harness.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    2. Detach 1 fixing buckle, and take out the main motor control module housing ground harness.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
