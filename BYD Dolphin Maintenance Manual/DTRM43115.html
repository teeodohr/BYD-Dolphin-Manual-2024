Installation and Removal of Pipe Beam Weldment

Removal

  1. Remove the instrument panel upper body assembly. See Installation and Removal of Instrument Panel Upper Body Assembly 

  2. Remove the instrument panel body assembly. See Installation and Removal of Instrument Panel Body Assembly 

  3. Remove the instrument panel lower body assembly. See Installation and Removal of Instrument Panel Lower Body Assembly  

  4. Remove the electric power steering column and universal joint assembly. See Installation and Removal of Electric Power Steering Column and Universal Joint Assembly 

  5. Remove 3 fixing bolts of dashboard harness.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  6. Remove 1 fixing bolts of dashboard harness.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  7. Disconnect the connector of dashboard harness.

    1. Disconnect 14 connector of host assembly.

    2. Disconnect 2 connector of floor harness assembly.

    3. Disconnect 1 connector of yaw rate sensor.

  8. Remove 1 fixing bolts of dashboard harness.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  9. Remove 2 fixing bolts of dashboard harness.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  10. Detach clips of instrument harness.

  11. Remove 2 fixing bolts from left side of the pipe beam welding assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  12. Remove 2 fixing nuts from the pipe beam welding assembly and the left strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  13. Remove 2 fixing nuts from the pipe beam welding assembly and the right strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  14. Remove 4 fixing bolts from the pipe beam welding assembly and the evaporation box assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  15. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the pipe beam welding assembly and the blower box assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  16. Remove 2 fixing bolts from right side of the pipe beam welding assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  17. Remove the front wiper drive system. See Installation and Removal of Front Wiper Drive System Assembly 

  18. Remove 3 fixing bolts from the pipe beam welding assembly and the vehicle body.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  19. Take out the pipe beam welding assembly.

    Caution

    • Take out the pipe beam welding assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

    • When taking out the pipe beam welding assembly, never scratch the body guard.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
