Removal and installation of rear bumper lower body

Removal

  1. Remove the rear bumper license plate frame. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear bumper license plate frame 

  2. Remove the rear bumper lower trim. Refer to Installation and Removal of the Lower Trim of the Rear Bumper 

  3. Remove the lower left trim of rear bumper. Refer to Removal and Installation of lower left trim of rear bumper 

  4. Remove the lower right trim of rear bumper. Refer to Removal and installation of lower right trim of rear bumper 

  5. Remove 3 fixing screws of the rear bumper lower body.

  6. Detach the clips and take out the rear bumper lower body.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
