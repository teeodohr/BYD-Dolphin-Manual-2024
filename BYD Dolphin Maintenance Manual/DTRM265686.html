Removal and installation of front bumper upper body

Removal

  1. Remove the lower left trim of front bumper. Refer toRemoval and installation of lower left trim of front bumper 

  2. Remove the lower right trim of front bumper. Refer toRemoval and installation of lower right trim of front bumper 

  3. Remove the upper mounting bracket of front bumper. Refer toRemoval and installation of upper mounting bracket of front bumper 

  4. Remove the left trim panel of front bumper. Refer toRemoval and installation of left trim panel of front bumper 

  5. Remove the right trim panel of front bumper. Refer toRemoval and installation of right trim panel of front bumper 

  6. Remove the front bumper tow eye cap. Refer toRemoval and installation of front bumper tow eye cap 

  7. Remove the front bumper license plate frame. Refer toFront bumper license plate frame 

  8. Remove the small probe. Refer toRemoval and installation of small probes 

  9. Remove the front bumper wire. Refer toRemoval and installation of front bumper wire 

  10. Remove the front-view panoramic image module. Refer toRemoval and installation of panoramic front-view image module 

  11. Remove the front bumper lower body. Refer toRemoval and installation of front bumper lower body 

  12. Take out the front bumper upper body.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
