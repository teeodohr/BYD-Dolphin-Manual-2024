Inspection and adjustment of four-wheel alignment

Wheel alignment parameter value

Reminder

  • If the caster angle and inclination angle of the front wheel kingpin are not within the specified range, check suspension components for bending or damage. If the suspension components are bent or damaged, replace the damaged components. Do not try to repair them by tapping or other methods.

  • If camber angles of front and rear wheels are not within the specified range, check suspension components for bending or damage. If the suspension components are bent or damaged, replace the damaged components. Do not try to repair them by tapping or other methods.

  • If the front wheel toe-in is correct, but the steering angle is not within the specified range or the left inner steering angle is different from the right one, check suspension components for bending or damage. If the suspension components are bent or damaged, replace the damaged components. Do not try to repair them by tapping or other methods.

  • The suspension device can be used to adjust the front wheel toe-in and rear wheel toe-in.

Toe-in angle of front wheel

Caution
In this model, only the toe-in value of the front wheel can be adjusted through the steering rod, and other parameters of wheel alignment cannot be adjusted.

  1. Inspection before four-wheel alignment:

    1. Release the parking brake to avoid incorrect measurement.

    2. Make sure that the suspension device is not changed.

    3. Make sure that the 4 tires are of the same specification, and that the tire pressure is within the specified range.

    4. Make sure that there are no people or extra loads in the vehicle.

    5. Check whether each fixing point of suspension is loose and deformed, and check the clearance of suspension ball heads to ensure it is not too large.

    6. Check the wheels for vibration.

    7. Check to confirm that both sides of the vehicle are level with each other.

    8. The steering wheel alignment tolerance is 0°±0.2°.

    9. Carry out a dynamic balance test on wheels, and install them back on the vehicle after the test.

    10. Make the wheels bounce up and down several times to stabilize the suspension.

    11. The test bench is in normal condition.

    12. Adjust the rear wheels first and then the front wheels.

  2. Test:

    1. Check the wheel alignment according to the operating instructions of the four-wheel aligner.

  3. Adjust the front wheel toe-in.

    1. Loosen the steering link lock nut.

    2. Place 1 open-end wrench at the hexagonal position of the steering links, and adjust the front wheel toe-in to the standard value by rotating the left or right link.

      • Front wheel toe-in value: 1.3±1.82mm

    3. Tighten the lock nut.

      • Tightening torque: 70 N•m

    Reminder

    The adjustment methods of the rear left and rear right wheel total toe-ins are the same.

  4. Adjustment of rear wheel toe-in

    Reminder

    The adjustment methods of the rear left and rear right wheel total toe-ins are the same.

    1. Fix the eccentric bolt with a tool.

    2. Loosen the lock nut.

    3. Rotate the rear wheel toe-in adjusting eccentric bolt until the toe-in is correct.

      • Rear wheel toe-in value: 1.56±3.56mm

    4. After adjustment, fix the eccentric bolt and tighten the lock nut.

      • Tightening torque: 70 N•m

    Caution

    • The connection points of camber eccentric bolts can only be installed and removed once after sales. After this, nuts need to be replaced with new ones.

    • Bolts and gaskets should be replaced as needed. In case of rust, it is recommended to replace them with new ones.

    • Make sure that the gasket and eccentric bolt gasket are both properly seated in the limit groove.

  5. Adjustment of rear wheel camber angle.

    Reminder

    The adjustment methods of the rear left and rear right wheel camber angles are the same.

    1. Fix the eccentric bolt with a tool.

    2. Loosen the lock nut.

    3. Rotate the eccentric bolt for adjusting the rear wheel camber angle until the camber angle is correct.

      • Rear wheel camber angle: -1.07±0.5°

    4. After adjustment, fix the eccentric bolt and tighten the lock nut.

      • Tightening torque: 70 N•m

    Caution

    • The connecting points of camber eccentric bolts can only be removed and installed twice after sales. After this, nuts need to be replaced with new ones.

    • Bolts and gaskets should be replaced as needed. In case of rust, it is recommended to replace them with new ones.

    • Make sure that the gasket and eccentric bolt gasket are both properly seated in the limit groove.
