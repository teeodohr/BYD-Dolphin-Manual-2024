Overview

The power battery pack is mainly composed of executive unit (BASU), BIC, and battery sampling line, which are placed inside the battery pack.

The main functions of BASU include charge and discharge management, contactor control, power control, abnormal battery state alarm and protection, SOC/SOH calculation, communication function, current sampling function, total voltage sampling, contactor sintering detection, leakage detection, etc.

The main functions of the BIC include cell voltage sampling, battery temperature sampling, and battery equalization.

The main function of the power battery sampling line is to connect BASU and BIC to realize communication and information exchange.

TBYD Dolphin is equipped with two power battery pack, the model of version 400 km of the battery pack is P94, and the model of version 300 km of the battery pack is P95.
