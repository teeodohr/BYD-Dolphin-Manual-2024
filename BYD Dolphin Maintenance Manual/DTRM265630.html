Removal and installation of rear left steering knuckle (multi-link suspension)

Removal

  1. Remove the rear left hub unit assembly (multi-link suspension). Refer toRemoval and installation of rear hub unit assembly (multi-link suspension) 

  2. Remove the rear left mudguard (multi-link suspension). Refer toRemoval and installation of rear left mudguard (multi-link suspension) 

  3. Remove the rear suspension lower arm assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension lower arm assembly 

  4. Remove the rear suspension left trailing arm assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of rear suspension left trailing arm assembly 

  5. Remove 1 fixing nut and detach the rear suspension control arm assembly from the rear left steering knuckle.

  6. Remove 1 fixing bolt of the rear left shock absorber assembly.

  7. Remove 1 nut/bolt and take out the rear left steering knuckle.

    Caution

    Detach all wiring harnesses and pipelines on the rear steering knuckle.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Finish four-wheel alignment after installation. Refer toInspection and adjustment of four-wheel alignment
