Removal and installation of rear wiper arm and blade assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Detach the cap, remove 1 fixing nut, and take out the rear wiper arm and blade assembly.

    Caution

    • As the rear wiper arm and blade assembly may be tightly connected, it is necessary to carefully shake it upwards by hand to remove it.

    • During removal, be careful not to damage the rear windshield due to the rebound of the wiper arm assembly.

Installation

Reminder

When installing the rear wiper arm and blade assembly, adjust its installation position and align the wiper blade with the locating dot under the rear windshield.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
