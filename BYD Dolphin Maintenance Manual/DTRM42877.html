Removal and installation of front left door glass assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Caution
Before removing the glass assembly, adjust the window glass assembly to the service hole so as to easily removing the fixing bolt from glass assembly.

  1. Remove the inner weather strip of front left door. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Door Inner Weatherstrip Assembly 

  2. Remove the front left door sealing plug II.

  3. Remove the repair hole patch of front left door glass assembly.

  4. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the front left door glass assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 10±1 N•m

  5. Remove the front left door glass assembly.

    1. Rotate the front left door glass assembly.

    2. Take out the front left door glass assembly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
