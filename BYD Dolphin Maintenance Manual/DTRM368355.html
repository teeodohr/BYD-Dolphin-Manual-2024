Bleeding of brake system

Caution

  • Do not reuse any brake fluid discharged.

  • The brake fluid is corrosive to paint. Remove any brake fluid accidentally splashing on the body paint with a cloth as soon as possible.

  • Please take caution to avoid skin contact with brake fluid. In the event of accidental splashing into any eye, rinse immediately with plenty of water and seek medical attention as soon as possible.

  • Please refrain from discharging brake fluid arbitrarily and ensure that it is recycled in accordance with local regulations.

  • Be sure to use genuine Sinopec HZY6 brake fluid. Using non-specified brake fluid may cause corrosion and shorten the service life of the brake system.

  • Bleeding requires brake fluid pressurizing equipment and VDS. Before bleeding, ensure that the brake fluid of pressurizing equipment is sufficient (about 2L), and set the filling pressure to 2 bar (0.2 MPa). Check that the filler cap is properly tightened; otherwise, the brake fluid will spray out.

  • After bleeding, make up the brake fluid or remove excess brake fluid as required to ensure that the fluid level is between the MIN and MAX marks of the brake fluid reservoir.

Brake system bleeding methods

Wet-type bleeding

Circumstances requiring wet-type bleeding: after replacing or removing and installing brake calipers, brake hoses, and brake pipes.

Dry-type bleeding

Circumstances requiring dry-type bleeding: after replacing the brake fluid, replacing the IPB system, removing and replacing brake calipers, brake hoses, and brake pipes directly without entering the "installation mode" of the IPB system first, or after failure in wet bleeding.

Wet-type bleeding

Caution

  • Use VDS to set the intelligent power braking system to "installation mode" and then change parts; if this operation isn’t preformed before replacement or removal and installation of the brake caliper, brake hose and brake pipe, dry air discharge must be carried out.

  • After air discharge, the ABS, ESC or braking system alarm must not be issued by the instrument panel of the vehicle, and the brake pedal must be depressed normally without producing any abnormal sounds.

  • The filling pressure (2 bar) of the brake fluid reservoir must not be increased, otherwise the brake fluid pot may be damaged.

  1. Power on the vehicle.

  2. Set the IPB system to "installation mode" with the VDS.

    1. Connect the VDS and tap "Vehicle diagnosis system" to select "Passenger car".

    2. Select the corresponding vehicle series.

    3. Select the corresponding vehicle model.

    4. Scan.

    5. Select the "Electro-hydraulic brake (Bosch)" module.

    6. Select "Calibration".

    7. Select "Installation mode" for calibration.

  3. Power off the vehicle.

  4. Connect the brake fluid pressurizing equipment, and make sure that the pressurizing equipment and the brake fluid reservoir opening are well sealed and firmly fixed without leakage.

  5. Connect the brake fluid collection container with the bleed screw via a clean and transparent exhaust hose.

  6. Turn on the brake fluid pressurizing equipment to increase the pressure to 2 bar, then loosen the brake bleed screw, and press the brake pedal normally once every 2 s until the brake fluid from the bleed hose is free of air bubbles, indicating that the air has been completely discharged. Press the brake pedal, and tighten the bleed screw.

    Caution
    Air discharge is only required for the removed hydraulic circuit.

  7. Power on the vehicle.

  8. Send the command "Write vacuum filling process byte + exit installation (factory mode)" using the VDS, and select the option "Filling completed, in good condition".

    1. Connect the VDS and tap "Vehicle diagnosis system" to select "Passenger car".

    2. Select the corresponding vehicle series.

    3. Select the corresponding vehicle model.

    4. Scan.

    5. Select the "Electro-hydraulic brake (Bosch)" module.

    6. Select "Calibration".

    7. Select "Wheel bleeding".

    8. Select "Exit installation mode".

    9. Select "Filling completed, in good condition".

  9. Read the "Electro-hydraulic brake (Bosch)" DTC using the VDS, and then clear it.

    Caution
    Check and confirm that the ABS, ESC or braking system alarm is not issued by the instrument panel of the vehicle and the brake pedal is depressed normally without producing abnormal sounds (which indicates that the air discharge is successful).

    1. Connect the VDS and tap "Vehicle diagnosis system" to select "Passenger car".

    2. Select the corresponding vehicle series.

    3. Select the corresponding vehicle model.

    4. Scan.

    5. Select the "Electro-hydraulic brake (Bosch)" module.

    6. Select "Fault detection".

    7. Select "Clear DTC".

  10. Turn off the brake fluid pressurizing equipment, and disconnect it after its pressure drops to 0.

  11. After bleeding, make up the brake fluid or remove excess brake fluid as required to ensure that the fluid level is between the MIN and MAX marks of the brake fluid reservoir.

Dry-type bleeding

Caution

  • The filling pressure of the brake fluid filling device is 2 bar. Do not increase the pressure; otherwise the brake fluid reservoir may be damaged!

  • Bleeding must be carried out as per the specified sequence; otherwise, bleeding is required again.

  • During bleeding, do not exit the VDS bleeding operation interface; otherwise, bleeding is required again.

  • VDS battery must be kept sufficient during bleeding.

  • During bleeding calibration, the brake pedal surface will push against the foot. In this case, the driver can stabilize the pedal, and it is forbidden to press the pedal firmly.

  • When the bleeding calibration is terminated by ECU, please restart this round of bleeding; in case of failure for multiple times, please wait for 2 min in the failure interface and then restart this round of bleeding.

  • After successful bleeding, the instrument panel cluster shall have no ABS, ESC or brake system alarm, and the process of pressing the brake pedal shall be normal without any abnormal sound.

  1. Power on the vehicle.

  2. Set the IPB system to "Installation mode" with the VDS.

    1. Connect the VDS and tap "Vehicle diagnosis system" to select "Passenger car".

    2. Select the corresponding vehicle series.

    3. Select the corresponding vehicle model.

    4. Scan.

    5. Select the "Electro-hydraulic brake (Bosch)" module.

    6. Select "Calibration".

    7. Select "Installation mode" for calibration.

  3. Connect the brake fluid collection container with the bleed screw via a clean and transparent exhaust hose.

  4. Connect the brake fluid pressurizing equipment, and make sure that the equipment and the brake fluid reservoir opening are well sealed and firmly fixed without leakage. Turn on the brake fluid pressurizing equipment and increase the pressure to 2 bar.

  5. Enter the "Dry-type bleeding" operation interface via the VDS for guidance.

    Caution

    • The operations must be performed in the specified air discharge sequence, otherwise air discharge must be restarted.

    • When releasing the bleed screw and stepping on the brake pedal to exhaust, the drive must keep the pedal in the current state rather than depressing it forcibly in case of bulge of the pedal surface.

    • If there are still bubbles in the brake fluid coming out of the exhaust hose, repeat the operation until there is no bubble.

    1. Connect the VDS and tap "Vehicle diagnosis system" to select "Passenger car".

    2. Select the corresponding vehicle series.

    3. Select the corresponding vehicle model.

    4. Scan.

    5. Select the "Electro-hydraulic brake (Bosch)" module.

    6. Select "Calibration".

    7. Select "Installation mode".

    8. Select "Wheel bleeding".

    9. Stage I: rear left wheel bleeding

      Loosen the brake bleed screw and press the brake pedal normally once every 2 s until the calibration is completed, and then no air bubbles should exist in the brake fluid from the bleed hose, indicating that the air has been completely discharged.

    10. Stage II: front left wheel bleeding

      Loosen the brake bleed screw and press the brake pedal normally once every 2 s until the calibration is completed, and then no air bubbles should exist in the brake fluid from the bleed hose, indicating that the air has been completely discharged.

    11. Stage III: front right wheel bleeding

      Loosen the brake bleed screw and press the brake pedal normally once every 2 s until the calibration is completed, and then no air bubbles should exist in the brake fluid from the bleed hose, indicating that the air has been completely discharged.

    12. Stage IV: rear right wheel bleeding

      Loosen the brake bleed screw and press the brake pedal normally once every 2 s until the calibration is completed, and then no air bubbles should exist in the brake fluid from the bleed hose, indicating that the air has been completely discharged.

    13. IPB Housing bleeding.

    14. PFS and TMC2 bleeding.

    15. Exit the installation (factory) mode.

    16. Select "Filling completed, in good condition".

  6. After bleeding, press the brake pedal and tighten the bleed screw.
