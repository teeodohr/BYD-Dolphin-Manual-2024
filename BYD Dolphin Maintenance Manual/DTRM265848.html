Removal and installation of front left door outer handle assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

Caution
Before removing the outer handle assembly of the left front door, raise the window glass assembly to the highest point.

  1. Remove the front left door shield assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front left door shield assembly 

  2. Remove the front door sealing plug.

    1. Remove the front left door sealing plug III.

    2. Remove the front left door sealing plug II.

  3. Disconnect the outer pull rod of the front left door lock.

  4. Remove 1 plug cap of the front left door outer handle assembly.

  5. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the front left door outer handle assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 6 N•m

  6. Press 3 clips and take out the front left door outer handle assembly.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
