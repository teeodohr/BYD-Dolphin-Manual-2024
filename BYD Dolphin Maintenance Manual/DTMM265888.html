System descriptions

Overview

The instrument panel cluster is located right in front of the driver and at the upper part of the steering column, providing the driver with required vehicle operation parameters, faults, mileage and other information.

The lighting of the instrument panel cluster is realized through adjustable LEDs located behind it, so as to illuminate the meters to achieve the necessary visibility. Each indicator on the instrument panel cluster is also illuminated through special LEDs. Each LED is integrally welded to the circuit board behind the instrument panel cluster housing. The connecting circuits connect the instrument panel cluster to the electrical system of the vehicle. They are integrated in the vehicle wiring harness, routed to different positions and fixed in many different ways.

Meters:

Name

Working logic

Speedometer

Based on the wheel speed sensor, the ABS converts the wheel speed signal into vehicle speed signal and transmits it to the instrument panel cluster through CAN.

Power meter

The BMS transmits the voltage/current signal to the instrument panel cluster through CAN bus.

SOC indicator

The BMS transmits SOC to the instrument panel cluster through CAN bus.

Warnings and indicators:

Name

Icon

Working logic

Turn signal indicator

The instrument panel cluster collects the turn signal of multi-function switch through hardwire.

High beam indicator

When the instrument panel cluster receives CAN information that the high beam is turned on, this indicator will illuminate and stay on; when the instrument panel cluster receives CAN information that the high beam is turned off, this indicator will go out. This indicator work simultaneously with the high beam.

Clearance light indicator

The clearance light switch signal (CAN) is received from the multi-function switch.

Main cruise control indicator

After operation of the cruise button on the steering wheel, the instrument panel cluster will receive the information sent by the gateway and control the indicator to illuminate or go out.

Cruise control indicator

When the instrument panel cluster receives a gateway signal, it will control the indicator to illuminate or go out, which can indicate whether the vehicle is under cruise control.

ECO indicator

The ECO mode signal is received from the TCU (CAN).

SPORT indicator

The SPORT mode signal is received from the TCU (CAN).

OK indicator

The OK indicator signal is received from the VCM controller (CAN).

Discharge indicator

The discharge indicator signal is received from the BMS (CAN).

ABS MIL

The ABS system fault information is received from the gateway and illuminates.

Main MIL

Fault information and prompt information (except backlight adjustment, door and trunk status information) is received.

ESP MIL

The ESP fault signal is received from the ESP system (CAN).

ESC OFF warning indicator

The ESP OFF signal is received (CAN).

Drive power limit indicator

The drive power limit indicator signal is received from the VTOG/VCM controller (CAN).

Driver's seat belt indicator

The seat belt switch signal is received from the BCM (CAN).

SRS MIL

The airbag fault signal is received from the SRS.

Charging system warning indicator

The charging system fault information is received from the charging system (hardwire).

High-voltage battery charging connection indicator

The high-voltage battery charging connection indicator signal is received from the BMS (CAN).

High-voltage battery overheat warning indicator

The high-voltage battery overheat warning indicator signal is received from the BMS (CAN).
