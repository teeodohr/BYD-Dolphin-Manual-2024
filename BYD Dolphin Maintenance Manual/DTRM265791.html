Removal and installation of floor wiring harness

Removal

  1. Remove the front door sill shield assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Front Sill Guard Assembly 

  2. Remove the auxiliary instrument panel body assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of auxiliary instrument panel body assembly 

  3. Remove the driver's seat assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of driver's seat assembly (power) 

  4. Remove the front passenger's seat assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front passenger's seat assembly 

  5. Remove the roof assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of roof assembly 

  6. Remove the carpet assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of carpet assembly 

  7. Remove the rear bumper assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of rear bumper assembly 

  8. Remove the rear left wheel mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Left Rear Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  9. Remove the rear right wheel mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Rear Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  10. Disconnect all connectors of the floor wiring harness.

  11. Remove all GND point fixing bolts of the floor wiring harness.

    • Tightening torque: 9±1 N•m

  12. Pry up all fixing clips and through-hole rubber sleeves of the floor wiring harness, and take out it.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
