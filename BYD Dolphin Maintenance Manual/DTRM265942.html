Removal and installation of gas-liquid separator assembly

Removal

Caution
After removing pipeline connections, properly seal all pipe fittings as soon as possible to prevent dust, impurities, and foreign objects from entering the A/C system and causing system faults.

  1. Power off the high-voltage electrical system. Refer toPower-off and power-on of high-voltage electrical system 

  2. Recycle the refrigerant. Refer to Refrigerant recovery and filling 

  3. Remove the fuse box bracket I. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Power Distribution Box Bracket I 

  4. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Subframe Mudguard 

  5. Remove the front left wheel mudguard assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Left Front Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  6. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the refrigeration pipe assembly 4 from the gas-liquid separator assembly.

  7. Remove 1 fixing bolt, and detach the compressor suction pipe assembly 1 from the gas-liquid separator assembly.

  8. Remove 2 fixing bolts and take out the gas-liquid separator assembly.

Installation

Caution

  • All the O-rings on the pipe fittings must be replaced with new ones.

  • Before installing the new O-ring, make sure that the pipeline and joints are clean and free of impurities, and evenly apply refrigeration oil to the O-ring.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
