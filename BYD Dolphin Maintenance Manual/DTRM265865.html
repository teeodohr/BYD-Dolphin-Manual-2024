Removal and installation of integrated brake control (IBC) system assembly

Removal

Caution

  • The brake fluid has a strong corrosive effect on the paint. If it is accidentally splashed on the paint of the vehicle body, it must be cleaned with a rag as soon as possible.

  • Avoid oil contact with skin. If it is accidentally splashed into the eyes, immediately rinse with plenty of water and seek medical attention as soon as possible.

  • Do not discharge the oil at will, and recycle it according to local regulations.

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to: Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Drain the brake fluid. Refer to: Draining and Filling of Brake Fluid 

  3. Remove the brake pedal assembly. Refer to: Removal and installation of brake pedal assembly 

  4. Remove the front gutter channel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front gutter channel assembly 

  5. Disconnect 1 connector of integrated brake control (IBC) system assembly.

  6. Disconnect 1 connector of integrated brake control (IBC) system assembly.

  7. Remove 1 pipe clamp and take out the brake fluid reservoir connecting hose assembly and brake fluid reservoir assembly.

  8. Remove the integrated brake control (IBC) system assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing nuts.

      • Tightening torque: 18±2 N•m

    2. Remove 2 fixing nuts and take out the integrated brake control (IBC) system assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 20±2 N•m

Installation

Warning
When moving the integrated control system assembly, hold the motor with one hand and assist with the other hand. Do not grasp the ECU port, push rod, brake fluid reservoir, motor and mounting plate with one hand.
Caution

  • If a new IPB assembly is used, calibration is required.

  • After connecting the IPB assembly push rod and the brake pedal, do not pull the pedal upward, otherwise the internal structural parts of the IPB assembly may be damaged.

  • After replacing the integrated control system assembly, it is necessary to adjust the stop light switch clearance.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation is completed, the brake system needs to be bled. Refer to Air Discharge of Brake System
