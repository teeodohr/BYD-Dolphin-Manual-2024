Installation and Removal of Front Hub Flange Assembly

Removal

Reminder
The removal and installation of the left and right front hub flange assemblies are the same. Take the left as an example.

  1. Remove the left front wheel assembly. Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove the left front wheel speed sensor. See Installation and Removal of Wheel Speed Sensor 

  3. Remove 1 fixing nut, and detach the left front stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly from the left front shock absorber strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 90N·m

    Caution
    When removing the front stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly, fix the intermediate stud and then remove the fixing nut.

  4. Remove 1 fixing nut of the left front drive half shaft assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 235±15N•m

    Caution

    • Before removing the drive half shaft nut, pry the half shaft nut lock with a tool.

    • The half shaft nut is a disposable part, and new parts must be used during installation.

  5. Remove 1 fixing pin of the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly.

  6. Detach the mechanical steering gear with tie rod assembly from the left front steering knuckle.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: 50N·m

    2. Remove the ball joint with the ball joint remover.

  7. Remove 1 fixing pin of the ball stud press assembly.

  8. Detach the left front lower swing arm assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut of the ball stud press assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 100N·m

    2. Detach the left front lower swing arm assembly downwards.

  9. Pull out the left front drive half shaft from the left front steering knuckle.

  10. Remove the front brake mudguard. See Installation and Removal of Front Brake Mudguard 

  11. Detach the harness clip and put aside the harness.

  12. Remove 2 fixing nuts of left front steering knuckle. Take out two fixing bolt, and take out the front hub flange assembly and left front steering knuckle assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 200N·m

  13. Remove the front hub flange assembly.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
