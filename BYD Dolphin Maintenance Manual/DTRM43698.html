Installation and Removal of Middle Tail Light

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  2. Remove the tailgate lower guard assembly. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Lower Guard Assembly 

  3. Remove the middle tail lamp.

    1. Disconnect 1 harness connector.

    2. Detach 1 harness connector clip.

  4. Remove 8 fixing nut.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  5. Remove the middle tail lamp.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.

Caution
Check if the middle tail light work normally after installation.
