Removal and installation of rear shock absorber assembly

Caution
For the rear shock absorber assembly, the removal and installation methods of the left one are the same as those of the right one. Take the left one as an example.

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the rear left wheel assembly. Refer toRemoval and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove the rear left wheel mudguard assembly. Refer toInstallation and Removal of Left Rear Wheel Mudguard Assembly 

  3. Remove the rear left shock absorber assembly.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut and take out the steering knuckle fixing bolt and nut.

      • Tightening torque: 200±10 N•m

    2. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: 60±5 N•m

    3. Remove 1 fixing nut and take out the rear left shock absorber assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 60±5 N•m

      Caution
      Take out the rear left shock absorber assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. Finish four-wheel alignment after installation. Refer toInspection and adjustment of four-wheel alignment
