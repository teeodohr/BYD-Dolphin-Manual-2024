Installation and Removal of Left Fender Assembly

Removal

  1. Remove the left combination headlight assembly. See Installation and Removal of Left Combination Headlight Assembly 

  2. Remove the left fender rear sealing plate assembly. See Removal and Installation of Left Fender Rear Sealing Plate Assembly 

  3. Remove the left bracket of front bumper, refer to Installation and Removal of Left Bracket of Front Bumper 

  4. Remove the left side skirt panel assembly. See Installation and Removal of Left Side Skirt Panel Assembly 

  5. Remove the left fender assembly fasteners.

    1. Remove 1 fixing bolt.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    2. Remove 1 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  6. Remove 2 fixing bolts of the left fender assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  7. Loosen 1 fixing bolt of the upper part of the left fender assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  8. Loosen 1 fixing bolt of the middle part of the left fender assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

  9. Remove 2 fixing bolts at the bottom and take out the left fender assembly.

    • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

Installation

Caution
When installing the fender assembly, confirm that the peripheral clearance is normal.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
