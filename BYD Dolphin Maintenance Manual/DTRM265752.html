Removal and installation of motor radiator assembly I

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing and installing the motor radiator assembly I. Do not bump the radiator fins. If the radiator fins are damaged, poor heat dissipation or coolant leakage will be caused.

  1. Remove the fully-enclosed deflector assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of fully-enclosed deflector assembly 

  2. Drain the coolant. Refer to Replacement of coolant 

  3. Loosen 2 fixing clamps, and detach the coolant pipe assembly 3 and 5 from the motor radiator assembly I.

  4. Remove 4 fixing bolts and take out the motor radiator assembly I.

Installation

Caution
After installation, check the pipeline joints to make sure there is no leakage. If any, the inspection and repair must be carried out again.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.

  2. After installation, add coolant. Refer to Replacement of coolant
