Precautions

Warning

  • Before maintenance, carefully read the precautions related to the airbag module.

  • Before maintenance, turn off the ignition switch, disconnect the negative terminal of 12 V battery for more than 3 min, and release static electricity.

  • Operate on the side of the airbag instead of on the front.

  • Be sure to check all seat belt assemblies after any collision. Replace the seat belt assembly with a new one unless a qualified professional technician confirms that the assembly can work normally without damage. In addition, check the seat belt assembly not used during the collision, and replace it with a new one if it is damaged. Installing a new seat belt assembly using an incorrect installation procedure may increase the risk of personal injury during a collision.

  • The seat belt pretensioner is a pyrotechnic device. Always wear goggles when repairing a seat belt equipped with a pretensioner. Do not probe the electrical terminals of a pretensioner, as this operation may deploy the pretensioner or airbag and cause personal injury.

  • Failure to follow the correct operation will result in accidental deployment of airbags and failure of safety protection devices when necessary.

Caution

  • Connect the seat belt with the retractor and buckle normally.

  • Keep sharp and destructive objects away from the seat belt.

  • Do not bleach or dye the seat belt (clean it only with neutral soap and warm water).

  • When installing the seat belt, pre-tighten the screw by hand to avoid slipping.

  • Do not attempt to repair the retractor or its cover. Replace the non-functioning assembly with a new one.

  • Keep the seat belt dry and clean.

  • Replace the seat belt that has been cut or damaged.

  • Do not fill anything into the trim panel hole through which the seat belt passes.

  • When disconnecting the seat belt connector, insert a small screwdriver wrapped with adhesive tape into the slot of the connector to pry up the buckle, and then remove the connector. - After installing the seat belt connector, check whether the buckle has been fully pressed in.

  • After installing the seat belt connector, check whether the buckle has been fully pressed in.

  • After removing fasteners, be sure to put them back in place. If a fastener needs to be replaced, use the fastener with the same part number. If a fastener with the same part number cannot be found, use one of the same size and strength (or higher). Remove any fastener, which cannot be reused or requires thread locking, from the vehicle. For fasteners requiring a specified tightening torque, tighten them to the specified torque during reinstallation. Failure to comply with the above requirements may result in damage to components or systems.

  • Seat belts and their accessories can affect the main components and systems of the vehicle. Therefore, they must be carefully inspected and replaced with original parts.

  • Do not use the pretensioner (retractor) that has been triggered, but replace it in the form of an assembly.
