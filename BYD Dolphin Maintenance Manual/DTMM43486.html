Systems Manual

Overview

Ceiling assembly

It is installed on the roof and wrapped with the roof sheet metal, which can effectively isolate the heat and noise from the outside and provide a comfortable environment for the interior of the vehicle.

Sun visor assembly

Turn over the sun visor assembly to protect your eyes from sunlight when driving or sitting on the front passenger seat.

Glass box assembly

Glasses and other small items can be stored.

Roof handle assembly

The roof handles are installed above the seat assembly of the right front, left rear and right rear seats. It is designed for the occupants. When the vehicle is driving on the uneven road, the body jumps, the seat belt alone can't fix the occupants firmly on the seat, thus reducing the ride comfort. The occupants can keep their body balanced by grasping the roof handle.
