Installation and Removal of Outside Rearview Mirror Left Mirror Holder Assembly

Removal

  1. Power off the low voltage electrical system. See Power-off and Power-on of Low Voltage Electrical System 

  2. Remove the left outside rearview mirror jigger.

    Caution
    The lens is easily damaged. When removing the lens, wear gloves and remove it from the edge of the lens to prevent scratches.

  3. Disconnect 2 connectors of left outside rearview mirror.

  4. Separate the outside rearview mirror left holder assembly from the left outside rearview mirror.

    Caution
    The lens is easily to break. Please wear gloves and use plastic crow plate to remove the lens carefully from the edge of the lens holder to avoid scratches.

Installation

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.

    Caution
    Apply the adhesive agent evenly to the back of the lens when installing the holder assembly and the lens.
