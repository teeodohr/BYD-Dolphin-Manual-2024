Removal and installation of auxiliary instrument panel body assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the front left shield assembly of auxiliary instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of left front shield assembly of auxiliary instrument panel 

  2. Remove the front right shield assembly of auxiliary instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of right front shield assembly of auxiliary instrument panel 

  3. Remove the rear trim panel assembly of auxiliary instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of rear trim panel assembly of auxiliary instrument panel 

  4. Remove the front storage box assembly of auxiliary instrument panel. Refer to Removal and installation of front storage box assembly of auxiliary instrument panel 

  5. Detach 6 clips of the instrument panel wiring harness.

  6. Pry out the auxiliary instrument panel plug cap.

    Reminder
    The left side is used as an example. The procedure for removing the right side is the same as that for removing the left side.

  7. Remove 2 fixing bolts of the auxiliary instrument panel body assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 4 N•m

    Reminder
    The left side is used as an example. The procedure for removing the right side is the same as that for removing the left side.

  8. Remove the rear fasteners of auxiliary instrument panel body assembly.

    1. Detach 2 clips of floor wiring harness.

    2. Remove 2 fixing bolts.

      • Tightening torque: 1.5 N•m

  9. Take out the auxiliary instrument panel body assembly.

    Caution

    • Take out the auxiliary instrument panel body assembly with the assistance of another maintenance technician.

    • When taking out the auxiliary instrument panel body assembly, take care to avoid scratching the interior trim panel.

Installation

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
