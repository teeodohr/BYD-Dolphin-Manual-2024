Warning

  • When high voltage electrical system power-off is required: The high voltage electrical system must be powered off as per the following requirements before maintenance of any high voltage electrical system component and powered on after maintenance unless otherwise specified in the operating procedure.

  • All high voltage system harnesses are orange in color. Before carrying out maintenance work on all components of the high voltage system, protective measures must be taken in accordance with the maintenance requirements of the high voltage system, such as wearing insulating gloves, insulating shoes, goggles, laying insulated mats, high voltage hazard warning signs, etc.

  • The maintenance of the high voltage electrical system must be carried out by the maintenance personnel with the qualification recognized by the company and local laws and regulations.

  • Violation of these safety warnings may result in personal injury or vehicle damage.

Preparations

Warning
Before power-off and power-on of the high voltage electrical system, the preparation work must be carried out according to the following steps, warning irrelevant personnel not to approach, to avoid safety accidents.

  1. After the vehicle enters the construction site, use warning fences to secure the construction area and place high-voltage warning signs in all four corners within the warning fences.

  2. The repair personnel shall take protective measures, such as wearing insulating gloves, insulating shoes and goggles.

  3. Take protective measures for vehicles, such as:

    1. Lay the insulating pads.

    2. Place high-voltage warning signs in eye-catching positions around the vehicle, and leave names and telephone numbers on the sign to prevent others from touching the vehicle by mistake.

    3. Place a "No Charging" sign on the surface of the charging port cap, and leave name and telephone number on the sign to prevent others from charging by mistake.

    4. Place a "No Switching" sign in an eye-catching position in the cab, and leave name and telephone number on the sign to prevent others from powering on the vehicle by mistake.

Power-off of high-voltage electrical system

  1. Switch the vehicle power supply to OFF position and wait for 5 min.

  2. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  3. Disconnect the high-voltage distribution harness subassembly from the drive motor control unit assembly:

    1. Press the clip inward.

    2. Push the latch plate upward to the top.

    3. Rotate the connector locking bracket upward.

    4. Pull out the connecting end of high-voltage distribution wiring harness subassembly.

  4. Wait for 1 min, and use a multimeter with a measuring range ≥ 1000 V (DC) to measure the terminal voltage of connector of high-voltage distribution wiring harness subassembly and that of drive motor control unit assembly.

    • Voltage standard: ≤60 V (DC)

    • If the voltages of connector terminals are all within the standard range, the high-voltage electrical system of the vehicle has been successfully powered off.

    • If only the voltage of the drive motor control unit assembly connector terminal exceeds the standard range, be sure to eliminate the fault cause. It is not allowed to repair the high-voltage system components except the high-voltage distribution wiring harness subassembly and high-voltage battery until the fault is eliminated.

    • If only the voltage of connector terminal of the high-voltage distribution wiring harness subassembly exceeds the standard range, be sure to eliminate the fault cause. It is not allowed to repair the high-voltage distribution wiring harness subassembly and the high-voltage battery until the fault is eliminated.

    • If the voltages of connector terminals exceed the standard range, the cause of the fault must be eliminated. It is not allowed to repair any component of the high-voltage system until the fault is eliminated.

    Warning
    After the connector is disconnected, it needs to be insulated and sealed in time to prevent impurities from entering the terminal, as well as to prevent personal injury or vehicle damage caused by skin or conductive objects inadvertently touching the terminals.

Power-on of high-voltage electrical system

  1. Before power-on, make sure that electrical circuits of the vehicle are well connected.

  2. Install others in the reverse order of removal.

  3. After all the circuits of the high-voltage electrical system and low-voltage electrical system are connected, turn on the vehicle power supply by pressing the "START/STOP" button to switch the vehicle power supply to OK mode, and check that the electric appliances of the vehicle are functioning normally and the vehicle is running properly. At this time, the high-voltage electrical system of the vehicle has been powered on.
