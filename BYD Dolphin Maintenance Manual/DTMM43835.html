Removal and Installation of General Claw Components

Some parts (such as interior trim) are fixedly assembled with claw type structure. If they are damaged after disassembly or assembly, or they are not securely installed due to aging, cracking and other reasons, they must be replaced with new ones.

The following is an example of the shape and disassembly of the general claw components.

Shape (example) Removal and installation (example)
Scenario 1: use a screwdriver with protective tape wrapped around the head to pry up one end first, then separate the claw to remove the cap or cover.
Scenario 2: use screwdrivers with protective tape wrapped around the head to pry up the claw simultaneously in different directions to remove the cap or cover.
Scenario 3: use screwdrivers with protective tape wrapped around the head to pry up simultaneously in opposite directions to separate the claw to remove the cap or cover.
