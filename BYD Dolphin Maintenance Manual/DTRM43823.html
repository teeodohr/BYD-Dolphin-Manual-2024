Installation and Removal of Tailgate Harness I

Removal

  1. Remove the rear spoiler assembly. See Installation and Removal of Rear Spoiler Assembly 

  2. Remove the left C-pillar upper guard board assembly. See Installation and Removal of Left C-Pillar Upper Guard Assembly 

  3. Remove the tailgate lower guard board assembly. See Installation and Removal of Tailgate Lower Guard Assembly 

  4. Disconnect the tailgate harness I from the floor harness.

    1. Disconnect 1 connector.

    2. Remove 1 ground point nut.

      • Tightening torque: xxxN•m

    3. Remove 1 fixing clip.

  5. Disconnect the tailgate harness I from the tailgate harness II.

    1. Detach 1 harness connector clip.

    2. Disconnect 1 connector.

  6. Remove the tailgate harness I.

    1. Remove 1 harness sealant sleeve.

    2. Remove 1 harness sealant sleeve and take out the tailgate harness I.

Installation

Caution

  • When installing the tailgate harness I, use a wire rope to connect one end of the harness firmly, the wire rope from the harness mounting hole and out of the other mounting hole.

  • In the process of pulling the wire rope, pull it slowly. If it is stuck, do not use brute force to pull it hard. To prevent damage to the harness, pull it back a distance until one end of the harness is pulled out of the mounting hole.

  1. Install in the reverse order of removal.
