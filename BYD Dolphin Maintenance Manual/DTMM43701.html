Precautions

Danger
Ensure that the environment is well ventilated when recycling or filling refrigerant. It may cause suffocation.
Warning

  • The maintenance of A/C system must be carried out by professional and technical personnel, who must wear goggles during the maintenance.

  • Avoid contact of the refrigerant and refrigerant oil with the eyes and skin. In case of contact with eyes, rinse immediately with clean water; In case of skin contact and frostbite, rinse with water and apply Vaseline ointment to the skin. If the situation is serious, seek medical attention as soon as possible.

Caution

  • Because the refrigeration oil has strong water absorption, when removing the system pipeline or the pipeline connection of the components, it is necessary to seal the pipeline and component connection interfaces immediately to prevent moisture and sundries from entering the interior.

  • When inspecting and cleaning the dirt or debris on the condenser radiator fin, do not use a high pressure water gun to flush it, as this may damage the radiator fin. It can be brushed with a soft brush.

  • When the total amount of the refrigeration oil of A/C system is as low as 80 ml due to the leakage, excessive wear of the compressor may occur.

  • Avoid overfilling the refrigerant, otherwise the cooling effect may be poor.

  • When recovering refrigerant, do not open the valve too much, otherwise the refrigerant will be discharged too quickly and the refrigeration oil in the system may also be brought out quickly.

Reminder
Clean and replace the HEPA filter regularly to maintain a good air conditioning quality.
