System descriptions

Overview

Side mirrors allow the driver, while sitting in the driver’s seat, to get a clear view of areas behind and to the sides of the vehicle, as well as lower areas on both sides. This minimizes the risk of accidents and ensures personal safety.

The lens and assembly of the side mirror are adjusted through the electric adjustment switch. The adjustment function varies with the model configuration. Before maintenance, it is necessary to confirm the vehicle configuration first.

The electric side mirror has the following functions:

  • Leveling of mirror

  • Vertical adjustment of mirror

  • Electric defrosting
