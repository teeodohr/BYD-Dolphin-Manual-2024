Removal and installation of PAD display screen assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Power off the low-voltage electrical system. Refer to Power-off and power-on of low-voltage electrical system 

  2. Remove the PAD mounting base shield assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of PAD mounting base shield assembly 

  3. Remove 2 fixing bolts from the PAD display screen assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 3 N•m

  4. Rotate the PAD display screen assembly and carefully take out it.

  5. Disconnect 1 connector and take out the PAD display screen assembly.

    Caution
    After the PAD display assembly is taken out, it needs to be placed properly to prevent heavy objects from crushing or impacting or scratching the screen.

Installation

Caution

After installation, test the PAD display screen assembly.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
