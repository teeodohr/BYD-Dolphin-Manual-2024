Inspection of front brake disc

End face runout

  1. Remove the front brake pad assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front brake pad assembly 

  2. Check whether the brake disc surface is damaged or cracked, thoroughly clean the brake disc and remove all rust.

  3. Install the dial indicator base on the body, and install the dial indicator measuring probe in place so that it is in contact with the friction surface of brake disc at 90° and is 10 mm away from the outer edge of brake disc.

  4. Rotate the brake disc until the reading of dial indicator reaches the minimum, and then zero the dial indicator.

  5. Rotate the brake disc until the reading of the dial indicator reaches the maximum, make a mark, and record the end face runout.

  6. If the end face runout of brake disc exceeds the service limit of 0.08 mm, repair the brake disc with an on-board brake lathe.

    • Standard thickness of brake disc: 25 mm

    • Maximum service limit of brake disc: 23 mm

    Caution
    If the brake disc exceeds the service limit, replace it.

Thickness and parallelism

  1. Remove the front brake pad assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of front brake pad assembly 

  2. Use a micrometer to measure the thickness of brake disc at 8 points 10 mm away from the outer edge of brake disc and at the intervals of about 45°.

    • Standard thickness of brake disc: 25 mm

    • Maximum service limit of brake disc: 23 mm

    • Parallelism of brake disc: max. 0.05 mm

    Caution

    • If the minimum measured value is less than the maximum service limit, replace the brake disc.

    • If the parallelism of the brake disc exceeds the service limit, repair the brake disc with an on-board brake lathe.
