Removal and installation of front right door shield assembly

Removal

Caution
Be careful when removing the trim panel to avoid scratching the surface material.

  1. Remove the front right window regulator switch set. Refer to Removal and installation of front right window regulator switch set 

  2. Remove the front right door upper trim panel assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right Front Door Upper Trim Panel Assembly 

  3. Remove the rubber pad of front right door inner handle.

  4. Remove 2 fixing screws of the front right door shield assembly.

  5. Carefully pry out the front right door shield assembly with a plastic pry plate.

    Caution
    Do not apply excessive force when removing the right front door guard assembly in order to prevent damage to the connector.

  6. Disconnect 1 connector of the right high-pitch speaker.

  7. Disconnect the door lock cable from the front right door inner handle assembly, and take out the front right door shield assembly.

  8. To replace the front right door shield assembly, remove the following accessories from it:

    • Remove the right high-pitch speaker. Refer to Installation and Removal of Right High-Pitch Speaker 

Installation

Caution

  • Before installation, it is necessary to check whether all the clips are missing or damaged, and if so, install new clips, and then install the trim panel;

  • After installation, check whether all clips are installed in place. The installation position of the trim panel may deviate, resulting in warping or abnormal gaps.

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
