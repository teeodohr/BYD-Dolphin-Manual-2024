Replacement of gear oil

After 24 months or 40,000 km for the first time, and then every 24 months or 48,000 km

Caution

Before replacing the gear oil, make sure that the vehicle has run for more than 3 km to facilitate drainage of gear oil after the rise of oil temperature.

Warning

The gear oil may be very hot, so wait until the oil temperature cools down to near room temperature; otherwise, there is a risk of scalding.

  • Do not step on the spilled engine oil.

  • Wear protective equipment.

Repair tools and equipment required

  • Torque wrench (5-50 N•m)

  • Waste oil collector

Drainage of differential gear oil

  1. Lift the vehicle and keep it in a level state.

  2. Remove the front subframe mudguard assembly. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Subframe Mudguard 

  3. Remove 1 oil filler plug.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2 N•m

    Caution

    • Place the collection container below the plug in advance.

    • Be careful when unscrewing the plug to avoid oil spilling out of the collecting container.

  4. Remove 1 oil drain plug, and install it after draining all gear oil.

    • Tightening torque: 50±3 N•m

    Caution

    • Before installing the screw plug, clean its threads and contact surface.

    • The discharged oil must be collected and treated according to the local laws and regulations. It is forbidden to discharge or discard the oil without permission.

Filling of differential gear oil

  1. Add gear oil to the oil filler plug hole.

    • Model: Castrol BOT384 or equivalent gear oil

    • Filling amount: 0.45±0.05 L.

  2. Install the oil filler plug.

    • Tightening torque: 37±2 N•m

  3. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
