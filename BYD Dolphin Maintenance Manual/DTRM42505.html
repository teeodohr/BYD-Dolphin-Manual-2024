Removal and installation of front right CV joint assembly

Removal 3D animation demonstration  

  1. Remove the front right wheel assembly. Refer to Removal and installation of wheel assembly 

  2. Remove the front subframe mudguards. Refer to Installation and Removal of Front Subframe Mudguard 

  3. Remove 1 fixing nut, and detach the front right stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly from the front right shock absorber strut assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 90 N•m

    Caution
    When removing the front stabilizer bar tie rod and ball joint assembly, fix the intermediate stud and then remove the fixing nut.

  4. Remove 1 fixing nut of the front right CV joint assembly.

    • Tightening torque: 235±15 N•m

    Caution

    • Before removing the drive half shaft nut, pry the half shaft nut lock with a tool.

    • The half shaft nut is a disposable part, and new parts must be used during installation.

  5. Remove 1 fixing pin of mechanical steering gear c/w tie rod assembly.

  6. Detach the mechanical steering gear c/w tie rod assembly from the front left steering knuckle.

    1. Remove 1 fixing nut.

      • Tightening torque: 50±5 N•m

    2. Remove the ball joint with a ball joint removal tool.

  7. Detach the front right lower control arm assembly.

    1. Remove 2 fixing nuts of the front right lower control arm assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 90 N•m

    2. Remove 1 fixing bolt of the front right lower control arm assembly.

      • Tightening torque: 90 N•m

    3. Detach the front right lower control arm assembly downward.

  8. Disengage the front right CV joint from the front right steering knuckle.

  9. Remove the front right CV joint assembly with a special tool (tool name: CV joint removal tooling; material No.: 13462005-00). Refer to Special Tools Manual

Installation

Caution
Use special tool (Tool Name: half shaft seal sleeve; Item No.: 13461864-00) to prevent oil seal damage to the half shaft during installation. See "Special Tools Manual"

  1. Install it in the reverse order of removal.
