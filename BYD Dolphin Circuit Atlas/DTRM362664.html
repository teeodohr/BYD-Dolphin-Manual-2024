Common Clamping Structure of Connectors

General clamping structure

  • Among the connectors shown below, the most common clamping type consists of male and female sheaths locked by clamping points and clips. During assembly, align the connector, plug it slowly, and then clamp it firmly in place. Generally, there will be a "click" sound when the clamping point bounces up (If there is no "click" sound, during disassembly, press the clamping point to release it from the clip, and then pull out the connector).

Connector with retention tab

  • As shown in the figure, the following connector features a retention tab. During assembly, withdraw the retention tab, insert the connector in place and then push the retention tab. During disassembly, withdraw the retention tab first, and then pull out the connector.
